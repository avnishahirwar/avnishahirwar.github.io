DMCA and Copyright Policy

How Friendly Calculator protects original work and handles copyright concerns.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Friendly Calculator respects copyright and expects visitors, contributors and partners to do the same. The website's calculator explanations, editorial guides, design elements, software and original illustrations are created for this service unless a page identifies a third-party source or licence.

Copyright concern? Send a complete notice to avnishahirwar415@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

What to include in a notice

To help us review a claim, include the claimant's name and contact details, a description of the copyrighted work, the exact Friendly Calculator URL or location of the material, an explanation of why you believe the use is not authorised, and a statement that the information supplied is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

If you are acting for the copyright owner, explain your authority to do so. A notice should identify the specific material rather than making a general complaint about an entire website. We may request clarification when a notice is incomplete or concerns a calculator formula, factual idea or public-domain concept rather than protected expression.

Review and response

We review credible notices and may remove, restrict or replace material while investigating. We may contact the page owner or request additional information. A notice does not automatically establish infringement, and we do not promise a particular outcome or response time. Where appropriate, we may restore material after receiving a valid counter-notice or evidence of permission.

We may retain a notice and related correspondence for recordkeeping, abuse prevention and legal compliance. Personal information in a notice is handled under the Privacy Policy.

Original content and third-party material

We aim to write original calculator guides and use lawful references. Facts, formulas, standards, government information and common mathematical methods are not claimed as private ownership merely because they appear on this site. Third-party names and logos remain the property of their respective owners. Links to another website do not mean Friendly Calculator copied or republishes that site's protected expression.

If you believe a page contains material that was unintentionally copied, identify the relevant passage and the original source. We will review it rather than relying on automated similarity claims alone.
