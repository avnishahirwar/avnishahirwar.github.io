How this calculator works

This tool applies a mathematical estimate to the values and assumptions entered above. Check the labels, units and method before relying on the result. Where a rate, fee, tax treatment or local conversion factor is requested, enter the value that applies to your situation.

The calculator does not connect to banks, card issuers, government portals, utility providers or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, official tariff treatment, tax classification or credit decisions.

Formula and calculation method

Credit utilization (%) = current outstanding balance ÷ total available credit limit × 100.

Unused available credit = total available credit limit − current outstanding balance.

Worked example

With ₹25,000 outstanding and ₹1,00,000 total available credit, the ratio is ₹25,000 ÷ ₹1,00,000 × 100 = 25%. Unused available credit is ₹75,000.

Enter both amounts in the same currency. The total available credit limit must be greater than zero. If the balance is higher than the limit, the result can exceed 100% and unused available credit will be negative.

What this result does not show

This calculator reports only the ratio from the two values you enter. It does not calculate a credit score, predict an approval decision or recommend borrowing.

Credit utilization questions

How do I calculate credit utilization?

Divide the current outstanding balance by the total available credit limit, then multiply by 100.

Can the result be above 100%?

Yes. If the balance you enter is greater than the available credit limit, the percentage can exceed 100%.

Does this calculator estimate a credit score?

No. It calculates a percentage only and does not model a credit scoring system or a lender decision.
