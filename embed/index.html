How to add it

  1. Select the calculator that fits your article.
  2. Copy the iframe snippet above.
  3. Paste it into your website's HTML or a custom HTML block.
  4. Preview the page on desktop and mobile before publishing.

The calculator runs on Friendly Calculator. Your site does not receive visitor inputs or calculation data from this embed. Do not place the iframe inside another iframe if your CMS can insert it directly.

What is a calculator embed?

A calculator embed lets you display a Friendly Calculator tool inside a page on your own website. Your visitors can use the calculator without leaving your article or resource page. The calculator itself is served from Friendly Calculator in a responsive iframe, so you do not need to copy its calculation code or maintain a separate calculator implementation.

Use an embed when a calculator directly supports the topic of your page—for example, an EMI calculator in a home-loan guide, a GST calculator in an invoicing tutorial, or a SIP calculator in an investing explainer. Choose the tool that matches the question your readers are trying to answer.

Privacy, attribution and compatibility

The embedded calculator runs on Friendly Calculator. Calculator inputs and results are processed by the calculator in the visitor's browser; the embed builder does not ask a publisher to collect a visitor's name, email address or phone number. Your website should explain any separate analytics or data collection that it operates outside the calculator.

Keep the visible “Powered by Friendly Calculator” attribution link in the supplied snippet. The iframe uses a responsive width, lazy loading and a descriptive title. Check the result on a phone and desktop after adding it to your page. Some content-management systems restrict iframe HTML; in that case, use the platform's approved custom-HTML or embed block.

How to choose the right calculator

  • For borrowing: choose a Loan EMI or Home Loan EMI calculator for estimating periodic repayments.
  • For tax and billing: choose the GST or Income Tax calculator that matches the jurisdiction and assumptions explained in your article.
  • For savings: choose SIP, FD or RD tools according to the contribution or deposit method being discussed.
  • For Hindi-language readers: select a Hindi calculator where one is available, and make sure the surrounding instructions on your page use the same language.

Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values and assumptions entered. They are educational tools, not a substitute for professional tax, legal, lending or investment advice. Explain relevant rates, dates and assumptions in your own content.

Frequently asked questions

Is the calculator embed free to use?

The calculator options shown in this builder are provided for free embedding. Keep the supplied Friendly Calculator attribution link with the embed code.

Does the iframe adapt to mobile screens?

The iframe code uses a 100% width so it can fit the width of its parent content area. The height is set in the snippet; adjust it if your page layout needs more or less vertical space, then test on mobile.

Do I need to host the calculator files?

No. The iframe loads the selected calculator from Friendly Calculator. Your site only needs to include the iframe snippet in a location where its content-management system permits embedded HTML.

Why does the embed URL contain a src parameter?

The src parameter tells the embed page which published calculator to display. Different calculator selections use the same embed-builder page, so these parameter variations share the canonical page at friendlycalculator.in/embed/.
