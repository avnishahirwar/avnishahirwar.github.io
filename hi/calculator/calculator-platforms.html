Online Calculator Platforms: Features & Our Roadmap

Calculator websites solve different problems. Some focus on a very broad library of everyday tools, some specialize in interactive graphing, and others teach mathematics through worked solutions. Friendly Calculator is an independent project focused on practical free tools, with particular attention to Indian finance, tax and everyday use cases.

Reviewed 15 November 2026. Competitor catalogue counts and feature descriptions are snapshots from the linked official pages and may change.

What Friendly Calculator is prioritizing

PriorityProduct directionRelease discipline
India-first finance and taxEMI, SIP, FD, GST, income tax, HRA, salary and provident-fund workflows with financial-year labels and relevant Indian assumptions.Rates and legal rules must cite primary sources, show effective dates and be rechecked before release.
Regional calculatorsState-specific Professional Tax, electricity tariffs by consumer category/DISCOM, land measurement conversions and property registration estimates.Do not publish regional constants or charges until official schedules, scope and exceptions are verified.
Learning-oriented resultsFormula explanations, input definitions, worked examples, step-by-step arithmetic and clear limitations.Every worked example must reconcile with the calculator formula; no fabricated reviewers or guarantees.
Interactive controlsSliders, charts, comparison scenarios, amortization schedules and share/print options where useful.Keep numeric inputs accessible and preserve keyboard/mobile usability.
Hindi and localizationHindi-first explanations and carefully translated calculator interfaces, then additional languages.Translate meaning, units and legal terminology; test calculations and layout per locale rather than relying on unreviewed machine translation.
Trust and transparencyEditorial policy, source links, updated dates, privacy explanations, corrections channel and methodology notes.Never imply a live feed, independent audit, professional approval or guaranteed result unless verified.

What is not being claimed

This page does not claim that Friendly Calculator already has a full symbolic algebra engine, AI math tutor, live bank-rate feed, nationwide automated tax coverage, every DISCOM tariff, or complete Hindi translation of every tool. Those capabilities require separate implementation, authoritative data, testing and privacy review. A roadmap item is not a released feature.

We do not copy competitor content or imply affiliation with other calculator, graphing or mathematics platforms discussed on this page.

Help improve the tools: Report a calculation issue or suggest an India-specific calculator through the Contact & Support page. Do not send passwords, OTPs, bank credentials or identity numbers.
