Metacam Dosage Calculator for Dogs

Arithmetic aid only: enter the dose already prescribed by your veterinarian. The volume result applies only to the U.S. 1.5 mg/mL oral suspension; this page cannot decide whether meloxicam is appropriate.

Enter values

How the calculation works

Prescribed amount (mg) = dog weight (kg) × veterinarian-prescribed dose (mg/kg). Volume (mL) = amount (mg) ÷ 1.5 mg/mL. No default dose is supplied.

The cited FDA label identifies the 1.5 mg/mL canine oral product and its prescription status. The interface intentionally requires the individual prescribed mg/kg value and does not prefill label directions.

Worked example

Symbolic arithmetic only: for weight W kg and veterinarian-prescribed dose D mg/kg, amount = W × D mg; with the scoped 1.5 mg/mL product, volume = amount ÷ 1.5. No dose value is recommended or prefilled.

Important limits

Prescription-drug arithmetic only. Verify the exact dispensed product, concentration, route and veterinarian instructions. Do not use this page to start/change/repeat treatment or assess an overdose; seek urgent veterinary help for an incorrect dose or adverse signs.
