Estimate vapour pressure from Antoine equation coefficients and temperature in degrees Celsius.

Formula

Antoine equation: log₁₀(P) = A − B/(C + T), with pressure P in mmHg for the supplied coefficient set.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change the inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Adjust one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

Antoine coefficients are substance- and temperature-range-specific. Enter a compatible coefficient set and confirm its pressure units and valid range; default values are for water over a limited temperature range.
