Formula and method

Basketball Game Score (Hollinger-style simplified box-score form) = PTS + 0.4×FGM − 0.7×FGA − 0.4×(FTA−FTM) + 0.7×ORB + 0.3×DRB + STL + 0.7×AST + 0.7×BLK − 0.4×PF − TO. Since separate offensive/defensive rebounds and fouls are not collected, this page reports a clearly labeled partial indicator, not the full statistic.

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite and use the stated units. Zero is rejected where a denominator must be positive. Results are estimates where noted; they are not medical, coaching, equipment-safety, or competition-rule advice. No values are sent to a server.
