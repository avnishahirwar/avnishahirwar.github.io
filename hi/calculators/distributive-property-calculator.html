Distributive Property Calculator

Enter one multiplier and numeric terms. The calculator applies the multiplier to each term and checks the evaluated total; it is a numeric demonstration, not a symbolic algebra parser.

Formula and method

For numeric terms b₁, b₂, …, a(b₁ + b₂ + …) = ab₁ + ab₂ + … . A negative term keeps its sign, so 3(2 − 4) = 3×2 + 3×(−4).

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

For 3(2 + 4), distribute 3: (3×2) + (3×4) = 6 + 12 = 18. Direct evaluation gives 3×(2+4)=18, so both sides agree.

Scope and limitations

  • This tool accepts numeric values only; it does not parse variables, powers or symbolic expressions.
  • It demonstrates distribution over addition and subtraction; distribution does not apply as a general rule over multiplication or arbitrary operations.
  • When using measured quantities, terms inside parentheses must have compatible units; multiplication scales each term.
  • Floating-point arithmetic can round long decimals.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter algebra variables such as x?

No. This page deliberately accepts numeric terms only and does not execute or parse user-supplied code.

How do I subtract a term?

Enter it as a negative number, for example 2, -4. The negative sign is carried into the distributed product.
