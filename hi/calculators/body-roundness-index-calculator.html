Body Roundness Index Calculator

Calculate the mathematical Body Roundness Index (BRI) from waist circumference and height. This page provides no diagnosis, cutoffs or health recommendations.

Calculator

Measure and enter both dimensions in centimetres. The output is an educational index only.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

BRI = 364.2 − 365.5 × √(1 − {[(waist in cm ÷ 2π)²] ÷ [(0.5 × height in cm)²]}). Enter waist and height in centimetres.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

For waist 85 cm and height 170 cm: the equation gives BRI ≈ 3.36 index points. This number is not a health category.

Assumptions and limitations

  • BRI is an anthropometric research index, not a diagnosis, body-fat measurement, or individual risk assessment.
  • This page deliberately gives no cutoffs, healthy/unhealthy labels, or recommendations. Do not use the number to make medical decisions.
  • Measurement technique, population and model validation affect interpretation; consult a qualified health professional for personal questions.
