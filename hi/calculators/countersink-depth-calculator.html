Countersink Depth Calculator

Estimate the ideal depth of a conical countersink from its diameters and included angle. This is geometry only, not a machining or structural instruction.

Calculator

Enter both diameters in the same unit. Select the display unit; the numeric values are not converted.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

For an ideal cone, depth h = (D − d) ÷ [2 tan(θ/2)], where D is the countersink diameter at the surface, d is the pilot-hole diameter and θ is the included angle. D and d must use the same length unit.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

With D = 12 mm, d = 5 mm and θ = 90°: h = (12 − 5) ÷ [2 tan(45°)] = 3.5 mm.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The formula assumes a perfectly conical, coaxial recess and measures depth from the surface to the pilot-hole edge.
  • Use the fastener and tool manufacturer’s specified angle and tolerances. Material, burrs, tool geometry, runout and setup affect the actual cut.
  • This result is not a machining procedure, structural design, fastener recommendation or safety certification.
