Simple Pendulum Calculator

Estimate ideal small-angle pendulum period from length and local gravitational acceleration.

Enter values

Formula and method

T = 2π√(L/g). Inputs must use the units stated above. This calculator performs only the displayed idealized calculation; it does not model unlisted effects.

Worked sample

For L = 1 m and g = 9.80665 m/s², T ≈ 2.007 s.

Validation and limitations

Inputs are checked for finite values and physically required positive quantities. Results are browser-only and are educational estimates; verify assumptions and units before practical use. This is not an engineering or safety certification tool.
