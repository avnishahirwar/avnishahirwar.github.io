Formula and definitions

Effective rent = monthly rent × 12 × (1 − vacancy rate). Net operating income = effective rent − operating expenses. Gross yield = scheduled annual rent ÷ purchase price. Cap rate = NOI ÷ total property cost. Cash-on-cash return = annual cash flow after debt service ÷ cash invested, where cash invested is the down payment plus entered purchase costs and improvements.

This is a screening estimate, not a property valuation, lender approval or investment recommendation. It excludes income tax, appreciation, principal paydown, refinancing, major capital expenditure unless entered, legal duties and market-specific rules.
