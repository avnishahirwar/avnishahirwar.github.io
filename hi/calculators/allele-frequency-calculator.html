Allele Frequency Calculator

Calculate allele frequencies p and q from counts of two homozygous and one heterozygous genotype.

Enter your values

Formula

p = (2 × AA + Aa) / (2N); q = (2 × aa + Aa) / (2N); p + q = 1.

How to use this calculator

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Change one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

This calculator derives sample allele frequencies from genotype counts. It does not test Hardy–Weinberg equilibrium, population structure, selection or sampling uncertainty.
