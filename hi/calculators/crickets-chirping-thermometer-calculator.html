Cricket Chirp Temperature Estimator

Enter the number of chirps counted in 15 seconds. This uses NOAA’s approximate field rule and is not a calibrated thermometer.

Count one caller for a full 15-second interval when possible. The rule is approximate and source-specific.

Formula

Approximate temperature (°F) = chirps counted in 15 seconds + 40. Convert to Celsius with °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. The Library of Congress describes a different +37°F rule; this page consistently uses NOAA’s convention.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

For 25 chirps in 15 seconds: 25 + 40 = 65°F; (65 − 32) × 5/9 ≈ 18.33°C.

Assumptions and limitations

  • This is an informal rule of thumb, not a calibrated or reliable substitute for a thermometer.
  • Different sources report different constants (+40°F from NOAA and +37°F from the Library of Congress); the displayed result follows NOAA.
  • Cricket species, location, time, background noise, overlapping calls and counting error affect the result.
