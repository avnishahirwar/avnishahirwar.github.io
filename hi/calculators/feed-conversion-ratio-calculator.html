How the calculation works

Feed conversion ratio (FCR) = total feed mass consumed ÷ liveweight gain, where liveweight gain = ending liveweight − starting liveweight. Feed basis (as-fed or dry matter) must be reported explicitly.

University Extension and a peer-reviewed beef-production study define FCR as feed consumed per unit of bodyweight gain. FAO guidance shows that aquaculture FCR conventions and feed/product bases differ.

Worked example

If the same animal or defined group consumes 10 kg of as-fed feed and liveweight rises from 40 kg to 45 kg during the same period, gain = 5 kg and FCR = 10 ÷ 5 = 2 kg as-fed feed/kg liveweight gain.

Important limits

Descriptive ratio only; this page gives no target, benchmark, ration assessment, or feed recommendation. Feed consumed and liveweight change must describe the same animal or defined group over the same period. Do not compare results with different feed bases or production boundaries. This simple calculation does not adjust for feed wastage, mortality, natural feed, or weighing variation. Zero or negative gain does not produce a conventional positive-gain FCR.
