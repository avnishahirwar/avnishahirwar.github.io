How the calculation works

Elapsed days = current date − recorded heat-start date. The illustrative reference window adds 5 to 11 calendar months to the recorded start; it is not an individual prediction.

Cornell reports that heat frequency commonly varies and gives a broad typical interval; Merck describes stage-duration variation.

Worked example

If the recorded heat start is 1 January, the broad 5–11 month reference interval runs from 1 June to 1 December of that year. It is not an individual prediction or fertile-window estimate.

Important limits

The reference interval does not identify ovulation, fertility, pregnancy, or a safe mating date. Do not rely on a calendar to prevent pregnancy or schedule breeding/spaying. Ask a veterinarian for individual advice.
