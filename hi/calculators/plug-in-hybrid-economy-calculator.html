Plug-in Hybrid Economy Calculator

Estimate energy/fuel use and emissions from electric and combustion driving shares.

Enter values

Formula and method

EV km × kWh/km × grid intensity + petrol km × L/km × kg CO₂/L

Worked sample

100 km EV × 0.18 × 0.4 + 100 km petrol × 0.06 × 2.3 = 21 kg

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Operating-use estimate only; excludes vehicle manufacture, upstream fuel and battery effects. Factors vary by grid, fuel and driving.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
