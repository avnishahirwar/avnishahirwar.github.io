Solar Savings & Rooftop Solar ROI Calculator

Estimate rooftop solar generation, monthly energy savings, simple payback and long-term modeled benefit. Enter local assumptions from your electricity bill and installer quotation.

Your solar project assumptions

Illustrative estimate only. Peak-sun hours, performance ratio, tariff and export settlement must be checked for your site and DISCOM. Fixed charges, demand charges, taxes, financing, battery costs, maintenance, inverter replacement and changing policy are not automatically included.

How the estimate works

Monthly generation = system size × peak-sun hours × days × performance ratio.

Self-consumption savings = solar units used at home × your grid tariff.

Export credit = surplus units × the export rate you enter. Actual net-metering or net-billing rules vary by DISCOM and connection category.

Simple payback is the first modeled month when cumulative savings equal net installation cost. Long-term estimates apply the annual tariff escalation and solar degradation assumptions you provide.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official subsidy calculator?

No. Enter only an incentive amount confirmed through the current government scheme portal, DISCOM or authorised vendor. Eligibility, caps and application windows can change.

Will my electricity bill become zero?

Not necessarily. Fixed charges, taxes, duties and other bill components may remain even when solar offsets much of your energy use.

Can I use this for Madhya Pradesh?

Yes, as a scenario estimator. Use the applicable MPERC/DISCOM tariff, approved export-credit arrangement and site-specific generation assumptions. This page does not claim a universal Madhya Pradesh solar yield or export rate.
