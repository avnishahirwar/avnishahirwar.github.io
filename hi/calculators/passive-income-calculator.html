How the estimate works

Required corpus = future annual income ÷ entered withdrawal rate. The future annual income grows with inflation. The accumulation model compounds current assets and monthly contributions at the assumed return and compares the balance with the future corpus required at each month.

A withdrawal rate is not a promise of sustainable income. Taxes, fees, sequence-of-returns risk, longevity, market losses, healthcare, changing spending and product terms are not fully modelled. Review important decisions with a qualified professional.
