ABV Calculator (Alcohol by Volume)

Enter pre-fermentation original gravity and completed-fermentation final gravity. The primary method estimates alcohol by weight and converts it to an approximate ABV.

Formula and method

ABW (%) = 76.08 × (OG − FG) ÷ (1.775 − OG). Estimated ABV (%) = ABW × (FG ÷ 0.794). A common quick approximation is (OG − FG) × 131.25; it may differ from the primary estimate.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

For OG 1.048 and FG 1.012: ABW = 76.08 × (1.048 − 1.012) ÷ (1.775 − 1.048) ≈ 3.7674%; ABV ≈ 3.7674 × (1.012 ÷ 0.794) = 4.80%. The quick approximation gives about 4.73%.

Scope and limitations

  • This is an estimate for a fermented beverage, not a laboratory measurement or a regulatory labeling method.
  • Gravity readings should be corrected for hydrometer calibration temperature. Refractometer readings after fermentation require an alcohol correction.
  • Final gravity should represent completed fermentation; incomplete fermentation or measurement error can change the estimate.
  • This tool does not estimate intoxication, impairment, safe consumption or an individual health risk.

Frequently asked questions

What do OG and FG mean?

OG is the specific gravity before fermentation; FG is the specific gravity after fermentation. Use readings from the same batch.

Why do two ABV formulas give slightly different results?

The linear 131.25 formula is a shortcut. The primary equation on this page estimates ABW and converts it to ABV, so small differences are expected.
