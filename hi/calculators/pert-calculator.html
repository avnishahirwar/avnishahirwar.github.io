PERT Calculator (Three-Point Estimate)

Use three estimates in the same unit to calculate one activity’s weighted expected duration. This is not a complete project schedule or completion-date forecast.

Calculator

Use the same time unit for all three estimates and order them from optimistic to pessimistic.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula and method

PERT weighted expected activity duration = (O + 4M + P) ÷ 6, where O is optimistic, M is most likely and P is pessimistic duration. All estimates must use the same unit.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

For O = 2 days, M = 4 days and P = 8 days: (2 + 4×4 + 8) ÷ 6 = 26 ÷ 6 ≈ 4.33 days.

Scope and limitations

  • This is a simplified estimate for one activity; the quality of the inputs determines its usefulness.
  • The weighted mean is not a guaranteed duration or a complete schedule model. It does not account for dependencies, resource constraints or critical paths.
  • Use realistic bounds and the same units for all three estimates.
  • Do not interpret a single activity estimate as a project finish-date forecast.

Frequently asked questions

Does PERT predict the project completion date?

No. This calculator estimates one activity. A project forecast also needs task dependencies, resources, parallel work and critical-path analysis.

Why is the most-likely estimate weighted by four?

The common PERT weighted mean gives the most-likely estimate four times the weight of either endpoint; it is a simplified three-point estimating model.
