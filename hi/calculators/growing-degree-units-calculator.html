Growing Degree Units (GDU) Calculator

Enter paired daily maximum and minimum temperatures, choose a crop-specific base temperature, and optionally apply an upper cutoff. The page labels the calculation method and does not choose a universal crop threshold.

Formula and method

For each day, adjusted high = the lower of the daily high and optional upper cutoff; adjusted low = the higher of the daily low and selected base temperature. Daily GDU = max(0, [(adjusted high + adjusted low) ÷ 2] − base temperature). Accumulated GDU is the sum of daily values. This is the modified maximum/minimum method when both cutoffs are used.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

For a corn illustration with high 80°F, low 60°F and selected base 50°F, the mean is (80 + 60) ÷ 2 = 70°F and daily GDU = 70 − 50 = 20°F·day. Crop thresholds and cutoff methods vary; the sample is not a default for every crop.

Scope and limitations

  • GDU is an approximate heat-accumulation index, not a direct measurement or guaranteed forecast of biological development.
  • Base temperatures and upper cutoffs depend on the crop, pest and model. Select values appropriate to the intended method; do not treat the sample 50°F base as universal.
  • Moisture, cultivar, field conditions, measurement quality and other factors are not included.
  • Use one temperature scale consistently; Fahrenheit degree-days and Celsius degree-days are not numerically interchangeable. The tool accepts up to 90 paired days.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the base temperature selectable?

Different crops and models use different thresholds. Select the threshold appropriate to your source and label the method used.

What does an upper cutoff do?

Some modified methods cap unusually high daily temperatures before averaging. Leave it blank when the selected method does not use a cap.
