Shannon Diversity Index Calculator

Calculate Shannon diversity H′ from species counts using natural logarithms.

Enter values

Formula and method

H′ = −Σ pᵢ ln(pᵢ), where pᵢ = countᵢ / total

Worked sample

Counts 10, 10 → H′ = ln(2) ≈ 0.693

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Counts must be nonnegative integers. The index depends on sampling effort and taxonomic identification; compare only suitable samples.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
