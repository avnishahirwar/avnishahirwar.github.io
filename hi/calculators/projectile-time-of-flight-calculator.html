Projectile Time of Flight Calculator

Calculate the equal-elevation flight time from initial speed, launch angle and gravitational acceleration. The model ignores air resistance and assumes constant gravity.

Formula and method

For launch and landing at the same elevation with no air resistance: T = 2v₀ sin(θ) ÷ g. Here v₀ is initial speed, θ is measured above the horizontal, and g is the positive magnitude of gravitational acceleration.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

For v₀ = 20 m/s, θ = 30° and g = 9.81 m/s²: T = 2×20×sin(30°) ÷ 9.81 = 40×0.5 ÷ 9.81 ≈ 2.04 seconds.

Scope and limitations

  • Assumes launch and landing are at the same elevation. Use a different vertical-motion equation if the elevations differ.
  • Neglects air resistance, wind, lift, spin and changes in gravity.
  • Use SI units: m/s for speed and m/s² for gravity.
  • This is an educational idealized estimate, not a real-world safety or trajectory prediction.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for a projectile landing at a different height?

No. The compact formula assumes equal launch and landing elevations.

Does the calculator include drag?

No. Air resistance and other forces are omitted from this idealized model.
