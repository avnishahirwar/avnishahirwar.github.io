Study Calculator

Estimate focused study time, planned breaks and weekly study time from the sessions you choose. Use it to organize a balanced schedule, not as a target to keep increasing study hours.

Plan your study sessions

Focused study time per day—Enter your session plan to see the schedule estimate.

How the calculation works

Daily focus time = sessions × minutes per session. Daily break time = (sessions − 1) × break minutes. Weekly totals = daily totals × study days per week.

The estimate assumes one break between consecutive sessions and no additional breaks before the first or after the last session. Actual schedules can vary.

Take breaks, leave room for meals, sleep, exercise and other responsibilities. There is no universal number of study hours that suits everyone; adjust the plan to your age, workload and wellbeing.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator recommend how long I should study?

No. It only totals the sessions and breaks you enter. Choose a manageable schedule and make time for rest.

Are breaks included in focused study time?

No. Focus time and break time are shown separately, along with the total scheduled time.

Can I use it for a weekly timetable?

Yes. Enter the number of study days per week to estimate weekly focused time, break time and total scheduled time.
