How the calculation works

Prescribed amount (mg) = dog weight (kg) × veterinarian-prescribed dose (mg/kg). Liquid volume (mL) = amount (mg) ÷ exact dispensed concentration (mg/mL).

VCA describes tramadol as veterinarian-directed/off-label in dogs; Merck lists overdose signs but does not provide a public canine toxic-dose threshold.

Worked example

Symbolic arithmetic only: for weight W kg and the exact prescribed dose D mg/kg, amount = W × D mg. If the dispensed liquid is labeled C mg/mL, volume = amount ÷ C. No dose value is recommended or prefilled.

Important limits

No dose recommendation or toxicity threshold is provided. Use only an exact veterinary prescription and the dispensed product label. For a suspected extra/incorrect dose, tremors, seizures, unusual sleepiness, agitation, or poor coordination, contact a veterinarian/emergency service immediately.
