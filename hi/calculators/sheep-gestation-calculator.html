How the calculation works

Estimated lambing date = service date + 147 calendar days. The illustrative range shown is service date + 144 to +151 days.

Montana State University reports a common 144–151 day range and an average near 147 days; Merck gives a similar normal range and notes service dates may be uncertain in flock settings.

Worked example

For a recorded service date of 1 January 2026, the 147-day estimate is 28 May 2026; the 144–151 day planning window is 25 May–1 June 2026.

Important limits

A planning estimate only; it does not confirm pregnancy or predict the exact birth date. Ask a veterinarian about concerning signs or overdue pregnancy.
