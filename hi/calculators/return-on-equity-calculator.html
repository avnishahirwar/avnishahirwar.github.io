Formula and methodology

ROE = Net income for the period ÷ Average shareholders' equity × 100. Average equity is generally the average of opening and closing equity.

Keep all inputs on the same reporting date, accounting period and currency basis. Results are arithmetic estimates, not investment recommendations or an assessment of a company's quality.

Worked example

Net income of ₹1.2 crore divided by average shareholders' equity of ₹8 crore gives ROE of 15%.
