Dream Reflection Calculator

Turn your own dream-journal notes into a simple reflection score. The result summarizes your selected recall, vividness and emotional clarity ratings; it does not decode dreams or predict events.

Rate your dream-journal entry

Your self-rated reflection score—Each rating contributes equally.

How the score works

Reflection score = (recall + vividness + emotional clarity) ÷ 15 × 100.

Each dimension is self-rated from 0 to 5 and weighted equally. The score is only a journaling aid. It is not a validated sleep, mental-health or personality measure.

Dreams do not have a scientifically established universal symbol dictionary. This calculator does not predict the future, diagnose mental-health conditions or assign hidden meanings to a dream.

Frequently asked questions

Does a high score mean a dream has a special meaning?

No. It only means you rated the entry as easier to recall, more vivid or easier to describe emotionally.

Can dreams predict the future?

This tool makes no prediction. Use it as a personal writing prompt, not as evidence about future events.
