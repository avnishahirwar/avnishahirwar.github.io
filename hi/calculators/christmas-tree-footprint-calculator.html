Christmas Tree Footprint Calculator

Compare an artificial or natural tree’s lifetime footprint using your own assumptions.

Enter values

Formula and method

Annualized footprint = total life-cycle emissions ÷ years used

Worked sample

Artificial tree: 30 kg over 10 years → 3 kg CO₂e/year

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Illustrative values only; actual impacts depend on sourcing, transport, reuse and disposal. Compare equivalent system boundaries.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
