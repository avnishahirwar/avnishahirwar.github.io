Working Days Calculator

Count weekdays between two dates, optionally excluding public or company holidays entered by you.

Choose the date range

How working days are counted

The start date and end date are both included in the selected range. Monday to Friday are counted as working days. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded.

To account for public, bank or company holidays, enter each applicable date in the holiday field using YYYY-MM-DD format. A holiday is subtracted only when it falls on a weekday; a holiday on a Saturday or Sunday is already excluded as a weekend.

Worked example

For a Monday-to-Friday range with no holidays entered, each weekday in the selected range is counted. If you enter a Wednesday as a custom holiday, that date is excluded from the working-day total.

Coverage and limitations

The calculator does not download national, state, bank or company holiday calendars. It cannot determine which holidays apply to a particular employer, school, court or region. Check the applicable official or organisational calendar and enter the dates yourself. The selected range must be no longer than ten years.

Worked date example

From Monday 2 November through Friday 6 November 2026, the inclusive range contains five weekdays. If Wednesday 4 November is entered as a custom holiday, four working days remain. A holiday entered on Saturday or Sunday does not reduce the total because weekends are already excluded.

Entering custom holidays

Enter each applicable date in YYYY-MM-DD format. You can separate dates with commas, spaces, semicolons or new lines. Duplicate dates are counted once. The calculator does not fetch national, state, bank, school, court or employer holiday calendars. Check the calendar that applies to your organisation, including substitute days and local observances.

Practical uses

This estimate can support an initial project schedule, an order-processing window, a service-level estimate or a simple staffing plan. Before relying on a deadline, confirm whether the organisation counts the start date, observes local holidays, closes on additional weekdays or follows a different workweek.

Frequently asked questions

Are the start and end dates included?

Yes. Both dates are included in the selected range.

Does the calculator automatically exclude Indian public holidays?

No. Add the applicable dates yourself. Holiday lists differ by state, employer, sector and year.

Can I change the weekend days?

Not in this version. Saturday and Sunday are always excluded. Alternative workweek patterns are not supported.

What happens when a holiday falls on a weekend?

It is not subtracted a second time because Saturday and Sunday are already excluded.

What is the maximum date range?

The calculator accepts ranges up to 3,660 days. This is approximately ten years; the limit is enforced by the calculation script.

Important assumptions and limitations

Results are estimates from the dates, values and assumptions you enter. This tool runs in your browser and does not connect to external accounts or live data. Verify important decisions with the relevant provider or official source.
