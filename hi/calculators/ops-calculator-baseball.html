Baseball OPS Calculator

Calculate on-base percentage (OBP), slugging percentage (SLG) and on-base plus slugging (OPS) from batting counts.

Calculator

Use whole-number batting counts from the same player and period.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

OBP = (H + BB + HBP) ÷ (AB + BB + HBP + SF). SLG = total bases ÷ AB, where total bases = 1B + 2×2B + 3×3B + 4×HR. OPS = OBP + SLG. H is the sum of singles, doubles, triples and home runs.

Worked example

With 500 AB, 80 singles, 30 doubles, 5 triples, 25 home runs, 70 walks, 5 hit-by-pitches and 5 sacrifice flies: H=140, total bases=255, OBP=215/580≈.37069, SLG=255/500=.51000, OPS≈.88069.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Enter a single consistent batting line. Hits must not exceed at-bats; walks, hit-by-pitches and sacrifice flies are not at-bats in these formulas.
  • OPS adds OBP and SLG with equal weight. It is a descriptive statistic, not a complete measure of player value; league, season, park and sample size matter.
  • Confirm official scoring definitions and data with the relevant league or scorekeeper.
