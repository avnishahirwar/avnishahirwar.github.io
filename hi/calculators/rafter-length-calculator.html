Rafter Length Calculator

Calculate geometric rafter length and roof angle from horizontal run, rise and overhang.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the horizontal run, vertical rise and horizontal overhang in inches to estimate the rafter's geometric length.

Formula and methodology

Rafter length = √((run + overhang)² + rise²). Roof angle = arctan(rise ÷ run).

Worked example

For a 120 in run, 60 in rise and 12 in overhang, the geometric length to the tail end is about 145.0 in.

Important limitations

This tool provides an approximate quantity or geometry estimate based only on the values entered. Confirm measurements, product specifications, material properties and local building requirements with an appropriately qualified professional before purchasing materials or beginning work. It is not structural engineering or code-compliance advice.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include birdsmouth cuts or structural sizing?

No. It is a geometry estimate only and does not determine safe member size, connections, bearing or code compliance.

Are results exact construction quantities?

No. Site conditions, cuts, waste, installation methods and supplier specifications can change the quantities required.
