Churn Rate Calculator

Formula and methodology

Customer churn rate = Customers lost during the period ÷ Customers at the start of the period × 100. New sign-ups are not included in this denominator.

Inputs are processed in your browser. Results are estimates based on the values and period definitions you enter. Keep all figures on the same accounting period and basis.

Worked example

If 50 of 1,000 starting customers leave, churn is 5% and period retention is 95%.
