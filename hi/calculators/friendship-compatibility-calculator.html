Friendship Compatibility Calculator

Review four friendship qualities with your own ratings. This simple reflection tool does not predict whether a friendship will last or measure another person's character.

Rate the friendship qualities

Use 1 (needs attention) to 5 (strong in your experience). No names or personal details are required.

Average self-rating—Equal weighting across four qualities.

Calculation method

Average self-rating = (trust + communication + mutual support + shared interests) ÷ 4.

This result reflects only your own ratings. It is not a scientific friendship test and cannot determine another person's intentions, loyalty or future behaviour. Healthy friendships allow both people to have boundaries, other interests and time apart.

Do not use a score to pressure someone, rank people or decide their worth. Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

Frequently asked questions

Can this calculator tell me who my best friend is?

No. Friendship is personal and cannot be reduced to a number. The average is only a prompt for reflection.

Are my answers stored?

No. This page calculates in your browser and does not submit these ratings to a server.
