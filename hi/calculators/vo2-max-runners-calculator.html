Formula and method

Daniels/VDOT-style estimate: velocity v = distance/time in m/min; VO₂ = −4.60 + 0.182258v + 0.000104v²; fractional utilization = 0.8 + 0.1894393e^(−0.012778t) + 0.2989558e^(−0.1932605t), with t in minutes; estimated VO₂max = VO₂/utilization. This approximation is intended for race-performance estimates.

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite and use the stated units. Zero is rejected where a denominator must be positive. Results are estimates where noted; they are not medical, coaching, equipment-safety, or competition-rule advice. No values are sent to a server.
