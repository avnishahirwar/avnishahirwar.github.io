Solar Panel Calculator

Estimate solar electricity output from array size, sun hours and performance ratio.

Enter values

Formula and method

Array kW × peak-sun-hours/day × 365 × performance ratio

Worked sample

5 kW × 4 h × 365 × 0.8 = 5,840 kWh/year

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Planning estimate only. Shading, tilt, weather, degradation and local solar resource affect real output; not a quote or engineering design.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
