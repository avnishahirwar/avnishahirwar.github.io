Formula and method

E = hc/λ; h = 6.62607015×10⁻³⁴ J·s, c = 299792458 m/s. Inputs must use the units stated above. This calculator performs only the displayed idealized calculation; it does not model unlisted effects.

Worked sample

A 500 nm photon has energy ≈ 3.97×10⁻¹⁹ J.

Validation and limitations

Inputs are checked for finite values and physically required positive quantities. Results are browser-only and are educational estimates; verify assumptions and units before practical use. This is not an engineering or safety certification tool.
