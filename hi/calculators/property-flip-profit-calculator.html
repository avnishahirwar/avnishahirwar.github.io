Formula and assumptions

Total project cost = purchase price + purchase costs + renovation + monthly holding costs × holding months. Net sale proceeds = expected sale price − selling costs − estimated tax. Profit = net sale proceeds − total project cost. Return on cash = profit ÷ total project cost. Annualized return is an illustrative compounding equivalent over the entered holding period.

This is a planning screen, not a valuation or guarantee. It does not model loan principal separately, cost overruns, delayed sale, market liquidity, legal risk, capital-gains rules, depreciation, depreciation recapture or local transaction duties beyond the values you enter.
