Dog Water Intake Calculator

Calculate one of two published maintenance-fluid reference equations for a dog. These are clinical fluid-planning estimates, not predictions of how much a dog will drink.

Enter values

How the calculation works

For 2–70 kg: 30 × body weight (kg) + 70 mL/24 h. Alternative: 132 × body weight (kg)^0.75 mL/24 h.

Merck describes both equations in its veterinary maintenance-fluid planning guidance.

Worked example

At 10 kg, the linear maintenance-fluid reference is 30 × 10 + 70 = 370 mL/24 h. This is not a drinking-water target.

Important limits

Not an individual drinking-water requirement, diagnosis, hydration check, or fluid-treatment prescription. Do not restrict water or administer fluids based on the output. Seek veterinary advice for sudden drinking changes or illness.
