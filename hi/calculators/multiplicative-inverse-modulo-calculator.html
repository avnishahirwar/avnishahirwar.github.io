Multiplicative Inverse Modulo Calculator

Find the least non-negative multiplicative inverse x for an integer a modulo m. The inverse exists only when gcd(a,m) = 1.

Calculator

The tool returns the least non-negative inverse, if one exists.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

Find integers u and v with au + mv = gcd(a,m) using the extended Euclidean algorithm. If gcd(a,m)=1, the inverse is x = u mod m, normalized to 0 ≤ x < m; otherwise no inverse exists.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

For a = 17 and m = 43, 17 × 38 = 646 = 15 × 43 + 1. Therefore 38 is the inverse of 17 modulo 43.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Inputs are integers; modulus m must be from 2 through 1,000,000,000,000, and |a| may not exceed that bound.
  • An inverse does not exist when a and m are not coprime. The tool reports that condition rather than returning a remainder as an inverse.
  • This educational arithmetic tool is not cryptographic security advice or a vetted cryptographic implementation.
