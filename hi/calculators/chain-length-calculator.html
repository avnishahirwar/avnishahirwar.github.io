Formula and method

Common two-chainring sizing approximation: links = 2C + (F+R)/4 + 1, where C = chainstay length ÷ 12.7 mm. Round up to an even link count.

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite and use the stated units. Zero is rejected where a denominator must be positive. Results are estimates where noted; they are not medical, coaching, equipment-safety, or competition-rule advice. No values are sent to a server.
