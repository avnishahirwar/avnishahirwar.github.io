How this wealth planner works

The model compounds current wealth annually and adds the annual saving at the end of each year. It solves for the first year in which the projected balance reaches the target. The passive-income estimate is target wealth multiplied by the withdrawal-rate assumption.

This is an educational scenario tool, not an investment recommendation or a promise of returns. Billionaire-scale wealth often depends on ownership, business value, liquidity, taxes, concentration risk and other factors this simple model does not measure.
