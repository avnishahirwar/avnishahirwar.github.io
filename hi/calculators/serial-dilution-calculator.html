Serial Dilution Calculator

Calculate the final concentration after repeated equal-step serial dilutions.

Enter your values

Formula

Final concentration = starting concentration / (dilution factor ^ number of steps).

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values using the units shown beside each field.
  2. Select the relevant option where a dropdown is provided.
  3. Choose Calculate to see the estimated result and intermediate values.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Change one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

Assumes each step uses the same dilution factor and transfers are performed accurately with complete mixing. It does not model pipetting error, adsorption or sample loss.

Updated: 17 November 2026. This educational calculator provides an estimate from the inputs and formula shown. Verify important laboratory or research work independently.
