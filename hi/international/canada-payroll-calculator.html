2026 official assumptions

  • CPP basic exemption: $3,500; YMPE: $74,600; YAMPE: $85,000.
  • CPP base plus first additional contribution: 5.95%; CPP2: 4% between $74,600 and $85,000.
  • EI maximum insurable earnings: $68,900.
  • Outside Quebec: employee EI 1.63%, employer EI 2.282%.
  • Quebec: employee EI 1.30%, employer EI 1.82%.

This is an annual contribution estimate. It excludes federal/provincial income-tax withholding, QPP/QPIP, pensionable/insurable exceptions, multiple employers, pay-period rounding and EI opt-in for self-employed people.
