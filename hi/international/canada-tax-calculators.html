Frequently asked questions

Are CPP2 contributions included?

Yes. The calculator includes the 2026 CPP contribution up to the year's maximum pensionable earnings and CPP2 between $74,600 and $85,000.

Does the RRSP calculator determine my official contribution room?

No. It estimates new room from 18% of prior-year earned income, the 2026 dollar cap, a pension adjustment and unused room. Check your CRA Notice of Assessment for your official limit.

Does the GST/HST calculator use provincial rates?

Yes. It loads standard GST/HST and selected PST rates by province. Place-of-supply, product type, exemptions and special rules may change the tax due.
