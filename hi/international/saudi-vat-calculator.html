Frequently asked questions

What is the standard VAT rate in Saudi Arabia?

ZATCA states that the standard VAT rate is 15%, while some supplies may be zero-rated or exempt.

Does this calculator determine the correct tax treatment for every product?

No. Product classification, exemptions, zero-rating and place-of-supply rules must be checked separately.
