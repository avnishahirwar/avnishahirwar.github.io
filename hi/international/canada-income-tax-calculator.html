2026 federal assumptions

  • Federal brackets: 14%, 20.5%, 26%, 29% and 33% on progressive portions of taxable income.
  • Federal basic personal amount: $16,452 maximum, tapering to $14,829 at higher income.
  • Employment amount: up to $1,501, with a 14% federal credit rate.
  • Tax year: 1 January–31 December 2026; return generally filed in 2027.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include provincial income tax?

No. It estimates federal income tax only. Provincial or territorial income tax is additional and depends on residence.

Which tax year does this calculator use?

It uses the 2026 calendar tax year for income earned in 2026 and filed in 2027.

Does this calculate my final tax return?

No. It is a simplified estimate and excludes provincial tax, most credits and deductions, surtaxes, benefits, special income and other adjustments.
