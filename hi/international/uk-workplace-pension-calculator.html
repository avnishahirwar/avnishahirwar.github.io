2026–27 qualifying earnings band

  • Lower qualifying earnings limit: £6,240 per year.
  • Upper qualifying earnings limit: £50,270 per year.
  • Usual minimum contribution rates: 5% worker and 3% employer, totalling 8% of qualifying earnings.

Qualifying earnings are the portion of annual pay above £6,240 up to £50,270. This estimate uses annualised pay and does not model pay-reference-period rounding.

Important coverage and limitations

  • This calculator models the Great Britain automatic-enrolment qualifying earnings baseline for 2026–27. Northern Ireland has separate legislation and guidance.
  • It does not determine automatic-enrolment eligibility, which also depends on age, worker status and earnings.
  • Some schemes use a different pensionable-pay definition or contribute more than the statutory minimum.
  • Salary sacrifice, tax relief method, contribution caps, bonuses timing, pay frequency and scheme-specific rules are not modelled.

Check your scheme documents and official guidance before relying on an estimate.
