How to use the rate

  1. Find the applicable combined rate for the delivery or purchase location from the relevant state and local tax authority.
  2. Enter the purchase amount before sales tax.
  3. Enter the combined rate, including applicable state, county, city and special-district components.
  4. Calculate the estimated tax and total.

Changing the display currency only changes the symbol and number formatting; it does not convert the purchase amount or update the tax jurisdiction.

Important US coverage limits

  • Sales-tax rates and taxable-product rules vary by state and local jurisdiction.
  • This tool does not automatically detect a location, look up rates, determine nexus, decide whether a product is taxable or calculate exemptions.
  • Shipping, discounts, marketplace-facilitator rules and special district taxes may change the actual taxable base or amount.
  • Use the rate and taxability guidance from the applicable state or local revenue department before relying on an estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator look up my state or city sales-tax rate?

No. Enter the combined rate supplied by the relevant state and local tax authority or shown by the seller.

Can sales-tax rates differ within a state?

Yes. State, county, city and special-district taxes can affect the combined rate, and taxability can vary by product and transaction.

Does this determine whether an item is taxable?

No. It performs arithmetic on the purchase amount and rate you enter. Product exemptions, shipping rules and special tax treatment are not determined.
