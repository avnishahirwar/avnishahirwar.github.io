Frequently asked questions

Who does this 2026 CPF estimate cover?

It covers Singapore citizens and permanent residents from their third year onwards, with monthly ordinary wages above $750.

What is the ordinary wage ceiling?

The 2026 ordinary wage ceiling is S$8,000 per calendar month. Additional wages use separate annual ceiling rules and are not included.

Does this cover first- or second-year permanent residents?

No. Graduated contribution rates for first- and second-year permanent residents are not included.
