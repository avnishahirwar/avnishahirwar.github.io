2026–27 rules

  • Employer secondary threshold: £5,000 per year.
  • Standard employer Class 1 NIC rate: 15% above the secondary threshold.
  • Effective period: 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.
  • Employment Allowance of up to £10,500 may reduce eligible employer-level liability, but is not applied here.
