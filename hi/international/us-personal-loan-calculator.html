How the estimate works

The monthly payment uses a standard fixed-rate amortization formula. The APR you enter is divided by 12 to estimate a monthly rate. The origination fee is calculated separately; net proceeds assume the fee is withheld from the disbursement, while monthly payments are based on the full loan amount.

Important limitations

  • This is a mathematical illustration, not a lender quote, credit decision or loan recommendation.
  • Actual APR, fees, eligibility, repayment dates and loan terms depend on the lender and your circumstances.
  • Some lenders calculate APR and fees differently; this estimate does not model daily interest, variable rates, late fees, prepayment penalties or add-on products.
  • Compare the total cost and all contract terms carefully; do not rely on the monthly payment alone.

Frequently asked questions

Is APR the same as the interest rate?

APR may include certain loan costs as well as interest. This calculator uses the APR you enter as the annual rate for its monthly payment estimate.

Does the calculator check my credit or show lender offers?

No. It does not access credit reports, check eligibility, compare lenders or provide a loan offer.

How is the origination fee treated?

The fee is shown separately as a percentage of the loan amount. Net proceeds are shown assuming the fee is withheld from the disbursement; the monthly payment is calculated on the full loan amount.
