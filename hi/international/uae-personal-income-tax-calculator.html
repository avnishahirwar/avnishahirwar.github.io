Frequently asked questions

Does the UAE levy personal income tax on individuals?

The UAE government states that it does not levy income tax on individuals.

Is the result the same as take-home pay?

No. It shows personal income tax only. Pension or social-security contributions, end-of-service benefits, employer arrangements, loans and other deductions are not calculated.

Does this calculator cover corporate tax?

No. Corporate tax, VAT, excise tax and business tax obligations are outside this individual income-tax estimate.
