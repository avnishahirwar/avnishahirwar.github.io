Formula and method

For a fixed monthly rate, the scheduled payment uses the standard amortization formula: payment = principal × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate)−number of payments). At 0% interest, principal is divided evenly across payments.

Extra monthly payments are applied to the outstanding balance after the scheduled payment. The final payment is capped at the remaining balance plus interest.

Important federal loan limitations

  • This is a fixed-rate mathematical estimate, not an official federal student-loan repayment estimator.
  • It does not calculate income-driven repayment, Public Service Loan Forgiveness, deferment, forbearance, subsidies, consolidation or eligibility.
  • Actual federal and private loan terms, interest accrual, fees and payment allocation may differ.
