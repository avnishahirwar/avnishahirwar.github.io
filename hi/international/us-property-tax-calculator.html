Estimate property tax

Enter your property value and local tax assumptions.

Formula and method

Assessed value = market value × assessment ratio. Estimated taxable value = max(0, assessed value − exemption). Estimated annual property tax = taxable value × effective tax rate.

Monthly equivalent = estimated annual property tax ÷ 12. Enter the combined effective rate that applies to your property.

Important local coverage limits

  • Property-tax rules, assessments, exemptions and billing schedules vary by state, county, city and special district.
  • This tool does not look up local rates, estimate future assessments or determine homestead eligibility.
  • Some jurisdictions use taxable-value caps, credits, millage rates or special assessments not represented here.
  • Use the assessed value and exemptions on your latest official assessment notice when possible.
