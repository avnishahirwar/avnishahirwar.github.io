# AVNISH AI — Global Product Strategy

## Positioning
AVNISH AI is a bilingual (Hindi and English) AI-assisted business operations platform. Its first product is an enquiry-to-customer workflow assistant for small, enquiry-driven businesses.

Tagline: Intelligence. Automation. Growth.

## Business model
Hybrid:
1. AI implementation services for early customer discovery and cash flow.
2. Subscription SaaS after workflows are validated.
3. Additional AI products only after the core product has repeatable adoption.

## Initial customer segment
Start with independent hotels, resorts, and other small service businesses that receive frequent enquiries. Expand to adjacent verticals only after customer evidence supports the move.

## Core product: AVNISH AI Business Desk
MVP capabilities:
- Capture enquiries through an embeddable web form and approved integrations.
- Store enquiry source, timestamp, customer-provided details, language, status, and consent.
- Suggest responses grounded in the business's approved FAQ and service information.
- Route uncertain, sensitive, or high-value requests to a human.
- Create follow-up reminders; send no marketing messages by default.
- Provide a simple dashboard for open enquiries, response time, and outcomes.
- Support Hindi and English user-facing content.

## Product principles
- Demonstrate measurable customer value; do not sell AI hype.
- Human approval for external messages, quotes, bookings, refunds, and commitments in the MVP.
- No autonomous bulk outreach, scraped private contacts, or unsolicited mass messaging.
- Respect opt-outs, suppression lists, platform terms, privacy, and applicable laws.
- Collect the minimum personal data required and provide deletion/retention controls.
- Never fabricate availability, prices, policies, or business facts.
- Secure tenant isolation, least-privilege access, audit logs, and secrets management before production.

## Commercial validation
Treat all prices as experiments, not established market rates. Interview prospective customers, run small paid pilots, measure response time, qualified enquiries, conversion, time saved, retention, support cost, and gross margin. Do not scale paid acquisition before retention and unit economics are understood.

## Suggested pilot offer
A fixed-scope setup for one business: approved FAQ knowledge base, enquiry capture, lead inbox, AI response drafts, human handoff, and a weekly outcomes report. The business owner approves all outbound communication.

## Pricing hypotheses to test
- Starter: INR 999/month
- Growth: INR 2,999/month
- Business: INR 7,999/month
- One-time setup service: from INR 5,000

These are test hypotheses only and must be validated with actual buyers and delivery costs.

## Milestones
1. Validate the problem with 10-15 target businesses.
2. Secure 2-3 pilot customers and document baseline metrics.
3. Build the smallest secure enquiry workflow.
4. Run pilots and measure customer outcomes.
5. Convert repeatable service work into subscription features.
6. Expand channels and verticals only after product-market evidence.

## Success metrics
- Time to first response
- Percentage of enquiries captured
- Qualified enquiry rate
- Human handoff rate
- Enquiry-to-booking or sale conversion (where customer supplies reliable data)
- Customer retention and churn
- Gross margin and support hours per account
- Opt-out, complaint, and message-failure rates

## Non-goals for the first release
- Claiming guaranteed revenue or autonomous business operation
- Mass messaging or contacting huge lists
- Processing payments or making financial decisions
- Autonomous booking, refunds, or contractual commitments
- Building many unrelated products at once
