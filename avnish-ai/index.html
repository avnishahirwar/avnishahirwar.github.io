TOOL GUIDES

Free online calculators and business document tools

AVNISH AI brings together practical browser-based tools for everyday calculations and small-business paperwork. Use each tool directly on this page without installing an app.

GST Calculator

Calculate GST on an amount or extract GST from a tax-inclusive price. Select a GST rate and choose whether to add or remove GST. The result shows the taxable base, tax amount and total.

Useful for quick estimates. Confirm the applicable GST rate and tax treatment for your transaction.

EMI Calculator

Estimate a monthly loan instalment using the principal, annual interest rate and repayment period. Review the estimated total repayment and interest before comparing loan options.

Actual lender schedules, fees and rates may differ.

Profit and Margin Calculator

Enter cost price and selling price to estimate gross profit, profit margin and markup. Use the result to understand basic product pricing.

This is a simple estimate and does not include every business expense or tax.

Discount Calculator

Calculate the discount amount and final price from an original price and discount percentage. Useful for quick sale-price checks.

Invoice Generator

Create a simple invoice with seller and customer details, line items, quantities, unit prices and a GST estimate. Print or save the generated document as PDF from your browser.

Quotation Maker

Prepare a basic quotation for products or services, including item descriptions, quantities, prices and notes such as payment terms or validity.
