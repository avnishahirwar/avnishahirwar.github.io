THE PLATFORM

One clear workflow from enquiry to action.

We are starting with a focused product for businesses that handle repeated customer questions and enquiries. More modules will follow only when customer needs are validated.

01 / CAPTURE

Enquiry inbox

Bring incoming requests into one organised view with source and status.

02 / ASSIST

AI reply drafts

Suggest clear Hindi and English replies based on approved business information.

03 / ORGANISE

Follow-up workflow

Track next steps, reminders and human handoffs so requests do not get lost.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN

Your team stays in control.

AI should assist people, not make unchecked promises on behalf of a business. Important replies and commitments remain subject to human approval.

PRIVACY & TRUST

Privacy is a product requirement, not a footer link.

Our intended design uses minimal data collection, role-based access, secure integrations, clear retention rules and human approval for important external actions. Production security controls and legal documents must be implemented and reviewed before customer data is collected.
