10 · HEALTH

BMI Calculator

Calculate Body Mass Index (BMI). Adult BMI categories are shown only for adults aged 18 and above.

Enter your age, height and weight to calculate BMI.

Adult reference categories: Underweight (below 18.5), Healthy (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25.0–29.9), Obesity (30.0 or above). BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. For people under 18, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth percentiles; this adult calculator does not provide that assessment.
