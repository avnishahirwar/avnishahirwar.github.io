EMI Calculator

Estimate a monthly loan instalment using the principal, annual interest rate and repayment period. Review the estimated total repayment and interest before comparing loan options.

Enter cost price and selling price to estimate gross profit, profit margin and markup. Use the result to understand basic product pricing.
