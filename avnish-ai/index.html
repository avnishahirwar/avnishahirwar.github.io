10 · HEALTH

BMI Calculator

Calculate Body Mass Index (BMI). Adult BMI categories are shown only for adults aged 18 and above.

Enter your age, height and weight to calculate BMI.

Adult reference categories: Underweight (below 18.5), Healthy (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25.0–29.9), Obesity (30.0 or above). BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. For people under 18, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth percentiles; this adult calculator does not provide that assessment.

11 · TRAVEL

Fuel Cost Calculator

Estimate the fuel required and total fuel expense for your journey.

Enter distance, mileage and fuel price to estimate your journey cost.

Estimate = distance ÷ mileage × fuel price. Actual fuel use and cost may vary with traffic, road conditions, driving style, vehicle load and fuel-price changes. Enter the total journey distance, including return travel if applicable.

12 · FINANCE

Basic Income Tax Calculator (India)

Estimate annual income tax for an individual under the old or new tax regime for Tax Year 2026–27.

Enter your annual income to estimate tax.

Indicative Tax Year 2026–27 estimate for an individual below age 60. Select residential status to apply Section 87A rebate only where eligible. Includes salary/pension standard deduction, applicable rebate and surcharge with marginal relief, plus 4% health & education cess. Assumes income taxed at normal slab rates; special-rate income (including certain capital gains), every exemption/deduction, tax credits, and individual-specific adjustments are not covered. Verify with the Income Tax Department or a qualified tax professional before filing.

16 · BUSINESS

Profit & Loss Calculator

Enter your cost price and selling price to calculate business profit or loss and its percentage on cost.

Enter cost price and selling price.

Profit / Loss = Selling Price − Cost Price. Profit or loss percentage = absolute difference ÷ Cost Price × 100. This basic calculation excludes taxes, fees, freight, discounts, overheads and other business expenses unless included in the cost price.

15 · SALARY & TAX

TDS Calculator on Salary

Estimate annual salary TDS under the new or old tax regime and the monthly deduction needed for the remaining months.

Enter annual salary to estimate TDS.

Indicative Tax Year 2026–27 estimate for an individual below age 60. Select residential status to apply Section 87A rebate only where eligible. Includes salary standard deduction, applicable rebate and surcharge with marginal relief, plus 4% cess. Monthly balance = (estimated annual tax − TDS already deducted) ÷ months remaining. Actual employer TDS may differ due to declarations, perquisite valuation, other income, previous-employer salary, exemptions, tax credits and payroll rounding. Confirm with your payroll team or a tax professional.

14 · UTILITIES

Electricity Bill Calculator

Estimate your electricity bill from units consumed and your per-unit rate.

Enter units and your tariff rate to estimate the bill.

Estimate = units × rate + fixed charges + other charges. Actual bills may include slab-wise tariffs, meter rent, fuel adjustments, electricity duty, subsidies and state/DISCOM-specific items. Enter these under Other charges where applicable; check your latest tariff and bill.

Important: Results are estimates for convenience. GST treatment, loan terms and business accounting depend on your actual circumstances; verify before making financial, tax or legal decisions.
