Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This notice explains how AVNISH AI handles information when you visit this website.

Calculator and document tools

The GST, EMI, profit and discount calculators process the values you enter in your browser. The invoice and quotation builder also prepares its document in your browser. These tool inputs are not intentionally sent to our server by those features.

Contact form

If you submit the contact form, the name, email address, enquiry category and message you provide are sent to our website service so that we can receive and respond to your enquiry. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, or sensitive personal information.

Advertising and cookies

This website may use Google AdSense or other advertising services if and when the website and publisher account are approved. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to their own policies and the choices available in your region. The presence of advertising code does not mean that ads are currently being served or that approval has been granted.

For information about Google's advertising technologies and controls, review Google's Advertising policies and technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

When you use the contact form or when advertising is enabled, information may be processed by the relevant hosting, form-delivery or advertising providers under their terms and privacy policies.

Data requests

For a privacy question or a request about information submitted through the contact form, please use the contact form on the home page. Do not include sensitive details in the message.

Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
