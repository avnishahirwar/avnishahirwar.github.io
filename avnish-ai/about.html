About AVNISH AI

AVNISH AI provides simple, browser-based tools intended to help individuals and small businesses with everyday calculations and basic business documents.

Available tools

  • GST calculator for basic estimates
  • Loan EMI calculator for indicative monthly instalments
  • Profit and margin calculator
  • Discount calculator
  • Invoice and quotation builder that runs in your browser

How the tools work

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser and are not submitted to our server by the calculator features. The invoice and quotation builder prepares a document locally for printing or saving as PDF.

These tools provide general estimates and document templates, not professional tax, legal, accounting, lending or investment advice. Verify important decisions with a qualified professional.
