# Official government calculator sources

Reviewed for the Friendly Calculator global government-calculator hub on 21 November 2026.

- OECD tax and benefits calculator: https://www.oecd.org/en/data/tools/oecd-calculator-of-taxes-and-benefits.html
  - OECD describes a calculator for how taxes and cash benefits affect working-age family incomes across supported countries and time periods.
  - Methodology/manual: https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-sub-issues/income-support-redistribution-and-work-incentives/OECD-TaxBEN-methodology-and-manual.pdf
- US IRS tax withholding estimator guidance: https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator
  - The IRS states it estimates federal withholding for W-2 jobs or pensions/annuities; it is not for nonresident taxpayers.
  - Tool: https://apps.irs.gov/app/tax-withholding-estimator
- UK HMRC residential Stamp Duty Land Tax rates: https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates
  - HMRC states rates depend on purchase date, price, relief/exemption eligibility and ownership circumstances.
  - Tool: https://www.tax.service.gov.uk/calculate-stamp-duty-land-tax/
- Australia ATO income tax estimator: https://www.ato.gov.au/calculators-and-tools/income-tax-estimator
- Australia ATO tax withheld calculator: https://www.ato.gov.au/calculators-and-tools/tax-withheld-calculator
- Canada CRA GST/HST calculator: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html
- Singapore IRAS calculator directory: https://www.iras.gov.sg/quick-links/calculators
