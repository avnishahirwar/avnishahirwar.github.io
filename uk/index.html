UK tax-year and coverage notes

  • UK tax year 2026–27 runs from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.
  • Income-tax bands differ in Scotland; the linked take-home calculator offers a Scottish region option.
  • Stamp Duty Land Tax applies to England and Northern Ireland; Scotland and Wales have separate regimes.
  • Mortgage repayments use a user-entered assumed rate and are not a lender quote or affordability assessment.
  • VAT and payroll estimates use the assumptions stated on each calculator page. They are not a full tax return or employer payroll filing.
