What Is Friendly Calculator?

Visitors can explore calculators for personal finance, loans and savings estimates, income tax and salary, GST, small-business calculations, mathematics, unit conversions and everyday planning. Friendly Calculator is an independent information and calculation website. It is not a bank, lender, investment platform, government department or professional advisory service. Friendly Calculator is a collection of browser-based tools for common calculations in personal finance, Indian tax and salary planning, small-business arithmetic and everyday tasks. Use a calculator in three steps

  1. Choose the calculator that matches your question and check the units shown beside each input.
  2. Enter your own figures. Where a rate or assumption is required, use a current figure from the relevant bank, employer, government department, utility provider or other authoritative source.
  3. Review the result, formula and limitations on the calculator page. Change one input at a time to compare scenarios and keep a note of the assumptions used.

What the results mean

Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values entered and the method described on each tool page. They are not bank offers, tax assessments, investment recommendations, medical diagnoses or guarantees of future outcomes. Actual results can differ because of fees, rounding, changing rates, eligibility rules, local tariffs and individual circumstances. Do not rely on an estimate alone for a financial, tax, health or legal decision.
