How Friendly Calculator helps you

Friendly Calculator is a collection of browser-based tools for common calculations in personal finance, Indian tax and salary planning, small-business arithmetic and everyday tasks. Each tool is designed around a specific question: estimate a loan instalment, compare a savings scenario, separate tax from a price, understand a discount, or estimate the cost of a journey. Choose the closest calculator above and read its instructions before entering values.

Use a calculator in three steps

  1. Choose the calculator that matches your question and check the units shown beside each input.
  2. Enter your own figures. Where a rate or assumption is required, use a current figure from the relevant bank, employer, government department, utility provider or other authoritative source.
  3. Review the result, formula and limitations on the calculator page. Change one input at a time to compare scenarios and keep a note of the assumptions used.

What the results mean

Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values entered and the method described on each tool page. They are not bank offers, tax assessments, investment recommendations, medical diagnoses or guarantees of future outcomes. Actual results can differ because of fees, rounding, changing rates, eligibility rules, local tariffs and individual circumstances. Do not rely on an estimate alone for a financial, tax, health or legal decision.

Our calculation approach

We aim to explain the formula, define important inputs, provide a worked example and identify material exclusions for each calculator. Where official rules or rates matter, check the relevant government or provider source because these can change. Read our calculation methodology for an overview of how the tools work, and visit the individual calculator page for its specific assumptions.
