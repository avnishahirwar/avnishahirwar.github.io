Frequently Asked Questions

Are Friendly Calculator tools free to use?

Yes. The calculators currently available on the website can be used without a subscription. Any future paid features will be identified clearly before use.

Are calculator results guaranteed to match a bank, employer or government calculation?

No. Results are estimates based on the inputs, formulas and assumptions shown on each calculator page. Actual terms, rounding, fees and applicable rules may change the result.

Does Friendly Calculator provide tax, legal or financial advice?

No. Friendly Calculator provides calculation tools and general information, not professional advice. Verify tax rules and important decisions with the relevant official source or a qualified professional.

How current are tax calculator rules?

Tax-year assumptions and review dates are shown on relevant tax calculator pages where applicable. Always confirm the current law and your individual circumstances with the Income Tax Department before filing.

Can I use Friendly Calculator on a mobile phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

What Is Friendly Calculator?

Friendly Calculator is an online calculator platform created by Avnish Ahirwar. It brings practical, browser-based calculation tools together in one place, helping people understand everyday numbers without having to install a separate app for each task. The platform focuses on useful calculations for India, alongside general-purpose maths, measurement, date and time tools.

Visitors can explore calculators for personal finance, loans and savings estimates, income tax and salary, GST, small-business calculations, mathematics, unit conversions and everyday planning. Each calculator has its own page so users can review the required inputs, calculation method and important limitations before relying on an estimate.

Friendly Calculator is an independent information and calculation website. It is not a bank, lender, investment platform, government department or professional advisory service. Results depend on the values and assumptions entered and should be checked against current official rules or provider information when making important decisions.

How Friendly Calculator helps you

Friendly Calculator is a collection of browser-based tools for common calculations in personal finance, Indian tax and salary planning, small-business arithmetic and everyday tasks. Each tool is designed around a specific question: estimate a loan instalment, compare a savings scenario, separate tax from a price, understand a discount, or estimate the cost of a journey. Choose the closest calculator above and read its instructions before entering values.

Use a calculator in three steps

  1. Choose the calculator that matches your question and check the units shown beside each input.
  2. Enter your own figures. Where a rate or assumption is required, use a current figure from the relevant bank, employer, government department, utility provider or other authoritative source.
  3. Review the result, formula and limitations on the calculator page. Change one input at a time to compare scenarios and keep a note of the assumptions used.

What the results mean

Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values entered and the method described on each tool page. They are not bank offers, tax assessments, investment recommendations, medical diagnoses or guarantees of future outcomes. Actual results can differ because of fees, rounding, changing rates, eligibility rules, local tariffs and individual circumstances. Do not rely on an estimate alone for a financial, tax, health or legal decision.

Our calculation approach

We aim to explain the formula, define important inputs, provide a worked example and identify material exclusions for each calculator. Where official rules or rates matter, check the relevant government or provider source because these can change. Read our calculation methodology for an overview of how the tools work, and visit the individual calculator page for its specific assumptions.
