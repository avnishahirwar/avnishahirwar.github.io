US tax-year and coverage notes

  • Federal individual income tax: calendar year 1 January–31 December 2026 for the linked federal estimator.
  • The federal estimator excludes state and local income taxes, tax credits, itemized deductions and several payroll adjustments.
  • State rules differ; a federal result is not a state tax or complete take-home-pay result.
  • Display currency can be changed independently, but changing a display currency does not convert the calculator's underlying inputs or tax rules.
