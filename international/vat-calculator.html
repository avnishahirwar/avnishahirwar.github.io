VAT formulas

Adding VAT: VAT amount = net price × VAT rate ÷ 100. Gross price = net price + VAT amount.

Removing VAT: Net price = gross price ÷ (1 + VAT rate ÷ 100). VAT amount = gross price − net price.

The calculator rounds displayed values to two decimal places. Calculations retain full precision until display.

VAT rates vary by country

Presets provide standard VAT/GST rates for selected jurisdictions only. Reduced, zero-rated and exempt supplies may use different treatment. Rates and rules can change; verify the rate for the product, service, place of supply, customer status and transaction date before using it.

Worked example

If the net price is 100 and the applicable VAT rate is 20%, VAT is 20 and the gross price is 120. If 120 already includes 20% VAT, the net price is 100 and the VAT component is 20.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator know my country's VAT rate?

No. Enter the current rate that applies to your specific transaction. This avoids presenting an outdated or incorrect country rate as universal.

Can I use this for GST?

The arithmetic is similar for a simple tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive percentage calculation. GST rules, input tax credits, place-of-supply and invoice requirements are different and should be checked separately.

Does changing currency convert the entered price?

No. The currency selector changes display formatting only. It does not convert the amount or exchange rate. Use the Currency Converter for currency conversion.
