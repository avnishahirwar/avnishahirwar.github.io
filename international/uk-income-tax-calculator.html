Included assumptions

  • Personal Allowance: £12,570, tapered by £1 for each £2 of income above £100,000.
  • England, Wales and Northern Ireland: 20%, 40% and 45% main income-tax rates.
  • Scotland: starter, basic, intermediate, higher, advanced and top bands.
  • Employee Class 1 NI uses 8% and 2% bands; weekly thresholds are annualised for this estimate.
  • Student loan: 9% above the selected Plan 1/2/4/5 threshold; postgraduate: 6% above £21,000.

This annual estimate does not model tax codes, benefits, pension salary sacrifice, bonuses paid in different periods, multiple jobs, other income, special reliefs or payroll-period rounding.

Frequently asked questions

Which UK tax year does this calculator use?

It uses the 2026–27 tax year, from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.

Does it include Scottish income tax rates?

Yes. Select Scotland to use the Scottish income-tax bands. National Insurance remains a separate UK-wide employee estimate.

Are student-loan repayments included?

You can select Plan 1, 2, 4 or 5 and optionally a postgraduate loan. Repayments are estimated from annual gross income and published thresholds.
