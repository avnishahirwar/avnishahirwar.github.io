2026 tariff bands

  • Up to €12,348: 0% basic allowance.
  • €12,349–€17,799: progressive zone 1.
  • €17,800–€69,878: progressive zone 2.
  • €69,879–€277,825: 42% marginal zone.
  • From €277,826: 45% marginal zone.

The statutory formula rounds taxable income down to a whole euro. This estimate excludes solidarity surcharge, church tax, income-splitting, child allowances, capital-income rules and special provisions.

Frequently asked questions

What income should I enter?

Enter your annual taxable income (zu versteuerndes Einkommen), not gross salary. Deductions and allowances must already be reflected in that amount.

Does this include the solidarity surcharge or church tax?

No. The estimate calculates the §32a EStG income-tax tariff only. Solidarity surcharge, church tax and individual credits or special cases are excluded.

Is this an ELSTER tax return?

No. This calculator is an independent estimate and does not submit a return. Use the official ELSTER portal for tax forms and filing.
