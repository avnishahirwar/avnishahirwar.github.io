Why use an official source?

Tax, benefit, payroll, GST, VAT and property rules change by country, tax year and personal circumstances. The links below open the responsible authority's current calculator or calculator directory. Friendly Calculator pages can explain general formulas, but the official tool should be the final check for a filing, payment, benefit claim or legal decision.
