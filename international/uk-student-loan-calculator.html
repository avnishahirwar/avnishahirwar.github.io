Formula and 2026–27 thresholds

  • Plan 1: £26,900 annual threshold; 9% of earnings above it.
  • Plan 2: £29,385 annual threshold; 9% of earnings above it.
  • Plan 4: £33,795 annual threshold; 9% of earnings above it.
  • Plan 5: £25,000 annual threshold; 9% of earnings above it.
  • Postgraduate Loan: £21,000 annual threshold; 6% of earnings above it.

Undergraduate formula: max(0, annual earnings − plan threshold) × 9%.

Postgraduate formula: max(0, annual earnings − £21,000) × 6%.

Example: with £40,000 annual earnings on Plan 2, the estimated undergraduate repayment is (£40,000 − £29,385) × 9% = £955.35 per year, or about £79.61 per month.

Frequently asked questions

Does the outstanding balance affect the payroll amount?

No. Income-contingent repayments are based on earnings above the relevant threshold, not the total balance outstanding.

Can I repay an undergraduate loan and a postgraduate loan together?

Some borrowers can have both deductions at once. The postgraduate option adds its separate 6% calculation above £21,000.

Is this an exact payslip calculation?

No. It annualises earnings and averages the result. Actual deductions are calculated per pay period and can vary with bonuses, overtime and payroll rounding.
