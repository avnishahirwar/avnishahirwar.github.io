Estimate your council tax

Enter your annual bill to estimate the payment schedule.

Formula and assumptions

Adjusted annual charge = annual council tax bill × (1 − discount percentage ÷ 100). Instalment amount = adjusted annual charge ÷ number of instalments.

This is a bill-planning calculator. Enter the annual amount on your latest council bill and only apply a reduction confirmed by your local authority.

Important UK coverage notes

  • Council tax charges depend on the local authority, property band and financial year.
  • Scotland, England and Wales have different local arrangements; Northern Ireland uses domestic rates rather than council tax.
  • Single-person discounts, exemptions, disability reductions and Council Tax Reduction have separate eligibility rules.
  • This tool does not look up council rates, assess eligibility or replace your bill.
