How this US auto loan estimate works

Estimated amount financed = vehicle price + entered sales tax + upfront fees − down payment − trade-in equity. Monthly payments use a standard fixed-rate amortization formula with the APR converted to a monthly rate.

Enter the taxable amount and rate that apply to your transaction. Sales-tax treatment of trade-ins, rebates, fees and vehicle purchases differs by state. This tool does not determine local tax law, dealer fees, lender approval, credit tier or the rate you qualify for.

Costs not included

  • Insurance, fuel, maintenance, repairs and depreciation.
  • Future registration, title and annual vehicle taxes unless included in your upfront fees.
  • Loan origination fees or add-ons not included in the amount you enter.
  • Variable rates, balloon payments, lease terms, negative equity and early payoff fees.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator determine my state sales tax?

No. Enter the applicable tax rate and taxable amount based on your location and transaction. Tax rules and taxable bases vary.

How is a trade-in applied?

Enter the trade-in equity amount that you expect to apply toward the purchase. State rules may treat trade-ins differently for sales-tax purposes.

Does the estimate include insurance and registration renewals?

No. Enter upfront dealer fees in the fees field if appropriate. Insurance, fuel, maintenance and future registration costs are excluded.
