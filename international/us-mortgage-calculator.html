Formula and assumptions

For a fully amortizing fixed-rate loan, the monthly principal-and-interest payment is calculated using the loan principal, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. Optional property tax is estimated from the home price and the annual rate you enter. Insurance is divided into 12 monthly amounts.

  • Assumes a fixed annual interest rate for the full selected term and monthly payments.
  • Property-tax and insurance amounts are user inputs, not county-specific quotes.
  • Excludes private mortgage insurance (PMI), closing costs, utilities, maintenance, escrow adjustments and lender fees.
  • Does not assess affordability, eligibility or loan approval.

For general consumer guidance, see the US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau home-buying resources.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use a live mortgage rate?

No. Enter an assumed rate from a lender quote or your own scenario. No live rate feed is connected.

Does the estimate include property tax?

Yes, if you enter an annual property-tax rate. Actual tax bills depend on local assessment and exemptions.

Does it include PMI?

No. PMI, closing costs and lender-specific charges are not included.
