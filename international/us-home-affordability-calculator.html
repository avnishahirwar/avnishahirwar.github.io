How the estimate is calculated

  1. Maximum monthly debt and housing budget = gross monthly income × selected DTI limit.
  2. Available principal-and-interest payment = that budget − existing monthly debts − monthly property tax − insurance − HOA dues.
  3. The calculator converts that payment into a mortgage principal using the selected rate and term.
  4. Estimated home price = mortgage principal + down payment.

Mortgage present-value formula: payment × [1 − (1 + monthly rate)−number of payments] ÷ monthly rate. At 0% interest, principal equals payment × number of payments.

Important assumptions

  • All amounts are in US dollars and all income/debt inputs are monthly.
  • Property tax is entered as an annual amount; insurance and HOA are entered monthly.
  • Closing costs, mortgage insurance (PMI), maintenance, utilities and lender fees are not included.
  • Actual underwriting depends on credit history, verified income and assets, loan type, property, location, interest-rate lock and lender rules.
  • State and local taxes vary. Enter your own property-tax estimate rather than assuming a national rate.

Further guidance

For a more complete home-buying budget, review the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's home-buying resources. Compare this estimate with a lender's written Loan Estimate before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tell me how much a lender will approve?

No. It is a planning estimate based on the assumptions you enter. Lenders use additional underwriting criteria.

Are taxes and insurance included?

Yes, the monthly property-tax, homeowners-insurance and HOA amounts you enter are deducted before estimating the principal-and-interest payment.

Are closing costs or PMI included?

No. Add those separately to your cash and monthly budget. They vary by loan, location and borrower.
