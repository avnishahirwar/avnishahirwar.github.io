Friendly Calculator Adds a Competitive Exam Maths Study Hub

26 November 2026 · Friendly Calculator · Product update

A new study hub groups existing free calculators for percentages, ratios, averages, number systems, algebra and probability into one exam-preparation pathway.

One place for common aptitude topics

Students often need to move between separate tools while practising arithmetic, number systems, algebra and probability. The new Competitive Exam Maths Calculators hub brings a curated set of existing Friendly Calculator tools into one study pathway.

The collection includes percentage, fraction, average, weighted average and ratio tools; LCM and HCF, square-root, cube-root and prime-factorisation tools; equation solvers; and probability, permutation, combination and factorial calculators.

Designed for checking practice work

The recommended workflow is simple: attempt the question by hand, write the formula, then use the matching calculator to check the arithmetic. This helps learners spot errors in signs, units, order of operations and the difference between related concepts such as percentage change and percentage points.

The hub is relevant to general quantitative aptitude practice for SSC, banking, railway and state-level recruitment exams, as well as school and college mathematics. It is not an official syllabus, and candidates should follow the latest instructions from their own exam authority.

Existing calculators, better discovery

This update does not add duplicate tools or claim that every examination topic is covered. It improves discovery by grouping existing calculators and linking each one to its own formula, assumptions and page. The wider Math Calculators catalogue remains available for geometry, trigonometry, statistics and other topics.

Explore the study hub

Open the Competitive Exam Maths Calculators hub to choose a topic and begin a practice session. Use calculators to verify your work, and check your examination rules before using a calculator during an actual test.
