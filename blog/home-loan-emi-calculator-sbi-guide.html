Home Loan EMI Calculator SBI: Estimate EMI Without Guessing the Rate

India-focused guide · Reviewed 14 November 2026

If you are searching for a home loan EMI calculator SBI, the key is to enter the interest rate and repayment tenure shown in the lender's current written illustration. Friendly Calculator is an independent estimation tool; it is not an SBI website and does not fetch or confirm SBI loan rates.

What does a home loan EMI calculator show?

It estimates the monthly instalment for a principal amount, annual interest rate and number of monthly instalments. It can also show total scheduled repayment and the interest component under a constant-rate, reducing-balance assumption.

Formula used

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate as a decimal, and n is the number of monthly instalments. At 0% interest, EMI = P ÷ n.

Worked example

For an illustrative principal of ₹30,00,000, annual rate of 8.5% and tenure of 240 months, the monthly rate is 0.085 ÷ 12. The calculator estimates the instalment using the reducing-balance formula. This is only a formula example: it is not an SBI rate quote, eligibility result or loan offer.

How to use an SBI loan illustration

  1. Use the sanctioned or proposed loan amount, not automatically the full property price.
  2. Enter the annual rate shown in the latest written loan illustration.
  3. Enter the repayment tenure in months.
  4. Compare EMI, total interest and total repayment together.
  5. Ask the lender whether the rate is fixed, floating or subject to reset, and request the amortisation schedule and full list of charges.

Assumptions and charges not included

This calculator assumes equal monthly instalments and a constant rate. Processing fees, legal and valuation charges, insurance, taxes, pre-EMI interest, disbursement timing and future floating-rate changes are not included unless explicitly entered elsewhere. The lender's own schedule controls the actual payment obligation.

Questions people ask about home loan EMI

Does this calculator show the latest SBI home loan interest rate?

No. It does not fetch live rates. Enter the rate from the lender's current official offer or written illustration.

Is this the official SBI EMI calculator?

No. Friendly Calculator is independent and is not affiliated with State Bank of India.

Why can my bank EMI differ from the estimate?

The lender may use a different rate reset date, rounding method, disbursement schedule, fee treatment or repayment convention.

Should I compare only the monthly EMI?

No. Compare the total repayment, total interest, fees, rate type, reset terms and tenure as well.
