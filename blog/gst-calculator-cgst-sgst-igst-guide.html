GST Calculator with CGST SGST: How the Tax Split Works

India-focused guide · Reviewed 14 November 2026

A GST calculator with CGST and SGST helps separate the central and state components when a taxable supply is intra-state. For an inter-state supply, IGST generally applies instead. The correct treatment depends on the place-of-supply rules and the transaction facts.

What are CGST, SGST and IGST?

For an intra-state supply, Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) are generally charged together, each representing half of the applicable combined GST rate. For an inter-state supply, Integrated GST (IGST) is generally charged at the combined rate. Special territories, supplies and place-of-supply rules can affect the result.

Formula to add GST

GST amount = taxable value × GST rate ÷ 100. Invoice total = taxable value + GST amount. For an intra-state supply at a combined 18% rate, the illustrative split is 9% CGST + 9% SGST.

Formula to remove GST from an inclusive amount

Taxable value = GST-inclusive amount × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate). GST amount = GST-inclusive amount − taxable value.

Worked example: ₹10,000 at 18%

If ₹10,000 is the taxable value and the applicable combined rate is 18%, GST is ₹1,800 and the total is ₹11,800. For an intra-state supply, the illustrative split is ₹900 CGST and ₹900 SGST. For an inter-state supply, the ₹1,800 would be shown as IGST. This example demonstrates arithmetic only; it does not determine the rate or place of supply for a real transaction.

How to choose the correct GST rate

Do not choose a GST rate from a product name alone. Confirm the HSN or SAC classification, description, exemption or concessional entry, current notification and place-of-supply rules. GST rate notifications and amendments are issued by the competent authorities; check the current official material before issuing an invoice or filing a return.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The arithmetic uses the rate entered by the user.
  • The tool does not classify goods or services by HSN/SAC.
  • It does not determine place of supply, input tax credit eligibility, reverse charge, exemptions or filing treatment.
  • Invoice rounding can differ from a simple calculator result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate CGST and SGST?

For a typical intra-state supply, split the combined GST rate equally between CGST and SGST. At 18%, the arithmetic split is 9% and 9%.

When is IGST charged instead of CGST and SGST?

IGST generally applies to inter-state supplies. Confirm the legal place of supply and transaction type before deciding.

How do I calculate GST from an inclusive price?

Multiply the inclusive amount by 100 and divide by 100 plus the GST rate to find the taxable value. Subtract that value from the inclusive amount to find GST.

Does the calculator tell me which GST rate applies?

No. It performs arithmetic using the rate entered. Confirm classification and rate from current CBIC notifications and official GST resources.
