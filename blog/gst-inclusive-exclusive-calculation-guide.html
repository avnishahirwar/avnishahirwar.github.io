GST Inclusive vs Exclusive: How to Calculate the Taxable Value

Learn the formulas for adding GST to a base price or extracting it from an inclusive price. · Published 13 November 2026

When a price is shown to a customer, it may be quoted before GST or as a GST-inclusive final amount. These are different starting points. Applying the rate directly to an inclusive price will overstate the tax component, so first identify whether the amount already includes GST.

1. GST exclusive price

A GST-exclusive price is the value before tax. To calculate the tax, multiply the taxable value by the applicable GST rate. Add the result to the base price to get the invoice total.

GST amount = Taxable value × GST rate ÷ 100
Invoice total = Taxable value + GST amount

Example: for a taxable value of ₹1,000 and an illustrative 18% rate, GST is ₹180 and the total is ₹1,180. The rate used in an actual invoice must match the classification and current rules for the supply.

2. GST inclusive price

A GST-inclusive price already contains the tax. To extract the tax component, multiply the inclusive amount by the rate and divide by 100 plus the rate.

GST component = Inclusive price × GST rate ÷ (100 + GST rate)
Taxable value = Inclusive price − GST component

Example: if the final price is ₹1,180 at an illustrative 18% rate, the GST component is ₹180 and the pre-tax value is ₹1,000.

3. CGST, SGST and IGST

For an intra-state supply, the applicable GST may be split between CGST and SGST/UTGST. For an inter-state supply, IGST generally applies. The correct treatment depends on place-of-supply rules, supplier registration and the nature of the transaction. Do not split a rate manually without checking the applicable invoice rules.

4. Common calculation mistakes

  • Applying the tax rate to a price that already includes GST.
  • Using a generic rate without confirming the goods or services classification.
  • Ignoring exemptions, reverse-charge provisions or special valuation rules where relevant.
  • Rounding each line differently from the accounting system or invoice rules.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Multiply the inclusive price by the GST rate and divide by 100 plus the GST rate. Subtract the tax component to get the taxable value.

Is 18% GST applicable to every product?

No. Rates depend on the goods or services classification and applicable notifications. Verify the rate before invoicing.

When is IGST used instead of CGST and SGST?

The treatment generally depends on whether a supply is inter-state or intra-state under GST place-of-supply rules. Check the specific transaction.
