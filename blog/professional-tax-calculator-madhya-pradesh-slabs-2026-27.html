Madhya Pradesh Professional Tax Calculator: Salary Slabs and Method for 2026–27

India-focused guide · Reviewed 14 November 2026

Madhya Pradesh professional tax is governed by the Madhya Pradesh Vritti Kar Adhiniyam, 1995. The employee salary schedule uses annual salary or wages and has different annual amounts across salary bands. This guide explains the employee schedule only; other classes of taxpayers have separate provisions.

What does the Madhya Pradesh professional tax calculator estimate?

The calculator estimates the employee professional-tax amount using the published annual salary bands. It is not a complete assessment tool for businesses, professionals, dealers, employers or people with multiple categories of income.

Employee salary slabs

Annual salary / wagesAnnual professional tax
Up to ₹2,25,000Nil
Above ₹2,25,000 up to ₹3,00,000₹1,500
Above ₹3,00,000 up to ₹4,00,000₹2,000
Above ₹4,00,000₹2,500

Formula and method

Annual estimate = professional-tax amount assigned to the annual salary band. For a partial year, the law provides for proportionate reduction in the employee entry where employment ends before the year closes. Monthly payroll deductions may be split across months; the exact monthly withholding schedule is not always the annual amount divided evenly by 12.

Worked examples

An employee with annual salary of ₹2,80,000 falls in the ₹1,500 annual band. An employee with annual salary of ₹3,60,000 falls in the ₹2,000 band. An employee with annual salary of ₹4,80,000 falls in the ₹2,500 band. These examples assume the employee entry applies and do not assess exemptions or other taxpayer classes.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The automatic schedule on Friendly Calculator is for Madhya Pradesh employees only.
  • For another state or Union Territory, use the official state schedule and enter the verified amount manually.
  • Professional tax rules may distinguish employees, employers, traders and specified professions.
  • Employment duration, salary definition, multiple employers and exemptions can affect liability.
  • This guide is informational and does not determine an individual's legal liability.

Frequently asked questions

What is the professional tax in Madhya Pradesh?

For employees, the published annual schedule ranges from nil up to ₹2,25,000 salary to ₹2,500 above ₹4,00,000, with intermediate bands of ₹1,500 and ₹2,000.

Is professional tax the same in every Indian state?

No. Professional tax is state-specific. Some states levy it and others do not; schedules and exemptions vary.

Is Madhya Pradesh professional tax capped at ₹2,500?

Article 276(2) of the Constitution limits professional tax to ₹2,500 per person per year.

Does this calculator cover business owners and professionals?

No. The automatic schedule is for employees. The Act has separate entries for other categories.
