Income Tax Calculator New Regime 2026-27: Tax Year and Slabs Explained

India-focused guide · Reviewed 14 November 2026

For income earned from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027, the Income-tax Act, 2025 uses the term Tax Year 2026–27. The Act came into force on 1 April 2026, replacing the 1961 Act. Check the tax year and the applicable law before interpreting an online estimate.

Why the wording changed from FY to Tax Year

The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026. It uses “Tax Year” for the relevant twelve-month period. Many people still search for “FY 2026-27” or “AY 2027-28”; when reading current official guidance, check whether it refers to the new Act's Tax Year terminology or a legacy reference under the earlier Act.

New regime slab bands for Tax Year 2026–27

The official tax-rate schedule for Tax Year 2026–27 lists these marginal slab bands for the relevant individual/HUF category under section 202:

  • Up to ₹4,00,000: Nil
  • ₹4,00,001–₹8,00,000: 5%
  • ₹8,00,001–₹12,00,000: 10%
  • ₹12,00,001–₹16,00,000: 15%
  • ₹16,00,001–₹20,00,000: 20%
  • ₹20,00,001–₹24,00,000: 25%
  • Above ₹24,00,000: 30%

These are slab rates, not a flat rate applied to the entire income. Rebate, deductions, surcharge, cess, special-rate income and eligibility conditions affect the final computation. This article does not calculate a complete personal tax liability.

How to use an income tax calculator for the new regime

  1. Select Tax Year 2026–27 where the calculator offers the new Act's tax-year convention.
  2. Check whether the tool estimates salary income only or includes other income heads.
  3. Read the listed assumptions for standard deduction, rebate eligibility, surcharge and cess.
  4. Compare the output with the official Income Tax Department calculator and applicable current guidance.

What a simple salary tax calculator may leave out

A salary-only estimate may not include business or professional income, capital gains, special-rate income, every exemption or deduction, tax credits, loss set-off, return-specific adjustments or all residential-status cases. A result should not be treated as a return-filing figure unless the tool explicitly supports those inputs and rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is FY 2026-27 the same period as Tax Year 2026-27?

It refers to the same twelve-month period, 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. The Income-tax Act, 2025 uses the term Tax Year.

Which law applies from 1 April 2026?

The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026, replacing the Income-tax Act, 1961.

Are the new regime slab rates the final tax amount?

No. Slab rates are only one part of a tax computation. Rebate eligibility, deductions, surcharge, cess, special-rate income and individual facts can change the result.

Can an online salary calculator replace the Income Tax Department calculator?

No. Use the official calculator and current legal guidance to verify a complete computation. A third-party calculator may model only a limited set of income and deduction fields.
