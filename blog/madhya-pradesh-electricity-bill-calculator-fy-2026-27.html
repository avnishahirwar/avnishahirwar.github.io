Madhya Pradesh Electricity Bill Calculator: Domestic Tariff for FY 2026–27

India-focused guide · Reviewed 14 November 2026

Madhya Pradesh's FY 2026–27 retail supply tariff order was passed by MPERC on 26 March 2026. The domestic LV-1 schedule distinguishes a very-low-load category from standard metered domestic consumers. This guide explains the standard LV-1.2 estimate and what it does not include.

Which Madhya Pradesh tariff does the estimator use?

The standard metered domestic estimate follows MPERC Tariff Schedule LV-1.2. A separate LV-1.1 option is provided for connections with sanctioned load not more than 100 watts and consumption not more than 30 units per month. Do not select LV-1.1 unless both conditions apply.

Standard domestic energy slabs

Monthly unitsEnergy charge
First 50 units₹4.71 per unit
Next 100 units (51–150)₹5.67 per unit
Next 150 units (151–300)₹7.05 per unit
Above 300 units₹7.24 per unit

Fixed charges

For standard LV-1.2, fixed charges up to 50 units are ₹81 per connection in urban areas and ₹67 in rural areas. For 51–150 units they are ₹134 urban and ₹111 rural. Above 150 units, the schedule calculates fixed charges at ₹30 urban or ₹28 rural for each 0.1 kW, considering every 15 units or part thereof. MPERC's example says 155 units corresponds to 1.1 kW for this fixed-charge calculation.

Worked example

For 155 units in an urban standard domestic connection, energy charges are 50 × ₹4.71 + 100 × ₹5.67 + 5 × ₹7.05 = ₹832.25. The fixed-charge calculation uses 1.1 kW, so the fixed component is 11 × ₹30 = ₹330. The subtotal before other bill components is ₹1,162.25. This is an illustration, not a final payable bill.

LV-1.1 low-load exception

For a metered connection with sanctioned load not more than 100 watts and consumption not more than 30 units per month, LV-1.1 specifies ₹3.72 per unit and no fixed charge. The estimator rejects consumption above 30 units for this category.

What is not included?

  • Monthly Fuel and Power Purchase Adjustment Surcharge (FPPAS).
  • Electricity duty, taxes, arrears, meter-related or other bill components.
  • Applicable rebates, subsidies, prepaid-meter benefits or time-of-day adjustments.
  • Other consumer categories such as non-domestic, industrial, agriculture and high-tension connections.

Frequently asked questions

What is the MP electricity rate per unit in 2026–27?

For standard domestic LV-1.2, the slab rates are ₹4.71, ₹5.67, ₹7.05 and ₹7.24 per unit across the published consumption bands.

Does the Madhya Pradesh bill calculator include fixed charges?

Yes. It estimates the LV-1.2 fixed charge using the urban or rural schedule and the consumption-based 0.1 kW rule above 150 units.

Does it calculate the exact amount on my bill?

No. It estimates energy and fixed charges. Fuel adjustment, duty, taxes, arrears, rebates and subsidy treatment must be checked on the current DISCOM bill.

Can I use these rates for another state?

No. These rates are specific to Madhya Pradesh's MPERC FY 2026–27 domestic schedule. Other states and DISCOMs have different orders and categories.
