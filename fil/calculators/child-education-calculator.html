How this calculator works

This tool applies a transparent mathematical estimate to the values entered above. Review the labels, units and result before relying on it. Where an interest rate, fee, tax rate, eligibility limit or scheme term is requested, enter the value that applies to your own situation and confirm it with the relevant official provider.

The calculator does not connect to banks, brokers, government portals or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, tax classification, exemptions or compliance obligations. It excludes charges and adjustments unless a field explicitly asks for them.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • All figures are based on user-entered assumptions and may differ from actual provider calculations.
  • Rounding, payment dates, compounding, fees and applicable law can affect real outcomes.
  • For tax and government-scheme matters, verify current rules and official notifications before acting.
  • This tool is educational and is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Worked example

If an education course costs ₹10,00,000 today, education costs rise by an assumed 6% each year, and the course begins in 10 years, the projected cost is ₹10,00,000 × (1.06)10 = approximately ₹17,90,848. This is a scenario, not a forecast of actual fees or investment returns.

Frequently asked questions

What does the projected education cost mean?

It compounds the current estimated cost by the annual increase rate for the number of years entered. It does not estimate how to fund that cost.

Does the calculator predict actual college fees?

No. Fee changes vary by course, institution, location and year. Use a realistic assumption and revisit it as current fee information becomes available.

Does it include scholarships or grants?

No. Enter the current estimated cost before any funding adjustment, or calculate a separate net target after considering confirmed assistance.

Are investment returns included?

No. This tool projects an expense only. It does not recommend an investment product or guarantee that savings will meet the future cost.
