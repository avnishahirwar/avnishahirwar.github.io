GST formula

GST on a base amount = amount × rate ÷ 100. For a GST-inclusive price, taxable value = inclusive amount ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). For an intra-state supply, GST is generally split into CGST and SGST; for an inter-state supply, IGST generally applies subject to place-of-supply rules.
