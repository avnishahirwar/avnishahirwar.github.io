## Scope decisions

- **Crickets Chirping Thermometer** already had a functioning page at `/calculators/crickets-chirping-thermometer-calculator.html`; it was not duplicated or changed.
- **Dog Size Calculator** was deferred: the reviewed veterinary chart evidence supports serial age/weight growth curves, not a universal one-measurement formula. A validated chart dataset is needed before producing an adult-size estimate.
- **Dog Life Expectancy Calculator** was deferred: available peer-reviewed evidence is population-specific. A defensible route needs a defined life-table dataset and provenance; no universal individual lifespan formula was presented.
- The two medication pages accept a user-entered veterinarian prescription and never prefill or recommend a mg/kg dose. The raisin page explicitly provides no safe amount, risk score, or toxic-dose boundary.
