EMI formula

Monthly rate r = annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100; n = years × 12. EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1). At 0% interest, EMI = P ÷ n. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but can increase total interest.

Compare loan offers fairly

Keep principal and tenure the same, change the rate, and compare total repayment as well as the EMI. Processing fees, insurance and floating-rate resets are outside this estimate.
