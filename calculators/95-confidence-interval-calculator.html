Formulas used by this calculator

The 95% Confidence Interval Calculator works with 13 quantities and 14 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Margin of error (margin_of_error) = standard_error × z
  • Standard error (standard_error) = margin_of_error/z
  • Upper bound (upper_bound) = margin_of_error + mean
  • Lower bound (lower_bound) = -margin_of_error + mean
  • Right tailed p value (right_tailed_p_value) = 1/2 - 1/2 × confidence_level
  • Confidence level (confidence_level) = 1 - 2 × right_tailed_p_value
  • Mean (mean) = lower_bound + margin_of_error
  • z (z) = margin_of_error/standard_error

6 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
