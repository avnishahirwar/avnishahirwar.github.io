Formulas used by this calculator

The Telescope Field of View Calculator works with 8 quantities and 8 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Magnification (magnification) = ft/fe
  • fe (fe) = ft/magnification
  • ft (ft) = fe × magnification
  • Field of view apparent (field_of_view_apparent) = field_of_view × magnification
  • Field of view area (field_of_view_area) = (1/4) × PI × pow(field_of_view, 2)
  • Field of view (field_of_view) = 2 × sqrt(field_of_view_area)/sqrt(PI)

2 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
