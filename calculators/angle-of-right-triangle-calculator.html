Formulas used by this calculator

The Angle of Right Triangle Calculator works with 6 quantities and 23 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Side a (side_a) = 2 × area/side_b
  • Side b (side_b) = 2 × area/side_a
  • Area (area) = (1/2) × side_a × side_b
  • Angle alpha (angle_alpha) = atan(side_a/side_b)
  • Angle beta (angle_beta) = atan(side_b/side_a)
  • Hypotenuse c (hypotenuse_c) = side_b/sin(angle_beta)

17 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
