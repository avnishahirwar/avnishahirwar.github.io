How to use this calculator

Enter the annual liability estimate and payments already made. The calculator divides any positive balance evenly across the remaining instalments you specify.

Formula and method

Remaining balance = max(0, annual tax estimate − tax already paid). Suggested instalment = remaining balance ÷ remaining instalments.

Understanding the result

Enter the annual liability estimate and payments already made. The calculator divides any positive balance evenly across the remaining instalments you specify.

Important assumptions and limitations

This is arithmetic only. It does not calculate income tax under current slabs, interest, surcharge, cess, exemptions, advance-tax applicability or due dates. Confirm obligations and payment schedules using official tax guidance or a qualified tax professional.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate my income tax liability?

No. Enter an annual tax estimate calculated separately; this tool only subtracts payments already made.

What if I have already paid more than the estimate?

The remaining balance is shown as zero. Review any excess payment or refund with the tax authority or a qualified adviser.

Does it calculate statutory instalment due dates?

No. It divides the estimated balance evenly by the number of instalments you enter.
