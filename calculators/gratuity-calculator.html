Gratuity Calculator India

Estimate gratuity using last-drawn eligible monthly wages and qualifying service. This simplified model is for a standard monthly-rated employee and does not determine legal entitlement.

Calculate estimated gratuity

Formula and eligibility

For a standard monthly-rated employee: Gratuity = (last-drawn eligible monthly wages × 15 × eligible service years) ÷ 26.

A completed year of service is counted, along with a part of a year that exceeds six months. The general five-year continuous-service threshold applies in ordinary cases; exceptions can apply for death, disablement and specified fixed-term employment situations. Wage definitions and legal treatment must be checked against the law in force and employee records.

Official references: Ministry of Labour and Employment — Gratuity FAQ (2026) and Payment of Gratuity Act text.

Worked example

For eligible wages of ₹25,000 per month and five qualifying years: ₹25,000 × 15 × 5 ÷ 26 = ₹72,115.38, before any applicable statutory ceiling or other adjustment.

Frequently asked questions

What wages should I enter?

Enter the last-drawn eligible wages under the applicable rules, commonly basic pay plus dearness allowance for a monthly-rated employee. Do not enter gross CTC without checking the wage definition.

How are partial years counted?

The standard rule counts completed years and a part of a year in excess of six months.

Does every employee need five years?

The ordinary rule generally requires five years of continuous service, but exceptions and fixed-term employment provisions may apply in specified cases.

Does this calculate tax or the statutory maximum?

No. This page estimates the formula amount only. Check the applicable statutory ceiling, tax treatment and current rules separately.
