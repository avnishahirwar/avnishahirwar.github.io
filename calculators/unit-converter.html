Supported unit conversions

  • Length: millimetres, centimetres, metres, kilometres, inches, feet and miles.
  • Weight / mass: milligrams, grams, kilograms, ounces and pounds.
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.
  • Volume: millilitres, litres, cubic metres and US gallons.
  • Speed: metres per second, kilometres per hour, miles per hour and knots.

How to use this unit converter

Choose a conversion category, enter the number, select the source and destination units, and press Convert. Use Swap to reverse the conversion. The result is calculated in your browser.

Conversion accuracy

Standard unit relationships are used for the listed measurements. Results are displayed to up to ten decimal places to avoid unnecessary trailing digits. Some customary units, such as the US gallon, differ from Imperial units; this tool labels the US gallon explicitly.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert kilograms to pounds?

Yes. Choose Weight / Mass, then kilograms and pounds.

Can I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Yes. Choose Temperature and select Celsius and Fahrenheit.

Is this converter free?

Yes. Conversions run in your browser without requiring an account.
