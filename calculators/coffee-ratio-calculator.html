Coffee Ratio Calculator

Enter ground coffee mass and water mass to calculate the brew ratio and concentration by mass.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter both masses in grams. The ratio compares the input amounts; it does not prescribe a taste or brewing method.

Formula and methodology

Brew ratio = water mass ÷ coffee mass, expressed as 1 : x. Concentration = coffee mass ÷ (coffee mass + water mass) × 100%.

Worked example

20 g of coffee with 320 g of water gives a 1:16 brew ratio.

Important limitations

Food quantities and preparation outcomes vary by ingredient, equipment, recipe and method. Treat yield factors and serving assumptions as estimates and adjust them to your own recipe.

Frequently asked questions

What does 1:16 mean?

It means one part coffee by mass for every sixteen parts water by mass.

Does this guarantee a particular taste?

No. Grind size, extraction, roast, water and brewing method also affect taste.
