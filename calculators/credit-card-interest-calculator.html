Credit card interest estimate




Worked example

For an outstanding balance of ₹25,000 at 36% annual interest for 30 days, the simple-interest illustration is ₹25,000 × 36% × 30 ÷ 365 = ₹739.73 (rounded). Actual card statements may use daily balances, transaction dates, compounding and issuer-specific rules.
