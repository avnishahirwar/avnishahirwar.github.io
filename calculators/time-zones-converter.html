Time Zones Converter

The time zones converter allows you to translate any date and time from one time zone to all other time zones. Thanks to this tool, you can have a world clock without the need for a time zone map and tedious calculations!

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Time Zones Converter works with 52 quantities and 100 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • ADT (ADT) = ART
  • ART (ART) = ADT
  • AST (AST) = EDT
  • EDT (EDT) = AST
  • EST (EST) = CDT
  • CDT (CDT) = EST
  • MDT (MDT) = CST
  • CST (CST) = MDT

92 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
