Formula and method

Water = dry rice weight × chosen multiplier; typical 1:1.5-2.2 by type.

Worked example

300 g of white rice at 1:1.8 needs 540 ml (g) of water.

Frequently asked questions

Cooker vs pot?

Absorption cookers and open pots evaporate differently; adjust by a splash either way.

Rinsing?

Rinse until the water runs clear to reduce surface starch.

Soaking?

Soaking basmati 20-30 minutes shortens cooking and improves grain length.
