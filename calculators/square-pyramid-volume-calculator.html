Formulas used by this calculator

The Square Pyramid Volume Calculator works with 11 quantities and 24 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Area lateral (area_lateral) = 4 × area_side_a
  • Area base (area_base) = pow(base_a, 2)
  • Base diagonal (base_diagonal) = sqrt(2) × base_a
  • Base a (base_a) = sqrt(area_base)
  • Height pyr (height_pyr) = 3 × volume/area_base
  • Area side a (area_side_a) = (1/4) × area_lateral
  • Height s (height_s) = 2 × area_side_a/base_a
  • Area total (area_total) = area_base + 4 × area_side_a

16 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
