Formula and step-by-step calculation

For a supported lifetime case, 2026 RMD = the IRA balance on 31 December 2025 ÷ the IRS Table III divisor for age on the birthday in 2026. The general beginning age is 73.

Worked example: a 75-year-old with a USD 100,000 prior-year-end balance uses a Table III divisor of 24.6; the estimated RMD is USD 4,065.04 before any account-specific considerations.

Frequently asked questions

Does this result guarantee a benefit or final liability?

This limited page calculates a living traditional IRA owner’s 2026 estimate with Table III. Other account types and beneficiary situations may require different rules and tables.

What should I verify before relying on a result?

The IRS generally requires traditional IRA owners to begin at age 73. Workplace-plan and special-case rules can differ; verify the rule for your account.
