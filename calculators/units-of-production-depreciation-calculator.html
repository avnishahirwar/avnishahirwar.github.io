Units of Production Depreciation Calculator

Calculate depreciation per unit and for the period for machinery whose wear depends on output rather than time.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Depreciation per unit = (cost − salvage value) ÷ total expected units. Period depreciation = depreciation per unit × units produced.

Worked example

With Asset cost 8,00,000, Salvage value 80,000, Total expected units over life 1,00,000, Units produced this period 12,500, the calculator gives: Depreciation per unit = 7.2; Depreciation for the period = 90,000; Share of life used (%) = 12.5.

How to read the result

This method matches the cost of equipment to how much it is actually used, which suits machines with variable output.

Frequently asked questions

When is this method suitable?

When wear follows usage, for example vehicles measured in kilometres or machines measured in output.

What if actual life differs from the estimate?

Revise the expected units and adjust future depreciation prospectively.
