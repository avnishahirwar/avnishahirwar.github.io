Worked example

For an eligible purchase amount of ₹50,000, the published doubling illustration gives ₹1,00,000 at maturity after 115 months, subject to the certificate issue terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the KVP maturity period?

India Post currently displays 115 months (9 years and 7 months). Confirm the period applicable on the purchase date.

How much does ₹50,000 become?

At the displayed doubling terms, ₹50,000 becomes ₹1,00,000 at maturity. This is an illustration, not an account statement.

Can KVP interest rates change?

Yes. Government small-savings rates and maturity periods may be revised. Certificate terms depend on issue date.

Is this a government calculator?

No. This is an independent estimate. India Post records and the certificate terms are authoritative for an actual account.
