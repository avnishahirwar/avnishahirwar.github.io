What this calculator does

Formula and calculation method

Daily pay = monthly salary ÷ working days. Hourly pay = daily pay ÷ working hours per day.

Worked example

₹30,000 monthly pay with 25 working days and 8 hours daily gives an estimated ₹1,200 per day and ₹150 per hour.

Guide: understanding your Salary Calculator results

Turning a monthly salary into hourly and daily rates

Dividing a monthly salary by the days you work gives the daily rate, and dividing that by hours per day gives the hourly rate. For ₹45,000 a month over 26 working days and 9 hours a day, the daily rate is ₹1,730.77 and the hourly rate is ₹192.31. The tool also estimates weekly pay as monthly × 12 ÷ 52 (₹10,384.62) and half-yearly pay as monthly × 6 (₹2,70,000).

Where this is useful

Freelancers pricing a project, employees comparing offers with different working hours, and employers estimating overtime or part-day deductions all need an hourly figure. When comparing two offers, compare the hourly value rather than the monthly figure if the working hours differ.

Gross, in-hand and CTC

Enter the figure that matches your question. CTC includes employer contributions and benefits, gross pay is before deductions, and in-hand pay is what reaches your bank. For comparing your time value, in-hand is usually the most honest number.

Worked example with real numbers

Monthly salary ₹45,000, 26 working days, 9 hours a day: ₹1,730.77 per day, ₹192.31 per hour, ₹10,384.62 per week and ₹2,70,000 per half-year. If the same job were only 22 working days and 8 hours, the hourly figure would be ₹255.68, showing how working hours change the value of the same salary.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using calendar days instead of working days.
  • Comparing CTC of one offer with in-hand pay of another.
  • Ignoring commute time, which adds to the hours the job really takes.
  • Using this figure for legally calculating overtime or leave encashment, where the employer's policy rules apply.

Important information

The tool does not deduct PF, TDS, professional tax, insurance or other payroll components. Use the working-day convention relevant to your agreement.

Frequently asked questions about the Salary Calculator

What is a fair freelance hourly rate compared with a salary?

Freelancers usually need a higher hourly rate than the salaried equivalent, because they pay their own tax, leave, equipment and idle time. Many people start by multiplying the salaried hourly rate by 1.5 to 2.

Why is weekly salary monthly × 12 ÷ 52?

There are 12 months and about 52 weeks in a year, so this gives a consistent weekly figure instead of assuming 4 weeks per month.

Does this deduct tax or PF?

No. It splits the amount you enter. Use the income tax calculator for tax.

How do I convert an annual package to monthly?

Divide by 12 and make sure you use the in-hand amount, not the CTC, if you want a realistic figure.

Can I calculate a per-minute rate?

Divide the hourly rate by 60.
