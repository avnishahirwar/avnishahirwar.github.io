Reverberation Time Calculator

The reverberation time calculator works out what is the time required for the sound intensity to decrease by 60 dB.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Reverberation Time Calculator works with 16 quantities and 20 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Reverberation time (reverberation_time) = (163/1000) × room_height × room_length × room_width/effective_area
  • Effective area (effective_area) = (163/1000) × room_height × room_length × room_width/reverberation_time
  • Room length (room_length) = (1000/163) × effective_area × reverberation_time/(room_height × room_width)
  • Room height (room_height) = (1000/163) × effective_area × reverberation_time/(room_length × room_width)
  • Room width (room_width) = (1000/163) × effective_area × reverberation_time/(room_height × room_length)
  • Number of windows (number_of_windows) = (ceiling_coeff × room_length × room_width + door_height × door_width × doors_coeff × number_of_doors - door_height × door_width × number_of_doors × walls_coeff - effective_area + floor_coeff × room_length × room_width + 2 × room_height × room_length × walls_coeff + 2 × room_height × room_width × walls_coeff)/(windows_height × windows_width × (walls_coeff - windows_coeff))
  • Windows height (windows_height) = (ceiling_coeff × room_length × room_width + door_height × door_width × doors_coeff × number_of_doors - door_height × door_width × number_of_doors × walls_coeff - effective_area + floor_coeff × room_length × room_width + 2 × room_height × room_length × walls_coeff + 2 × room_height × room_width × walls_coeff)/(number_of_windows × windows_width × (walls_coeff - windows_coeff))
  • Windows width (windows_width) = (ceiling_coeff × room_length × room_width + door_height × door_width × doors_coeff × number_of_doors - door_height × door_width × number_of_doors × walls_coeff - effective_area + floor_coeff × room_length × room_width + 2 × room_height × room_length × walls_coeff + 2 × room_height × room_width × walls_coeff)/(number_of_windows × windows_height × (walls_coeff - windows_coeff))
