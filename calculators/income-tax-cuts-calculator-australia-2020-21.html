Formula and step-by-step calculation

The page applies the resident 2020–21 marginal tax brackets, then subtracts the Low Income Tax Offset (LITO) and the one-year Low and Middle Income Tax Offset (LMITO), with tax floored at zero.

Worked example: at taxable income of AUD 50,000, base income tax is AUD 6,717.00, LITO is AUD 250.00, and LMITO is AUD 1,080.00, giving AUD 5,387.00 before the Medicare levy.

Limitations

Resident individuals entitled to the full tax-free threshold only. The estimate excludes the 2% Medicare levy and Medicare levy surcharge, deductions, other offsets, part-year residency, non-resident rules and individual assessment details.
