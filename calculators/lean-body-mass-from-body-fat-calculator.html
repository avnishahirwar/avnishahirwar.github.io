Lean Body Mass from Body Fat Calculator

Split body weight into fat mass and lean mass using a measured or estimated body fat percentage.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Fat mass = weight × body fat % ÷ 100. Lean body mass = weight − fat mass.

Worked example

With Body weight 70, Body fat 20, the calculator gives: Fat mass (kg) = 14; Lean body mass (kg) = 56; Lean mass share (%) = 80.

How to read the result

Body fat estimates from scales, calipers or scans can differ by several percentage points, so track the same method over time.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the result?

It is only as accurate as your body fat measurement, which varies by device and hydration.

Is lean mass the same as muscle mass?

No. Lean mass also includes water, bone and organs.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives general informational estimates only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Individual needs vary with age, health conditions, medicines and activity, so speak with a qualified health professional before changing diet, training or medication.
