Formula

Doubling time = elapsed time × ln(2) ÷ ln(final value ÷ initial value)

Use consistent units and check the assumptions before applying a result to a real-world decision.

Worked example

A value that grows from 100 to 200 in 8 days has a doubling time of 8 days.

Frequently asked questions

How does this calculator work?

The calculation assumes continuous exponential growth at a constant proportional rate. It is an estimate, not a forecast.

Are results rounded?

Results are formatted for readability. Small differences may appear when a displayed result is rounded.
