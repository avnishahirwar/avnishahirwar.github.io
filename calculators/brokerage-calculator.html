How to use this calculator

Enter the trade turnover and the rates from your broker's published tariff. This estimate includes only brokerage and GST on brokerage.

Formula and method

Brokerage = max(turnover × rate ÷ 100, minimum brokerage), when turnover is positive. GST = brokerage × GST rate ÷ 100. Total = brokerage + GST.

Important assumptions and limitations

Exchange transaction charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, STT, DP charges, other taxes and broker-specific rounding are excluded. Actual charges depend on the segment, order, broker tariff and current rules. This is not investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include all trading charges?

No. It estimates brokerage and GST on brokerage only; exchange and statutory charges are excluded.

Where do I find the brokerage rate?

Check your broker's current tariff or contract note. Do not assume that a sample rate applies to your account.

Is the estimate the exact amount charged by my broker?

Not necessarily. Broker rules, rounding and other charges may change the final amount.
