How it works

Average speed is distance divided by elapsed time. Pace is elapsed time divided by distance. The result assumes the distance and time are measured consistently; it does not account for hills, stops, intervals, wind, surface or GPS error.

Training note: This is a timing calculation, not a recommendation for how fast you should run. Adjust training to your experience and health, and stop if you feel unwell.
