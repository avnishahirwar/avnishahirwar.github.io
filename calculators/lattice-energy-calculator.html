Lattice Energy Calculator

Estimate lattice enthalpy for a 1:1 ionic solid from formation, atomization, ionization, bond-dissociation and electron-affinity enthalpies using a Born-Haber cycle.

Enter your values

Formula

For a 1:1 ionic solid: ΔHlattice(form.) = ΔHf − [ΔHatomization + IE + ½D + EA].

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change the inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Adjust one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

This simplified Born-Haber cycle assumes a 1:1 ionic solid and the entered enthalpy sign conventions. Multi-charge ions, polyatomic ions and more complex stoichiometries require additional terms.
