How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The 529 Calculator works with 21 quantities and 19 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Total College Cost (totalCollegeCost) = x - y
  • Annual Interest (annualInterest) = 12 × interestPerPeriod
  • x (x) = totalCollegeCost + y
  • Total Pay Periods (totalPayPeriods) = 12 × investmentEndYears
  • Interest Per Period (interestPerPeriod) = (1/12) × annualInterest
  • y (y) = -totalCollegeCost + x
  • Investment Start Years (investmentStartYears) = -childAge + collegeAge
  • Investment End Years (investmentEndYears) = (1/12) × totalPayPeriods

11 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
