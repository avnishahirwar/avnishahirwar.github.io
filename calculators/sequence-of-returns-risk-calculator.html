Sequence of Returns Risk Calculator

Illustrate how the order of annual portfolio returns can affect a portfolio when fixed withdrawals are taken each year.

Enter your values

Enter values and select Calculate.

This calculator is an educational illustration based only on the values and assumptions you enter. It is not investment, tax, legal or financial advice, a product recommendation or a guarantee. Actual market results, costs, taxes, fees and applicable rules may differ.

Formula and calculation method

At each year start, subtract the withdrawal (capped at the balance), then apply that year's return. Compare the entered return sequence with its reverse.

Worked example

The same set of annual returns can produce different ending balances when withdrawals occur, even if the arithmetic average return is unchanged.

How to use

  1. Enter values using the units shown.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result.
  3. Change one assumption at a time to compare scenarios.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Inputs are user-supplied; no live market, broker, bank or tax data is retrieved.
  • Fees, taxes, spreads, liquidity, product terms and other real-world effects are excluded unless stated.
  • Results are mathematical estimates and should be independently checked before a financial decision.
