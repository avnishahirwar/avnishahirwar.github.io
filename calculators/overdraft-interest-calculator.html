Overdraft Interest Calculator

Estimate interest on an overdraft or revolving bank balance using a daily-rate approximation.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Estimated overdraft interest = drawn balance × annual rate ÷ 100 × days ÷ 365.

Use consistent reporting periods, currency units and accounting definitions. This is a planning estimate and does not determine regulatory compliance or a customer’s approved product terms.

Worked example

A 100,000 overdraft at 12% for 45 days costs approximately 1,479.45 in interest before fees.
