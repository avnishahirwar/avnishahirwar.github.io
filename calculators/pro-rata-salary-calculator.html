Pro-Rata Salary Calculator

Calculate pay for a part month from the monthly salary, days worked and the number of days in the pay period.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Pro-rata pay = monthly salary × days worked ÷ days in the pay period.

Worked example

With Monthly salary 75,000, Days worked or paid 18, Days in the pay period 30, the calculator gives: Pro-rata pay = 45,000; Amount not paid = 30,000; Pay per day = 2,500.

How to read the result

Employers differ on whether to divide by calendar days, working days or a fixed 30 days. Use the method in your contract or policy so the result matches your payslip.

Frequently asked questions

Which days should I count?

Use the day basis in your employment contract. Calendar days, working days and a fixed 30-day month all give different answers.

Does this include deductions?

No. It is gross pay before tax, provident fund or other deductions.
