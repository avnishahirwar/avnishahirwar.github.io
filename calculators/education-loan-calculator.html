Education loan EMI estimate





Worked example

For ₹10,00,000 at 9% simple interest during a 24-month moratorium, estimated accrued interest is ₹1,80,000. If added to principal, the illustrative repayment principal is ₹11,80,000. Over 10 years at 9%, the estimated EMI is approximately ₹14,928. Lender capitalization and concession rules can differ.

How to use this calculator

Enter the loan principal, annual interest rate, repayment tenure and estimated moratorium months. This simplified model assumes simple interest during the moratorium and does not model tranche-wise disbursement, capitalisation rules or lender concessions.

Enter figures in the same currency and time period, check the assumptions and use the result as an estimate. The calculator runs in your browser and does not verify a lender offer, bank statement or investment product.

Formula and calculation method

Moratorium interest estimate = principal × annual rate × moratorium months ÷ 12. The estimated repayment principal is principal plus this interest; EMI then uses the standard reducing-balance formula.

Understanding the result

Compare both the periodic payment and the total amount paid. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest. Fees, insurance, taxes, rate resets, repayment dates and provider-specific terms may affect the actual amount.

Important limitations

This page provides general arithmetic estimates, not personalised financial, tax, credit or investment advice. It does not recommend a loan or investment product, guarantee eligibility or returns, or fetch live rates. Confirm current terms with the relevant regulated provider and read the official agreement before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions
