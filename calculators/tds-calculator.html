Guide: understanding your Salary TDS Calculator results

What salary TDS is

Your employer deducts tax at source from your salary every month, based on an estimate of your tax for the whole year. At the end of the year the amount deducted is shown in Form 16. This tool estimates the annual tax, subtracts the TDS already deducted and spreads the balance across the months left.

A worked example

Take an annual salary of ₹18,00,000 in the new regime. After the standard deduction, the tool estimates tax of ₹1,45,000 plus 4% cess of ₹5,800, giving ₹1,50,800 for the year. With no TDS deducted yet and 12 months remaining, the suggested monthly TDS is ₹12,566.67. If only 4 months remained and ₹1,00,000 had already been deducted, the monthly figure would be ₹50,800 ÷ 4 = ₹12,700.

Why your payslip may differ

Employers also consider declared investments, house rent allowance, income from a previous employer and perquisites. If you start a new job midway through the year, give the new employer your previous employer's salary details so the tax is not under- or over-deducted.

Worked example with real numbers

Annual salary ₹18,00,000, new regime, resident, below 60, no TDS so far, 12 months remaining: taxable salary ₹17,25,000, income tax ₹1,45,000, cess ₹5,800, estimated annual tax ₹1,50,800, suggested monthly TDS ₹12,566.67.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Forgetting to enter TDS already deducted this year.
  • Using months remaining of the financial year incorrectly (the current month counts).
  • Not telling the employer about other income or a previous job.
  • Expecting the monthly figure to be identical to the payslip.

Frequently asked questions about the Salary TDS Calculator

What if too much TDS was deducted?

You can claim a refund by filing your income tax return. The refund is the tax deducted minus your actual tax.

What is Form 12BB?

A form where you declare investments, HRA and other claims to your employer so TDS can be reduced. It applies mainly to the old regime.

Can I reduce my monthly TDS?

Only by legitimate deductions or by choosing the regime that results in lower tax. Declare them to your employer early in the year.

Is TDS the same as my final tax?

No. TDS is an advance payment. The final tax is computed in your return, and the difference is refunded or paid.

Does the tool apply to non-salary TDS?

No. It models salary TDS only. Interest, rent and professional fees have separate TDS rules.
