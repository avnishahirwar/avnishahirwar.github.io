Body Adiposity Index (BAI) Calculator

Calculate the published BAI equation from hip circumference and height. The result is an index estimate, not a measured body-fat percentage or health diagnosis.

Calculator

Measure hip circumference consistently around the widest part of the hips/buttocks. Height is converted from centimetres to metres in the formula.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula and method

BAI = [hip circumference in centimetres ÷ (height in metres)1.5] − 18. The published output is conventionally described as an approximate body-adiposity percentage, but it is not a direct measurement.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

Using hip circumference 97 cm and height 173 cm (1.73 m): 97 ÷ (1.731.5) − 18 ≈ 24.63 BAI points. This is an index calculation only.

Scope and limitations

  • BAI is an educational, non-diagnostic estimate. It does not measure body fat, diagnose obesity or determine an individual’s health risk.
  • The original relationship was developed in a specific study population; the cited evaluation found BAI was not more accurate than BMI or circumferences and reported sex-related bias.
  • Do not apply diagnostic cutoffs or treatment decisions from this result. Measurement technique and population differences affect interpretation.
  • For health questions, use a qualified clinician and validated assessment methods; this page does not provide medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is BAI a direct body-fat measurement?

No. It is an equation-based index using hip circumference and height, not a measurement such as DXA.

Can I use BAI to diagnose a health condition?

No. This calculator provides no diagnosis or treatment guidance; the research also reports limitations in individual accuracy.
