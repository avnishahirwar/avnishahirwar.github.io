Formula

Momentum p = mv. Impulse J = Δp = m(v_f − v_i). If the interval is known, average net force F_avg = Δp/Δt. SI units: kg·m/s, N·s and N.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

For m = 2 kg, v_i = +3 m/s and v_f = −1 m/s: Δp = 2(−1 − 3) = −8 N·s. Over 0.5 s, F_avg = −8 ÷ 0.5 = −16 N.

Assumptions and limitations

  • This is a one-dimensional signed model. Positive and negative velocities refer to the direction chosen by the user.
  • Impulse uses the change in momentum due to net force over the interval; force from the optional time field is an average, not an instantaneous value.
  • Use consistent SI units. The model does not describe complex multidimensional collisions or provide safety/engineering advice.
