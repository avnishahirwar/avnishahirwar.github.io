Formula and methodology

This estimate counts calendar days from the first day of the last menstrual period. The conventional estimated due date is 280 days (40 weeks) after that date. The trimester labels use week 14 and week 28 as approximate boundaries.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values requested in the form.
  2. Check the units and assumptions before calculating.
  3. Review the result and use it as an estimate, not a substitute for professional advice.

Calculations run locally in your browser. Do not enter passwords, account numbers or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a medical diagnosis?

No. It is a date estimate only. A qualified healthcare professional can confirm pregnancy dating and care needs.

Can cycle length change the estimate?

Yes. The standard 280-day estimate assumes a 28-day cycle with ovulation around day 14. Individual cycles vary.
