Enterprise Value Calculator

Estimate enterprise value by combining equity value, debt, preferred equity and minority interest, then subtracting cash. Enter consistent reporting-period values to estimate the result.

Formula and methodology

Enterprise value = Market capitalization + Total debt + Preferred equity + Minority interest − Cash and cash equivalents. Use consistent valuation dates and currency.

Keep all inputs on the same reporting date, accounting period and currency basis. Results are arithmetic estimates, not investment recommendations or an assessment of a company's quality.

Worked example

₹250 crore market capitalization + ₹40 crore debt − ₹15 crore cash = enterprise value of ₹275 crore.

Frequently asked questions

Are these figures guaranteed market values?

No. Enter current figures from reliable financial statements or market data. This tool does not retrieve live share prices or filings.

Does the calculator provide investment advice?

No. It explains a calculation only. Consider company risks, reporting quality and other relevant information before making financial decisions.

What currency should I use?

Use one currency consistently for all monetary inputs. The default examples use Indian rupees, but the formulas are currency-neutral.
