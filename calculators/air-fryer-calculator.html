How the oven-to-air-fryer estimate works

The calculator applies a simple starting rule: reduce the oven temperature by about 15°C (25°F) and shorten the cooking time to about 80% of the oven time. The reverse conversion estimates oven settings from air-fryer settings.

Worked example

An oven recipe set to 200°C for 20 minutes converts to an estimated air-fryer setting of 185°C for 16 minutes.

Why results can vary

Air-fryer basket size, airflow, food thickness, quantity and appliance design affect cooking. Treat the result as a starting estimate, follow the recipe and appliance guidance, and check food is fully cooked before serving.
