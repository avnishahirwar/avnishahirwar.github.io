Earnings Yield Calculator

Calculate a share earnings yield and the matching price-to-earnings ratio from earnings per share and market price.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Earnings yield (%) = earnings per share ÷ share price × 100. Price-to-earnings ratio = share price ÷ earnings per share.

Worked example

With Earnings per share 45, Share price 900, the calculator gives: Earnings yield (%) = 5; Price-to-earnings ratio = 20.

How to read the result

Earnings yield is the inverse of the P/E ratio and makes a share easy to compare with the yield on a bond or deposit. It uses accounting earnings, which can include one-off items.

Frequently asked questions

Is a higher earnings yield always better?

Not necessarily. A high yield can signal risk or falling earnings, while a low yield can reflect expected growth.

Which EPS should I use?

Use trailing twelve-month EPS for history or forecast EPS for expectations, and say which you used when comparing.
