Formula and method

Cost per cup = bag price ÷ bag grams × dose + extras.

Worked example

An 800 rupee, 500 g bag at 15 g a cup gives about 24 plus 4 rupees of extras, or 28 rupees a cup.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Include equipment?

Add machine amortisation by dividing its price by expected cups.

How many cups per bag?

Bag grams ÷ dose; espresso pulls 15-18 g while filter uses 12-15 g.

Is homemade cheaper?

Usually far cheaper than café prices at the same quality tier.
