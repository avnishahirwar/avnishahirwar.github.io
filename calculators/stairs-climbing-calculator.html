Formula and method

Vertical gain = steps × step height. Work (kJ) = 9.81 × weight × metres ÷ 1,000; kcal ≈ work ÷ 4.184 ÷ 0.33.

Worked example

Climbing 100 steps of 17 cm at 70 kg raises you 17 m and costs roughly 9 kcal of metabolic energy.

Frequently asked questions

Why divide by 0.33?

Human muscle converts about a third of metabolic energy into mechanical work, so energy cost is larger than the work itself.

Does descending burn calories?

Yes, but less than climbing. This calculator models ascent only.

Are all steps the same height?

No. Building codes vary; measure your staircase for accuracy.
