NSC formula and current rate

Updated: 13 November 2026. The NSC rate is 7.7% per annum for Q3 FY 2026–27 (1 October–31 December 2026). NSC interest is compounded annually and paid at maturity. Verify the quarterly rate notification with the Department of Economic Affairs.

Formula: Maturity = P × (1 + r)t, where P is the investment, r is the annual rate as a decimal and t is the number of years. This calculator's NSC compounding input defaults to 1 (annual).

Worked example: ₹1,00,000 invested for 5 years at 7.7% annual compounding gives an illustrative maturity of about ₹1,44,903.38 and interest of about ₹44,903.38. The official certificate schedule and rounding govern the actual value.

How this calculator works

This tool applies a transparent mathematical estimate to the values entered above. Review the labels, units and result before relying on it. Where an interest rate, fee, tax rate, eligibility limit or scheme term is requested, enter the value that applies to your own situation and confirm it with the relevant official provider.

The calculator does not connect to banks, brokers, government portals or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, tax classification, exemptions or compliance obligations. It excludes charges and adjustments unless a field explicitly asks for them.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current NSC interest rate?

The rate used here is 7.7% per annum for 1 October–31 December 2026. Rates are notified quarterly; verify the latest government notification before investing.

How is NSC interest compounded?

NSC interest is compounded annually and paid at maturity. This page defaults to annual compounding for its estimate.

Is the displayed maturity guaranteed?

This is an estimate based on the entered principal, rate and tenure. The certificate issue date, official scheme rules and rounding determine the actual maturity amount.
