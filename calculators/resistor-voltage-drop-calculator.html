Resistor Voltage Drop Calculator

The resistor voltage drop calculator is a tool to easily find the required resistance and the minimum power it must have to drop the voltage.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Resistor Voltage Drop Calculator works with 12 quantities and 14 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Resistance total (resistance_total) = Voltage/current
  • Resistance (resistance) = power/pow(current, 2)
  • Current (current) = sqrt(power/resistance)
  • Voltage (Voltage) = current × resistance_total
  • Power (power) = pow(current, 2) × resistance
  • V supply (V_supply) = V_load + current × resistance
  • V load (V_load) = V_supply - current × resistance
  • Resistance 1 (resistance_1) = resistance_2 × (V_in - V_out)/V_out

6 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
