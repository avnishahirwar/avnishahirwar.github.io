Mortgage Comparison Calculator

Mortgage calculator is a simple tool that helps you estimate the cost of your mortgage.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formula and calculation method

Both scenarios use the same loan balance and compounding convention. Each payment is calculated from its own annual rate and repayment term.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a mathematical comparison, not a lender offer. Fees, points, insurance, tax and rate-change clauses are not included unless considered separately.

How to use this calculator

Enter the first rate and term, then the alternative rate and term to compare the payment and interest estimates.

Frequently asked questions

Does the selected country automatically supply local mortgage rules?

No. The selected country and Display currency settings control the website's regional presentation and currency formatting. Enter the rate, fees, taxes and other amounts that apply to your lender and location.

Is this a lender quote or financial advice?

No. Results are educational estimates based on the values entered. Confirm rates, charges, repayment dates and contract terms with your lender.
