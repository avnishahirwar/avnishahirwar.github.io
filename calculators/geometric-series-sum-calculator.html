Geometric Series Sum Calculator

Find the nth term and the sum of a geometric series from the first term, common ratio and number of terms.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

nth term = a × r^(n − 1). Sum = a × (1 − r^n) ÷ (1 − r) for r ≠ 1, and a × n when r = 1.

Worked example

With First term 3, Common ratio 2, Number of terms 10, the calculator gives: nth term = 1,536; Sum of the series = 3,069.

How to read the result

A ratio above 1 grows quickly, while a ratio between 0 and 1 shrinks toward a finite limit as terms increase.

Frequently asked questions

What is the sum of an infinite geometric series?

It equals a ÷ (1 − r) when the ratio is between 0 and 1. This tool sums a finite number of terms.

Where does this appear in finance?

In compound growth, repeated discounting and loan schedules.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool applies standard textbook formulas to the values you enter. Check units and rounding, and confirm important results with a second method.
