How the SSY maturity estimate works

The estimate applies one annual contribution at the beginning of each of the first 15 financial years, then compounds the balance for the remaining six years. The annual deposit is assumed to be the same in every contribution year. The calculator does not model irregular deposit dates, missed minimum deposits, withdrawals, account defaults, penalties or premature closure.

Under the scheme rules, deposits can be made for up to 15 years from account opening and the account ordinarily matures after 21 years from opening. Actual interest is calculated monthly on the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of each month, then credited annually. This annual-compounding illustration can therefore differ from an official account statement.

SSY deposit limits and current rate

  • Minimum annual deposit: ₹250.
  • Maximum annual deposit: ₹1,50,000.
  • Subsequent deposits are made in multiples of ₹50.
  • Contribution period: up to 15 years from account opening.
  • Normal maturity: 21 years from account opening.
  • Default rate in this calculator: 8.2% p.a., the India Post-listed rate for the October–December 2026 quarter. The rate is editable and may change in later quarters.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an SSY account take to mature?

The normal maturity point is 21 years from the date the account was opened.

For how many years can deposits be made?

Deposits may be made for up to 15 years from the date of opening. The estimate assumes no additional deposits during the remaining six years.

What interest rate does this calculator use?

The default is 8.2% per annum, the rate listed for October–December 2026. The rate can change quarterly, so verify the current notification before relying on an estimate.

Is this an official maturity quotation?

No. It is a simplified estimate using annual compounding and a single beginning-of-year deposit. Actual scheme interest is calculated monthly using the eligible balance, and account-level rules can affect the result.
