Formulas used by this calculator

The RV Loan Calculator works with 8 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Total paid (total_paid) = payment × periods
  • Payment (payment) = total_paid/periods
  • Periods (periods) = total_paid/payment
  • Down payment (down_payment) = -amount + rv_price
  • Cost of loan (cost_of_loan) = -amount + total_paid
  • Rv price (rv_price) = amount + down_payment
  • Amount (amount) = -down_payment + rv_price

5 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
