Bank Reconciliation Calculator

The bank reconciliation calculator is a tool we created to help you account for the difference shown between your company's bank account and its accounting books.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Bank Reconciliation Calculator works with 12 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Adjusted Ending Balance Bank (adjustedEndingBalanceBank) = bankBalance + depositInTransit - outstandingCheques
  • Unreconciled Difference (unreconciledDifference) = abs(adjustedEndingBalanceBank - cbAdjustedEndingBalance)
  • Outstanding Cheques (outstandingCheques) = -adjustedEndingBalanceBank + bankBalance + depositInTransit
  • Deposit In Transit (depositInTransit) = adjustedEndingBalanceBank - bankBalance + outstandingCheques
  • Bank Balance (bankBalance) = adjustedEndingBalanceBank - depositInTransit + outstandingCheques
  • Cb Adjusted Ending Balance (cbAdjustedEndingBalance) = -automaticBankPayments - bankCharges + cashBookBalance + interestEarned - nSFCheques + receivable
  • Automatic Bank Payments (automaticBankPayments) = -bankCharges + cashBookBalance - cbAdjustedEndingBalance + interestEarned - nSFCheques + receivable
  • Cash Book Balance (cashBookBalance) = automaticBankPayments + bankCharges + cbAdjustedEndingBalance - interestEarned + nSFCheques - receivable

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
