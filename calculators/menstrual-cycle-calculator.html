Menstrual Cycle & Period Date Calculator

Estimate upcoming calendar dates from the first day of a period and an average cycle length.

Enter a date and choose an average cycle length.
Important: This is a calendar estimate, not a diagnosis or contraception. Cycle length can change and the result cannot confirm pregnancy, ovulation or a health condition.

How the estimate works

The calculator adds the selected average cycle length to the first day of the previous period. It repeats that addition for the number of periods requested. For example, a 28-day cycle starting on 1 January produces an estimated next start around 29 January. The calculation uses calendar dates and does not account for symptoms, hormonal medicines, postpartum changes, breastfeeding, perimenopause or irregular cycles.

When to seek help

Talk with a qualified healthcare professional if cycles are persistently very irregular, bleeding is unusually heavy or prolonged, pain is severe, or you are worried about pregnancy or another symptom. Seek urgent local medical care for severe pain, fainting or heavy bleeding. Keep a private record of dates and symptoms if that helps you speak with a clinician.
