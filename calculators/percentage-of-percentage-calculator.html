Percentage of a Percentage Calculator

Enter a base amount and two percentage rates to calculate the compounded percentage portion.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the base amount and both percentage rates. The calculator applies the rates sequentially, not by adding them.

Formula and methodology

Result = base amount × first percentage ÷ 100 × second percentage ÷ 100.

Worked example

20% of 50% of 1,000 is 100; the combined percentage is 10%.

Important limitations

Results follow the mathematical definitions and assumptions stated on this page. Check the displayed inputs and rounding before using results in coursework or technical work.

Frequently asked questions

Do I add the two percentages?

No. This tool calculates one percentage of another, so the rates are multiplied as proportions.

Can the base amount be negative?

No. Enter a non-negative base amount.
