RC Time Constant Calculator

Calculate the time constant, settling time and cut-off frequency of a resistor-capacitor circuit.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Time constant τ = R × C. The capacitor reaches about 99% of its final charge after 5τ. Cut-off frequency f = 1 ÷ (2π × R × C).

Worked example

With Resistance 10,000, Capacitance 100, the calculator gives: Time constant (seconds) = 1; Time to about 99% charge (seconds) = 5; Cut-off frequency (Hz) = 0.1592.

How to read the result

After one time constant a charging capacitor reaches about 63% of the supply voltage. Component tolerances can shift real timings noticeably.

Frequently asked questions

Why is capacitance entered in microfarads?

Microfarads are the common unit for timing and filter capacitors. The calculator converts to farads internally.

What is the cut-off frequency used for?

It marks where an RC low-pass or high-pass filter passes about 70.7% of the signal amplitude.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
