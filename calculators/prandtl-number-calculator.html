Formulas used by this calculator

The Prandtl Number Calculator works with 15 quantities and 22 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Diffusivity (diffusivity) = viscosity/density
  • Viscosity (viscosity) = density × diffusivity
  • Density (density) = viscosity/diffusivity
  • Specific heat (specific_heat) = Prandtl × conductivity/viscosity
  • Conductivity (conductivity) = specific_heat × viscosity/Prandtl
  • Diffusivity custom (diffusivity_custom) = viscosity_custom/density_custom
  • Viscosity custom (viscosity_custom) = density_custom × diffusivity_custom
  • Density custom (density_custom) = viscosity_custom/diffusivity_custom

14 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
