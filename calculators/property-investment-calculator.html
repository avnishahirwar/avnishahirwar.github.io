Formula and limitations

Gross rental yield = annual rent ÷ purchase price. Annual net cash flow = annual rent − entered annual costs. Cash-on-cash return = annual net cash flow ÷ own cash invested. This simple tool excludes financing interest, appreciation, tax treatment, legal costs and local vacancy patterns unless you include them in your inputs.

Use a local valuation, lender quote and professional tax/legal advice before committing to a property.
