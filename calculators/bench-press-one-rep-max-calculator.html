Formula and method

Epley formula: 1RM = weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30).

Worked example

90 kg for 8 reps: 90 × (1 + 8/30) ≈ 114 kg estimated one-rep max.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.
