Formulas used by this calculator

The Pump Horsepower Calculator works with 9 quantities and 14 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Shaft Power (ShaftPower) = HydPower/Efficiency
  • Efficiency (Efficiency) = HydPower/ShaftPower
  • Hyd Power (HydPower) = Efficiency × ShaftPower
  • Spec Speed (SpecSpeed) = sqrt(Discharge) × Revs/pow(Gravity × Head, 3/4)
  • Discharge (Discharge) = Efficiency × ShaftPower/(Density × Gravity × Head)
  • Gravity (Gravity) = pow(sqrt(Discharge) × Revs/SpecSpeed, 4/3)/Head
  • Density (Density) = Efficiency × ShaftPower/(Discharge × Gravity × Head)
  • Revs (Revs) = SpecSpeed × pow(Gravity × Head, 3/4)/sqrt(Discharge)

6 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
