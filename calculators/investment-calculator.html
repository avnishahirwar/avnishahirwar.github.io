Investment Calculator

Estimate the future value of an initial investment and regular monthly contributions using an assumed annual return and investment period.

Enter your values

How this calculator works

This calculator combines an initial amount with recurring monthly contributions and compounds both at the annual return assumption entered. Monthly contributions are treated as deposits made at the end of each month. The return is hypothetical and remains constant in the model; real investment returns fluctuate and can be negative.

Calculation method

Future value = future value of the initial amount + future value of monthly contributions. The initial amount compounds monthly. Monthly contributions are accumulated as an ordinary annuity using the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12). Total invested is the initial amount plus all monthly deposits; illustrative growth is projected value minus total contributions.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter an initial amount and/or monthly contribution.
  2. Enter an assumed annual return and investment period.
  3. Select Calculate and review the projected value and total contributions.
  4. Change one assumption at a time to compare scenarios.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • Results are illustrations, not forecasts, guarantees or investment recommendations.
  • Returns are assumed constant and compounded monthly; actual investment values fluctuate.
  • Taxes, fees, inflation, contribution timing changes and market volatility are not included.
  • This calculator does not connect to investment accounts or recommend products.

Frequently asked questions

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. The annual return is an assumption used for illustration, not a forecast or guarantee. Market-linked investments can lose value.

When are monthly contributions assumed?

Each contribution is treated as deposited at the end of the month. Beginning-of-month deposits would produce a slightly different result.

Does this recommend a product?

No. It does not assess suitability, risk tolerance, taxes, fees or a specific investment product.
