Rebar Calculator

Estimate nominal reinforcing-bar mass and material cost from bar diameter, length, quantity and price per kilogram.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the measurements in the units shown beside each field. Review the assumptions and use the results as an initial material-planning estimate.

Formula and methodology

Approximate nominal mass per metre (kg/m) = bar diameter² (mm²) ÷ 162. Total mass = mass per metre × total bar length.

Worked example

A nominal 12 mm bar is approximately 0.889 kg/m. Twenty 12 m bars are approximately 213.3 kg.

Important limitations

The d²/162 rule is a rounded estimating convention. Confirm actual bar mass, grade, laps, hooks, bends, wastage and bar schedule with project drawings and applicable standards. This is not structural design advice.

Frequently asked questions

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are estimates. Confirm product specifications, site measurements, local requirements and supplier packaging before ordering.

Can I use this for structural approval?

No. This calculator does not replace engineering design, construction drawings, building-code checks or advice from a qualified professional.
