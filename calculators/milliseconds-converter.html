Formulas used by this calculator

The Milliseconds Converter works with 6 quantities and 10 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Days (days) = ms
  • Seconds (seconds) = ms
  • Minutes (minutes) = hrs
  • Hrs (hrs) = days
  • ms (ms) = days
  • Other units (other_units) = minutes

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
