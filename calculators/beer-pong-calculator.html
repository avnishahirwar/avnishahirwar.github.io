Formulas used by this calculator

The Beer Pong Calculator works with 19 quantities and 22 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • No of teams (no_of_teams) = no_of_games + 1
  • No of games (no_of_games) = no_of_teams - 1
  • Beer per tournament (beer_per_tournament) = beer_costs/cost_of_beer
  • Beer per team per game (beer_per_team_per_game) = beer_in_cup × cups_per_team
  • Cost of beer (cost_of_beer) = beer_costs/beer_per_tournament
  • Beer costs (beer_costs) = beer_per_tournament × cost_of_beer
  • Max beer per person (max_beer_per_person) = alcohol_weight/percentage_content
  • Cups per team (cups_per_team) = beer_per_team_per_game/beer_in_cup

14 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
