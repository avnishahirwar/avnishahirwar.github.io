Smoking Recovery Calculator

Watch your body heal and see how much longer you'll live after you quit smoking! This smoking recovery calculator shows you the exact dates when the withdrawal syndrome is resolved, when your circulatory system recovers and when the risk of cancer decreases.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Smoking Recovery Calculator works with 17 quantities and 19 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Date (date) = bpm - 1
  • Anxiety (anxiety) = date + 3
  • Bpm (bpm) = date + 1
  • Less withdrawal (less_withdrawal) = date + 14
  • No withdrawal (no_withdrawal) = date + 28
  • No heart dis (no_heart_dis) = floor((4/1461) × date) + 1985
  • Lung (lung) = floor((4/1461) × date) + 1980
  • Pancreatic (pancreatic) = floor((4/1461) × date) + 1990

11 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
