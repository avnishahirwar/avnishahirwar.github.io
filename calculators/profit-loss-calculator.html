What this calculator does

Compare cost with selling price. This basic calculation excludes overheads, taxes, shipping, discounts, platform fees and financing costs unless included in the cost figure.

How to use it

  1. Enter cost price
  2. enter selling price
  3. review profit or loss amount
  4. review percentage on cost.
Calculate now

Formula and calculation method

Profit/loss = selling price − cost price. Percentage = absolute difference ÷ cost price × 100. Positive means profit; negative means loss.

Worked example

A total cost of ₹800 and selling price of ₹1,000 gives ₹200 profit and 25% profit on cost. This differs from margin on sales.

Important information

Profit percentage on cost is not the same as gross margin. Gross margin is generally profit divided by selling price.
