How the Payment Calculator works

Enter the purchase price, down payment, any fees added to the financed balance, annual interest rate and repayment term. Select a payment frequency to estimate the regular instalment. The model assumes a fixed nominal annual rate divided by the number of payments per year and equal payments over the term.

Formula

Amount financed = Purchase price − Down payment + Financed fees. For periodic rate r and number of payments n, payment = Amount financed × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n). When the rate is 0%, payment = Amount financed ÷ n.

Example

For a ₹10,00,000 purchase, ₹2,00,000 down payment, no financed fees, 9% annual rate and a five-year monthly schedule, the estimate uses ₹8,00,000 as the financed balance and 60 monthly payments. Your result will change when you change the inputs or payment frequency.

This is an illustrative fixed-rate estimate, not a lender quote. Actual payment schedules may use different day-count conventions, compounding, payment dates, fees, insurance, taxes, balloon payments or rate resets. Check the written agreement before making a financial decision.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between payment and EMI calculators?

This tool supports monthly, biweekly and weekly payment intervals and lets you include fees in the financed balance. The Loan EMI Calculator is focused on the standard monthly reducing-balance loan instalment.

Does a higher payment frequency always reduce interest?

Not necessarily. This calculator divides the nominal annual rate by the selected number of payments per year. Real lenders may use different accrual and compounding rules, so compare the lender's disclosed schedule.

Are taxes and insurance included?

No. Add only fees that are financed into the balance. Taxes, insurance and ongoing ownership costs are excluded unless you separately include them in your own purchase-price assumptions.
