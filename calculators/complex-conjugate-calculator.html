Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the real part and imaginary coefficient. The argument is reported in degrees in the principal range from −180° to 180°.

Formula and methodology

For z = a + bi, its conjugate is a − bi. Its modulus is √(a² + b²), and its principal argument is atan2(b, a).

Worked example

The conjugate of 3 + 4i is 3 − 4i, and its modulus is 5.

Important limitations

Frequently asked questions

What is a complex conjugate?

It is formed by changing the sign of the imaginary component while keeping the real component unchanged.

Does conjugation change the modulus?

No. A complex number and its conjugate have the same modulus.
