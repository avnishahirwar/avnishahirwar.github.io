How it works

Formulas used by this calculator

The Triangle Length Calculator works with 6 quantities and 15 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Angle B (angleB) = -angleA - angleC + PI
  • Angle C (angleC) = -angleA - angleB + PI
  • Angle A (angleA) = -angleB - angleC + PI
  • a (a) = sqrt(pow(b, 2) - 2 × b × c × cos(angleA) + pow(c, 2))
  • b (b) = sqrt(pow(a, 2) - 2 × a × c × cos(angleB) + pow(c, 2))
  • c (c) = sqrt(pow(a, 2) - 2 × a × b × cos(angleC) + pow(b, 2))

9 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
