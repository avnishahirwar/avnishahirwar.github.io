Formulas used by this calculator

The Lottery Calculator works with 15 quantities and 17 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Num matches (num_matches) = num_match - 1
  • Num match (num_match) = num_matches + 1
  • Balls to be drawn (balls_to_be_drawn) = drawn_match + num_match
  • Matches bonus pool (matches_bonus_pool) = balls_to_drawn - bonus_match
  • Drawn match (drawn_match) = balls_to_be_drawn - num_match
  • Balls in pool (balls_in_pool) = balls_to_be_drawn + pool_drawn
  • Balls to drawn (balls_to_drawn) = bonus_match + matches_bonus_pool
  • Pool drawn (pool_drawn) = balls_in_pool - balls_to_be_drawn

9 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
