Coin Toss Streak Probability Calculator

Calculate the probability of at least one run of k consecutive heads in n independent tosses, using the probability of heads you enter.

Calculator

Probability of heads is a decimal from 0 to 1; use 0.5 for a fair coin.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

Dynamic programming tracks the probability of no target run and r trailing heads (r = 0,…,k−1). A tail resets the trailing-head state to 0; a head advances it, while a head from state k−1 enters the target-run event. Summing the event mass gives the probability of at least one run.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

For 20 fair tosses (p = 0.5) and a target of 5 consecutive heads, the probability is approximately 0.2498703, or 24.987%.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Assumes independent tosses with a constant probability of heads for every toss.
  • The event is at least one overlapping or non-overlapping run of k heads; it is not the probability that the longest run equals k.
  • This is a mathematical probability, not a prediction of the next toss or gambling advice. Floating-point output is approximate.
