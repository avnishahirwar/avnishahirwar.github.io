Formula and method

Cost = watts × hours × days ÷ 1,000 × price per kWh.

Worked example

A 1,200 W AC for 6 h/day over 30 days uses 216 kWh, about ₹1,728 at ₹8/kWh.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Frequently asked questions

Which wattage do I enter?

Use the rated input on the nameplate, or compressor draw for inverter ACs.

Star rating?

Efficient inverter units draw less for the same cooling; check the label.

Thermostat habits?

Each degree higher saves roughly 6% on cooling energy.
