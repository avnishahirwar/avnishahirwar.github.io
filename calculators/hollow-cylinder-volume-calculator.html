Formulas used by this calculator

The Hollow Cylinder Volume Calculator works with 8 quantities and 11 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Inner diameter (inner_diameter) = 2 × inner_radius
  • Outer diameter (outer_diameter) = 2 × outer_radius
  • Inner radius (inner_radius) = (1/2) × inner_diameter
  • Outer radius (outer_radius) = (1/2) × outer_diameter
  • Thickness (thickness) = -1/2 × inner_diameter + (1/2) × outer_diameter
  • Cylinder volume hollow (cylinder_volume_hollow) = (1/4) × PI × height × (-pow(inner_diameter, 2) + pow(outer_diameter, 2))
  • Height (height) = -4 × cylinder_volume_hollow/(PI × (pow(inner_diameter, 2) - pow(outer_diameter, 2)))

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
