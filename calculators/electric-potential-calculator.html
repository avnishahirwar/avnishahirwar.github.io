Electric Potential Calculator

The electric potential calculator allows you to work out the electric potential at a point either due to a single point charge or a system of point charges.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Electric Potential Calculator works with 33 quantities and 32 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Charge add (charge_add) = work/pot_diff
  • Pot diff (pot_diff) = work/charge_add
  • Relative permittivity (relative_permittivity) = 8987551788/constant
  • Work (work) = charge_add × pot_diff
  • Electric potential (electric_potential) = charge × constant/distance
  • Constant (constant) = 8987551788/relative_permittivity
  • Distance (distance) = charge × constant/electric_potential
  • Charge (charge) = distance × electric_potential/constant

24 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
