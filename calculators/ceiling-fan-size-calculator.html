Formula and method

Fan diameter follows room area bands; low ceilings mount flush and tall ceilings add rod length to keep blades 2.1-2.7 m high.

Worked example

A 4 × 5 m room is 20 sq m, so a 1,400-1,600 mm fan suits it best.

Frequently asked questions

Blade height?

Keep blades about 2.1-2.7 m above the floor for safety and airflow.

Two fans?

Large or L-shaped rooms often perform better with two smaller fans.

Summer mode?

Blades spin counter-clockwise in summer to push air down.
