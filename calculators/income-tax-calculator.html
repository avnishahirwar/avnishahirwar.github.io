Income Tax Calculator India

Estimate salary income tax for Tax Year 2026–27 from gross salary, standard deduction, age, residential status and tax regime. This is an indicative salary-tax estimate, not a return-filing calculation.

Tax Year 2026–27 · Updated: 13 November 2026 · Slab reference: Income Tax Department.

Tax-year assumptions

The on-page estimate is configured for Tax Year 2026–27. Enter annual gross salary and the standard deduction applicable to the selected regime; the calculator subtracts the deduction to estimate taxable salary income. The old-regime slab calculation uses age bands for resident individuals below 60, aged 60–79 and aged 80 or above. The model includes the resident-individual Section 87A rebate, marginal relief near the ₹12 lakh new-regime threshold, applicable surcharge bands and surcharge marginal relief. It does not model non-salary income, special-rate capital gains, every deduction or exemption, or tax-return-specific adjustments. Verify your complete position using the Income Tax Department’s AY 2026–27 slab and surcharge guidance before filing. For the standard deduction, see the Department’s official deductions guidance.

What is an income tax calculator?

An income tax calculator estimates tax from taxable income and the rules selected for a particular tax year. This page currently provides a simplified estimate for ordinary slab-rate income; it does not prepare a tax return or determine final liability.

How to use this income tax calculator

  1. Enter your annual gross salary before the standard deduction.
  2. Enter the standard deduction that applies to your selected regime (₹75,000 for the new regime or ₹50,000 for the old regime under the assumptions used here).
  3. Enter the age you attain at any time during the tax year and select your residential status.
  4. Select the tax regime, calculate the estimate and review the rebate, surcharge, marginal relief and cess breakdown.

Formula and calculation method

The tool subtracts the entered standard deduction from gross salary, applies the selected tax-year slab rates, calculates Section 87A rebate and marginal relief where eligible, applies surcharge and surcharge marginal relief where applicable, and adds 4% health and education cess. It models salary income only and does not calculate special-rate income or every possible deduction and exemption.

Verified worked example — new regime

For a resident salaried individual with gross salary of ₹12,75,000, a standard deduction of ₹75,000 and the new regime selected, estimated taxable salary income is ₹12,00,000. Slab tax is ₹60,000; the Section 87A rebate is ₹60,000; surcharge and cess are ₹0. Estimated tax in this salary-only illustration: ₹0. This example excludes special-rate and non-salary income.

Income-tax calculation questions

Should I enter gross salary or taxable income?

Enter annual gross salary before the standard deduction. The calculator subtracts the standard deduction amount you enter to estimate taxable salary income. Add other income and eligible adjustments separately when checking your complete tax position; this salary-only tool does not model every income head.

Who should select resident-individual eligibility for Section 87A?

Select that option only if you meet the applicable resident-individual conditions under the relevant tax-year rules. Residency and rebate eligibility are legal tax questions; verify them against official guidance before relying on the estimate.

How does the calculator treat surcharge?

It applies the standard surcharge bands to the estimated salary tax and includes a simplified surcharge marginal-relief cap at the applicable income thresholds. Special-rate income and exceptions to surcharge treatment are not modelled, so verify complex cases using official guidance.

Does age affect the old-regime calculation?

Yes. For resident individuals, the old-regime basic exemption threshold differs for people below 60, aged 60–79, and aged 80 or above. Age does not change the new-regime slab thresholds in this calculator.

Which standard deduction should I enter?

For the assumptions used on this page, enter ₹75,000 under the new regime or ₹50,000 under the old regime. Check the applicable rules for the tax year and your salary circumstances before relying on the estimate.

Before relying on an income-tax estimate

Indian income-tax results depend on the financial year and assessment year, tax regime, type of income, eligible deductions, exemptions, rebate rules, surcharge and cess. Two people with the same gross salary can have different tax outcomes because their income components and eligibility differ.

Verify the applicable year and rules

Confirm the selected tax year and current provisions using the Income Tax Department's official guidance and calculator. This tool provides an indicative estimate from the inputs and configured assumptions; it does not prepare or file a return, determine final liability or replace advice from a qualified tax professional.

Income tax calculator guide for India

This page estimates salary tax using gross salary, a user-entered standard deduction, age, residential status and selected tax regime. It includes ordinary slab rates, resident-individual Section 87A rebate and marginal relief, surcharge bands and a surcharge marginal-relief cap. It does not calculate non-salary income, special-rate income, every exemption or deduction, TDS credits, or all return-specific adjustments, so it is not a complete personal tax assessment.

Understand the calculation sequence

A full tax computation may identify income under applicable heads, consider eligible exemptions and deductions, apply relevant slab or special rates, and then consider surcharge, cess, rebates or relief. This calculator does not collect all those details: it applies its configured simplified slab schedule to the taxable-income figure you enter, checks the selected rebate-eligibility option and adds 4% cess. Eligibility and sequence depend on current law for the relevant year.

Compare tax regimes carefully

India's tax regimes can differ in slab structure and in how deductions and exemptions are treated. This page does not ask you to enter deductions or exemptions and does not calculate them. It is therefore not a complete old-versus-new regime comparison. Do not assume a deduction available under one regime is also available under another; verify your full situation using official tools.

Information needed for a full tax calculation

A complete tax assessment may also need pension, business or professional income; interest, dividends, capital gains and special-rate income; eligible deductions or exemptions; TDS and advance tax; and additional residential or personal details. This calculator is limited to salary income and the fields shown in its form.

Estimate versus final liability

The calculator may not include every allowance, perquisite, loss set-off, capital-gain category or special provision. Reconcile Form 16, AIS, TIS, Form 26AS, bank certificates and other records before filing. Confirm current provisions on the official Income Tax Department portal or with a qualified tax professional.

Limitations

This is an educational estimate, not tax advice or a return-filing service. It does not access government records, validate documents or submit a return. Never enter passwords, OTPs or sensitive identity details into a public calculator.

Results are estimates based on entered values and assumptions. They are not professional advice. Verify important decisions with the relevant provider or a qualified professional.

Who should use this income-tax calculator?

This estimator is for readers who want an indicative comparison of salary-tax outcomes under the tax-year and assumptions stated on this page. It is not a tax-return preparation system and should not be treated as a final liability calculation.

Inputs explained

Use the annual gross salary and the standard deduction field as described on the page. Select the relevant tax regime, age band and residential status where requested. The estimate only includes the income types, deductions, rebates and reliefs explicitly listed in the calculator's assumptions; do not assume an unlisted item is included.

Common mistakes and practical use

  • Using a tax year that does not match the relevant income period.
  • Entering gross salary while forgetting that the calculator's taxable-income sequence applies only its stated deduction assumptions.
  • Assuming capital gains, business income, every exemption, TDS adjustment or special-rate income is included when it is not listed.

Use the result as a preliminary scenario comparison, then reconcile it with Form 16, AIS/TIS, tax-credit records and the official filing utility or a qualified tax professional.
