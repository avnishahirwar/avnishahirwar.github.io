Guide: understanding your Income Tax Calculator India results

Old regime and new regime

Individuals can choose between two ways of being taxed. The new regime has lower slab rates and a higher standard deduction but allows few deductions. The old regime has higher slab rates but lets you claim deductions such as Section 80C investments, health insurance and home-loan interest. Which is better depends on how much of your income you can legitimately deduct.

A worked comparison

Take a salary of ₹15,00,000. In the new regime as modelled here, the standard deduction leaves a taxable income of ₹14,25,000 and the total tax with cess is ₹97,500. In the old regime with ₹2,50,000 of eligible deductions on top of the standard deduction, taxable income is ₹12,00,000 and total tax is ₹1,79,400. For this person the new regime is clearly cheaper, but the gap narrows as deductions grow.

Rebate, surcharge and cess

A rebate under Section 87A can reduce tax to nil for incomes up to a limit, which is why a salary of ₹9,00,000 in the new regime shows zero tax in this tool. Higher incomes may attract surcharge with marginal relief, and a 4% health and education cess is added to the final tax. The tool applies these steps in order.

Worked example with real numbers

Salary ₹15,00,000, new regime, resident, below 60: taxable income ₹14,25,000, tax after rebate ₹93,750, cess ₹3,750, estimated total tax ₹97,500. Same salary, old regime with ₹2,50,000 deductions: taxable income ₹12,00,000, tax ₹1,72,500, cess ₹6,900, total ₹1,79,400.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Entering deductions for the new regime, where they mostly do not apply.
  • Mixing salary income with capital gains, which are taxed at special rates not modelled here.
  • Forgetting other income such as interest and rent.
  • Using the result as the final return amount without checking credits such as TDS paid.

Frequently asked questions about the Income Tax Calculator India

Which regime should I choose?

Run both in the calculator with your real income and deductions. If your eligible deductions are large, the old regime may win; otherwise the new regime is usually simpler and cheaper.

Is the standard deduction included?

Yes, for salary or pension income the tool applies the standard deduction for the chosen regime.

Does it handle senior citizens?

Yes. Select the age category for the old regime's higher basic exemption. The new regime does not use age-based slabs.

Why does my tax show zero?

The rebate can bring tax to nil at moderate incomes in the new regime. It does not mean you have no filing obligation; check the rules on filing returns.

Can I use this to file my return?

No. It is an estimate. Use the official income tax portal, your Form 16 and Form 26AS/AIS for filing.
