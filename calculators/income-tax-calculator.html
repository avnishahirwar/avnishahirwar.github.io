Tax-year assumptions

The on-page estimate is configured for Tax Year 2026–27. It asks for taxable income, regime and residency status; it does not calculate salary deductions or exemptions from gross pay. The old-regime option is a simplified slab estimate for an individual below 60. Surcharge and special-rate income are not modelled. The new-regime Section 87A rebate is applied only when the user selects resident-individual eligibility. Confirm your position using the Income Tax Department portal and current official rules before filing.

What is an income tax calculator?

An income tax calculator estimates tax from taxable income and the rules selected for a particular tax year. This page currently provides a simplified estimate for ordinary slab-rate income; it does not prepare a tax return or determine final liability.

How to use this income tax calculator

  1. Enter your annual taxable income, not gross salary before deductions.
  2. Select the new regime or the simplified old regime for an individual below 60.
  3. Select whether you are a resident individual eligible for the Section 87A rebate.
  4. Calculate and review the tax, rebate and cess breakdown.

Formula and calculation method

The tool applies the selected slab rates to taxable income, applies the simplified Section 87A rebate only when resident-individual eligibility is selected, then adds 4% health and education cess. Surcharge, special-rate income and deductions from gross salary are outside this simplified model.

Verified worked example — new regime

For an eligible resident individual with taxable income of ₹12,00,000 in Tax Year 2026–27, the slab calculation gives ₹60,000 before rebate. The Section 87A rebate reduces this by ₹60,000; 4% cess on the remaining tax is ₹0. Estimated tax in this simplified example: ₹0. Eligibility and income composition matter; special-rate income may be treated differently.

Income-tax calculation questions

Should I enter gross salary or taxable income?

Enter the annual taxable-income figure after the adjustments that apply to your situation. This simplified page does not calculate deductions or exemptions from gross salary for you.

Who should select resident-individual eligibility for Section 87A?

Select that option only if you meet the applicable resident-individual conditions under the relevant tax-year rules. Residency and rebate eligibility are legal tax questions; verify them against official guidance before relying on the estimate.

Does this calculator include surcharge and special-rate income?

No. The simplified model does not calculate surcharge or separately tax special-rate income such as certain capital gains. It should not be treated as a complete tax computation or return-filing result.

Does the old-regime estimate include deductions and exemptions?

No. The old-regime option applies the simplified slab estimate described on this page. It does not collect separate deduction, exemption, age, income-head or TDS details.

Before relying on an income-tax estimate

Indian income-tax results depend on the financial year and assessment year, tax regime, type of income, eligible deductions, exemptions, rebate rules, surcharge and cess. Two people with the same gross salary can have different tax outcomes because their income components and eligibility differ.

Verify the applicable year and rules

Confirm the selected tax year and current provisions using the Income Tax Department's official guidance and calculator. This tool provides an indicative estimate from the inputs and configured assumptions; it does not prepare or file a return, determine final liability or replace advice from a qualified tax professional.

Income tax calculator guide for India

This page provides a simplified estimate using only three inputs: annual taxable income, selected tax regime and resident-individual eligibility for the Section 87A rebate. It does not have separate fields for gross salary, deductions, exemptions, income heads, TDS, age or other personal-profile details. Indian tax outcomes can depend on those additional facts, so this page cannot calculate a complete personal tax liability or prepare a return.

Understand the calculation sequence

A full tax computation may identify income under applicable heads, consider eligible exemptions and deductions, apply relevant slab or special rates, and then consider surcharge, cess, rebates or relief. This calculator does not collect all those details: it applies its configured simplified slab schedule to the taxable-income figure you enter, checks the selected rebate-eligibility option and adds 4% cess. Eligibility and sequence depend on current law for the relevant year.

Compare tax regimes carefully

India's tax regimes can differ in slab structure and in how deductions and exemptions are treated. This page does not ask you to enter deductions or exemptions and does not calculate them. It is therefore not a complete old-versus-new regime comparison. Do not assume a deduction available under one regime is also available under another; verify your full situation using official tools.

Information needed for a full tax calculation

The following information may be needed for a complete tax assessment, but these are not input fields in this simplified calculator: salary, pension, business or professional income; interest, dividends, capital gains and special-rate income; eligible deductions or exemptions; TDS and advance tax; and detailed residential or personal status. The calculator only accepts taxable income, tax regime and a resident-individual rebate-eligibility selection.

Estimate versus final liability

The calculator may not include every allowance, perquisite, loss set-off, capital-gain category or special provision. Reconcile Form 16, AIS, TIS, Form 26AS, bank certificates and other records before filing. Confirm current provisions on the official Income Tax Department portal or with a qualified tax professional.

Limitations

This is an educational estimate, not tax advice or a return-filing service. It does not access government records, validate documents or submit a return. Never enter passwords, OTPs or sensitive identity details into a public calculator.

Results are estimates based on entered values and assumptions. They are not professional advice. Verify important decisions with the relevant provider or a qualified professional.
