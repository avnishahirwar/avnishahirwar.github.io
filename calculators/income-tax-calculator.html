What this calculator does

This calculator provides an indicative estimate based on inputs and configured rules. Slabs, rebates, surcharge, cess, deductions and special-income rules can change by assessment year. Verify that the calculator assumptions match the relevant year.

Formula and calculation method

The tool applies its configured regime and slab assumptions to entered taxable income, then adds modelled surcharge and cess. It is not an official tax computation.
