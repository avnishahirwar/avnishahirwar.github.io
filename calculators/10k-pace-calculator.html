Frequently asked questions

Include taper fitness?

Goal times assume current race fitness, not an aspirational PB.

Pacing strategy?

Even or slightly negative splits produce the best results at most levels.

Course profile?

Hills and heat slow pace; adjust the goal, not the effort.
