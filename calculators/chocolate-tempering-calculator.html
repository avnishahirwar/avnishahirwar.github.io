Formula and method

Melt, cool to the work temperature, then seed with tempered chocolate; work temperatures vary slightly by type.

Worked example

Dark chocolate melts around 50 °C and is worked near 31 °C; reserve a quarter for seeding.

Frequently asked questions

What is tempering?

Realigning cocoa butter crystals so the set chocolate snaps and shines.

No thermometer?

Tempering without one is unreliable; a probe helps.

Re-temper?

If chocolate blooms grey, remelt and restart the cycle.
