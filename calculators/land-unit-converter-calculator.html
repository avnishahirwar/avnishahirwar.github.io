Land Area Converter (Bigha, Acre, Hectare, Sq Ft & Guntha)

Convert an area in square feet into Bigha, Guntha and Cent using local conversion factors you can edit.

Local measurement warning

Bigha is not a single uniform national unit. The sample square-feet-per-Bigha value is editable and is not an official Madhya Pradesh land-record conversion. Madhya Pradesh land records are maintained through the MP Bhulekh portal; use the hectare/official recorded area and confirm any local Bigha definition with the relevant revenue office before a land transaction.

Enter area and unit

Conversion results

The input area is converted into all listed units. Change the local Bigha factor to match the definition used by your district.

Formula and step-by-step conversion

  1. Convert the entered area to square feet using the selected input unit.
  2. Convert square feet to acres by dividing by 43,560.
  3. Convert square feet to hectares by dividing by 107,639.104167.
  4. Convert square feet to local Bigha, Guntha and Cent using the displayed factors.

This tool uses 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft, 1 hectare ≈ 107,639.104167 sq ft, 1 Guntha = 1,089 sq ft and 1 Cent = 435.6 sq ft. The Bigha factor is editable because it varies by locality.
