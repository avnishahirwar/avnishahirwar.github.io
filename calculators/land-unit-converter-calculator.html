Convert an area in square feet into Bigha, Guntha and Cent using local conversion factors you can edit.

Local measurement warning

Bigha is not a single uniform national unit. The sample square-feet-per-Bigha value is editable and is not an official Madhya Pradesh land-record conversion. Madhya Pradesh land records are maintained through the MP Bhulekh portal; use the hectare/official recorded area and confirm any local Bigha definition with the relevant revenue office before a land transaction.
