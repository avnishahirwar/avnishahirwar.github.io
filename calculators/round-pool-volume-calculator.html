Round Pool Volume Calculator

The round pool volume calculator finds water volume and filling cost for round and oval pools with flat or sloped bottoms.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Round Pool Volume Calculator works with 16 quantities and 28 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Oval volume above ground (oval_volume_above_ground) = (1/4) × PI × depth × length × width
  • Oval pool water cost (oval_pool_water_cost) = 1000 × oval_volume × water_price
  • Circle volume above ground (circle_volume_above_ground) = (1/4) × PI × depth × pow(length, 2)
  • Circle pool water cost (circle_pool_water_cost) = 1000 × circle_volume × water_price
  • Oval volume (oval_volume) = (1/8) × PI × length × width × (depth1 + depth2)
  • Circle volume (circle_volume) = (1/8) × PI × pow(length, 2) × (depth1 + depth2)
  • Depth2 (depth2) = -depth1 + 8 × oval_volume/(PI × length × width)
  • Depth1 (depth1) = -depth2 + 8 × oval_volume/(PI × length × width)

20 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
