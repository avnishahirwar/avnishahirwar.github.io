Frequently asked questions

What is an arithmetic progression?

It is a sequence in which the difference between consecutive terms is constant.

Can the common difference be negative?

Yes. A negative common difference makes the sequence decrease.

What does the sum represent?

It is the total of the first n terms of the sequence.
