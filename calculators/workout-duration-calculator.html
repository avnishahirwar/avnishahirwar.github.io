Workout Duration Calculator

Estimate total session time from exercises, sets, repetitions, work duration and rest.

Enter your session structure.

Formula

Estimated time is exercises × sets × repetitions × seconds per repetition, plus exercises × (sets − 1) × rest seconds. This excludes transitions, warm-up, cool-down, equipment setup and unexpected pauses, so real sessions can take longer.

Training note: The result is a planning estimate, not a required workout duration. Use rest that supports safe technique and your individual programme.
