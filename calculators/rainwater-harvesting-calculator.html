Formula and method

Litres = roof area (sq m) × rainfall (mm) × efficiency. 1 mm of rain on 1 sq m = 1 litre.

Worked example

A 100 sq m roof with 800 mm annual rain at 85% efficiency yields about 68,000 litres a year.

Frequently asked questions

Which efficiency is right?

0.8-0.9 covers most pitched tile roofs; flat roofs lose more to evaporation.

Storage sizing?

Size tanks to your dry-season demand, not the yearly total.

First-flush?

Divert the first 1-2 mm of a storm to keep debris out.
