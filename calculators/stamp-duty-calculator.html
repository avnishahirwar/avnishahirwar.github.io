How the estimate is calculated

Stamp duty = valuation base × stamp duty rate ÷ 100. Registration fee = valuation base × registration fee rate ÷ 100. Other entered percentage charges are calculated on the same base and added to the total.

Before paying or signing, confirm the exact deed classification, valuation method, current rate, registration fee cap or minimum, applicable concession and payment process with the official state registration authority or a qualified local professional.

Frequently asked questions

Does India have one stamp duty rate for every state?

No. Rates and calculation rules are state-specific and can also vary by deed type, property category and buyer or transaction circumstances.

Should I use the agreement value or circle rate?

Use the valuation base required by the state authority. Some jurisdictions compare the stated consideration with a government valuation benchmark; check the current local rule.

Are concessions and fee caps included?

No. This calculator is percentage-only. Add or adjust the rate only after confirming the actual treatment and any cap, minimum, surcharge or concession that applies.

Is this an official quotation?

No. It is an arithmetic estimate from the values you enter, not a government assessment, legal opinion or payment demand.
