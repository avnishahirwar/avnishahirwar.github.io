Formula and method

Equal rooms split evenly; with one larger room, a common approach gives it 10% extra of the total.

Worked example

A 45,000-rupee rent across three roommates with one big room: about 16,500 for the big room and 14,250 each for the others.

Frequently asked questions

Weighting 5-15% extra for the larger or en-suite room is common.

Sudden vacancy?

Decide in advance whether remaining tenants absorb the share or re-sell the room.
