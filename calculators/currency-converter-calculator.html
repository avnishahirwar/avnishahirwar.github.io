How to use this calculator

Enter an amount and the exchange rate in target units per one source unit.

Formula and method

Converted amount = source amount × the retrieved reference exchange rate. You can enter a rate manually if the rate service is temporarily unavailable.

Understanding the result

Enter the amount and a current rate from a source you trust. The result uses the rate exactly as entered.

Important assumptions and limitations

Rates are reference rates, not live trading quotes or guaranteed transaction rates. The rate date is displayed below the inputs. Bank spreads, transfer fees, card charges and taxes are not included. Verify the final rate and fees with your provider before a transaction. Data source: Frankfurter exchange-rate API.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use live exchange rates?

The calculator retrieves the latest available reference rate automatically. Rates are not live trading quotes and may be delayed.

Where can I get an exchange rate?

The calculator displays the reference-rate date and links to the Frankfurter rate API. Check your bank or payment provider for the final transaction rate and fees.

Will I receive exactly this amount?

Not necessarily. Provider spreads, transfer charges and taxes can change the amount received.
