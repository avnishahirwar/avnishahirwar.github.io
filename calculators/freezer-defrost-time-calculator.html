Formula and method

Time scales with weight and inversely with ambient temperature; dense blocks take longer per kg than flat cuts.

Worked example

A 2 kg flat item at 20 degrees takes roughly 3 hours; a dense block closer to 6 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Is counter thawing safe?

Only for short periods; fridge or cold-water thawing is safer.

Blur high-heat?

A microwave defrosts but partially cooks surfaces; cook straight after.

Thaw bagged?

Seal items to keep the surface out of the melt water.
