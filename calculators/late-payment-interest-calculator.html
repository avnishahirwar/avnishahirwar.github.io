Late Payment Interest Calculator

Estimate simple interest charged on an overdue invoice or bill from the amount, annual rate and days late.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Late interest = overdue amount × annual rate ÷ 100 × days overdue ÷ 365.

Worked example

With Overdue amount 1,00,000, Annual late-payment rate 18, Days overdue 45, the calculator gives: Late-payment interest = 2,219.18; Total now due = 1,02,219.18; Interest per day = 49.32.

How to read the result

Use this to compare the cost of paying late against the cost of borrowing elsewhere. Contracts and local law may set different day-count rules, grace periods or compounding.

Frequently asked questions

Is the interest simple or compound?

This calculator uses simple interest on the overdue amount. Some contracts compound monthly, which gives a slightly higher figure.

Which day-count basis is used?

A 365-day year. Some agreements use 360 days, which increases the interest a little.
