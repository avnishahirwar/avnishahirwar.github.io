Formula and method

Pace per 100 m = total seconds ÷ (distance ÷ 100).

Worked example

Swimming 1,000 m in 20:30 gives 1,230 seconds ÷ 10 = 123 seconds, or 2 minutes 3 seconds per 100 m.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter yards?

This page uses metres. For a 25-yard pool, convert the distance to metres first (1 yard = 0.9144 m).

What is a good pace?

Recreational swimmers often hold 2:00-2:30 per 100 m; competitive swimmers are faster. Goals are individual.

Does rest count?

Enter your moving time including wall rest exactly as you want it analysed.
