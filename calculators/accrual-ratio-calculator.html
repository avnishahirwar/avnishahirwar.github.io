Our accrual ratio calculator helps you to calculate two types of accrual ratios to evaluate the quality of earnings of companies.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Accrual Ratio Calculator works with 12 quantities and 15 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Beg net operating assets (beg_net_operating_assets) = beg_op_asset - beg_op_liabilities
  • End net operating assets (end_net_operating_assets) = end_op_asset - end_op_liabilities
  • Beg op liabilities (beg_op_liabilities) = -beg_net_operating_assets + beg_op_asset
  • End op liabilities (end_op_liabilities) = -end_net_operating_assets + end_op_asset
  • Beg op asset (beg_op_asset) = beg_net_operating_assets + beg_op_liabilities
  • End op asset (end_op_asset) = end_net_operating_assets + end_op_liabilities
  • Bs accrual ratio (bs_accrual_ratio) = 2 × (-beg_net_operating_assets + end_net_operating_assets)/(beg_net_operating_assets + end_net_operating_assets)
  • Cash flow accrual (cash_flow_accrual) = 2 × (-cash_flow_investing - cash_flow_operating + net_income)/(beg_net_operating_assets + end_net_operating_assets)

7 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
