Katch-McArdle BMR Calculator

Estimate basal metabolic rate and daily calorie needs from lean body mass using the Katch-McArdle formula.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Lean body mass = weight × (1 − body fat ÷ 100). BMR = 370 + 21.6 × lean body mass (kg). Daily needs = BMR × activity multiplier.

Worked example

With Body weight 70, Body fat 20, Activity multiplier 1.55, the calculator gives: Lean body mass (kg) = 56; Basal metabolic rate (kcal/day) = 1,579.6; Estimated daily energy need (kcal) = 2,448.38.

How to read the result

This formula uses lean mass, so it can suit muscular people better than formulas based only on weight. All BMR formulas are estimates with real-world error.

Frequently asked questions

What activity multiplier should I use?

About 1.2 for little exercise, 1.55 for moderate exercise and 1.9 for very hard training or physical work.

Is this a diet plan?

No. It is an estimate of energy use, not advice to eat a specific amount.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives general informational estimates only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Individual needs vary with age, health conditions, medicines and activity, so speak with a qualified health professional before changing diet, training or medication.
