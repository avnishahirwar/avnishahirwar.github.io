How it works

Formulas used by this calculator

The Fractional Odds Calculator works with 21 quantities and 19 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Probability of winning (probability_of_winning) = x/(x + y)
  • Probability of losing (probability_of_losing) = y/(x + y)
  • Decimal odds (decimal_odds) = ((x + y)/x)
  • Total return (total_return) = (stake × ((x + y)/x))
  • American odds pos (american_odds_pos) = (100 × ((x + y)/x) - 100)
  • American odds neg (american_odds_neg) = (-100/(((x + y)/x) - 1))
  • Potential net profit (potential_net_profit) = (stake × (((x + y)/x) - 1))
  • x (x) = -y + y/probability_of_losing

11 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
