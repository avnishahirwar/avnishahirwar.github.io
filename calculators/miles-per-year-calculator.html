How it works

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Miles per Year Calculator works with 48 quantities and 84 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Per Day M (perDayM) = perDay
  • Per Day Km (perDayKm) = perDay
  • Per Day Yd (perDayYd) = perDay
  • Per Day Mi (perDayMi) = perDay
  • Per Day (perDay) = perDayM
  • Per Week M (perWeekM) = perWeek
  • Per Week Km (perWeekKm) = perWeek
  • Per Week Yd (perWeekYd) = perWeek
