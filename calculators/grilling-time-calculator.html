Frequently asked questions

Use a thermometer

Thickness, starting temperature and grill vary; doneness temperature beats every time estimate.

Resting matters

Resting redistributes juices and firms the texture.

How about chicken?

Poultry must reach a safe internal temperature regardless of visual cues.
