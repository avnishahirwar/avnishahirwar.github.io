Formula and method

Frequently asked questions

What is the Riegel formula?

An endurance-exponent model from Peter Riegel (1977) used to predict race times across distances.

Does it assume training?

It assumes equally prepared events. A marathon prediction needs the corresponding endurance training.

Recent result?

Use a result within about six weeks on similar terrain for best accuracy.
