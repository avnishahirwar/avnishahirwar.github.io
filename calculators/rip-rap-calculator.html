Formulas used by this calculator

The Rip Rap Calculator works with 15 quantities and 17 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Volume (volume) = area × depth
  • Depth (depth) = volume/area
  • Price Per Weight (pricePerWeight) = cost/weight
  • Area (area) = volume/depth
  • Total Volume (totalVolume) = weight/density
  • Density (density) = weight/totalVolume
  • Weight (weight) = density × totalVolume
  • Cost (cost) = pricePerWeight × weight

9 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
