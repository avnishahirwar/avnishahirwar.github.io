Pipe Weight Calculator

Enter pipe dimensions and material density to estimate the weight of one pipe and a group of identical pipes.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter dimensions in millimetres and metres. Use the material's specified density and confirm nominal versus actual dimensions with the supplier.

Formula and methodology

Inner diameter = outside diameter − 2 × wall thickness. Pipe volume = π ÷ 4 × (outside diameter² − inner diameter²) × length. Weight = volume × material density.

Worked example

For a 60 mm outside diameter, 3 mm wall and 6 m length, the inner diameter is 54 mm. The mass estimate depends on the entered material density.

Important limitations

These are planning estimates, not construction drawings or engineering specifications. Confirm actual dimensions, product specifications, local building requirements and supplier quantities before purchase or installation.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for every pipe material?

Yes, as a geometric estimate when the material density is known. Use the correct density for the specific alloy, plastic or other material.

Does this include fittings or fluid inside the pipe?

No. It estimates the pipe wall only, excluding fittings, coatings and any contents.
