Steel Weight Calculator

Estimate the mass of rectangular steel plates from dimensions, quantity and material density.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the measurements in the units shown beside each field. Review the assumptions and use the results as an initial material-planning estimate.

Formula and methodology

Plate volume (m³) = length (mm) × width (mm) × thickness (mm) × quantity ÷ 1,000,000,000. Mass (kg) = volume × density.

Worked example

A 2,000 × 1,000 × 6 mm steel plate at 7,850 kg/m³ weighs approximately 94.2 kg.

Important limitations

The calculation models rectangular solid plates only. Actual alloy density, tolerances, coatings, holes and cut-outs change the measured mass.

Frequently asked questions

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are estimates. Confirm product specifications, site measurements, local requirements and supplier packaging before ordering.

Can I use this for structural approval?

No. This calculator does not replace engineering design, construction drawings, building-code checks or advice from a qualified professional.
