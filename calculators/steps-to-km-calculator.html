Formula and method

Distance (km) = steps × average step length (cm) ÷ 100,000.

For example, 5,000 × 75 ÷ 100,000 = 3.75 km. This is a unit conversion, not a measurement of actual distance travelled.

Frequently asked questions

Is the distance exact?

No. It is an estimate based on the step length entered. Stride length can vary with pace, terrain and individual movement.

Can I change the step length?

Yes. Enter a measured average step length or a device estimate you trust.

Does this access my phone's step counter?

No. It only uses the values typed into the form.
