The estimate excludes any cost or benefit you have not entered, including interest, land opportunity cost, quality deductions, commission, spoilage, insurance, subsidies and taxes. Add these to “Other costs” or adjust the selling price where appropriate.

How the calculation works

Total crop quantity = cultivated acres × yield per acre

Gross revenue = total crop quantity × selling price per quintal

Total cultivation cost = sum of all cost inputs

Net return = gross revenue − total cultivation cost

Break-even price = total cultivation cost ÷ total crop quantity

Worked example

For 2 acres, yield of 18 quintals per acre and a selling price of ₹2,400 per quintal, expected output is 36 quintals and gross revenue is ₹86,400. If total entered cultivation cost is ₹31,300, estimated net return is ₹55,100 before any omitted costs.

Important assumptions

  • Use the actual crop, season, plot and local cost figures for your farm.
  • Yield and selling price are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.
  • Market prices, quality, procurement rules and transport costs can change by location and date.
  • This is a planning calculator, not an agronomy recommendation, loan approval or government benefit determination.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for different crops?

Yes. Enter the yield, selling price and cost categories that apply to the crop and season you are estimating. Save a separate result for each crop or plot.

Is net return the same as farm income?

Not necessarily. This estimate subtracts only the costs entered. Household labour, land opportunity cost, loan interest, depreciation, post-harvest losses and other expenses may need to be added.

Does the calculator use a fixed MSP?

No. Enter the price you reasonably expect to receive. A notified MSP does not mean every crop or farmer will automatically receive that price in every market.
