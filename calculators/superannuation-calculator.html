2026–27 Australian reference settings

For 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027, the general SG rate is 12%. The ATO lists the 2026–27 maximum contribution base as A$270,830. From 1 July 2026, SG is calculated on qualifying earnings and Payday Super applies. Eligibility and qualifying-earnings rules matter; this estimate assumes the entered annual earnings are eligible.

Worked example

For A$85,000 in qualifying earnings at 12%, the estimated annual employer SG contribution is A$10,200 before fund fees, taxes or other adjustments.

Projection method

Annual employer contribution = minimum of qualifying earnings and the annual maximum contribution base × SG rate. The projection adds that contribution at each year-end, applies the assumed annual return to the opening balance, and subtracts the annual fee input. Returns are not guaranteed; tax, contribution tax, preservation rules, withdrawals and personal contributions are not modelled.

FAQs

Is the SG rate 12%?

The general rate is 12% for 2026–27. Special cases and eligibility rules may apply.

Does this calculate my actual fund balance?

No. It is a simplified scenario estimate and does not connect to your super fund or include all fund-specific costs and taxes.

Where can I verify the rules?

See the Australian Taxation Office super guarantee rates and ATO Payday Super guidance.
