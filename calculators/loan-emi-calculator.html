What this calculator does

Estimate a fixed monthly instalment for a reducing-balance loan. Actual lender schedules may differ because of fees, rate resets, insurance, payment dates and rounding.

How to use it

  1. Enter principal
  2. annual interest rate
  3. tenure in years
  4. review EMI, total repayment and estimated interest.
Calculate now

Formula and calculation method

Monthly rate r = annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100; n = years × 12. EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1). At 0% interest, EMI = P ÷ n.

Worked example

For a ₹5,00,000 loan at 10% annually over 5 years, the calculator applies a monthly rate of 10% ÷ 12 and 60 instalments.

Important information

The result is an estimate, not a loan offer or approval. Lenders may apply processing fees, insurance, variable rates and different day-count conventions.
