How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formula and calculation method

Accelerated biweekly payments are modelled as one-half of the monthly payment every two weeks. Regular biweekly payments are calculated as 12 monthly payments divided across 26 periods.

Assumptions and limitations

Actual lender payment dates, compounding conventions and rounding can change the result. Confirm the schedule with your lender.

How to use this calculator

Compare the regular and accelerated payment amounts, total interest and estimated payoff periods.
