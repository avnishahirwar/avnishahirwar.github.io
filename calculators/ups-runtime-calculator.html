Formula and method

Runtime = battery Wh × efficiency ÷ load watts.

Worked example

A 600 Wh battery at 85% efficiency powering a 150 W load runs for about 3 hours 24 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

VA vs watts?

VA is apparent power; multiply VA by power factor (often 0.6-0.9) to get watts.

Battery ageing?

Older lead-acid batteries hold perhaps 60-80% of rated capacity.

Critical vs comfort loads?

Keep only essential devices on UPS-supplied circuits to extend runtime.
