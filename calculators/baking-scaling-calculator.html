Frequently asked questions

Cook time changes?

Yes; larger batches bake slower and deeper pans change browning.

Rounding?

Round to measurable amounts; keep small ingredients to 0.1 g precision.

Leaveners?

Scale with the flour, but check yeast hydration on very large batches.
