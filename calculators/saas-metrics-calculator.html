SaaS Metrics Calculator

Estimate annual recurring revenue, average revenue per customer, customer churn and net customer change from monthly SaaS inputs.

Use the SaaS Metrics Calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter current monthly recurring revenue, starting active paying customers, customers lost during the month and new customers acquired during the month. The model assumes these customer counts refer to the same reporting period.

Formula

ARR = MRR × 12. ARPU = MRR ÷ active paying customers. Monthly customer churn = customers lost ÷ starting active customers × 100.

Worked example

With MRR of 100,000 and 200 active customers, ARPU is 500 and ARR is 1,200,000. If 5 customers churn and 20 are added, net customer change is +15.

Important limitations

This calculator provides a general mathematical estimate based on user-entered values. It does not connect to financial, advertising, accounting or payment accounts, verify source data, determine tax treatment or replace professional advice. Check the applicable contracts, accounting policy and official guidance before relying on results.

Frequently asked questions

Is ARR a revenue forecast?

No. ARR annualises the current MRR by multiplying it by 12; it assumes the monthly run rate stays constant.

Does churn include revenue churn?

No. This page calculates customer (logo) churn only, not revenue churn or net revenue retention.
