How to use this converter

Enter a non-negative length in feet. The converter returns equivalent customary and metric units.

Conversion formula

1 foot = 12 inches = 30.48 centimetres = 0.3048 metres.

Worked example

5 feet equals 60 inches, 152.4 centimetres and 1.524 metres.

Important limitations

Conversions use standard unit definitions and the assumptions shown. Check local conventions and measurement tolerances where a value is used for surveying, construction or procurement.

Frequently asked questions

How many inches are in one foot?

One international foot equals exactly 12 inches.

Is the foot-to-metre conversion exact?

Yes. One international foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 metres.
