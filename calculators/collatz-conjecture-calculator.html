Collatz Conjecture Calculator

Formula and methodology

Starting from a positive integer n, divide by 2 when n is even; otherwise calculate 3n + 1. Repeat the rule and record the number of steps to reach 1.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values in the fields shown above.
  2. Check the order, sign and number of terms before calculating.
  3. Select Calculate to see the results and intermediate values.

Worked example

Starting at 6 gives 6 → 3 → 10 → 5 → 16 → 8 → 4 → 2 → 1, which takes 8 steps.
