Formulas used by this calculator

The Hexagonal Pyramid Surface Area Calculator works with 8 quantities and 15 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Pyramid area lat (pyramid_area_lat) = 3 × side × slant
  • Pyramid perimeter (pyramid_perimeter) = pyramid_base × side
  • Pyramid base (pyramid_base) = pyramid_perimeter/side
  • Pyramid area base (pyramid_area_base) = (3/2) × sqrt(3) × pow(side, 2)
  • Slant (slant) = (1/3) × pyramid_area_lat/side
  • Side (side) = (1/3) × pyramid_area_lat/slant
  • Pyramid area regular (pyramid_area_regular) = (3/2) × side × (sqrt(3) × side + 2 × slant)
  • Height (height) = (1/2) × sqrt(-pow(side, 2) + 4 × pow(slant, 2) × pow(tan(PI/pyramid_base), 2))/tan(PI/pyramid_base)

7 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
