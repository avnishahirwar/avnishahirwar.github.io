How the estimate works

Net pay = gross pay − income tax withheld − payroll/social insurance contributions − pre-tax deductions − post-tax deductions. Annualized amounts multiply the paycheck result by the selected number of pay periods.

This is a calculator for entered amounts, not a tax engine. It does not determine federal, state, provincial or local tax liability, tax credits, benefit eligibility, or employer-specific payroll treatment. Some pre-tax deductions may change taxable wages; this simple summary does not recalculate withholding.

Worked example

If gross biweekly pay is 2,500 and all entered deductions total 625, estimated net pay is 1,875 per paycheck. Annualized net pay is 48,750 over 26 pay periods.

FAQs

Does this calculate income tax automatically?

No. Enter your expected withholding amounts. Tax rules vary by jurisdiction, tax year, filing status and personal circumstances.

What is semimonthly pay?

Semimonthly payroll generally means 24 pay periods per year, often twice each month. Biweekly payroll normally has 26 pay periods.

Can I use another currency?

Yes. Enter a three-letter currency code. The calculator formats the result with that code but does not convert currencies.
