What this calculator does

Estimate fuel required and approximate journey cost. Actual consumption changes with traffic, driving style, road conditions, vehicle load, tyre pressure and fuel price.

How to use it

  1. Enter distance in kilometres
  2. mileage in km/litre
  3. fuel price
  4. review litres and cost.
Calculate now

Formula and calculation method

Fuel required = distance ÷ mileage. Estimated cost = fuel required × price per litre.

Worked example

A 300 km journey at 15 km/litre uses an estimated 20 litres. At ₹100 per litre, estimated fuel cost is ₹2,000.

Important information

The result excludes tolls, parking and maintenance. Use a realistic mileage figure based on actual driving conditions.
