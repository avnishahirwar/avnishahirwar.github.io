Formula and method

Devine formula: 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet (men), 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 feet (women).

Worked example

For a man of 175 cm (68.9 inches): 50 + 2.3 × 8.9 = 70.4 kg.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is ideal weight exact?

No. It is a population reference. Body composition, frame size and health status matter more than a single number.

Why two rows of results?

The BMI-based range shows the weight span clinicians often use for your height.

Who created the Devine formula?

Dr B.J. Devine described it in 1974 for medication dosing; it became a common weight reference.
