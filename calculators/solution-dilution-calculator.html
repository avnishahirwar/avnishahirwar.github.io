Solution Dilution Calculator

Calculate the stock-solution volume and diluent volume required to reach a target concentration and final volume.

Enter your values

Formula

C₁V₁ = C₂V₂; stock volume V₁ = C₂V₂ ÷ C₁; diluent volume = V₂ − V₁.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change the inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Adjust one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

Stock and target concentrations must use the same units. Target concentration must not exceed stock concentration. The equation assumes additive volumes and does not give chemical preparation or handling advice.
