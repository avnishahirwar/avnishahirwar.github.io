Metacam Dosage Calculator for Cats

Arithmetic aid only: enter the amount per kilogram already prescribed by the veterinarian. Volume conversion applies only to the U.S. 5 mg/mL injectable product; it is not authorization to administer medication.

Enter values

How the calculation works

Prescribed amount (mg) = cat weight (kg) × veterinarian-prescribed dose (mg/kg). Volume (mL) = amount (mg) ÷ 5 mg/mL. No default dose is supplied.

The U.S. FDA label is for a single pre-surgical injectable feline dose. FDA warns that repeat use in cats has been linked to acute renal failure and death; this calculator does not supply the labeled dose.

Worked example

Symbolic arithmetic only: for weight W kg and a veterinarian-prescribed dose D mg/kg, amount = W × D mg; with the scoped 5 mg/mL product, volume = amount ÷ 5. No dose value is recommended or prefilled.

Important limits

Prescription-drug arithmetic only. U.S. feline oral use and repeat injectable doses are not approved. Verify the exact product, concentration, route, jurisdiction and prescription. Do not use this calculator to start or change treatment; contact a veterinarian for urgent concerns.
