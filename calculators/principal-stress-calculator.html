Principal Stress Calculator

This principal stress calculator helps you calculate the normal stress acting on a single major plane. Find the maximum, minimum, and angle of principal stress using this calculator.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Principal Stress Calculator works with 9 quantities and 18 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Shear Stress yx (Shear_Stress_yx) = -Shear_Stress_xy
  • Shear Stress xy (Shear_Stress_xy) = -Shear_Stress_yx
  • Angle of Minimum Shear Stress (Angle_of_Minimum_Shear_Stress) = Angle_of_Principal_Stress - 1/4 × PI
  • Angle of Maximum Shear Stress (Angle_of_Maximum_Shear_Stress) = Angle_of_Principal_Stress + (1/4) × PI
  • Angle of Principal Stress (Angle_of_Principal_Stress) = Angle_of_Maximum_Shear_Stress - 1/4 × PI
  • Stress x (Stress_x) = 2 × Shear_Stress_xy/tan(2 × Angle_of_Principal_Stress) + Stress_y
  • Stress y (Stress_y) = -2 × Shear_Stress_xy/tan(2 × Angle_of_Principal_Stress) + Stress_x
  • Minimum Principal Stress (Minimum_Principal_Stress) = (1/2) × Stress_x + (1/2) × Stress_y - 1/2 × sqrt(4 × pow(Shear_Stress_xy, 2) + pow(Stress_x - Stress_y, 2))

10 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
