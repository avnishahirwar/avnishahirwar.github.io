Formulas used by this calculator

The Ending Inventory Calculator works with 6 quantities and 8 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Beginning Inventory (beginning_Inventory) = cost_of_goods_sold + ending_Inventory - net_Purchases
  • Cost of goods sold (cost_of_goods_sold) = beginning_Inventory - ending_Inventory + net_Purchases
  • Ending Inventory (ending_Inventory) = beginning_Inventory - cost_of_goods_sold + net_Purchases
  • Net Purchases (net_Purchases) = -beginning_Inventory + cost_of_goods_sold + ending_Inventory
  • Inventory Turnover (inventory_Turnover) = 2 × cost_of_goods_sold/(beginning_Inventory + ending_Inventory)

3 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
