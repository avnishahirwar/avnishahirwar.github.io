What this calculator does

Estimate interest on an unchanged savings balance. Banks may calculate interest on daily closing balances and credit it periodically; actual results depend on balance movements and bank rules.

How to use it

  1. Enter balance
  2. annual rate
  3. number of days
  4. review estimated interest.
Calculate now

Formula and calculation method

Simple estimate = balance × annual rate ÷ 100 × days ÷ 365. Leap-year and bank-specific rules may vary.

Worked example

For ₹50,000 at 3% annually over 90 days, estimate = ₹50,000 × 0.03 × 90 ÷ 365.

Important information

Banks may credit interest quarterly or at another interval. Tax treatment is not included; check account terms and current tax rules.
