Formula and method

Hydration percent = water ÷ flour × 100 (baker's percentage).

Worked example

500 g of flour with 350 g of water is 70% hydration, a typical sandwich-loaf dough.

Frequently asked questions

Include starter water?

Yes; total water ÷ total flour, including the starter's own flour and water.

Higher hydration benefits?

More open crumb and better oven spring, but the dough is harder to handle.

Whole grains?

They absorb more water; many bakers add about 5% more hydration.
