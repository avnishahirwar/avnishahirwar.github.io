Formula and calculation method

Basic estimate = units × entered per-unit rate + fixed charges + other charges. This is a flat-rate estimate unless tariff slabs are separately represented.

Worked example

200 units at ₹6 per unit plus ₹100 fixed and ₹20 other charges gives a basic estimate of ₹1,320.

Guide: understanding your Electricity Bill Calculator results

How the estimate is built

An electricity bill is energy charge plus fixed charge plus other charges. Energy charge is units consumed multiplied by the rate per unit. For 350 units at ₹7, that is ₹2,450. Adding a fixed charge of ₹120 and ₹40 of other charges gives a bill of ₹2,610, or an average of ₹7.46 per unit.

Slab tariffs and extra items

Most DISCOMs charge a higher rate as consumption rises, so the first units cost less than the last. If your bill has slabs, enter an average rate or run the calculator for each slab and add the results. Fuel and power purchase adjustment, electricity duty and meter rent appear as separate lines; put them under other charges.

Finding what drives your bill

Units are kilowatt-hours: watts × hours ÷ 1,000. A 1,500 W air conditioner used for 8 hours a day for 30 days uses 1.5 × 8 × 30 = 360 units, which is more than the entire 350-unit example above. Heavy appliances such as air conditioners, geysers and water heaters dominate bills, so a 5-star rating and sensible thermostat settings pay back quickly.

Worked example with real numbers

350 units at ₹7 per unit, fixed charge ₹120, other charges ₹40: energy charge ₹2,450, estimated bill ₹2,610, average ₹7.46 per unit. If a new 1,500 W AC runs 8 hours a day for 30 days, it adds 360 units, which at the same rate is about ₹2,520 a month.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using one rate when the bill has several slabs.
  • Forgetting fixed charges and taxes.
  • Comparing bills of different lengths without converting to units per day.
  • Not checking the meter reading dates on the bill.

Important information

This is not an official DISCOM bill. Check your electricity provider's current tariff; the basic calculation does not automatically apply slab-wise rates.

Frequently asked questions about the Electricity Bill Calculator

How do I calculate units from meter readings?

Subtract the previous reading from the current reading. If the meter has a multiplying factor, multiply by it.

What is the unit of electricity?

One unit is one kilowatt-hour: a 1,000 W appliance running for one hour.

Why is the actual bill higher?

Slab rates, fuel adjustment charges, duty, taxes and late payment charges can add up. Enter them under other charges.

Can I use it for commercial connections?

Yes, if you enter the commercial tariff rate and fixed charge from your bill.

How can I lower the bill?

Check high-wattage appliances, switch to efficient ones, use timers and avoid standby consumption.
