Battery Life Calculator

Estimate how long a battery will run a load from its capacity, the load current and an efficiency factor.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Runtime (hours) = battery capacity × usable efficiency ÷ 100 ÷ load current.

Worked example

With Battery capacity 5,000, Average load current 250, Usable capacity and efficiency 85, the calculator gives: Estimated runtime (hours) = 17; Estimated runtime (days) = 0.71; Usable capacity (mAh) = 4,250.

How to read the result

Temperature, discharge rate and battery age all reduce real capacity. Use the average load rather than the peak load for a realistic result.

Frequently asked questions

Why is usable capacity lower than rated capacity?

Conversion losses, cut-off voltage and ageing mean you cannot draw the full rated capacity.

Can I use this for a car battery?

It works for any battery if you enter capacity and load in the same units, but starter batteries are not designed for deep discharge.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
