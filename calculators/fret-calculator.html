Fret Calculator

Planning to build your very own DIY guitar or ukulele? This fret calculator will help you determine the precise positions where you should place the frets on your instrument's fingerboard. Hop in to learn more about it.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Fret Calculator works with 28 quantities and 54 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Scale Length (scaleLength) = 2 × distFret12
  • Dist Fret12 (distFret12) = (1/2) × scaleLength
  • Dist Fret24 (distFret24) = (3/4) × scaleLength
  • Dist Fret6 (distFret6) = (1/2) × scaleLength × (2 - sqrt(2))
  • Dist Fret8 (distFret8) = (1/2) × scaleLength × (2 - cbrt(2))
  • Dist Fret18 (distFret18) = (1/4) × scaleLength × (4 - sqrt(2))
  • Dist Fret20 (distFret20) = (1/4) × scaleLength × (4 - cbrt(2))
  • Dist Fret2 (distFret2) = (1/2) × scaleLength × (2 - pow(2, 5/6))

46 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
