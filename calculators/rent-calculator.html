Rent Calculator

Rent calculator can help you determine how much of your income should go to rent, if a rent is affordable or if you meet a landlord's rent-to-income ratio requirement.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Rent Calculator works with 9 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Monthly income (monthly_income) = income
  • Required annual gross income (required_annual_gross_income) = 40 × mothly_rent
  • Income (income) = monthly_income
  • Rent to income ratio (rent_to_income_ratio) = mothly_rent/income
  • Mothly rent (mothly_rent) = income × rent_to_income_ratio
  • Monthly expenses (monthly_expenses) = -affordable_rent - monthly_debt + monthly_income - monthly_savings
  • Affordable rent (affordable_rent) = -monthly_debt - monthly_expenses + monthly_income - monthly_savings
  • Monthly savings (monthly_savings) = -affordable_rent - monthly_debt - monthly_expenses + monthly_income

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
