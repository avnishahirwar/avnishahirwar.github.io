Workout Volume Calculator

Calculate total lifted volume from the number of sets, repetitions and load.

Enter sets, repetitions and load.

Formula

Total volume load is calculated as sets × repetitions per set × load per repetition. For example, 3 sets of 10 repetitions at 50 kg equals 1,500 kg of calculated volume. This simple total does not measure effort, range of motion, tempo, technique or how challenging a set felt.

Training note: A higher number is not automatically better. Increase training gradually and use recovery, technique and individual guidance to make decisions.
