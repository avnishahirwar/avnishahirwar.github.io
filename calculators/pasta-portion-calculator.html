Formula and method

90-100 g per person for a main, about 60 g for a side; filled pasta around 125 g.

Worked example

A four-person main of spaghetti needs about 400 g dry, yielding roughly 880 g cooked.

Frequently asked questions

Cooked weight?

Pasta roughly doubles in weight after boiling as it absorbs water.

Salt the water?

About 10 g per litre seasons the pasta as it cooks.

Filled pasta?

It is denser, so portions per plate run smaller but heavier.
