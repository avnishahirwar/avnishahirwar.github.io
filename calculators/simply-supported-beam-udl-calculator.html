Simply Supported Beam with UDL Calculator

Calculate maximum bending moment, support reactions and midspan deflection for a simply supported beam under a uniform load.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Maximum moment M = wL² ÷ 8. Each reaction R = wL ÷ 2. Midspan deflection δ = 5wL⁴ ÷ (384 E I).

Worked example

With Uniform load 12, Span 6, Modulus of elasticity 200, Second moment of area 8,000, the calculator gives: Maximum bending moment (kN·m) = 54; Support reaction at each end (kN) = 36; Total load (kN) = 72; Midspan deflection (mm) = 12.66; Span-to-deflection ratio = 474.07.

How to read the result

Codes commonly limit deflection to a fraction of the span, such as span ÷ 250 or span ÷ 360 depending on use. Include self-weight and load factors as your design code requires.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a structural design tool?

No. It uses idealised formulas for learning and quick checks. A licensed engineer must verify real designs.

What units must I use?

Load in kN/m, span in m, E in GPa and I in cm⁴. The calculator converts internally.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
