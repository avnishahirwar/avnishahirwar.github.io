Formulas used by this calculator

The Rectangle Scale Factor Calculator works with 8 quantities and 17 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Area Scale Factor (areaScaleFactor) = newArea/oldArea
  • Old Length (oldLength) = oldArea/oldWidth
  • New Length (newLength) = newArea/newWidth
  • Scale Factor (scaleFactor) = newWidth/oldWidth
  • Old Width (oldWidth) = oldArea/oldLength
  • New Width (newWidth) = newArea/newLength
  • Old Area (oldArea) = oldLength × oldWidth
  • New Area (newArea) = newLength × newWidth
