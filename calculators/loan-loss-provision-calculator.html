Loan Loss Provision Calculator

Estimate a planning provision from exposure, expected loss rate and recoveries.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Gross expected loss = exposure × expected loss rate ÷ 100; planning provision = gross expected loss − recoveries, floored at zero.

Use consistent reporting periods, currency units and accounting definitions. This is a planning estimate and does not determine regulatory compliance or a customer’s approved product terms.

Worked example

A 5 million exposure at 4% expected loss less 30,000 recoveries gives a 170,000 planning provision.

Assumptions and limitations
