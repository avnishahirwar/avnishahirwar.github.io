How this calculator works

This tool applies a transparent mathematical estimate to the values entered above. Review the labels, units and result before relying on it. Where an interest rate, fee, tax rate, eligibility limit or scheme term is requested, enter the value that applies to your own situation and confirm it with the relevant official provider.

The calculator does not connect to banks, brokers, government portals or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, tax classification, exemptions or compliance obligations. It excludes charges and adjustments unless a field explicitly asks for them.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • All figures are based on user-entered assumptions and may differ from actual provider calculations.
  • Rounding, payment dates, compounding, fees and applicable law can affect real outcomes.
  • For tax and government-scheme matters, verify current rules and official notifications before acting.
  • This tool is educational and is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Worked example

For ₹50,000 converted to EMI at an assumed annual rate of 18% over 12 months, the monthly reducing-balance rate is 1.5%. The estimated EMI is ₹4,584.00, total of 12 instalments about ₹55,008.00, and interest about ₹5,008.00. Actual card issuers may add processing fees, taxes or different terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is the annual rate converted to a monthly rate?

Yes. This estimate divides the entered annual nominal rate by 12 and applies a reducing-balance EMI formula.

Are processing fees and GST included?

No. The example excludes processing fees, GST and other issuer charges unless separately included in the amount you enter.

Will my card issuer offer the same EMI?

Not necessarily. Issuer rates, eligible transactions, tenure choices, fees and rounding rules vary. Confirm the final schedule with your card issuer.

Does converting a balance cancel the original card bill?

Do not assume that it does. Check the issuer's conversion confirmation and payment instructions so you understand which amount remains due and when.
