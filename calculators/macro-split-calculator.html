Formula and method

Grams: protein and carbohydrate = calories × percent ÷ 100 ÷ 4; fat = calories × percent ÷ 100 ÷ 9.

Worked example

At 2,400 kcal with a 30/40/30 split: 180 g protein, 240 g carbohydrate and 80 g fat.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Why 4 and 9 kcal per gram?

Protein and carbohydrate supply about 4 kcal per gram and fat about 9 kcal per gram.

Can percentages be zero?

Enter a minimum of 5% per macro so the plan stays nutritionally balanced.

Is this medical nutrition advice?

No. It is arithmetic for a chosen split. Dietitians personalise medical nutrition therapy.
