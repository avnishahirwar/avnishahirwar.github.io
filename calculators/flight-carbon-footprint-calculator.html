Formula and method

Estimated emissions (kg CO₂e) = passenger distance (km) × user-entered emission factor (kg CO₂e per passenger-km). For multiple legs, add each leg’s distance × its matching factor. The factor’s source, year, route/haul scope, cabin class and treatment of non-CO₂ effects matter.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

Illustrative arithmetic only: 1,200 passenger-km × 0.150 kg CO₂e/passenger-km = 180 kg CO₂e = 0.18 t CO₂e. The factor is hypothetical and must not be treated as an official or universal flight factor.

Scope and limitations

  • Emission factors vary by aircraft, route, distance band, cabin class, occupancy, cargo allocation, year and accounting boundary.
  • Do not compare outputs as equivalent when factors use different scopes. CO₂-only and CO₂e factors are not interchangeable.
  • This distance-based estimate does not reproduce ICAO’s route- and aircraft-specific model and may not include aviation non-CO₂ effects.
  • No default factor is endorsed by this page. Record the factor source and scope; use ICAO’s tool when detailed route-specific modeling is required.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the calculator ask me for the emissions factor?

There is no single universal factor for every flight. A factor must match the calculation’s unit and scope, including route, year, cabin class and gases included.

Should I enter distance for a round trip?

Enter the passenger-kilometres for all legs being estimated. Add outbound and return legs separately when their distances or factors differ.
