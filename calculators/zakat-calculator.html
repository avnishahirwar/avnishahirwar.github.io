How this estimate works

Net zakatable wealth = entered eligible assets − entered deductible liabilities. If net zakatable wealth meets or exceeds the Nisab threshold, the calculator applies the selected rate (commonly entered as 2.5% for a full lunar-year calculation). Otherwise, the estimate is zero.

Asset eligibility, debts that may be deducted, the treatment of investments, gold and silver Nisab, the lunar-year requirement, agricultural or livestock Zakat, and payment timing can differ by scholarly interpretation and circumstance. Enter only amounts that a trusted scholar or recognised Zakat organisation confirms are applicable to you.

FAQs

Does the calculator set the Nisab price automatically?

No. Gold and silver prices and the Nisab method can change. Enter a current threshold in the currency you use.

Is 2.5% always the correct rate?

2.5% is a common rate for qualifying monetary wealth held for a lunar year, but not every Zakat category uses the same method. Confirm your circumstances with a qualified scholar.

Does it calculate business or agricultural Zakat rules in full?

No. It provides a simple entered-assets estimate and does not replace a category-specific assessment.
