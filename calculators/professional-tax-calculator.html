Madhya Pradesh professional tax slabs — 2026–27

For employees, the published Madhya Pradesh Vritti Kar schedule uses annual salary or wages: up to ₹2,25,000 is nil; above ₹2,25,000 up to ₹3,00,000 is ₹1,500 per year; above ₹3,00,000 up to ₹4,00,000 is ₹2,000 per year; above ₹4,00,000 is ₹2,500 per year. The Act contains other classes of taxpayers and separate provisions, so this employee calculator must not be used to determine a trader's, professional's or employer's full liability.

At the ₹2,000 annual band, the published monthly withholding illustration is ₹166 for eleven months and ₹174 in the twelfth; at ₹2,500, it is ₹208 for eleven months and ₹212 in the twelfth. Actual deductions and liability should be checked against the current law, employment period and employer's payroll treatment.

Professional tax by state: how to verify your rate

Professional tax is not a single national payroll rate. States and Union Territories differ: some levy it, some do not, and certain local schedules depend on occupation, salary, municipality or employer category. Check the latest state law, notification and payroll guidance before entering a rate. Do not assume that a neighbouring state's slab applies to your workplace.

Frequently asked questions

Does this determine my state professional tax liability?

No. State-specific slabs, exemptions and rules are not built in. Enter an amount confirmed for your circumstances.

Can I calculate less than a year?

Yes. Enter the number of months in the period, from 1 to 12.

Are penalties or interest included?

No. The result is only the monthly amount multiplied by the number of months.
