SCSS formula and current rate

Updated: 13 November 2026. The SCSS rate is 8.2% per annum for Q3 FY 2026–27 (1 October–31 December 2026), with quarterly interest payouts. Verify the Department of Economic Affairs notification.

Formula: Quarterly interest = Principal × annual rate ÷ 4. Total interest over the full tenure = quarterly interest × 4 × years. The principal is returned separately at maturity, subject to scheme rules.

Worked example: A ₹10,00,000 deposit at 8.2% generates an illustrative quarterly interest of ₹20,500 and annual interest of ₹82,000. Over five years, total interest is ₹4,10,000, with the ₹10,00,000 principal returned separately at maturity.

Frequently asked questions

What is the SCSS interest rate for October–December 2026?

The rate is 8.2% per annum for Q3 FY 2026–27. Confirm the latest quarterly notification and eligibility rules.

How often is SCSS interest paid?

Interest is paid quarterly under the scheme's applicable rules. This calculator estimates the quarterly amount from the entered principal and annual rate.

Is principal included in the quarterly payout?

No. The quarterly payout is interest. The principal is returned separately at maturity, subject to applicable scheme terms.
