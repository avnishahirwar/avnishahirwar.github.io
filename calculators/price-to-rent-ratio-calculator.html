Price-to-Rent Ratio Calculator

Compare the price of a home with a year of comparable rent to judge whether buying or renting looks more expensive.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Price-to-rent ratio = property price ÷ (monthly rent × 12). Gross rental yield = annual rent ÷ price × 100.

Worked example

With Property price 75,00,000, Monthly rent for a similar home 25,000, the calculator gives: Price-to-rent ratio = 25; Gross rental yield (%) = 4; Years of rent equal to price = 25.

How to read the result

A high ratio suggests renting is cheaper relative to buying, a low ratio suggests the opposite. It ignores maintenance, taxes, loan interest, appreciation and your time horizon.

Frequently asked questions

What ratio counts as high?

There is no universal line. Compare the ratio with other neighbourhoods and with your own city history.

Is this a buy-or-rent decision tool?

It is a quick screen only. A full comparison needs financing costs, taxes and expected stay length.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool is educational and informational. It is not tax, investment, lending, accounting, legal or professional advice. Rates, fees, taxes and product terms differ by provider and country, so confirm the figures with the relevant institution before acting.
