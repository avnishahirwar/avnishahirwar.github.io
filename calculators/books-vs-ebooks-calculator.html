Books vs e-Books Calculator

Compare user-specified lifecycle impact per printed book and e-book reading.

Enter values

Formula and method

Printed impacts = books × impact/book; digital impacts = device impact amortized per reading

Worked sample

5 printed books × 1 kg vs 100 device kg/500 reads = difference 3 kg

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Use comparable lifecycle impact factors. Digital reading electricity and device lifetime assumptions are user inputs; output is not universal.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
