Formula and method

Base 20 BTU per sq ft, adjusted ±10% for sun exposure, plus 600 BTU per extra person and 4,000 BTU for a kitchen.

Worked example

A 150 sq ft average-light room for two people and not a kitchen: about 3,000 BTU/h; a sunny kitchen adds 400 and 4,000 BTU.

Frequently asked questions

What is a BTU?

A British thermal unit is the energy to warm one pound of water by 1°F; air conditioners are rated in BTU per hour.

Bigger is not better

Oversized units cool fast but dehumidify poorly and short-cycle.

Tonnage?

12,000 BTU/h equals one ton of refrigeration capacity.
