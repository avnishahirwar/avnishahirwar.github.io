Accumulated Depreciation Calculator

Estimate straight-line annual and accumulated depreciation, remaining book value and depreciated percentage from asset details.

Use the Accumulated Depreciation Calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the asset's purchase cost, estimated salvage value, useful life and elapsed years. This simple model assumes straight-line depreciation starts at the beginning of the useful life and does not model partial-year conventions or impairment.

Formula

Annual straight-line depreciation = (Asset cost − Salvage value) ÷ Useful life. Accumulated depreciation = Annual depreciation × Years elapsed, capped at useful life.

Worked example

An asset costing 500,000 with salvage value 50,000 and a five-year useful life has annual straight-line depreciation of 90,000. After two years, accumulated depreciation is 180,000 and estimated book value is 320,000.

Important limitations

This calculator provides a general mathematical estimate based on user-entered values. It does not connect to financial, advertising, accounting or payment accounts, verify source data, determine tax treatment or replace professional advice. Check the applicable contracts, accounting policy and official guidance before relying on results.

Frequently asked questions

Can accumulated depreciation exceed asset cost?

No. The calculator caps depreciation at the depreciable amount over the stated useful life.

Is this tax depreciation?

No. Tax depreciation rules and accounting policies may use different methods, rates and conventions. Verify the treatment that applies.
