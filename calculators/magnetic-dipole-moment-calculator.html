Formulas used by this calculator

The Magnetic Dipole Moment Calculator works with 11 quantities and 13 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Loop Length (loopLength) = 2 × PI × loopRadius
  • Loop Radius (loopRadius) = (1/2) × loopLength/PI
  • Loop Current (loopCurrent) = loopMoment/loopArea
  • Loop Moment (loopMoment) = loopArea × loopCurrent
  • Sol Area (solArea) = PI × pow(solRadius, 2)
  • Loop Area (loopArea) = loopMoment/loopCurrent
  • Sol Radius (solRadius) = sqrt(solArea)/sqrt(PI)
  • Sol Moment (solMoment) = solArea × solCurrent × solTurns

5 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
