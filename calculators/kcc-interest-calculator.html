KCC Interest Calculator

Estimate simple interest on a Kisan Credit Card balance using the interest rate and any effective concession rate confirmed by your bank.

Enter your figures and select Calculate.

This tool is an estimate, not a loan sanction, subsidy approval, market-price forecast or professional advice. Use current local figures and confirm important details with your bank, agriculture department or qualified adviser.

Frequently asked questions

Estimated simple interest = average outstanding balance × (annual rate − effective concession rate) × months ÷ 12 ÷ 100. Enter only a concession that your bank confirms applies to your account and period.
