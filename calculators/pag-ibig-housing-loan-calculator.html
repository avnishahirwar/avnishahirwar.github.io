How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Sources, assumptions and limitations

Content review date: 21 November 2026. This page performs a mathematical estimate from the values entered by the visitor. It does not fetch live rates, confirm eligibility, determine legal classification or replace a bank statement, tax return, lender schedule or professional advice.

Before relying on the result, verify the current rule, rate, notification and product terms with the relevant authority. Useful starting points are the Income Tax Department, GST portal, Reserve Bank of India, EPFO, PFRDA and SEBI. The applicable source depends on the calculator topic and jurisdiction.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Pag-IBIG Housing Loan Calculator works with 18 quantities and 25 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Gross Annual Income (grossAnnualIncome) = 12 × grossMonthlyIncome
  • Gross Monthly Income (grossMonthlyIncome) = (1/12) × grossAnnualIncome
  • Principal And Interest (principalAndInterest) = (7/20) × grossMonthlyIncome
  • Fixed Pricing Period (fixedPricingPeriod) = 100 × annualInterest/loanAmount
  • Monthly Insurance Premium (monthlyInsurancePremium) = loanAmount × multiplierForInsurance
  • Multiplier For Insurance (multiplierForInsurance) = monthlyInsurancePremium/loanAmount
  • Annual Interest (annualInterest) = (1/100) × fixedPricingPeriod × loanAmount
  • Loan Amount (loanAmount) = 100 × annualInterest/fixedPricingPeriod

17 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
