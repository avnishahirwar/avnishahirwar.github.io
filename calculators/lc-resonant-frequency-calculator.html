LC Resonant Frequency Calculator

Calculate the resonant frequency of an inductor-capacitor circuit from inductance and capacitance.

Formula and methodology

Resonant frequency f = 1 ÷ (2π × √(L × C)), with L in henries and C in farads.

Worked example

With Inductance 10, Capacitance 100, the calculator gives: Resonant frequency (Hz) = 159.155; Resonant frequency (kHz) = 0.159; Period (milliseconds) = 6.283.

How to read the result

Real components have resistance and tolerance, so measured resonance and damping will differ from the ideal value.

Frequently asked questions

What happens at resonance?

Inductive and capacitive reactance cancel, so a series circuit has minimum impedance and a parallel circuit has maximum impedance.

How do I tune the frequency?

Increase either L or C to lower the frequency, or decrease them to raise it.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
