Paint Calculator

Estimate paint volume for a set of walls using your paint label's stated coverage and number of coats.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the dimensions and product information that match your project, then select Calculate. Confirm measurements and product coverage before purchasing materials.

Formula and methodology

Net paintable area = combined wall length × wall height × number of sets − opening area. Litres = net area × coats ÷ coverage per litre.

Worked example

A net paintable area of 300 sq ft, two coats and coverage of 100 sq ft per litre requires about 6 litres before an allowance.

Important limitations

These are planning estimates, not construction drawings, structural calculations, building-code determinations or installation instructions. Actual quantities vary with site conditions, product dimensions, cuts, patterns and workmanship. Have a qualified installer confirm measurements and safety-critical design.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find coverage per litre?

Check the paint manufacturer's product label or technical data sheet.

Does this account for primer or textured walls?

No. Primer and unusual surfaces may require separate estimates.
