Formulas used by this calculator

The Helium Balloons Calculator works with 11 quantities and 8 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Number of balloons basic (number_of_balloons_basic) = (3/500) × volume_of_helium_basic/(PI × pow(balloon_diameter, 3))
  • Volume of helium basic (volume_of_helium_basic) = (500/3) × PI × pow(balloon_diameter, 3) × number_of_balloons_basic
  • Balloon diameter (balloon_diameter) = (1/10) × cbrt(6) × cbrt(volume_of_helium_basic/number_of_balloons_basic)/cbrt(PI)
  • Gas type VS (gas_type_VS) = -object_weight/volume_of_helium_basic + 5/4 - 3/500 × balloon_weight/(PI × pow(balloon_diameter, 3))
  • Balloon weight (balloon_weight) = (125/3) × PI × pow(balloon_diameter, 3) × (-4 × object_weight + volume_of_helium_basic × (5 - 4 × gas_type_VS))/volume_of_helium_basic
  • Object weight (object_weight) = -gas_type_VS × volume_of_helium_basic + (5/4) × volume_of_helium_basic - 3/500 × balloon_weight × volume_of_helium_basic/(PI × pow(balloon_diameter, 3))
