What does the mortgage calculator estimate?

A mortgage is a loan secured against property. A typical home-loan instalment pays down part of the borrowed principal and covers interest charged on the outstanding balance. This calculator estimates that principal-and-interest payment for a fixed annual rate and equal monthly instalments.

It can also include annual property tax, annual home insurance and a monthly maintenance or housing-society charge. Those costs are converted into monthly amounts and added to the loan instalment. This gives a broader planning estimate instead of showing the loan EMI alone.

For an India-focused estimate, enter the property price and down payment in Indian rupees. The calculator does not assume a lender's eligibility rules, stamp duty, registration costs or a specific bank's interest rate. Use the rate and term from the offer you are evaluating.

Mortgage payment formula

The principal-and-interest component uses the standard fixed-rate amortising loan formula:

Monthly payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)
  • P is the home price minus the down payment.
  • r is the monthly interest rate, calculated as the annual rate divided by 1,200.
  • n is the total number of monthly payments, calculated as loan years multiplied by 12.

When the interest rate is 0%, the principal is divided evenly across the monthly payments. The monthly housing-cost estimate then adds annual property tax divided by 12, annual home insurance divided by 12 and the monthly maintenance amount entered.

Worked example

Suppose a property costs ₹80,00,000 and the down payment is ₹16,00,000. The loan principal is ₹64,00,000. At an illustrative fixed annual rate of 8.5% over 20 years, the estimated principal-and-interest instalment is about ₹55,541 per month.

If annual property tax is ₹20,000, annual home insurance is ₹12,000 and monthly society maintenance is ₹2,500, the estimated total monthly housing cost is about ₹60,707. Actual amounts depend on the lender's schedule, property charges and insurance terms.

How to use the mortgage calculator

  1. Enter the property's purchase price and the down payment you plan to make.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and loan term. Use the rate and tenure from the lender's current offer where available.
  3. Add annual property tax and home insurance if you want them included in the monthly estimate.
  4. Add monthly maintenance or society charges, or leave the field at zero.
  5. Select Calculate mortgage payment and review the loan amount, monthly instalment, monthly cost components and estimated total interest.

Try changing the down payment, rate or term to compare scenarios. A longer term may reduce the monthly instalment while increasing total interest. A larger down payment reduces the amount borrowed, but keep enough funds for other purchase costs and a suitable emergency reserve.

What is not included?

This estimate does not include lender processing fees, stamp duty, registration, legal or valuation charges, repairs, utilities, furnishings, mortgage protection, private mortgage insurance or variable-rate changes. Some lenders collect taxes and insurance separately; others may include them in an escrow-style monthly payment. Enter only costs that apply to your situation and avoid counting the same charge twice.

The result is an educational estimate, not a loan approval, rate offer, affordability decision or financial recommendation. Confirm the repayment schedule and all charges directly with the lender and relevant property professionals before making a commitment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for an Indian home loan?

Yes. Enter the property price, down payment and loan costs in rupees. The calculation uses a fixed annual rate and equal monthly instalments, so compare it with the lender's repayment schedule.

Does the monthly total include property tax and insurance?

Yes, if you enter those amounts. The calculator divides each annual amount by 12 and adds it to the principal-and-interest instalment and monthly maintenance.

Is this the same as a loan EMI calculator?

The principal-and-interest formula is similar. This mortgage calculator also starts from a property price and down payment and can include property tax, home insurance and monthly society charges.

Will my actual EMI stay the same?

Only if the loan's rate, repayment structure and payment schedule stay the same. Floating-rate changes, fees, prepayments and lender-specific rounding can change the actual schedule.
