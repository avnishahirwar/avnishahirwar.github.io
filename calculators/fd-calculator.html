Formula and calculation method

A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t), where P is principal, r annual rate as decimal, n compounding periods yearly and t years. Interest = A − P.

Worked example

A ₹1,00,000 deposit at 7% compounded quarterly for one year is estimated using n = 4 and t = 1. Bank terms can change the actual amount.

Guide: understanding your Fixed Deposit Calculator results

How FD interest compounds

A fixed deposit earns interest on the principal and on interest already added. With the formula A = P × (1 + r ÷ n)^(n × t), the maturity amount grows faster when interest is added more often. At 7% for five years on ₹2,00,000, quarterly compounding gives ₹2,82,955.64 while yearly compounding gives ₹2,80,510.35, a difference of about ₹2,445.

Cumulative and payout FDs

This calculator models a cumulative deposit, where interest is reinvested and paid at maturity. In a payout FD, interest is paid out monthly, quarterly or yearly and the principal stays the same, so the total return is lower but you get regular income. Choose the type that fits whether you need income now or growth later.

Tax and real returns

FD interest is taxable at your slab rate in the year it accrues, even for a cumulative FD that pays at maturity. Banks deduct TDS if interest crosses the annual threshold set by the rules; submit Form 15G or 15H if your income is below the taxable limit and you qualify. Compare the after-tax return with inflation: a 7% FD in the 30% bracket earns about 4.9% after tax before the effect of cess.

Worked example with real numbers

Deposit ₹2,00,000 at 7% for 5 years, compounded quarterly. Maturity value is ₹2,82,955.64 and interest is ₹82,955.64. With yearly compounding it is ₹2,80,510.35 and interest is ₹80,510.35.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing rates quoted with different compounding frequencies.
  • Forgetting that premature withdrawal usually reduces the interest rate by a penalty.
  • Ignoring tax on the interest when comparing with tax-saving options.
  • Assuming the deposit is covered above the insurance limit; deposits in a bank are insured up to a fixed amount per depositor per bank.

Important information

This is not a bank quote or guaranteed return. Verify the bank's official deposit terms and applicable tax rules.

Frequently asked questions about the Fixed Deposit Calculator

Which compounding frequency do Indian banks use?

Most banks compound FD interest quarterly, though some use monthly or half-yearly options. Check the compounding in the FD certificate or the bank's rate card.

Is the interest on a 5-year tax-saver FD tax-free?

No. A tax-saver FD can give a deduction on the deposit under the old regime within the overall limit, but the interest is taxable.

How do I compare an FD with a debt mutual fund?

Compare after-tax returns over the same period and consider liquidity and risk. FD returns are fixed by the bank; debt fund returns vary.

What happens if I break the FD early?

Banks generally pay the rate applicable for the period actually run, minus a penalty of around 0.5–1%, and may not allow withdrawal within a lock-in. Check your bank's terms.

Does the calculator show the monthly payout?

No. It shows the cumulative maturity value. For a payout FD, interest per period is roughly principal × rate ÷ number of payouts per year.
