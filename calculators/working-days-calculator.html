Calculation methodology · Disclaimer

How working days are counted

The start date and end date are both included in the selected range. The calculator checks each calendar date and counts Monday through Friday as weekdays. Saturdays and Sundays are excluded. This is a standard Monday-to-Friday workweek, not a custom shift calendar.

For example, Monday 2 November through Friday 6 November 2026 contains five weekdays. If you enter 2026-11-04 as a holiday, the result becomes four working days. A holiday that falls on Saturday or Sunday does not reduce the weekday count because weekends are already excluded.

How to enter holiday dates

Enter only the dates that should be excluded for your organisation or location, using YYYY-MM-DD format. Separate dates with commas, spaces, semicolons or new lines. The calculator does not download national, state, bank or company holiday calendars. You are responsible for checking which holidays apply, including substitute days and local observances.

Practical uses

This estimate can help with a first-pass project schedule, an order-processing window, a service-level estimate or a simple staffing plan. For a deadline, confirm whether the organisation counts the start date, observes local holidays, closes on additional weekdays or uses a different weekend pattern.

Range and calculation limits

  • The start date and end date are inclusive.
  • The selected range must be no longer than ten years.
  • Only Saturday and Sunday are treated as weekends.
  • Only manually entered holidays that fall on weekdays are subtracted.
  • No live public-holiday, regional-calendar or employer data is fetched.

Frequently asked questions

Are the start and end dates included? Yes. Both dates are included when the calculator checks weekdays.

Does it automatically exclude Indian public holidays? No. Add the applicable holiday dates yourself. Holiday lists differ by state, employer, sector and year.

Can I change the weekend days? Not in this version. Saturday and Sunday are always excluded; alternative workweek patterns need a separate calculation.

What happens if a holiday is on a weekend? It does not reduce the count further because that date is already excluded as a weekend.
