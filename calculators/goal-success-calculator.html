Goal Success Calculator

Turn a goal into measurable milestones. Calculate completion percentage and compare your elapsed time with the planned duration. The result tracks progress; it does not predict success.

Milestones completed—Enter values to compare schedule pace.

Calculation method

Completion = completed milestones ÷ total milestones × 100. Time elapsed = days elapsed ÷ planned days × 100.

Milestones are treated as equally weighted. If tasks differ in effort or importance, use smaller milestones or track them separately. The pace comparison is descriptive and does not estimate a probability of success.

A plan can change. Use the result as a planning aid, allow reasonable breaks and revise the schedule when circumstances change.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate my chance of success?

No. It calculates milestone completion and elapsed schedule percentage only.

What if milestones have different sizes?

Break large tasks into smaller milestones or use a separate weighted project plan. This simple calculator gives each milestone equal weight.
