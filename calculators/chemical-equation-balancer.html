Chemical Equation Balancer

Balance a chemical equation by solving elemental conservation equations for the smallest whole-number coefficients.

Enter your values

Formula

Find positive integer coefficients so that the count of every element is equal on both sides of the reaction arrow.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter formulas separated by + signs.
  2. Use one arrow: →, ->, => or =.
  3. Use element symbols with correct capitalization and parentheses for grouped atoms.

Worked example

Try the sample iron oxide equation.

Assumptions and limitations

Supports standard element symbols and simple parenthesized groups. Hydrate dot notation, ionic charges, redox half-reactions and equations with multiple independent balancing solutions are not supported.
