Thin Lens Equation Calculator

Calculate the image distance and magnification of a converging lens from object distance and focal length.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ u + 1 ÷ v, so image distance v = f × u ÷ (u − f). Magnification m = −v ÷ u.

Worked example

With Object distance 30, Focal length 10, the calculator gives: Image distance (cm) = 15; Magnification = -0.5; Image height as a multiple of object height = 0.5.

How to read the result

A negative magnification means the image is inverted. For objects inside the focal length a converging lens makes a virtual, upright image, which this tool does not model.

Frequently asked questions

Why must the object be beyond the focal length?

Inside the focal length the image is virtual and the formula gives a negative image distance.

Does this work for thick lenses?

No. It assumes a thin lens with negligible thickness.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
