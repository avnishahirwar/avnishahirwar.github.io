Dollars in Words Converter

Convert a USD amount into US English words. The result includes a check-style cents fraction and a spoken-style alternative.

Calculator

Use plain digits, optionally followed by one or two decimal places (for example, 130.45).

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

Split the exact decimal amount into whole dollars and 0–99 cents. Spell each integer in US English; display check-style cents as two digits over 100. No binary floating-point rounding is used for the amount.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

$130.45 → “one hundred thirty dollars and 45/100” (spoken style: “one hundred thirty dollars and forty five cents”).

Assumptions and limitations

  • This page handles non-negative US dollars and US English wording only; it does not support other currencies or regional wording conventions.
  • Enter no commas and no more than two digits after the decimal point. Extra fractional digits are rejected rather than rounded.
  • Maximum supported amount is $999,999,999,999.99. Verify legal, banking and document wording requirements with the receiving institution.
