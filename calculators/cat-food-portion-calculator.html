Formula and method

RER = 70 × weight^0.75; daily kcal = RER × factor (neutered 1.2, intact 1.4, weight loss 0.8).

Worked example

A neutered 4 kg cat needs about 238 kcal, roughly 60 g of 4,000 kcal/kg food a day.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Indoor or outdoor?

Outdoor cats burn more; indoor neutered cats sit at the lower factor.

Wet and dry mix?

Count calories from both; grams differ by density.

Vet-guided weight loss?

Confirm any weight-loss plan with a vet before cutting portions sharply.
