Wire Gauge Calculator

This wire gauge calculator computes the diameter, cross-sectional area, and resistance per unit length of a wire once given a wire gauge number in either the American Wire Gauge (AMG) or Standard Wire Gauge (SWG) systems.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Wire Gauge Calculator works with 11 quantities and 8 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Cross section awg (cross_section_awg) = (1/4) × PI × pow(diameter_awg, 2)
  • Cross section swg (cross_section_swg) = (1/4) × PI × pow(diameter_swg, 2)
  • Resistance per unit length awg (resistance_per_unit_length_awg) = (resistivity/cross_section_awg)
  • Resistance per unit length swg (resistance_per_unit_length_swg) = (resistivity/cross_section_swg)
  • Diameter awg (diameter_awg) = 2 × sqrt(cross_section_awg)/sqrt(PI)
  • Diameter swg (diameter_swg) = 2 × sqrt(cross_section_swg)/sqrt(PI)
  • Awg (awg) = 39 × log((127/500000) × pow(44881150836777617408, 1/13)/diameter_awg)/log(92)
