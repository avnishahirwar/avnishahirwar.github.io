Formula and method

Baseline 35 ml per kg plus roughly 600 ml per hour of hard training.

Worked example

A 70 kg athlete training 1 hour needs about 2.5 + 0.6 = 3.1 litres.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Sweat rate test?

Compare body weight before and after a standard session to personalise the hour rate.

Electrolytes?

Long sessions over 90 minutes benefit from sodium replacement too.

Over-hydration risk?

Drinking far beyond thirst can dilute sodium; drink to plan plus thirst.
