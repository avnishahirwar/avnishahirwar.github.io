Centripetal Force Calculator

Calculate the force, acceleration and g-force needed to keep an object moving in a circle.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Centripetal acceleration a = v² ÷ r. Centripetal force F = m × v² ÷ r.

Worked example

With Mass 1,200, Speed 15, Radius of the circle 50, the calculator gives: Centripetal force (N) = 5,400; Centripetal acceleration (m/s²) = 4.5; Acceleration in g = 0.46.

How to read the result

Doubling speed quadruples the force needed, which is why sharp bends at high speed are demanding on tyres or structures.

Frequently asked questions

What provides the force on a car in a bend?

Friction between tyres and road, plus any banking of the road.

Is centripetal force a separate force?

No. It is the net inward force that comes from tension, friction, gravity or another real force.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
