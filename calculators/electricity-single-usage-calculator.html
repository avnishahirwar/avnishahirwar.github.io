Electricity Cost per Use Calculator

Calculate the approximate energy and cost for one appliance use, such as one wash cycle or one cooking session.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the values that match your situation, then select Calculate. Review the assumptions and units before relying on the estimate.

Formula and methodology

Energy (kWh) = watts × number of devices × minutes ÷ 60 ÷ 1,000. Cost = energy × tariff per kWh.

Worked example

A 1,500 W appliance running for 30 minutes uses 0.75 kWh. At ₹7 per kWh, the estimated cost is ₹5.25.

Important limitations

Results are estimates based only on the values and assumptions you enter. They do not replace an actual meter reading, supplier quote, employer policy or professional assessment where one is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate a full electricity bill?

No. It estimates the energy charge for the selected use. Fixed charges, taxes and slab adjustments are excluded.

Can I compare two appliances?

Run the calculator separately for each appliance using consistent usage time and tariff assumptions.
