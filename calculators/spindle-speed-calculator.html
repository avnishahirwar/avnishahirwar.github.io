Formulas used by this calculator

The Spindle Speed Calculator works with 6 quantities and 7 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Diameter of part (diameter_of_part) = 1000 × cutting_speed/(PI × spindle_speed)
  • Spindle speed (spindle_speed) = 1000 × cutting_speed/(PI × diameter_of_part)
  • Number of teeth (number_of_teeth) = feed_rate/(feed_per_tooth × spindle_speed)
  • Feed per tooth (feed_per_tooth) = feed_rate/(number_of_teeth × spindle_speed)
  • Cutting speed (cutting_speed) = (1/1000) × PI × diameter_of_part × spindle_speed
  • Feed rate (feed_rate) = feed_per_tooth × number_of_teeth × spindle_speed

1 further rearrangement of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
