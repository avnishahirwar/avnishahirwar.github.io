Window-to-Wall Ratio Calculator

Estimate window-to-wall ratio for a façade using opening area and gross wall area.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Window-to-wall ratio = total opening area ÷ gross wall area × 100.

Use consistent units and measured project data. The result is a planning estimate; applicable accessibility, planning, building, fire, energy and professional standards may require additional calculations.

Worked example

24 m² of openings in a 120 m² façade equals a 20% window-to-wall ratio.

Assumptions and limitations

This calculator is for preliminary design and architecture planning only. It is not a structural design, building-permit determination, accessibility certification, energy model, quantity survey, contract, safety advice or professional design service. Verify assumptions with the relevant local code, standard and qualified professional before construction or procurement.
