Frequently asked questions

What is included in net purchases?

Use purchases after purchase returns and discounts, measured consistently with your accounting policy. Do not subtract returns twice.

Should inward freight be included?

Freight and other directly attributable costs of bringing inventory to its present location and condition may be included under the applicable accounting framework.

Why is closing inventory subtracted?

Closing inventory represents goods not yet sold during the period, so its cost remains an asset rather than part of current-period COGS.

Is COGS the same as operating expenses?

No. COGS relates to inventory sold. Selling, administrative and many other overhead expenses are generally presented separately.
