Formula and method

Wood kg = demand × hours × days ÷ (4.0 kWh/kg useful output × efficiency). One cord ≈ 350 kg of seasoned hardwood.

Worked example

A 3 kW average demand over 120 days at 10 h/day burns about 4,320 kWh of useful heat — roughly 1.4 tonnes of seasoned hardwood at 75% efficiency.

Frequently asked questions

Which moisture content?

The energy content assumes about 20% moisture; greener wood burns much hotter and dirtier.

Stove efficiency

Old open stoves sit near 0.5; modern clean-burn units reach 0.75-0.9.

Can I mix species?

Yes; use weighted averages for the energy content.
