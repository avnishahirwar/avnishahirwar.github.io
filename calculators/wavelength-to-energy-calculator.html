Wavelength to Energy Calculator

Convert vacuum wavelength to photon energy in electronvolts.

Enter values

Formula and method

E(eV) = hc/(λe), using exact SI values for h, c and elementary charge e. Inputs must use the units stated above. This calculator performs only the displayed idealized calculation; it does not model unlisted effects.

Worked sample

A 500 nm photon has energy ≈ 2.480 eV.

Validation and limitations

Inputs are checked for finite values and physically required positive quantities. Results are browser-only and are educational estimates; verify assumptions and units before practical use. This is not an engineering or safety certification tool.
