Percentage Point Calculator

Enter an original rate and a new rate to distinguish percentage-point change from relative percentage change.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the original and new rates as percentages, such as 20 and 25 for 20% and 25%.

Formula and methodology

Percentage-point change = new rate − original rate. Relative change = (new rate − original rate) ÷ |original rate| × 100%, when the original rate is non-zero.

Worked example

A rate rising from 20% to 25% increases by 5 percentage points. Relative to the original rate, that is a 25% increase.

Important limitations

This tool performs a mathematical calculation on the values entered. It does not replace subject-specific judgement. Check the method and assumptions before using results in academic or professional work.

Frequently asked questions

Are percentage points the same as percent?

No. Percentage points measure the arithmetic difference between two percentages; relative percent change compares that difference with the original rate.

Can relative change be calculated from a zero original rate?

No. The relative change denominator would be zero, so the calculator reports it as undefined.
