Formula and method

TV size in cm × about 1.2 for 4K, or 1.8 for Full HD, as a mid-point viewing distance.

Worked example

A 55-inch 4K TV suits seating roughly 1.3-2.0 metres away.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sit closer with 4K?

Yes; higher resolution allows shorter distances without visible pixels.

Mixed content?

Sports and cinema favour slightly farther seating than gaming.

Eye strain?

Comfortable distances reduce strain; brightness and lighting also matter.
