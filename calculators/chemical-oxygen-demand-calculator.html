Chemical Oxygen Demand Calculator

Estimate chemical oxygen demand from blank and sample titration volumes, titrant normality and sample volume.

Enter your values

Formula

COD (mg/L as O₂) = (blank − sample) × normality × 8000 ÷ sample volume.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.

Assumptions and limitations

This is a calculation for the conventional titration expression and assumes the method's reagents, blank correction and normality are appropriate. It is not a substitute for an accredited water-quality test.
