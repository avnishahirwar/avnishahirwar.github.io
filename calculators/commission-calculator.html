Frequently asked questions

Is commission calculated on gross or net sales?

That depends on the written commission agreement. The calculator uses the eligible sales value you enter, so apply the contract's definition first.

Are refunds and cancelled orders deducted?

Only if your commission policy requires it. Adjust the eligible sales amount to reflect approved exclusions and clawbacks.

Does this include GST or income tax?

No. It calculates the percentage amount only. Tax treatment, invoicing and withholding depend on the transaction and applicable rules.

Can commission be tiered?

This version applies one flat rate to the entire eligible amount. For slabs or thresholds, calculate each tier separately and add the results.
