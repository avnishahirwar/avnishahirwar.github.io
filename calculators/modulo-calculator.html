Modulo Calculator

Find the remainder after dividing one number by a non-zero divisor.

How to use this calculator

Enter a dividend and a non-zero divisor. The result shows the remainder and quotient rounded down for positive inputs. Negative inputs follow the language's remainder convention.

Check that inputs match the stated domain and units. The result is generated from the values you enter; no external data or account information is required.

Formula and method

Remainder = dividend % divisor. The sign follows JavaScript remainder semantics and may differ from mathematical modulo for negative values.

Understanding the result

Use the result as a mathematical aid and review the method before applying it to coursework, measurement or other calculations. Some numerical displays are affected by finite-precision arithmetic.

Important limitations

This calculator is designed for general arithmetic and learning. It does not replace a teacher's required method, specialist software or domain-specific rounding rules. For high-precision scientific work, verify results with an appropriate precision-aware tool.
