CAGR Calculator

Estimate the compound annual growth rate between a beginning value and an ending value over a stated period. CAGR expresses a smoothed annual growth rate; it does not show the actual path taken between the two values.

Use the CAGR Calculator

Updated: 13 November 2026 — content and calculation guidance reviewed.

How to use this calculator

Enter the beginning value, ending value and period in years. Both values must be greater than zero. Fractional years are accepted.

Formula

CAGR = (Ending value ÷ Beginning value)^(1 ÷ Years) − 1. Multiply by 100 to express it as a percentage.

Worked example

If a value grows from ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 over 3 years, CAGR = (1,50,000 ÷ 1,00,000)^(1 ÷ 3) − 1 = approximately 14.47% per year. This is a mathematical illustration, not a forecast or guaranteed return.

Important limitations

This calculator provides a general mathematical estimate. It does not verify documents, contractual terms, tax treatment, fees or external market data. Results are not financial advice or a guaranteed outcome.

Frequently asked questions

What does CAGR mean?

CAGR is the constant annual growth rate that would connect the beginning and ending values over the selected period.

Does CAGR mean returns were steady every year?

No. It smooths the start and end values and does not represent year-by-year volatility.

Can I use CAGR for investments?

You can calculate a historical or hypothetical growth rate, but it is not a forecast or investment recommendation.
