Enter a load and repetition count.
Important: This estimate is not a target or safety clearance. Do not test a true maximum without suitable technique, supervision and equipment.

Epley formula

The estimate is weight × (1 + repetitions ÷ 30). Different formulas can produce different results, especially at higher repetition counts. Strength also varies with exercise, skill, fatigue, equipment and range of motion.

Training context

Warm up appropriately and use qualified guidance when learning a lift. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness or other concerning symptoms. For injury, illness or return-to-training questions, seek individual professional advice.
