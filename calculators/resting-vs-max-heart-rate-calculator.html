Formula and method

Maximum heart rate ≈ 220 − age; zones are percentages of that maximum.

Worked example

At age 30: max ≈ 190 bpm, so Zone 2 runs from about 114 to 133 bpm.

Frequently asked questions

Is 220 minus age accurate?

It is the classic estimate with a standard deviation near 10-12 bpm. Field tests give personal values.

What is Zone 2?

A comfortable conversational effort around 60-70% of maximum, used for aerobic base training.

Should medication change zones?

Beta-blockers and other drugs lower heart rate. Follow clinical advice for targets.
