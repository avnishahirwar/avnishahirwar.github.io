Personal loan EMI: formula, example and what to check

Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n is the number of monthly instalments. Total interest = EMI × n − P.

Worked example

A ₹5,00,000 personal loan at 12% a year for 36 months has a monthly rate of 1%. The reducing-balance formula gives an EMI of about ₹16,607.15, total repayment of about ₹5,97,858 and total interest of about ₹97,858. Enter values in the units shown on the form fields to reproduce a similar result.

Inputs explained

  • Loan amount: the principal you borrow, not including processing fee or insurance premiums that a lender may add.
  • Interest rate: use the rate in your sanction letter or key fact statement. Personal loans are unsecured, so rates are usually higher than home or car loans.
  • Tenure: a longer tenure lowers the EMI but raises total interest.

Common mistakes

  • Comparing offers by EMI alone instead of EMI plus total interest plus fees.
  • Ignoring processing fees and prepayment or foreclosure charges.
  • Taking a longer tenure only to reduce the EMI without checking the extra interest.
  • Assuming a flat-rate quote and a reducing-balance quote are the same rate.

Frequently asked questions

How is personal loan EMI calculated?

Using the reducing-balance formula above, where interest each month is charged on the outstanding principal and the instalment stays constant.

Does a shorter tenure save money?

Usually yes. The EMI is higher, but you pay interest for fewer months, so total interest falls.

Does this include processing fees?

No. Fees, GST on fees, insurance and other lender charges are excluded; add them separately when comparing offers.

Is this a loan offer?

No. It is a mathematical estimate. The lender decides eligibility, rate and final schedule.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Personal Loan Calculator works with 5 quantities and 6 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Total interest paid (total_interest_paid) = -loan_amount + loan_term × monthly_payment
  • Monthly payment (monthly_payment) = (loan_amount + total_interest_paid)/loan_term
  • Loan amount (loan_amount) = loan_term × monthly_payment - total_interest_paid
  • Loan term (loan_term) = (loan_amount + total_interest_paid)/monthly_payment

2 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
