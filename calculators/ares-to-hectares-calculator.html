How to use this converter

Enter a non-negative area in ares. The converter returns hectares, square metres and acres.

Conversion formula

1 are = 100 square metres. 100 ares = 1 hectare.

Worked example

250 ares equals 2.5 hectares and 25,000 square metres.

Important limitations

Conversions use standard unit definitions and the assumptions shown. Check local conventions and measurement tolerances where a value is used for surveying, construction or procurement.

Frequently asked questions

How many ares are in one hectare?

There are exactly 100 ares in one hectare.

What is one are?

One are is an area of 100 square metres.
