Sand Calculator

Enter area dimensions and depth to estimate sand volume, approximate weight and optional material cost.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Measure the area in feet and depth in inches. Use a density in kg/m³ supplied by your sand vendor because grading and moisture change bulk density.

Formula and methodology

Volume = length × width × depth. Convert depth from inches to feet; cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. Mass = volume × bulk density.

Worked example

A 20 ft × 10 ft area filled to 3 inches has 50 cubic feet, or about 1.85 cubic yards, before allowance.

Important limitations

These are planning estimates, not construction drawings or engineering specifications. Confirm actual dimensions, product specifications, local building requirements and supplier quantities before purchase or installation.

Frequently asked questions

Is sand density fixed?

No. Moisture content, grain size and compaction affect bulk density. Use a supplier specification for procurement.

Does this calculate a mortar mix?

No. It estimates loose sand volume and mass for a rectangular fill area.
