Restaurant Food Cost Calculator

Calculate restaurant food cost percentage and gross profit from ingredient cost and food sales revenue.

Enter your values

Formula and method

Food cost percentage = ingredient cost ÷ food sales revenue × 100. Gross profit before other expenses = food sales revenue − ingredient cost.

Worked example

Example: ingredient cost of ₹30,000 and food sales of ₹100,000 gives a food cost ratio of 30% and gross profit before other expenses of ₹70,000.

Assumptions and limitations

Enter values from the same reporting period and use consistent units. The result is an estimate, not a quote, audited KPI or engineering specification. Confirm contract terms, carrier tariffs, production standards, fabric marker plans and local requirements before operational decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator store my inputs?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and this page does not submit the entered values to a server.

Can I use the result for a final business quote?

Use it as a planning estimate only. Validate assumptions, exclusions, taxes, rounding and supplier or customer contract terms first.
