Portfolio Risk Contribution Calculator

Estimate how equity, bonds and cash contribute to a three-asset portfolio's volatility using weights, standard deviations and pairwise correlations.

Formula and calculation method

Portfolio variance = w′Σw. Portfolio volatility = √(w′Σw). Each asset's component contribution to volatility = wᵢ(Σw)ᵢ ÷ portfolio volatility; component contributions sum to total portfolio volatility. Correlations and standard deviations must refer to the same period.

Worked example

If an asset has a 60% portfolio weight, 18% annual volatility and positive correlations with the other holdings, its risk contribution depends on both its own volatility and its covariance with the rest of the portfolio.

How to use

  1. Enter weights that total 100%.
  2. Enter annual volatility and pairwise correlation assumptions for the same period.
  3. Select Calculate to see total volatility and each asset's component risk.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Three assets are modelled: equity, bonds and cash.
  • Standard deviations and correlations are user inputs; historical estimates can change and do not predict future risk.
  • Fees, taxes, liquidity, changing correlations and non-normal return distributions are not modelled.

Frequently asked questions

Can an asset have a negative risk contribution?

Yes. Under some covariance assumptions an asset can reduce total portfolio volatility through diversification. This is a mathematical attribution, not a guarantee of protection.

Do the correlations have to be valid together?

Yes. Three pairwise correlations must form a mathematically valid correlation matrix; the calculator checks this before calculating.

Does this recommend an asset allocation?

No. It only calculates risk attribution for the weights and assumptions entered.
