ATM Transaction Cost Calculator

Estimate average ATM transaction cost from fixed operating costs, maintenance and transaction volume.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Average ATM transaction cost = (fixed costs + maintenance + transactions × variable cost) ÷ transactions.

Use consistent reporting periods, currency units and accounting definitions. This is a planning estimate and does not determine regulatory compliance or a customer’s approved product terms.

Worked example

15,000 fixed and maintenance cost plus 25,000 transactions at 0.08 gives 0.68 average cost per transaction.
