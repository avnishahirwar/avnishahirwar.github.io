Formula and method

Fridge thawing takes roughly 4.5-10.5 hours per kg depending on shape; cold water takes about half the fridge time.

Worked example

A 1 kg flat cut thaws in a fridge in about 4.5 hours; the same roast needs closer to 10 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Cook from frozen?

Safe for thin cuts with adjusted cooking times, not large roasts.

Microwave thaw?

Use the defrost setting and cook immediately afterwards.

Counter thawing?

Not recommended; the surface sits in the danger zone.
