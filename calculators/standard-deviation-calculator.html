Calculate both population and sample standard deviation from the same data set. Results include the mean and both variance measures.

How to use this calculator

Enter at least two finite numbers separated by commas, spaces or new lines. The calculator treats the complete list as a population and also calculates the sample estimate from the same observations.

Formulas and methodology

Population variance = Σ(x − μ)² ÷ N. Population standard deviation = √(population variance).

Sample variance = Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (n − 1). Sample standard deviation = √(sample variance).

Worked example

For the values 2, 4 and 6, the mean is 4. Population variance is 8 ÷ 3 ≈ 2.6667 and population standard deviation is approximately 1.6330. Sample variance is 4 and sample standard deviation is 2.

Scope and limitations

This is a descriptive statistics tool. It does not test whether a data set is representative, independent, normally distributed or suitable for a particular statistical model. Choose the population or sample result that matches your study design.

Frequently asked questions

When should I use population standard deviation?

Use it when the entered values represent the entire population you want to describe.

When should I use sample standard deviation?

Use it when the entered observations are a sample intended to estimate the variability of a larger population.

Why does sample variance use n − 1?

The n − 1 denominator is the conventional Bessel correction for estimating population variance from a sample.
