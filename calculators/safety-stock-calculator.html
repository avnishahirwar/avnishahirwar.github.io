Formulas used by this calculator

The Safety Stock Calculator works with 14 quantities and 19 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Reorder Point (reorderPoint) = avgDailyUsage × avgLeadTime + safetyStock
  • Avg Daily Usage (avgDailyUsage) = (reorderPoint - safetyStock)/avgLeadTime
  • Safety Stock (safetyStock) = -avgDailyUsage × avgLeadTime + reorderPoint
  • Avg Lead Time (avgLeadTime) = (reorderPoint - safetyStock)/avgDailyUsage
  • Max Daily Usage (maxDailyUsage) = (avgDailyUsage × avgLeadTime + safetyStock)/maxLeadTime
  • Stats Reorder Point (statsReorderPoint) = statsAvgDailyUsage × statsAvgLeadTime + statsSafetyStock
  • Max Lead Time (maxLeadTime) = (avgDailyUsage × avgLeadTime + safetyStock)/maxDailyUsage
  • Stats Avg Daily Usage (statsAvgDailyUsage) = (statsReorderPoint - statsSafetyStock)/statsAvgLeadTime

11 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
