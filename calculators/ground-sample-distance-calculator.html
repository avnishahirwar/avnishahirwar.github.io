Ground Sample Distance (GSD) Calculator

Estimate nominal ground sample distance (GSD) for a level, nadir image over flat ground. The result is a pixel-footprint estimate, not mapping accuracy.

Calculator

Enter height above ground in metres, sensor and focal length in millimetres, and image width in pixels.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

GSD (cm/pixel) ≈ [height above ground (m) × sensor width (mm) × 100] ÷ [actual focal length (mm) × image width (pixels)].

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

At 120 m above ground with a 13.2 mm sensor width, 8.8 mm actual focal length and 4,000-pixel image width: GSD ≈ 4.5 cm/pixel.

Assumptions and limitations

  • This approximation assumes level ground, nadir imaging and the stated sensor/focal-length geometry. Terrain relief, oblique images, lens distortion, stabilization, cropping and processing can change effective GSD.
  • Use height above ground, not altitude above sea level, and the camera’s actual focal length.
  • GSD is not positional accuracy, survey accuracy, or a guarantee that an object of a given size can be detected.
