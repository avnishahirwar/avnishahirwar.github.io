How the 16:9 ratio is calculated

A 16:9 image has 16 equal parts of width for every 9 equal parts of height. The aspect ratio is width divided by height, or 16 ÷ 9.

Formula

Height = width × 9 ÷ 16. Conversely, Width = height × 16 ÷ 9.

Worked example

For a 1,920-pixel-wide image: 1,920 × 9 ÷ 16 = 1,080 pixels high. A 3,840-pixel-wide image is 2,160 pixels high.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Width and height must use the same unit, such as pixels, centimetres or inches.
  • The calculator preserves the shape of the image; it does not crop, pad, resample or change image quality.
  • Rounded pixel dimensions can differ slightly from the exact mathematical ratio for unusual widths.

Interesting fact

Full HD (1,920 × 1,080) and UHD 4K (3,840 × 2,160) both use the same 16:9 shape; the 4K dimensions are twice as large in each direction.

Frequently asked questions

What does 16:9 mean?

It means 16 units of width for every 9 units of height. The numeric ratio is approximately 1.7778:1.

Can I enter height instead of width?

Yes. Enter one dimension and leave the other blank; the calculator solves the missing dimension.

Does 16:9 guarantee the whole image will fit a screen?

No. It matches the frame shape, but a display or platform may still crop or letterbox content depending on its own settings.
