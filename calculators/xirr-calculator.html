XIRR Calculator

Estimate an annualised money-weighted return from dated cash flows. Enter investments as negative amounts and withdrawals or proceeds as positive amounts.

Enter dated cash flows

Use negative values for money invested and positive values for money received. Enter at least one of each.

Calculation method

XIRR finds the annual rate that makes the present value of all dated cash flows equal to zero, using actual day differences divided by 365. Results can be ambiguous when cash flows change sign more than once; compare with the original cash-flow schedule.

Important assumptions and limitations

Results are estimates from the dates, values and assumptions you enter. This tool runs in your browser and does not connect to external accounts or live data. Verify important decisions with the relevant provider or official source.
