Formula and method

A common adult ceiling is about 4 mg of caffeine per kg of body weight per day.

Worked example

A 70 kg adult tops out near 280 mg, about two strong coffees plus tea.

Frequently asked questions

Is 4 mg/kg universal?

It is a general adult guide; pregnancy, medication and health conditions lower it.

Coffee vs tea mg?

Coffee is about 95 mg a cup, tea about 40 mg, cola about 30 mg a can.

Timing?

Halve intake by early afternoon to protect sleep.
