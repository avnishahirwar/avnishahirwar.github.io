How the calculation works

Medium-dog chart convention: age 1 = 15 human years; age 2 = 24; add about 5 human years per additional dog year. Research option: 16 × ln(dog age in years) + 31, derived from a Labrador DNA-methylation study.

The AVMA chart is explicitly approximate and based on a medium-sized dog; UC Davis describes the methylation equation and its Labrador-based research.

Worked example

For a 2-year-old dog, the approximate medium-dog chart convention gives 24 human-equivalent years. The separate DNA-methylation method can give a different estimate.

Important limits

A rough comparison only, not a biological measurement, diagnosis, life-stage assessment or care recommendation. The research equation came from Labradors and should not be assumed to fit every breed.
