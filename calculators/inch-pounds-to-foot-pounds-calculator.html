Inch-Pounds to Foot-Pounds Converter

Convert torque or moment between pound-force inches (lbf·in) and pound-force feet (lbf·ft).

Calculator

Torque/moment unit conversion only; no fastener setting is recommended.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

1 lbf·ft = 12 lbf·in. Therefore, lbf·ft = lbf·in ÷ 12. The reverse conversion is lbf·in = lbf·ft × 12.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

120 lbf·in ÷ 12 = 10 lbf·ft.

Assumptions and limitations

  • This converts units of torque or moment; it does not determine a suitable fastener torque.
  • Check the unit markings on the instrument. lbf·in and lbf·ft are not interchangeable without the factor of 12.
  • Negative values retain sign to indicate direction under the selected convention.
