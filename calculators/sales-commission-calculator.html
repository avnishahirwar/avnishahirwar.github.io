Formulas used by this calculator

The Sales Commission Calculator works with 71 quantities and 21 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Com Per (ComPer) = Com/SalesG
  • Sales G (SalesG) = Com/ComPer
  • Disc Per (DiscPer) = Disc/SalesG
  • Com (Com) = ComPer × SalesG
  • Disc (Disc) = DiscPer × SalesG
  • Profit G (ProfitG) = (-COGS + SalesG)
  • Labor Cost (LaborCost) = (Com + SalaryBase)
  • Margin Gross2 (MarginGross2) = ((-COGS + SalesG)/SalesG)

13 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
