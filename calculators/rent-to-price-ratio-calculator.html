Rent-to-Price Ratio Calculator

Compare annual gross rent with a property purchase price as a quick screening ratio.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Rent-to-price ratio = annual gross rent ÷ property price × 100.

Use consistent currency, area and reporting-period units. Property markets, financing terms, taxes and regulations vary by location and project; validate assumptions before making a transaction or development decision.

Worked example

1,800 monthly rent on a 300,000 property gives a 7.2% gross rent-to-price ratio.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool is an informational planning estimate, not property valuation, appraisal, mortgage approval, investment advice, tax advice, legal advice, construction advice, planning permission or a guaranteed return. Confirm local rules, contracts, market evidence, lender terms and professional reviews before relying on a result.
