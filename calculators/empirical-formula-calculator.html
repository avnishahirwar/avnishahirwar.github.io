Empirical Formula Calculator

Estimate the simplest whole-number atom ratio from the masses and atomic masses of two elements.

Enter your values

Formula

Mole ratio = (element mass / atomic mass) divided by the smallest mole amount.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Change one input at a time to see how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

This two-element educational estimator searches small integer multipliers and may not identify the correct formula for noisy measurements or compounds containing more than two elements. Confirm ratios using experimental context.
