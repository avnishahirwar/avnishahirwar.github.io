ARM Mortgage Calculator

With the ARM mortgage calculator you can model and estimate the repayment and interest costs of a given adjustable-rate mortgage.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formula and calculation method

A variable-rate scenario tool. It recalculates the remaining monthly payment at each reset using your entered rate change, reset cap and lifetime cap.

Assumptions and limitations

The model applies your entered adjustment at the selected reset interval. Real contracts may use an index, margin, floors, payment caps and jurisdiction-specific rules that are not modelled here.

How to use this calculator

Enter the opening balance, initial annual rate, loan term and the adjustment terms stated in your lender documents. The output is an illustration, not a forecast of future market rates.

Frequently asked questions

Does the selected country automatically supply local mortgage rules?

No. The selected country and Display currency settings control the website's regional presentation and currency formatting. Enter the rate, fees, taxes and other amounts that apply to your lender and location.

Is this a lender quote or financial advice?

No. Results are educational estimates based on the values entered. Confirm rates, charges, repayment dates and contract terms with your lender.

Formulas used by this calculator

The ARM Mortgage Calculator works with 30 quantities and 3 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Loan Amount (LoanAmount) = CostProc/CostPerc
  • Cost Proc (CostProc) = CostPerc × LoanAmount
  • Cost Perc (CostPerc) = CostProc/LoanAmount
