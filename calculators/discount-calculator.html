Discount Calculator

Calculate discount amount, final sale price and savings from an original price and discount percentage.

Open Discount Calculator

What this calculator does

Calculate a price reduction before separately applicable taxes, delivery fees or other charges. Enter the marked price and discount percentage to estimate the savings and sale price.

How to use it

  1. Enter original price
  2. enter discount percentage
  3. review amount saved
  4. check final price.
Calculate now

Formula and calculation method

Discount = original price × discount percentage ÷ 100. Sale price = original price − discount.

Worked example

A ₹2,000 product with a 15% discount has a ₹300 discount and a sale price of ₹1,700 before additional charges.

Important information

The calculator does not decide tax treatment. Check whether the seller applies the discount before or after tax on the invoice.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this calculator?

It provides an estimate based on the values and assumptions entered. Verify important decisions with the relevant provider or a qualified professional.

Does this replace professional advice?

No. It is an informational tool and does not replace tax, legal, accounting, lending, investment or medical advice.

Check the final sale price, not just the discount

For an item marked at ₹2,000 with a 15% discount, the saving is ₹300 and the discounted price is ₹1,700 before any additional charges. Comparing the final payable price across sellers is more useful than comparing headline discount percentages alone.

Common offer conditions

Coupons, bank offers, delivery fees, minimum-cart values, product exclusions and taxes may change the checkout total. A crossed-out list price may not represent the price at which the item is usually sold. Verify the seller's terms and final checkout amount before purchasing.
