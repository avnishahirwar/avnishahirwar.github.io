Formula and calculation method

Price = Σ[CFₜ ÷ (1 + y/m)^t]. Macaulay duration = Σ[(t/m) × PV(CFₜ)] ÷ price. Modified duration = Macaulay duration ÷ (1 + y/m).

Worked example

Macaulay duration is the present-value-weighted average time to receive bond cash flows; modified duration approximates price sensitivity to a small yield change.
