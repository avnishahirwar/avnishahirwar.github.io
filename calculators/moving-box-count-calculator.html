Formula and method

Boxes ≈ 15 per room + 10 per person + 8 per year lived in the home.

Worked example

A 3-room flat for 4 people after 3 years needs about 95 boxes in mixed sizes.

Frequently asked questions

Which mix of sizes?

Medium boxes carry books and pantry; large for linens; wardrobes for hanging clothes.

Buy spare?

Returns are easy for unused flat-packed boxes; keep 10% extra.

Label by room?

Yes, and number the boxes against a master list.
