Matrix Multiplication Calculator

The matrix multiplication calculator is your little helper in finding the product of two matrices up to 3x3 in size.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Matrix Multiplication Calculator works with 184 quantities and 108 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • C11 1 (c11_1) = (a1 × x1)
  • C12 121 (c12_121) = (a1 × x2)
  • C13 131 (c13_131) = (a1 × x3)
  • C21 211 (c21_211) = (b1 × x1)
  • C22 211 (c22_211) = (b1 × x1)
  • C22 221 (c22_221) = (b1 × x2)
  • C23 211 (c23_211) = (b1 × x1)
  • C23 221 (c23_221) = (b1 × x2)

100 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
