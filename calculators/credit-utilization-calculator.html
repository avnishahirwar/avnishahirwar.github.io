What is a credit utilization ratio?

Credit utilization is the share of your available revolving credit that is currently used. It is commonly expressed as a percentage by comparing outstanding card balances with the credit limits attached to those accounts. This calculator estimates the ratio from the combined balance and combined limit that you enter.

Credit utilization formula

Credit utilization (%) = total outstanding balance ÷ total available credit limit × 100

The unused credit shown by this calculator is the total limit minus the outstanding balance. Enter a positive total credit limit; a balance above the limit can produce a utilization result above 100%.

Worked example

If the combined credit limit is ₹1,00,000 and the combined outstanding balance is ₹25,000, utilization is ₹25,000 ÷ ₹1,00,000 × 100 = 25%. The unused portion is ₹75,000.

How to calculate utilization across multiple cards

  1. Add the credit limits for the cards you want to include.
  2. Add the outstanding balances for those same cards.
  3. Enter the two totals above to calculate the combined ratio.

Do not take a simple average of each card's utilization percentages when you want an overall ratio. A card with a larger limit contributes more to the combined limit and should be reflected in the totals.

Why reported utilization can differ

Card issuers and credit bureaus may use balances reported on different dates. A payment made today may not appear in the latest report immediately. Individual-card utilization can also differ from the combined ratio, so review both account-level balances and the overall total.

Credit scores and limitations

Credit utilization may be one input in some credit-scoring systems, but scoring models, bureau data, lender policies and reporting schedules vary. This calculator does not predict a credit score, prescribe a universal target or guarantee a lending decision. Check your latest statement and credit report for the figures actually reported.

Frequently asked questions

Is credit utilization the same as credit limit?

No. The credit limit is the amount of revolving credit available. Utilization compares the outstanding balance with that limit and expresses the result as a percentage.

Can credit utilization be above 100%?

Yes. If the balance entered is greater than the total limit, the mathematical ratio is above 100%. Check for fees, pending transactions or an account that has exceeded its limit.

Should I average the utilization of each card?

For an overall ratio, add the balances and add the limits, then divide the total balance by the total limit. A simple average of card-level percentages can give a misleading result.
