Mortgage Points Calculator

The mortgage points calculator helps you estimate and compare the savings gained when buying mortgage points.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formula and calculation method

Points cost equals the loan amount multiplied by the points percentage. The break-even period divides that cost by the monthly payment reduction from the entered rate discount.

Assumptions and limitations

Actual points pricing and eligibility are lender-specific. The simple break-even estimate does not include tax effects, time value of money or the chance you move or refinance sooner.

How to use this calculator

Enter the points cost and the rate reduction quoted by the lender, then compare the monthly savings.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Mortgage Points Calculator works with 35 quantities and 14 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Mortgage Points1 (mortgagePoints1) = 100 × pointCost1/loanAmount
  • Mortgage Points2 (mortgagePoints2) = 100 × pointCost2/loanAmount
  • Loan Amount (loanAmount) = 100 × pointCost1/mortgagePoints1
  • Point Cost1 (pointCost1) = (1/100) × loanAmount × mortgagePoints1
  • Point Cost2 (pointCost2) = (1/100) × loanAmount × mortgagePoints2
  • Interest Rate Base (interestRateBase) = discountPercent1 × mortgagePoints1 + interestRate1
  • Discount Percent1 (discountPercent1) = (-interestRate1 + interestRateBase)/mortgagePoints1
  • Discount Percent2 (discountPercent2) = (-interestRate2 + interestRateBase)/mortgagePoints2

6 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
