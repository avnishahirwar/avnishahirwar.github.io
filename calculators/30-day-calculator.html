The start date is day zero. This tool adds 30 calendar days and includes weekends.

How the 30-day calculation works

Choose a start date. The calculator adds exactly 30 calendar days using date-only arithmetic, then displays the resulting date and weekday. The start date itself is not counted as day one.

Formula and calculation method

Target date = Start date + 30 calendar days. The calculation moves forward by 30 whole calendar days on the Gregorian calendar. It does not add 30 business days.

Worked example

Starting from 1 January 2025, adding 30 calendar days gives 31 January 2025. The start date is excluded from the count.

Assumptions and limitations

  • All dates are treated as calendar dates, not times of day.
  • Weekends and public holidays are included.
  • The result does not account for business-day conventions, local holiday calendars or contractual counting rules.
  • Leap days are handled automatically by the Gregorian calendar.

Interesting fact

A 30-day period is not always one calendar month: months contain 28, 29, 30 or 31 days. Adding 30 days is therefore different from moving to the same date in the next month.

Frequently asked questions

Is the start date counted as day one?

No. The selected start date is day zero; the result is the date 30 full calendar days later.

Are weekends and holidays included?

Yes. This is a calendar-day calculation. It does not skip weekends or public holidays.

Is this the same as adding one month?

No. A fixed number of calendar days is not the same as adding calendar months, whose lengths vary.
