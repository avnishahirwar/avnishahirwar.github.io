Dog Chocolate Toxicity Calculator

How the calculation works

Estimated exposure (mg/kg) = amount eaten (g) × reference methylxanthine concentration (mg/g) ÷ dog weight (kg).

Merck and ASPCA publish approximate methylxanthine content and dose-effect ranges, but content varies by product and individual response varies.

Worked example

For 10 g of semisweet/dark chocolate and a 10 kg dog, the reference estimate is 10 × 5.6 ÷ 10 = 5.6 mg/kg. This does not rule out poisoning; contact a veterinarian for any suspected ingestion.

Important limits

Contact a veterinarian, emergency veterinary hospital, or animal poison-control service promptly for any suspected ingestion. Do not delay care, treat at home, or interpret a result as “safe”. Product content varies; this estimate does not assess fat/sugar complications or other ingredients.
