Arithmetic Series Sum Calculator

Find the nth term and the sum of an arithmetic series from the first term, common difference and number of terms.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

nth term = a + (n − 1)d. Sum = n ÷ 2 × (2a + (n − 1)d).

Worked example

With First term 5, Common difference 3, Number of terms 20, the calculator gives: nth term = 62; Sum of the series = 670; Average of the terms = 33.5.

How to read the result

The sum equals the number of terms times the average of the first and last term. This calculator takes non-negative values only.

Frequently asked questions

What if the difference is negative?

The series decreases. Enter positive values here and subtract manually, as this tool accepts non-negative inputs only.

How is this used in practice?

For regular savings that rise by a fixed amount, staircase pay scales and evenly spaced schedules.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool applies standard textbook formulas to the values you enter. Check units and rounding, and confirm important results with a second method.
