Biking Life Gain Calculator

With the Biking Life Gain tool you can evaluate how much longer you'll live on average if you cycle on a regular basis.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Biking Life Gain Calculator works with 9 quantities and 9 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Life expectancy (Life_expectancy) = -1/31557600 × Life_gained + Total_life
  • Total life (Total_life) = Life_expectancy + (1/31557600) × Life_gained
  • Life gained (Life_gained) = -31557600 × Life_expectancy + 31557600 × Total_life
  • Biking start (Biking_start) = Biking_end - 100/1564287 × Life_gained/(Biking_daily × Biking_days × Biking_months)
  • Biking months (Biking_months) = (100/1564287) × Life_gained/(Biking_daily × Biking_days × (Biking_end - Biking_start))
  • Biking daily (Biking_daily) = (100/1564287) × Life_gained/(Biking_days × Biking_months × (Biking_end - Biking_start))
  • Biking end (Biking_end) = Biking_start + (100/1564287) × Life_gained/(Biking_daily × Biking_days × Biking_months)
  • Biking days (Biking_days) = (100/1564287) × Life_gained/(Biking_daily × Biking_months × (Biking_end - Biking_start))

1 further rearrangement of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
