100 Day Calculator

Our 100 Day Calculator will help you find the exact date 100 days after the selected date.

Calculate 100 days after a date

The start date is day zero. This tool adds 100 calendar days and includes weekends.

How the 100-day calculation works

Choose a start date. The calculator adds exactly 100 calendar days using date-only arithmetic, then displays the resulting date and weekday. The start date itself is not counted as day one.

Formula and calculation method

Target date = Start date + 100 calendar days. The calculation moves forward by 100 whole calendar days on the Gregorian calendar. It does not add 100 business days.

Worked example

Starting from 1 January 2025, adding 100 calendar days gives 11 April 2025. The start date is excluded from the count.

Assumptions and limitations

  • All dates are treated as calendar dates, not times of day.
  • Weekends and public holidays are included.
  • The result does not account for business-day conventions, local holiday calendars or contractual counting rules.
  • Leap days are handled automatically by the Gregorian calendar.

Interesting fact

A 100-day interval can cross 29 February in a leap year, so the same start month and day can lead to a different calendar result in leap and non-leap years.

Frequently asked questions

Is the start date counted as day one?

No. The selected start date is day zero; the result is the date 100 full calendar days later.

Are weekends and holidays included?

Yes. This is a calendar-day calculation. It does not skip weekends or public holidays.

Is this the same as adding about three months?

No. A fixed number of calendar days is not the same as adding calendar months, whose lengths vary.
