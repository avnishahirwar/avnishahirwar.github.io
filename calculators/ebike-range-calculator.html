Formula and method

Range km = battery Wh ÷ consumption Wh per km.

Worked example

A 500 Wh battery at 12 Wh per km of mixed assist rides about 42 km.

Frequently asked questions

Headwinds and hills?

They raise consumption steeply; expect 20-40% less range into wind or uphill.

Rider weight?

Heavier total weight consumes more watt-hours per kilometre.

Battery ageing?

Cells lose capacity over years; plan range on the current measured Wh.
