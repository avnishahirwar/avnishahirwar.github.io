Formula and method

P₁V₁ = P₂V₂; P₂ = P₁V₁/V₂. Use absolute pressures and consistent volume units. Inputs must use the units stated above. This calculator performs only the displayed idealized calculation; it does not model unlisted effects.

Worked sample

1 atm in 2 L compressed to 1 L gives 2 atm absolute.

Validation and limitations

Inputs are checked for finite values and physically required positive quantities. Results are browser-only and are educational estimates; verify assumptions and units before practical use. This is not an engineering or safety certification tool.
