How this estimate works

Buying costs include the down payment, loan EMIs paid during the comparison period and estimated maintenance, less the assumed future property value. Renting costs include monthly rent with annual increases and the assumed future value of the unspent down payment. Stamp duty, registration, tax effects, brokerage, major repairs, selling costs, rent deposit, and the opportunity cost of all monthly cash-flow differences are not fully modelled.

Property appreciation and investment returns are uncertain. Actual mortgage rates, fees, taxes, maintenance, vacancy, resale price and rent escalation vary. Do not treat the lower modelled number as a guaranteed better decision.

FAQs

Does this decide whether I should rent or buy?

No. It compares only the assumptions entered. Location, stability, liquidity, household needs and legal due diligence also matter.

Are taxes and stamp duty included?

No. Add them separately to your own comparison; actual rates and deductions depend on location and eligibility.
