Barbell Plate Loading Calculator

Find a balanced plate combination for a target barbell weight.

Enter a target, bar and plate size.

Formula and limitations

This simple loading estimate calculates the required weight per side as (target total − bar weight) ÷ 2, then reports how many of the selected largest plate would fit per side. It does not create a full mixed-plate inventory or account for collars, plate availability or equipment limits.

Equipment note: Check the actual bar, plates, collars and rack before lifting. Do not load a weight that exceeds equipment ratings or your safe ability.
