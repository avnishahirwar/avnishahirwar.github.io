Formulas used by this calculator

  • Angle4 (angle4) = PI - angle3

  • Base B (baseB) = -baseA + 2 × median
  • Slant (slant) = height/sin(angle4)
  • Median (median) = (1/2) × baseA + (1/2) × baseB
