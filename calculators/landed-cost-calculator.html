Landed Cost Calculator

Estimate the total cost of bringing a shipment to its destination, including goods, transport, insurance, customs duty and handling charges entered by you.

Enter shipment costs

Enter all amounts in the same currency. Use the actual charges from your supplier, carrier, customs broker and applicable authority.

Formula and method

Total landed cost = goods purchase value + origin inland freight + international freight + cargo insurance + customs duty amount + customs clearance and brokerage + destination inland delivery + other shipment charges.

Landed cost per unit = total landed cost ÷ number of units in the shipment.

Worked example

For 100 units with a goods value of 50,000, freight and insurance of 8,000, customs duty of 5,000 and other clearance and delivery charges of 2,000 (all in the same currency), total landed cost is 65,000 and landed cost per unit is 650.

Assumptions and limitations

  • This is a cost aggregation tool, not a customs classification, duty-rate or tax calculator.
  • Enter statutory duty, import VAT/GST, excise, anti-dumping duty and other applicable taxes as separate charges in the appropriate cost fields if they belong in your accounting definition.
  • Customs valuation, Incoterms, exchange rates, duty bases, recoverable taxes, transfer pricing and accounting treatment vary by jurisdiction and transaction. Confirm the applicable treatment with official customs/tax guidance or a qualified customs professional.
  • All entries must use one currency and one consistent shipment scope. Do not mix per-unit and total-shipment values.

Frequently asked questions

What does landed cost include?

It commonly combines the purchase cost with costs required to transport and clear goods to a defined destination. The exact accounting and customs treatment depends on the transaction and jurisdiction.

Does this calculator calculate customs duty automatically?

No. Customs duty depends on classification, origin, valuation, trade measures and current law. Enter a duty amount verified for your shipment.

Can I compare suppliers?

Yes. Calculate each supplier's shipment using the same currency, quantity basis and destination scope, then compare the per-unit result.
