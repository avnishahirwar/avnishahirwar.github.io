How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Fence Stain Calculator works with 19 quantities and 33 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Size Of Fence (sizeOfFence) = fenceHeight × fenceLength
  • Fence Length (fenceLength) = sizeOfFence/fenceHeight
  • Fence Height (fenceHeight) = sizeOfFence/fenceLength
  • Area That Needs Stain (areaThatNeedsStain) = numberOfSides × sizeOfFence
  • Number Of Sides (numberOfSides) = areaThatNeedsStain/sizeOfFence
  • Box Fence Area That Needs Stain (boxFenceAreaThatNeedsStain) = (3/2) × numberOfSides × sizeOfFence
  • Total Transparent Stain (totalTransparentStain) = areaThatNeedsStain/sizePerGallon
  • Total Solid Color Stain (totalSolidColorStain) = 2 × areaThatNeedsStain/sizePerGallon

25 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
