Formula and method

Target = current × (1 + 0.1 × weeks). Weekly increase = (target − current) ÷ (5 × weeks).

Worked example

Going from a 60-second plank in 8 weeks targets 108 seconds, an increase of about 1.2 seconds per training day.

Important limitations

Frequently asked questions

Is 10% per week safe?

It is a moderate, sustainable full-core progression for most healthy adults.

Should I plank daily?

Five focused sessions a week with rest days works well for most people.

Quality first?

Yes. Stop the hold when the hips sag or the back arches; form beats duration.
