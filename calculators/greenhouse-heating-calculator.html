Formula and method

Watts ≈ area × temperature gap × fabric heat-loss factor.

Worked example

A 10 sq m single-glass greenhouse held 20 °C above outside needs 10 × 20 × 8 = 1,600 W.

Frequently asked questions

What is the factor?

It is the U-value-lumped fabric loss in W per sq m per °C for the covering material.

Thermal screens help?

Yes, screens and bubble wrap can cut losses 30-50%.

Propane option?

Convert 1 kWh to fuel; ensure combustion venting in a sealed house.
