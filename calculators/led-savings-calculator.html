How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The LED Savings Calculator works with 19 quantities and 30 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Reg bulb lifetime sec (reg_bulb_lifetime_sec) = reg_bulb_lifetime
  • Reg bulb lifetime (reg_bulb_lifetime) = reg_bulb_lifetime_sec
  • Led bulb xyr cost (led_bulb_xyr_cost) = reg_bulb_xyr_cost - savings
  • Reg bulb xyr cost (reg_bulb_xyr_cost) = led_bulb_xyr_cost + savings
  • Savings (savings) = -led_bulb_xyr_cost + reg_bulb_xyr_cost
  • Break even after (break_even_after) = (31557600 × led_bulb_1yr_cost/reg_bulb_1yr_cost)
  • Led bulb 100yr cost (led_bulb_100yr_cost) = (487/48000) × daily_usage × energy_price × led_consumption + led_bulb_price × ceil(36525 × daily_usage/led_bulb_lifetime)
  • Led bulb 1yr cost (led_bulb_1yr_cost) = (487/4800000) × daily_usage × energy_price × led_consumption + led_bulb_price × ceil((1461/4) × daily_usage/led_bulb_lifetime)

22 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
