How this compatibility reflection works

This page is a light self-reflection activity. It asks you to rate four relationship qualities: mutual respect, communication, trust and reliability. Each rating is between 1 and 5, and the calculator finds the simple average. It does not compare two people, read names or birth dates, analyse private messages, or predict whether a relationship will succeed.

Relationships are shaped by choices, circumstances, communication and how people treat one another over time. A single number cannot measure a person's feelings, personal worth, compatibility, consent or future. The score is only a summary of the ratings entered by the visitor.

Formula and worked example

Average self-rating = (respect + communication + trust + reliability) ÷ 4.

For example, if someone enters 4 for respect, 3 for communication, 4 for trust and 5 for reliability, the average is (4 + 3 + 4 + 5) ÷ 4 = 4. The result simply averages those four self-selected numbers. It is not a scientific compatibility score or a judgement about either person.

How to use this calculator

  1. Read the four qualities and their rating scale before entering anything.
  2. Choose a rating from 1 to 5 based only on your own experience and comfort.
  3. Select Calculate reflection average to see the arithmetic mean of the four ratings.
  4. Use the result as a prompt for thoughtful reflection, not as a reason to pressure someone or make a major decision.

Understanding the four qualities

Mutual respect

Respect includes listening, treating each other fairly, accepting differences and recognising personal boundaries. People can disagree while still speaking respectfully.

Communication

Communication includes listening as well as speaking. It can involve asking questions, explaining concerns clearly and allowing time to think before responding. People may communicate differently, and a rating cannot capture every context.

Trust and honesty

Trust develops through consistent, honest behaviour. It should not require sharing passwords, private messages, account access or information someone is not comfortable sharing.

Reliability and care

Reliability means following through on reasonable commitments and being considerate. It does not mean being available every moment or giving up personal time, friendships, school, work or other responsibilities.

Benefits and limitations

  • Provides a quick way to organise four self-reflection ratings.
  • Shows the calculation openly so the result can be checked.
  • Does not ask for names, dates of birth, contact details or private conversations.
  • Runs locally in the browser; the ratings are not sent to the website by this calculator.
  • Cannot measure love, consent, safety, emotional wellbeing or future relationship outcomes.

If a relationship or friendship involves pressure, threats, controlling behaviour or fear, a score is not the right tool for deciding what to do. Consider speaking with a trusted adult or appropriate support person. Everyone has the right to boundaries and to say no.

Tips for using the result thoughtfully

Answer honestly, and remember that a rating can change as circumstances change. Do not treat the average as a pass/fail result, compare it with another person's score, or use it to demand proof of someone's feelings. A low rating in one area may simply mean that the topic deserves a calm conversation, or that you need more time to understand the situation.

Healthy connections leave room for school, family, friendships, rest, interests and individual goals. People should be able to disagree, ask for space and make decisions without pressure. If you feel unsure, take time to reflect and talk with someone you trust rather than relying on a calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Does this score predict whether a relationship will last?

No. It is a simple average of four ratings and has not been validated as a prediction of relationship outcomes.

Do I need to enter names or personal details?

No. The tool uses four optional ratings only. Do not enter private information about yourself or another person.

Does a high score mean two people are compatible?

No. The result reflects only the ratings entered. It cannot assess feelings, consent, safety, circumstances or the future.

Can I use the score to compare people?

No. People and relationships are different, and the average is not a fair or reliable way to rank or compare anyone.

What should I do if a relationship feels uncomfortable or unsafe?

Do not rely on a score. Consider speaking with a trusted adult or appropriate support person, and remember that personal boundaries should be respected.
