HELOC Calculator

HELOC calculator is a tool that determines your eligibility for a Home Equity Line of Credit. It'll also help plan the associated payments during the draw period and the repayment period required to pay off the HELOC loan.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The HELOC Calculator works with 37 quantities and 3 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.
