Fiber Intake from Calories Calculator

Estimate a daily dietary fiber target from your calorie intake using the common 14 g per 1,000 kcal guideline.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Fiber target (g) = daily calories ÷ 1,000 × 14.

Worked example

With Daily calorie intake 2,000, the calculator gives: Suggested fiber (g per day) = 28; Suggested fiber per meal if 3 meals (g) = 9.33.

How to read the result

Raise fiber gradually and drink enough water to limit bloating. Needs differ for people with digestive conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Where does 14 g per 1,000 kcal come from?

It is a widely used dietary guideline for adults, based on intake linked with lower cardiovascular risk.

Does this replace advice from a dietitian?

No. Individual needs and medical conditions can change the target.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives general informational estimates only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Individual needs vary with age, health conditions, medicines and activity, so speak with a qualified health professional before changing diet, training or medication.
