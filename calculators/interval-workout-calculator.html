Enter interval settings.

How it works

The calculator adds warm-up time, cool-down time, all work intervals and recovery periods between rounds. It does not add recovery after the final round because many sessions finish when the last work interval ends. Add that time manually if your plan includes it.

Safety note: Interval intensity should match your experience and health. This tool does not determine a safe heart rate or workout intensity.
