Formulas used by this calculator

The Capacitive Transformerless Power Supply Calculator works with 8 quantities and 9 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Voltage out (voltage_out) = -voltage_d + voltage_z
  • Voltage z (voltage_z) = voltage_d + voltage_out
  • Voltage d (voltage_d) = -voltage_out + voltage_z
  • Capacitance 1 (capacitance_1) = -current_in/(PI × frequency × (2 × current_in × resistance_1 - sqrt(2) × voltage_rms + voltage_z))
  • Frequency (frequency) = -current_in/(PI × capacitance_1 × (2 × current_in × resistance_1 - sqrt(2) × voltage_rms + voltage_z))
  • Current in (current_in) = PI × capacitance_1 × frequency × (sqrt(2) × voltage_rms - voltage_z)/(2 × PI × capacitance_1 × frequency × resistance_1 + 1)
  • Resistance 1 (resistance_1) = (1/2) × (PI × capacitance_1 × frequency × (sqrt(2) × voltage_rms - voltage_z) - current_in)/(PI × capacitance_1 × current_in × frequency)
  • Voltage rms (voltage_rms) = (1/2) × sqrt(2) × (PI × capacitance_1 × frequency × (2 × current_in × resistance_1 + voltage_z) + current_in)/(PI × capacitance_1 × frequency)

1 further rearrangement of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
