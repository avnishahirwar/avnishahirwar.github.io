What daylight duration means

Daylight duration is the interval between sunrise and sunset for a selected date and location. The calculator uses the date, latitude, longitude, time zone and selected twilight or solar-angle assumptions to estimate the times.

Formula

Daylight duration = sunset time − sunrise time

Worked example

If sunrise is 06:10 and sunset is 18:20 local time, the daylight duration is 12 hours and 10 minutes.

Seasonal and geographic limits

Day length changes with season and latitude. Near the polar circles, some dates can have continuous daylight or no sunrise; results also depend on the selected definition of sunrise and sunset.
