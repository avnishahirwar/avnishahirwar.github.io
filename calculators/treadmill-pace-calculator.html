Formula and method

Seconds per kilometre = 3,600 ÷ speed (km/h).

Worked example

At 10 km/h, 3,600 ÷ 10 = 360 seconds, which is 6 minutes 0 seconds per kilometre.

Frequently asked questions

Why 3,600 seconds?

One hour is 3,600 seconds, so seconds per kilometre equal 3,600 divided by the speed in km/h.

Does this work for mph?

This page uses km/h. Convert an mph speed to km/h by multiplying it by 1.609 first.

Is treadmill pace equal to road pace?

Not exactly. Wind, hills, belt calibration and air resistance are not modelled here.
