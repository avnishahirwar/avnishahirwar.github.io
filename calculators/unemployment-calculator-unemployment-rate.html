Formula and step-by-step calculation

Unemployment rate = unemployed ÷ labor force × 100, where labor force = employed + unemployed. For BLS Current Population Survey classification, “unemployed” has specific availability and active-search or temporary-layoff criteria.

Worked example: 6 unemployed people and 294 employed people produce a labor force of 300 and a calculated rate of 2.00%.

Official reference table

TermMeaning
UnemployedNot employed, available for work, and actively searched in the reference period or temporarily laid off under CPS criteria

Limitations

The CPS universe is the civilian noninstitutional population age 16 and older. A calculation from user-entered counts may differ from a BLS published rate because official estimates use survey and estimation methods. Unemployment insurance status does not determine CPS classification.

Frequently asked questions

Does this result guarantee a benefit or final liability?

No. This formula gives a statistical rate for a population. Benefit programs have separate federal and state eligibility requirements.

What should I verify before relying on a result?

Use counts that refer to the same population, location and period. For a BLS statistic, apply the CPS definitions and methodology.
