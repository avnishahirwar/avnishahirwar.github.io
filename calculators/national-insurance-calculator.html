Worked example

For annual earnings of £40,000, the standard category A estimate is 8% × (£40,000 − £12,570) = £2,194.40, with no earnings above the £50,270 upper limit.

Formula

NI = 8% × earnings between the primary threshold and upper earnings limit + 2% × earnings above the upper earnings limit.

FAQs

Is this for every National Insurance category?

No. It models the standard category A employee rate. Other category letters, deferment, directors, multiple jobs, special cases and self-employment can produce different results.

Does this include employer National Insurance?

No. It estimates the employee deduction only.

Which tax year is used?

The displayed defaults are for 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027. Update the thresholds and rates if the official rules change.
