Formula and step-by-step calculation

For the historical published categories, the result is selected from the FTB payment schedule using income, wages, filing, residency, dependent-credit and GSS I status. The amount is not inferred from income alone.

Worked example: a qualifying SSN filer with 2020 AGI and wages within the limits, no GSS I qualification, and a dependent credit falls in the published USD 1,100 category, subject to all general requirements.

Limitations

The FTB bulletin defines four categories. Other combinations return no supported category here. This tool is retrospective only and cannot request, issue or guarantee a payment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this as a current benefit eligibility calculator?

No. The California Franchise Tax Board says the Golden State Stimulus programs are complete and no new payments have been issued since July 2022.

What should I verify before relying on a result?

No. It only reconstructs listed 2020 payment categories and does not prove an individual’s legal entitlement.
