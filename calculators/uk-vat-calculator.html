Formula and guidance

To add VAT: VAT = net price × rate; gross price = net price + VAT. To extract VAT: net price = gross price ÷ (1 + rate); VAT = gross price − net price.

In the UK, the standard VAT rate is 20%, the reduced rate is 5%, and the zero rate is 0%. Some goods and services are VAT-exempt or outside the scope of VAT; these are not the same as zero-rated. Check the correct supply category before applying a rate.

Frequently asked questions

Does a 0% rate mean VAT-exempt?

No. Zero-rated supplies are still taxable supplies for VAT purposes. VAT exemption is a different category.

Are all goods charged at 20%?

No. Some goods and services are reduced-rated at 5%, zero-rated at 0%, or exempt. Check official HMRC guidance.
