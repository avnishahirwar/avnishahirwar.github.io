Formulas used by this calculator

The Angular Acceleration Calculator works with 9 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Angular acceleration2 (angular_acceleration2) = angular_acceleration
  • Angular acceleration (angular_acceleration) = angular_acceleration2
  • Tangential acceleration (tangential_acceleration) = angular_acceleration × radius
  • Angular velocity difference (angular_velocity_difference) = final_velocity - initial_velocity
  • Initial velocity (initial_velocity) = -angular_acceleration × time + final_velocity
  • Final velocity (final_velocity) = angular_acceleration × time + initial_velocity
  • Radius (radius) = tangential_acceleration/angular_acceleration
  • Time (time) = (final_velocity - initial_velocity)/angular_acceleration

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
