How to convert square yards and acres

An acre is an area unit equal to 4,840 square yards. Enter the area value and choose the direction of conversion; the calculator uses the same fixed relationship in either direction.

Conversion formula

Acres = square yards ÷ 4,840

Square yards = acres × 4,840

Worked example

To convert 9,680 square yards to acres: 9,680 ÷ 4,840 = 2 acres. To convert 2 acres back, multiply 2 × 4,840 = 9,680 square yards.

Units and limitations

This is an area conversion, not a conversion from a linear yard measurement. The result uses the standard international acre relationship and does not account for land-survey boundary differences or local title measurements.
