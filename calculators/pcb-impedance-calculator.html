PCB Impedance Calculator

Why would anyone calculate PCB impedances by hand, when creating some new electronics? Exactly! At external calculator source thought the same, and hence we have developed this PCB Impedance calculator: the only tool you'll need to get your numbers in a second.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The PCB Impedance Calculator works with 38 quantities and 18 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Height (height) = b
  • Diameter (diameter) = width
  • b (b) = height
  • Height1 (height1) = height
  • Width (width) = diameter
  • Pep (pep) = (diameter + 2 × height)/diameter
  • Impedance4 (impedance4) = log(4 × height/(PI × diameter)) × (120 × PI)/(2 × PI × sqrt(relative_permitivity))
  • Relative permitivity (relative_permitivity) = (2 × effective_permitivity_more + sqrt(width/(12 × height + width)) - 1)/(sqrt(width/(12 × height + width)) + 1)

10 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
