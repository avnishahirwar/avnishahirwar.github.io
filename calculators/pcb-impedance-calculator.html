What affects PCB trace impedance?

Characteristic impedance depends on the transmission-line geometry and the board stack-up. Relevant inputs can include trace width, copper thickness, dielectric height, relative permittivity and spacing between conductors.

Common trace geometries

Microstrip traces run on an outer layer over a reference plane. Stripline traces sit between reference planes. Differential pairs and coupled traces also depend on the spacing between conductors and the selected stack-up model.

How to use an estimate

Use material and geometry values supplied for the actual PCB stack-up. Manufacturing tolerances, solder mask, copper roughness, frequency and the fabricator's process can affect the final impedance. Confirm controlled-impedance targets and stack-up details with your PCB manufacturer before production.
