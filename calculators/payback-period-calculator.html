Worked example

If an equipment purchase requires ₹5,00,000 upfront and is expected to generate ₹1,25,000 in net cash inflow each year, simple payback is ₹5,00,000 ÷ ₹1,25,000 = 4 years.

Frequently asked questions

What is the simple payback period?

It is the time required for cumulative net cash inflows to equal the initial investment, assuming the stated inflow pattern.

Does this method account for the time value of money?

No. Simple payback does not discount future cash flows. A discounted-payback analysis can produce a different result.

What if annual cash inflows change?

This simplified calculator assumes a constant annual net inflow. For uneven cash flows, add each period's expected inflow cumulatively instead.

Does a shorter payback prove an investment is profitable?

No. It does not measure cash flows after payback, total return, risk or project value. Consider those factors separately.
