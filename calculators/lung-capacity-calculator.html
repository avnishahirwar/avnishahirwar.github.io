Lung Capacity Calculator

The lung capacity calculator uses measured lung volumes to find vital capacity, total lung capacity, inspiratory capacity, and functional residual capacity.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Lung Capacity Calculator works with 8 quantities and 15 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Inspiratory reserve volume (inspiratory_reserve_volume) = inspiratory_capacity - tidal_volume
  • Inspiratory capacity (inspiratory_capacity) = inspiratory_reserve_volume + tidal_volume
  • Functional residual capacity (functional_residual_capacity) = expiratory_reserve_volume + residual_volume
  • Expiratory reserve volume (expiratory_reserve_volume) = functional_residual_capacity - residual_volume
  • Tidal volume (tidal_volume) = inspiratory_capacity - inspiratory_reserve_volume
  • Residual volume (residual_volume) = -expiratory_reserve_volume + functional_residual_capacity
  • Vital capacity (vital_capacity) = expiratory_reserve_volume + inspiratory_reserve_volume + tidal_volume
  • Total lung capacity (total_lung_capacity) = expiratory_reserve_volume + inspiratory_reserve_volume + residual_volume + tidal_volume

7 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
