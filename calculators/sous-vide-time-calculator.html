Formula and method

Time scales roughly with the square of thickness (thermal diffusion); every extra degree of core gap adds a small penalty.

Worked example

A 40 mm steak from a 20 degree fridge-temperature core needs about 1.3 hours before holding.

Frequently asked questions

Can I hold longer?

Yes; at bath temperature most cuts hold well for several hours.

Frozen start?

Add roughly 50% more time for frozen portions.

Thickness matters most

Measure at the thickest point, not the average.
