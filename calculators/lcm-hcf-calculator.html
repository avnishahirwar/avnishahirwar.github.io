What are LCM and HCF?

The highest common factor (HCF), also called greatest common divisor (GCD), is the largest positive integer that divides every input number without a remainder. The least common multiple (LCM) is the smallest positive integer that is a multiple of every input number.

Formula

For two positive integers a and b: LCM(a,b) × HCF(a,b) = a × b

For more than two numbers, calculate the HCF or LCM successively across the list. This calculator uses the Euclidean algorithm for HCF and the relationship between HCF and LCM.

Example

For 12, 18 and 30, the HCF is 6 and the LCM is 180. The result can help with fraction denominators, repeating schedules and common multiples in arithmetic problems.

Limitations

Enter between two and ten positive whole numbers. Decimal values, negative values and zero are not accepted. Very large inputs may exceed the exact integer range supported by JavaScript.

Frequently asked questions

Is HCF the same as GCD?

Yes. HCF and GCD are two names for the greatest positive integer that divides all the given integers.

Can I calculate LCM for more than two numbers?

Yes. Enter two to ten comma-separated positive integers.

How is LCM related to HCF?

For two positive integers, their product equals their LCM multiplied by their HCF.
