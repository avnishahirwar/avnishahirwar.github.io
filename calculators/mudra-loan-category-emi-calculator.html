Formula and step-by-step calculation

EMI uses the reducing-balance formula. The interest rate and repayment terms are lender-specific. Tarun Plus requires successful repayment of a previous Tarun loan.

  1. Classify the proposed amount: Shishu up to ₹50,000; Kishore up to ₹5 lakh; Tarun up to ₹10 lakh; Tarun Plus up to ₹20 lakh subject to previous Tarun repayment.
  2. Convert annual interest to a monthly rate by dividing by 1,200.
  3. Calculate EMI using the reducing-balance formula: principal × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate)−months).
  4. Calculate total repayment = EMI × number of months; lender terms determine the actual offer.

The tool explains the arithmetic used; it does not access private bank, employer, land-record or government-beneficiary databases.

Official source and verification

Reference: Department of Financial Services — PMMY

Official source checked: 14 November 2026. PMMY categories are Shishu up to ₹50,000; Kishor above ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh; Tarun above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh; and Tarun Plus above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh only for entrepreneurs who previously availed and successfully repaid a Tarun loan. The lender sets the interest rate and approval; the EMI is an estimate using the rate entered.
