Option A at 8.5% has an estimated EMI of ₹9,847.40, total repayment of ₹17,72,531.20 and interest of ₹7,72,531.20. Loan-comparison questions

Why must I compare the same loan amount and tenure?

Changing the principal or repayment period at the same time as the interest rate makes it difficult to identify what caused the difference. Hold the amount and tenure constant first, then compare rates and fees separately.

Does the lower EMI always mean the cheaper loan?

No. A longer repayment period can reduce the monthly EMI while increasing total interest. Compare total repayment, processing fees, insurance, prepayment conditions and rate-reset clauses.

Are lender fees included in the comparison?

No. The displayed comparison is based on principal and scheduled interest. Add processing fees and other charges separately when reviewing a lender's written quotation.

Are the compared rates fixed for the whole term?

The illustration assumes each entered annual rate remains constant throughout the selected tenure. It does not forecast floating-rate changes or future lender decisions.
