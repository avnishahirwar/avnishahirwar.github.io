Frequently asked questions

Why compare the same loan amount?

Keeping the principal the same makes the effect of rates and tenures easier to compare.

Does a lower EMI always mean a cheaper loan?

No. A longer tenure may lower the EMI but increase total interest.

Are fees included?

No. Add processing fees, insurance and other charges from the lender's written quote separately.
