Formulas used by this calculator

The Thermal Efficiency Calculator works with 20 quantities and 21 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Efficiency total energy (efficiency_total_energy) = W/Q_in
  • Efficiency energy per mass (efficiency_energy_per_mass) = w/q_in
  • Q out (Q_out) = Q_in - W
  • Q out (q_out) = q_in - w
  • Q in (Q_in) = Q_out + W
  • Q in (q_in) = q_out + w
  • W (W) = Q_in - Q_out
  • w (w) = q_in - q_out

13 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
