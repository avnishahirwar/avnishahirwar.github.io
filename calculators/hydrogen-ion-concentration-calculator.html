Formula

Hydrogen ion concentration (mol/L) = 10^(−pH).

Assumptions and limitations

This reports the concentration implied by the pH value entered. Real solutions can require activity corrections; temperature and composition affect interpretation. The optional temperature field is recorded for context only and is not used in the equation.
