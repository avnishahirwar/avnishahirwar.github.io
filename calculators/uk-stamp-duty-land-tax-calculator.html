Formula and step-by-step calculation

For the ordinary residential case, apply progressive bands: 0% on the first £125,000; 2% on £125,000–£250,000; 5% on £250,000–£925,000; 10% on £925,000–£1.5 million; and 12% above £1.5 million.

Worked example: an ordinary £300,000 purchase gives £2,500 on the £125,000–£250,000 slice and £2,500 on the £250,000–£300,000 slice, for £5,000 before reliefs or surcharges.

Limitations

SDLT covers England and Northern Ireland; Scotland and Wales have separate property transaction taxes. Effective date, property type, reliefs, surcharges, leases and linked transactions can change the result. Use HMRC guidance for complex cases.

Frequently asked questions

Does this result guarantee a benefit or final liability?

No. It provides only the ordinary residential baseline. First-time buyer relief and other surcharges may change the payable tax.

What should I verify before relying on a result?

No. Scotland uses Land and Buildings Transaction Tax, while Wales uses Land Transaction Tax.
