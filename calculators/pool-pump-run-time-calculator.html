Frequently asked questions

What is a turnover?

Filtering the whole pool volume once. One turnover a day is a common baseline; summer often needs two.

Can I split the runs?

Yes. Two runs with a midday break keep chemicals circulated.

Variable-speed pumps?

Run longer at lower speed for equal turnover with less energy.
