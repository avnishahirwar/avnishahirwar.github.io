Formula and method

Centimetres = inches × 2.54. Yards = inches ÷ 36. Metres = yards × 0.9144. Simple length conversions, no seam allowances or shrinkage applied.

Worked example

A 44-inch width is 111.8 cm. 3 yards of it is 108 feet long, or 2.74 metres.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate. Verify the bolt label from your supplier; real widths vary between mills.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a purchase decision without checking supplier measurements.

Frequently asked questions

How does this calculator work?

It applies a transparent formula to the values you enter, entirely in your browser.

Are the results exact?

They are close estimates for typical conditions; real-world results can shift with rounding and measurement error.

Who is this page for?

Anyone who needs a quick, free answer without signing up. Values are never sent to a server.
