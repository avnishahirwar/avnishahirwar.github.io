How it works

Formulas used by this calculator

The Audiobook Speed Calculator works with 5 quantities and 9 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Total listening time (total_listening_time) = audiobook_length/playback_speed
  • Audiobook length (audiobook_length) = playback_speed × total_listening_time
  • Playback speed (playback_speed) = audiobook_length/total_listening_time
  • Listening time saved (listening_time_saved) = audiobook_length - total_listening_time
  • Listening time extra (listening_time_extra) = -audiobook_length + total_listening_time

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
