Formulas used by this calculator

The Cattle per Acre Calculator works with 22 quantities and 18 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Forage available custom (forage_available_custom) = total_forage_custom/area
  • Cattle per area custom (cattle_per_area_custom) = total_cattle_custom/area
  • Total cattle custom (total_cattle_custom) = area × cattle_per_area_custom
  • Total forage custom (total_forage_custom) = area × forage_available_custom
  • Total animal units month custom (total_animal_units_month_custom) = total_forage_custom/lb_to_aum_ratio
  • Forage production custom (forage_production_custom) = forage_available_custom/utilization_rate
  • Forage production aum custom (forage_production_aum_custom) = forage_production_custom/lb_to_aum_ratio
  • Area (area) = total_cattle_custom/cattle_per_area_custom
