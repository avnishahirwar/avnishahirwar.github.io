Estimate connected electrical load, demand-adjusted load, monthly energy consumption and electricity charges for appliances or equipment.

Formula and method

Connected load (kW) = quantity × rated watts ÷ 1,000. Demand load = connected load × demand factor. Monthly energy (kWh) = demand load × operating hours/day × operating days.

Worked example

Four 1,500 W units at a 75% demand factor running 8 hours/day for 25 days use an estimated 900 kWh. At 6 currency units per kWh, the energy charge is 5,400 units.

Assumptions and limitations

This is an energy-planning estimate, not a wiring, breaker, cable, transformer or electrical-safety sizing tool. It excludes fixed charges, taxes, power-factor penalties, time-of-use rates and utility demand charges. Have electrical installations assessed by qualified professionals.

Frequently asked questions

Is connected load the same as energy use?

No. Connected load is rated power; energy use also depends on operating time and demand.

Does this calculate the required breaker size?

No. Protective-device and conductor sizing require applicable electrical codes and installation details.
