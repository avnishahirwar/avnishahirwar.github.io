EPS pension formula and worked example

The simplified Employees’ Pension Scheme formula is:

Monthly pension = pensionable salary × pensionable service ÷ 70.

For an illustrative pensionable salary of ₹15,000 per month and 20 years of pensionable service: ₹15,000 × 20 ÷ 70 = approximately ₹4,285.71 per month. This is a mathematical example, not a forecast of an individual entitlement.

Enter the average pensionable salary and service period recognised by EPFO. Do not substitute gross salary or CTC. Actual pension depends on membership history, wage ceilings, past-service provisions, eligible service, higher-pension options and the rules applicable to the member. Official reference: EPFO — EPS pension formula document.

Frequently asked questions

What is the EPS pension formula?

The simplified formula is pensionable salary multiplied by pensionable service, divided by 70.

Should I enter gross salary?

No. Enter the average pensionable salary recognised under EPS rules, not gross CTC or take-home pay.

Is this an official EPFO pension calculation?

No. The tool is a simplified estimate. EPFO records and the applicable scheme provisions determine actual eligibility and pension.
