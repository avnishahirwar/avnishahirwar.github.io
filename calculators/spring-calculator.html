Spring Calculator

The famous spring calculator helps you determine the relationship between spring deformation and the force or torque applied.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Spring Calculator works with 26 quantities and 48 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Delta x (delta_x) = -force_f/k_tc
  • Force f (force_f) = -delta_x × k_tc
  • Spring torque M (spring_torque_M) = angle × k_torsion
  • K tc (k_tc) = -force_f/delta_x
  • K torsion (k_torsion) = spring_torque_M/angle
  • Spring index C (spring_index_C) = mean_diam_D/wire_diam_d
  • Active coils Na (active_coils_Na) = (Log - pitch_p)/pitch_p
  • Mean diam D (mean_diam_D) = out_diam_OD - wire_diam_d

40 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
