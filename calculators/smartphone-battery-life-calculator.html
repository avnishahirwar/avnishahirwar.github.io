Formula and method

Hours = (mAh × volts ÷ 1,000) ÷ (drain mA × volts ÷ 1,000); voltage cancels, so hours = mAh ÷ mA.

Worked example

A 5,000 mAh phone at a 350 mA average drain runs about 14.3 hours of mixed use.

Frequently asked questions

Gaming uses more?

Yes; heavy gaming can draw two to four times the mixed-use average.

Standby drain?

Idle drain is far lower, often a few mA, so days of standby are normal.

Battery health?

Aged batteries lose 15-25% capacity after years of charge cycles.
