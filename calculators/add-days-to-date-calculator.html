How to use this calculator

Choose a starting date and enter a non-negative whole number of days. The result is the date after adding those calendar days; it does not count business days or public holidays.

Review the date, duration, unit and assumptions before relying on the output. These tools use browser-side arithmetic and do not fetch public holiday, payroll or named time-zone databases.

Formula and method

Target date = starting date + number of calendar days. The calculation uses the browser's local calendar date.

Understanding the result

Calendar-day estimates may differ from business-day schedules and official rules. For employment, travel or legal deadlines, verify the relevant policy and local calendar separately.

Important limitations

This calculator provides general date/time arithmetic, not a legal, payroll or employment determination. Device date settings and daylight-saving rules can affect real-world schedules.
