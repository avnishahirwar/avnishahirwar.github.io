Formula and method

Factor = new pan area ÷ original pan area.

Worked example

Scaling a 20 cm square pan recipe to a 25 cm square pan grows the area by about 1.56 times.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

Frequently asked questions

Cook time?

Bigger pans usually cook slower at the same depth.

Filled depth?

Use each pan's actual filled depth when depths differ.

Odd shapes?

Use measured area, not diameter, when the shape is unusual.
