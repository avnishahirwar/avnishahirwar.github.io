Dog Raisin Toxicity Calculator

This page records exposure amount relative to body weight only. It cannot estimate toxicity or identify a safe dose because the harmful amount is unpredictable.

How the calculation works

Exposure context = reported number of fruit pieces ÷ body weight (kg), or reported fruit mass (g) ÷ body weight (kg). Neither value is a toxicity threshold.

Merck notes that no definitive safe exposure threshold exists; Cornell advises immediate veterinary contact for any ingestion.

Worked example

For a 10 kg dog with 2 reported pieces, the exposure context is 2 ÷ 10 = 0.2 pieces/kg. This is not a toxic-dose threshold or a safe/unsafe classification.

Important limits

There is no established safe amount or reliable toxic-dose cutoff. Contact a veterinarian or animal poison-control service immediately for any grape/raisin ingestion, even if the dog appears well. Do not induce vomiting or give treatment unless directed by a veterinarian.
