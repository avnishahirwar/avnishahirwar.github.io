Formula and methodology

Customer acquisition cost (CAC) = Total attributable acquisition spend ÷ Number of new customers acquired in the same period.

Inputs are processed in your browser. Results are estimates based on the values and period definitions you enter. Keep all figures on the same accounting period and basis.

Worked example

₹1,30,000 combined acquisition spend for 100 new customers gives an estimated CAC of ₹1,300 per customer.
