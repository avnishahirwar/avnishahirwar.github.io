How to use this calculator

Enter a whole number from 0 to 170 to calculate its factorial.

Formula and method

n! = n × (n − 1) × … × 2 × 1, and 0! = 1.

Understanding the result

Factorials multiply every positive integer up to n. The value for zero is defined as one.

Important limitations

This calculator accepts whole numbers from 0 to 170. Factorials grow very quickly; the upper limit keeps the result within a practical exact-integer range for this tool.

Frequently asked questions

What is zero factorial?

By definition, 0! equals 1.

Where are factorials used?

They appear in counting, permutations, combinations and probability.

Why is there an upper limit?

Factorials grow rapidly and can exceed ordinary numeric precision.
