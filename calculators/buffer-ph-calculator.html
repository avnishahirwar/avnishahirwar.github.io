Formula

pH = pKa + log₁₀([A⁻] / [HA]).

Assumptions and limitations

The Henderson–Hasselbalch equation is an approximation for suitable buffer systems. Activity effects, temperature, ionic strength and buffer capacity can affect measured pH. This is not a substitute for a calibrated pH meter.
