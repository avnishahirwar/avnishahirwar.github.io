Formula and method

Maintenance ≈ weight(kg) × 22 + exercise bonus + step bonus.

Worked example

For 70 kg, 3 weekly training hours and 8,000 steps per day: 1,540 + 240 + 196 ≈ 1,976 kcal per day.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is this shortcut?

It is a quick planning estimate. Weigh yourself weekly and adjust intake based on real trends.

Should I eat back exercise calories?

This estimate already includes an exercise bonus, so add extras only for unusual sessions.

Does metabolism vary?

Yes, by plus or minus 10-15% between individuals at the same size.
