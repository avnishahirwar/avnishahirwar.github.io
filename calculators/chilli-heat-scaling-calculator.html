Formula and method

Heat per serving is roughly pods × average Scoville ÷ servings, ignoring extraction variability.

Worked example

Two 5,000 SHU chillies across four servings give a mild-to-medium heat of about 2,500 units.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Cooking changes heat?

Capsaicin is stable; longer cooking distributes it through the dish.

Dairy cools it?

Yes; milk fat and yoghurt bind capsaicin better than water.

Individual tolerance

This is an average; individuals vary widely in perceived heat.
