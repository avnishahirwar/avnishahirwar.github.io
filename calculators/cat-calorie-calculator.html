How the calculation works

RER = 70 × body weight (kg)^0.75. Estimated MER = RER × selected healthy-adult factor: intact 1.4, neutered 1.2, obesity-prone 1.0.

MSD Veterinary Manual gives the RER equation and category multipliers; WSAVA supplies an alternative adult-cat chart and warns values are guidance.

Worked example

For a 4 kg neutered adult cat, RER is about 198 kcal/day; applying the 1.2 factor gives about 238 kcal/day. Individual needs vary.

Important limits

Starting estimate only, not a feeding prescription or weight-loss plan. Do not use for kittens, pregnancy/lactation, illness, recovery or other special needs without veterinary guidance. Monitor body condition and consult a veterinarian.
