Concrete Tube Volume Calculator

Calculate the ideal volume for one or more straight cylindrical forms. The result is a quantity estimate only; it does not design or validate a structure or concrete mix.

Use the tube’s inside diameter. Any allowance is shown separately from the geometric volume and is not prescribed by this calculator.

Formula and method

For a cylinder, V = πr²L = π(D ÷ 2)²L. With diameter in inches and length in feet, convert the radius to feet: r = D ÷ 24. Cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27. An optional entered allowance is geometric volume × allowance percentage.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

For one tube with 12-inch inside diameter and 8-foot fill length, r = 12 ÷ 24 = 0.5 ft. V = π × 0.5² × 8 = 2π ≈ 6.283 ft³, or 6.283 ÷ 27 ≈ 0.233 yd³ before any allowance.

Scope and limitations

  • Assumes a straight circular cylinder with constant inside diameter and a completely filled length. Use inside diameter, not outside diameter.
  • Tapered, irregular, sloped, obstructed or enlarged forms are not represented; actual placed volume can differ.
  • This is not a structural design, strength, reinforcement, mix or bag-count recommendation.
  • Any overage percentage is user-supplied and is not a site-specific waste recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use the outside tube diameter?

No. The cylinder-volume estimate uses the inside diameter available for concrete.

Does the allowance tell me how much extra concrete to order?

No. It displays only the percentage you enter. Site conditions and supplier ordering practices are outside this geometric estimate.
