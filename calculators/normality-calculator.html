Normality Calculator

Calculate solution normality from solute mass, equivalent weight and final solution volume.

Enter your values

Formula

Normality (eq/L) = solute mass (g) / [equivalent weight (g/eq) × solution volume (L)].

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change inputs to compare scenarios.

Assumptions and limitations

Equivalent weight depends on the specific reaction and conditions. Select a chemically appropriate equivalent weight; this tool does not determine reaction stoichiometry.
