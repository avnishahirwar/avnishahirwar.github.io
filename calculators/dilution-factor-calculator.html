Dilution Factor Calculator

Calculate the dilution factor, stock volume and diluent volume needed to prepare a target concentration.

Enter your values

Formula

Dilution factor = stock concentration ÷ target concentration; stock volume = final volume ÷ dilution factor.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change the inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Adjust one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

Stock and target concentrations must use the same units. The calculation assumes additive volumes and that the stock concentration is known accurately.
