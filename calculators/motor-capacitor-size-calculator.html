Capacitance, stored energy and voltage

This calculator uses the ideal capacitor energy relationship. It can solve for capacitance, voltage or stored energy when the other two values are supplied.

Formula

Stored energy: E = ½CV²

Capacitance: C = 2E ÷ V²

Voltage: V = √(2E ÷ C)

Worked example

If an ideal capacitor stores 1 joule at 10 volts, its capacitance is 2 × 1 ÷ 10² = 0.02 farads (20,000 microfarads).

Important limitation

This is not a motor start/run capacitor selection tool. Motor capacitor ratings depend on the equipment design and manufacturer specifications. Do not use this simplified energy equation to choose or replace a motor component; consult the equipment documentation and a qualified technician.
