Data Usage Calculator

Estimate how much mobile or broadband data a mix of common online activities may use over a selected period.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the values that match your situation, then select Calculate. Review the assumptions and units before relying on the estimate.

Formula and methodology

Activity data = hours per day × assumed GB per hour. Total period data = daily activity totals × number of days.

Worked example

At 1.5 GB per hour, one hour of video uses an estimated 1.5 GB. Add music and browsing estimates to calculate the period total.

Important limitations

Results are estimates based only on the values and assumptions you enter. They do not replace an actual meter reading, supplier quote, employer policy or professional assessment where one is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Are the data rates exact?

No. Data use varies with resolution, app settings, compression, caching and network conditions.

Does this track my device?

No. Calculations use only the values you enter; the tool does not inspect device or account usage.
