HRA exemption formula

Exemption = least of: (1) actual HRA received; (2) rent paid − 10% of (basic + DA); (3) 50% of (basic + DA) in metro cities, 40% elsewhere
Example: HRA ₹2.4L, basic ₹6L, rent ₹3L, metro → (1) ₹2.4L, (2) ₹2.4L, (3) ₹3L → exemption ₹2.4L, taxable HRA ₹0.

Frequently asked questions

Is HRA exemption available in the new tax regime?

No — Section 10(13A) exemption applies only under the old regime.

What if I pay rent to my parents?

You can, with valid rent receipts/agreement; they must show it as their income.

Do I need landlord PAN?

Yes, if annual rent exceeds ₹1 lakh.

HRA exemption calculator guide under Indian tax rules

House Rent Allowance (HRA) may form part of a salary package. Under applicable Indian tax provisions, an eligible salaried person who pays rent and meets the required conditions may claim an exemption. The amount is not automatically equal to the HRA received; calculation depends on salary components, rent paid, location, tax regime and relevant year.

How the common HRA calculation is structured

For eligible cases under the applicable provisions, the exemption is generally the least of specified amounts: actual HRA received, rent paid minus the prescribed percentage of salary, and a prescribed percentage of salary that varies by location category. The statutory definition of salary and applicable percentages must be checked for the relevant year and circumstances.

Inputs to organise

  • Actual HRA received for the period.
  • Basic salary and any components included in the statutory salary definition.
  • Rent actually paid and months covered.
  • Location information relevant to the calculation.
  • Tax regime and financial year.

Keep supporting records

Retain rent receipts, rental agreement and payment evidence, as well as landlord details where required. A calculator cannot verify that rent was genuinely paid, that the accommodation qualifies or that statutory conditions are met.

Regime and special situations

Do not assume HRA exemption is available in the same way under every tax regime. Cases such as living in an owned house, paying rent to a relative, changing residence during the year or receiving HRA for only part of the year require review of the actual facts and current rules. Keep all inputs aligned to the same period; mixing annual rent with monthly salary can distort the result.

Limitations

This is an educational estimate, not a determination of tax eligibility. Rules, definitions and documentation requirements may change. Verify the result with official Income Tax Department guidance or a qualified tax professional before making a claim.
