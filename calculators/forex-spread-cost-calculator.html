Forex Spread Cost Calculator

See the hidden cost of a currency conversion by comparing the mid-market rate with a rate that includes the provider spread.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Cost with spread = amount × mid-market rate × (1 + spread ÷ 100). Hidden cost = cost with spread − cost at mid-market.

Worked example

With Foreign currency amount to buy 1,000, Mid-market rate 83.2, Provider spread 1.5, the calculator gives: Cost at mid-market rate = 83,200; Cost including spread = 84,448; Hidden spread cost = 1,248; Effective rate you pay = 84.45.

How to read the result

Compare providers on the effective rate, not only on the stated fee. Add any fixed transfer fee separately to the total.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find the mid-market rate?

Use a reputable market-rate source at the time of the transaction. Rates move during the day.

Do card or bank charges apply on top?

They may. Enter only the spread here and add fixed fees afterwards.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool is educational and informational. It is not tax, investment, lending, accounting, legal or professional advice. Rates, fees, taxes and product terms differ by provider and country, so confirm the figures with the relevant institution before acting.
