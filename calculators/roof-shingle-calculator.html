Roof Shingle Calculator

Estimate shingle bundles and material cost from measured roof area, package coverage and a waste allowance.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the measurements in the units shown beside each field. Review the assumptions and use the results as an initial material-planning estimate.

Formula and methodology

Required bundles = round up [roof area × (1 + waste allowance) ÷ coverage per bundle]. Estimated material cost = bundles × price per bundle.

Worked example

For 1,800 sq ft, 10% allowance and 33.3 sq ft coverage per bundle, the estimate is 60 bundles.

Important limitations

Actual bundle coverage depends on the product, shingle type, roof pitch and installation method. Check the manufacturer's current packaging and local code before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

Are the results exact purchase quantities?

No. They are estimates. Confirm product specifications, site measurements, local requirements and supplier packaging before ordering.

Can I use this for structural approval?

No. This calculator does not replace engineering design, construction drawings, building-code checks or advice from a qualified professional.
