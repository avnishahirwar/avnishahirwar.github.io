Stopping Distance Calculator

Estimate level-road braking distance for constant deceleration, plus reaction distance.

Enter values

Formula and method

dbrake = v²/(2a); dtotal = vtᵣ + dbrake. Speed is converted from km/h to m/s. Inputs must use the units stated above. This calculator performs only the displayed idealized calculation; it does not model unlisted effects.

Worked sample

At 50 km/h, 7 m/s² braking deceleration, 1 s reaction: braking ≈13.78 m; total ≈27.67 m.

Validation and limitations

Inputs are checked for finite values and physically required positive quantities. Results are browser-only and are educational estimates; verify assumptions and units before practical use. This is not an engineering or safety certification tool.
