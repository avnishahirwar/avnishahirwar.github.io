Bank Fee Impact Calculator

Estimate the annual customer or business impact of recurring account and transaction fees.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Monthly fee impact per account = monthly account fee + transactions × fee per transaction; annual total = monthly impact × accounts × 12.

Use consistent reporting periods, currency units and accounting definitions. This is a planning estimate and does not determine regulatory compliance or a customer’s approved product terms.

Worked example

12 monthly fee plus 20 transactions at 0.50 across 1,000 accounts equals 264,000 annual impact.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool is educational and informational. It is not banking, accounting, tax, investment, lending, regulatory, legal or professional advice. Banks and financial institutions should validate definitions, reporting rules, local regulation, contracts and source data before relying on a result. Do not enter confidential customer or institution information.
