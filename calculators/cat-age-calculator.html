How the calculation works

International Cat Care convention: year 1 = 15 human years; year 2 = 24; add 4 per additional cat year. Cornell convention: year 1 = 16; year 2 = 21; then add 4 per additional year.

International Cat Care and Cornell publish different approximations for early cat years; the result shows the chosen convention.

Worked example

For a 2-year-old cat, the International Cat Care convention gives about 24 human-equivalent years; Cornell’s convention gives about 21.

Important limits

Approximate educational comparison only; it does not measure biological age, health or lifespan. Ask a veterinarian to assess an individual cat’s life stage and needs.
