Raoult's Law Calculator

Estimate the vapour pressure of an ideal binary solution from solvent mole fraction and pure-solvent vapour pressure.

Enter your values

Formula

For a non-volatile solute: Psolution = Xsolvent × P°solvent, where Xsolvent = nsolvent ÷ (nsolvent + nsolute).

Assumptions and limitations

This simplified form assumes an ideal solution and a non-volatile solute. Non-ideal interactions, volatile solutes and temperature-dependent vapour pressure require a more complete model.
