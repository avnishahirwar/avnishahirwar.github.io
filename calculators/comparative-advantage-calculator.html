How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Comparative Advantage Calculator works with 8 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Comparative advantage 1x (comparative_advantage_1x) = output_2x/output_1x
  • Comparative advantage 2x (comparative_advantage_2x) = output_1x/output_2x
  • Comparative advantage 1y (comparative_advantage_1y) = output_2y/output_1y
  • Comparative advantage 2y (comparative_advantage_2y) = output_1y/output_2y
  • Output 1x (output_1x) = output_2x/comparative_advantage_1x
  • Output 2x (output_2x) = comparative_advantage_1x × output_1x
  • Output 2y (output_2y) = comparative_advantage_1y × output_1y
  • Output 1y (output_1y) = output_2y/comparative_advantage_1y

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
