Frequently asked questions

What counts as a cash inflow?

Examples include customer receipts, loan proceeds, asset-sale proceeds and owner contributions. Classify them consistently for the period being reviewed.

What counts as a cash outflow?

Examples include supplier payments, payroll, rent, taxes, debt repayments and equipment purchases. Avoid double-counting transfers between your own accounts.

Is net cash flow the same as profit?

No. Profit uses accrual accounting and may include non-cash items or credit sales. Cash flow tracks cash received and paid.

Does this separate operating, investing and financing cash flows?

No. This simplified tool totals the amounts entered and does not prepare a formal cash-flow statement.
