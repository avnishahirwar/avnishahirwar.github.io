Formulas used by this calculator

The Zulu Time Converter works with 44 quantities and 3 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Local time (local_time) = 3600 × time_zone + zulu_time
  • Zulu time (zulu_time) = local_time - 3600 × time_zone
  • Time zone (time_zone) = (1/3600) × local_time - 1/3600 × zulu_time
