Formula and methodology

Cost per transaction = (fixed cost + variable cost) ÷ completed transactions.

All inputs are processed in your browser. Keep units, dates and reporting periods consistent before comparing scenarios.

Worked example

80,000 total operating cost ÷ 4,000 transactions = 20 per transaction.

Assumptions and limitations

This is an informational planning estimate, not accounting, tax, legal, engineering, employment, service-credit or professional advice. Organisations should validate definitions, contracts, policies, rounding and source data before making a decision. Do not enter confidential personal or commercially sensitive information.
