Formula and method

Hours = target litres ÷ (rating × duty cycle ÷ 24).

Worked example

Removing 50 litres with a 20 L/day machine at 70% duty takes about 86 running hours, or 3.6 days.

Frequently asked questions

What is the rating?

Manufacturers test at 30 °C and 80% humidity; real rooms extract less.

Duty cycle?

Compressors and fans cycle on and off; 0.6-0.8 is a typical real duty.

Continuous drain?

A hose lets the machine run without tank-emptying pauses.
