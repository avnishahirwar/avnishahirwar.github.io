Ideal Body Weight (Devine) Calculator

Estimate ideal body weight with the Devine formula from height, using a base of 50 kg for men or 45.5 kg for women.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Ideal body weight = base weight + 2.3 kg × inches of height above 5 feet (60 inches).

Worked example

With Height 172, Base weight at 5 ft (kg) 50, the calculator gives: Inches above 5 feet = 7.72; Estimated ideal body weight (kg) = 67.75.

How to read the result

The Devine formula was created for drug dosing, not as a target for individuals. Healthy weight ranges are wide and depend on build, age and health.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I enter a base weight?

The formula uses 50 kg for men and 45.5 kg for women. Enter the value that applies.

Should I aim for this number?

Treat it as a rough reference only and discuss goals with a health professional.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives general informational estimates only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Individual needs vary with age, health conditions, medicines and activity, so speak with a qualified health professional before changing diet, training or medication.
