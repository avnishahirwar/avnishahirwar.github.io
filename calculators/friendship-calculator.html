Friendship Calculator

Enter two first names to generate a light-hearted friendship score. Share the result for a little fun with friends.

Calculate your fun friendship score

This score is generated from the names for entertainment. It does not assess your relationship.

Just for fun: a name-based score is not scientific and cannot tell how strong, healthy or meaningful a friendship is. Real friendships grow through trust, kindness, communication and shared experiences.

How this friendship calculator works

The tool normalizes the two names and uses a repeatable, browser-side calculation to produce a playful score from 1 to 100. Entering the same pair of names produces the same score. The result is an entertainment feature, not a psychological test, prediction or compatibility assessment.

How to use it

  1. Enter your first name.
  2. Enter your friend's first name.
  3. Select Calculate friendship score to see the playful result.
  4. Use Copy result if you want to share the text.

Privacy

The calculation runs in your browser. The names are not submitted to a server or saved by this calculator. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Frequently asked questions

Is the friendship score scientifically accurate?

No. It is a playful name-based result created for entertainment. It cannot measure trust, loyalty, personality or the quality of a friendship.

Can a low score mean we are not good friends?

No. The score has no real-world meaning. A good friendship is built through mutual respect, honesty, kindness and how people treat one another.

Are the names stored?

No. This calculator processes the entered names locally in your browser and does not send them to a server.

Will the same names give the same result?

Yes. The score is repeatable for the same pair of names, but it remains a game and not a real assessment.
