Q10 Calculator

Calculate the temperature coefficient Q10 from measured rates at two temperatures expressed in degrees Celsius.

Enter your values

Formula

Q10 = (rate₂ ÷ rate₁)^(10 ÷ (T₂ − T₁)).

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change the inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Adjust one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

This empirical coefficient assumes rates were measured under comparable conditions and over a temperature interval where the relationship is meaningful. It is not a universal constant.
