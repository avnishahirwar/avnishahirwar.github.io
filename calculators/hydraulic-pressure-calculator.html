Formulas used by this calculator

The Hydraulic Pressure Calculator works with 8 quantities and 16 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • First distance (first_distance) = work_done/first_force
  • Total pressure (total_pressure) = first_force/first_area
  • Second distance (second_distance) = work_done/second_force
  • First force (first_force) = work_done/first_distance
  • Second force (second_force) = work_done/second_distance
  • First area (first_area) = first_force/total_pressure
  • Work done (work_done) = first_distance × first_force
  • Second area (second_area) = second_force/total_pressure

8 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
