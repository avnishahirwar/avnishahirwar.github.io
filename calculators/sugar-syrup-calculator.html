Formula and method

Stage ratios: light thread 1:1, soft ball 1:2.2, hard ball 1:3, water to sugar by weight.

Worked example

A 500 g batch of light (1:1) syrup uses about 500 g of sugar boiled to 110 °C.

Frequently asked questions

Use a thermometer

Visual cues are unreliable in humid kitchens.

What stages for what?

Light syrups for drinks, soft ball for fudge, hard ball for praline.

Crystallisation?

Brush the pan sides with water to prevent early sugar crystals.
