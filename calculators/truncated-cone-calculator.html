Formulas used by this calculator

The Truncated Cone Calculator works with 9 quantities and 20 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Top Area (topArea) = PI × pow(radiusTop, 2)
  • Bottom Area (bottomArea) = PI × pow(radiusBase, 2)
  • Radius Top (radiusTop) = sqrt(topArea)/sqrt(PI)
  • Radius Base (radiusBase) = sqrt(bottomArea)/sqrt(PI)
  • Lateral Area (lateralArea) = PI × slantHeight × (radiusBase + radiusTop)
  • Slant Height (slantHeight) = lateralArea/(PI × (radiusBase + radiusTop))
  • Total Area (totalArea) = lateralArea + PI × pow(radiusBase, 2) + PI × pow(radiusTop, 2)
  • Cone Volume (coneVolume) = (1/3) × PI × height × (pow(radiusBase, 2) + radiusBase × radiusTop + pow(radiusTop, 2))

12 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
