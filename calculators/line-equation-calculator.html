Line Equation Calculator

Calculate the slope and equation of a line through two coordinate points.

Enter values and select Calculate.

Formula and method

Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); slope-intercept form: y = mx + c, where c = y₁ − mx₁.

Limitations

This browser-based calculator uses the stated formula and the values you enter. It is intended for learning and general estimation; check units, signs and assumptions before using a result in coursework or technical work.
