Formula and methodology

Percentage = (points earned ÷ points possible) × 100. The letter grade uses a simple configurable top threshold and descending 10-percentage-point bands; it is not an official institutional grading scale.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values requested in the form.
  2. Check the units and assumptions before calculating.
  3. Review the result and use it as an estimate, not a substitute for professional advice.

Calculations run locally in your browser. Do not enter passwords, account numbers or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Does every school use the same grade scale?

No. Schools, colleges and universities may use different cutoffs, weighting and rounding policies.

Can I use weighted assignments?

Combine your weighted scores into total points earned and total points possible before entering them.
