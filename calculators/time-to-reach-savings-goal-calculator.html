Time to Reach Savings Goal Calculator

Estimate how many months it takes to reach a savings target from a starting balance, monthly deposits and an assumed return.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Months n = ln((goal + P ÷ i) ÷ (start + P ÷ i)) ÷ ln(1 + i), where P is the monthly deposit and i the monthly return.

Worked example

With Savings goal 10,00,000, Current savings 1,00,000, Monthly deposit 15,000, Annual return 7, the calculator gives: Months to reach goal = 49.92; Years to reach goal = 4.16; Total deposits made = 7,48,795.73; Growth from returns = 1,51,204.27.

How to read the result

Raising the monthly deposit usually shortens the timeline more reliably than hoping for a higher return. If the goal is already met, the result is zero months.

Frequently asked questions

Does inflation change the answer?

Yes. If the goal is in today’s money, use a return after inflation or increase the target each year.

Is the return guaranteed?

No. Use a conservative rate and recalculate periodically.
