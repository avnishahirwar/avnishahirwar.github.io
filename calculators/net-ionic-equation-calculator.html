Formula / method

Cancel only identical aqueous species appearing on both sides, subtracting their coefficients.

How to use

Enter the complete ionic equation with species separated by + and an arrow (→ or ->), e.g. Ag+(aq) + NO3-(aq) + Na+(aq) + Cl-(aq) -> AgCl(s) + Na+(aq) + NO3-(aq).

Assumptions and limitations

This tool does not predict products or balance atoms/charge. Enter already-balanced complete ionic equations and use identical species notation on both sides. Weak electrolytes, solids, liquids and gases should remain molecular.

Inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to Friendly Calculator by this page.
