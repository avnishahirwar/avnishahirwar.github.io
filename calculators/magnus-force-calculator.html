How it works

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Magnus Force Calculator works with 10 quantities and 15 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Density (density) = fluid
  • Fluid (fluid) = density
  • Angular velocity (angular_velocity) = 2 × PI × rate_of_rotation
  • Rate of rotation (rate_of_rotation) = (1/2) × angular_velocity/PI
  • Vortex strength (vortex_strength) = 2 × PI × radius × rotational_speed
  • Rotational speed (rotational_speed) = 2 × PI × radius × rate_of_rotation
  • Radius (radius) = (1/2) × vortex_strength/(PI × rotational_speed)
  • Free stream velocity (free_stream_velocity) = magnus_force/(density × length × vortex_strength)

7 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
