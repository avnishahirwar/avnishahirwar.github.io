How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Grass Seed Calculator works with 17 quantities and 23 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Area rectangle (area_rectangle) = length × width
  • Area elipsis (area_elipsis) = PI × a_axis × b_axis
  • Area circle (area_circle) = PI × pow(radius, 2)
  • Area triangle (area_triangle) = (1/2) × base × height
  • Length (length) = area_rectangle/width
  • Height (height) = 2 × area_triangle/base
  • Width (width) = area_rectangle/length
  • Base (base) = 2 × area_triangle/height

15 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
