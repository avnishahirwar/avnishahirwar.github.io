How it works

Formulas used by this calculator

The Post Hole Concrete Calculator works with 10 quantities and 13 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Concrete for round post (concrete_for_round_post) = 2 × PI × post_depth × pow(post_diameter, 2)
  • Concrete for cuboid post (concrete_for_cuboid_post) = 8 × post_depth × post_thickness × post_width
  • Total concrete for round post (total_concrete_for_round_post) = concrete_for_round_post × number_of_posts
  • Total concrete for cuboid post (total_concrete_for_cuboid_post) = concrete_for_cuboid_post × number_of_posts
  • Number of posts (number_of_posts) = total_concrete_for_round_post/concrete_for_round_post
  • Post thickness (post_thickness) = (1/8) × concrete_for_cuboid_post/(post_depth × post_width)
  • Post depth (post_depth) = (1/2) × concrete_for_round_post/(PI × pow(post_diameter, 2))
  • Post width (post_width) = (1/8) × concrete_for_cuboid_post/(post_depth × post_thickness)

5 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
