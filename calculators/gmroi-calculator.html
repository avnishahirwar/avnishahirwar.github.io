GMROI Calculator

Estimate GMROI for one consistent accounting period using gross margin dollars and average inventory at cost.

Calculator

All four monetary inputs should use the same currency and accounting period.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula

Gross margin dollars = net sales − cost of goods sold (COGS). Simple average inventory at cost = (beginning inventory + ending inventory) ÷ 2. GMROI = gross margin dollars ÷ average inventory at cost. Multiply by 100 to express it as a percentage.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

Net sales $100,000, COGS $60,000, beginning inventory $20,000 and ending inventory $30,000: gross margin = $40,000; average inventory = $25,000; GMROI = 40,000 ÷ 25,000 = 1.60 (160%).

Assumptions and limitations

  • Use net sales, COGS and inventory measured on a consistent accounting basis and for the same period.
  • Beginning/ending average is a simplified estimate; seasonal businesses may need monthly or more frequent inventory averages.
  • GMROI is gross-margin dollars per inventory dollar, not net profit, cash return, or a standalone investment recommendation. Comparisons depend on sector and accounting practice.
