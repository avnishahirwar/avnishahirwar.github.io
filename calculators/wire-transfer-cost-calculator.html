Wire Transfer Cost Calculator

Estimate the total cost and recipient amount for a transfer with fees and foreign-exchange spread.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

FX spread cost = amount sent × spread ÷ 100; total sender cost = transfer fee + FX spread cost; indicative received amount = sent − spread cost.

Use consistent reporting periods, currency units and accounting definitions. This is a planning estimate and does not determine regulatory compliance or a customer’s approved product terms.

Worked example

A 1,000 transfer with a 25 fee and 1.5% spread has an estimated total cost of 40.
