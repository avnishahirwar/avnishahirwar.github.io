Inflation-Adjusted Salary Calculator

See what salary you would need in future to keep the same purchasing power as your salary today.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Required future salary = current salary × (1 + inflation ÷ 100)^years.

Worked example

With Current annual salary 6,00,000, Expected inflation 6, Years ahead 10, the calculator gives: Salary needed to match today’s purchasing power = 10,74,508.62; Increase needed = 4,74,508.62; Average rise needed per year (%) = 6.

How to read the result

Any pay rise below inflation is a real pay cut. Use the result as a floor when negotiating or planning long-term income.

Frequently asked questions

Which inflation rate should I use?

Use a figure that reflects your own spending pattern. Official headline inflation may differ from your personal inflation.

Does this include promotions?

No. It only maintains purchasing power and does not model real career growth.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool is educational and informational. It is not tax, investment, lending, accounting, legal or professional advice. Rates, fees, taxes and product terms differ by provider and country, so confirm the figures with the relevant institution before acting.
