Hole Volume Calculator

The hole volume calculator finds the volume of a circular or rectangular hole. It also calculates the volume of concrete needed for concrete holes, considering the loss in the concreting process.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Hole Volume Calculator works with 12 quantities and 17 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Diameter (Diameter) = 2 × hole_radius
  • Hole radius (hole_radius) = (1/2) × Diameter
  • Hole volume cylinder (hole_volume_cylinder) = PI × hole_depth × pow(hole_radius, 2)
  • Hole volume rectangle (hole_volume_rectangle) = hole_depth_rectangle × hole_length × hole_width
  • Hole depth (hole_depth) = hole_volume_cylinder/(PI × pow(hole_radius, 2))
  • Hole depth rectangle (hole_depth_rectangle) = hole_volume_rectangle/(hole_length × hole_width)
  • Concrete volume cylinder (concrete_volume_cylinder) = hole_volume_cylinder × quantity_of_posts × (waste + 1)
  • Concrete volume rectangle (concrete_volume_rectangle) = hole_volume_rectangle × quantity_of_posts × (waste + 1)

9 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
