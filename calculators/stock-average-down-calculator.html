Stock Average Down Calculator

Find your new average cost per share after a second purchase and the price needed to get back to break-even.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Average cost = (shares1 × price1 + shares2 × price2) ÷ (shares1 + shares2).

Worked example

With Shares bought first 100, First purchase price 250, Shares bought second 50, Second purchase price 200, the calculator gives: Average cost per share = 233.33; Total invested = 35,000; Total shares held = 150; Fall in break-even versus first price (%) = 6.67.

How to read the result

Averaging down lowers the break-even price but also increases the money exposed to one share. Decide the maximum position size before buying more.

Frequently asked questions

Do brokerage fees matter?

Yes. Add fees to the invested amount for a precise average cost.

Is averaging down a good strategy?

Only if your reason to own the share is unchanged. Buying more of a falling share can increase losses.
