Fresnel Zone Calculator

The cool Fresnel zone calculator helps you determine the first Fresnel zone so you can keep your signal transmission as strong as possible. It also determines the other Fresnel zones, if you need them.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Fresnel Zone Calculator works with 14 quantities and 26 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • d1 (d1) = D - d2
  • d2 (d2) = D - d1
  • D (D) = d1 + d2
  • Rmax (rmax) = (433/50) × sqrt(D/frequency)
  • Frequency (frequency) = (187489/2500) × D/pow(rmax, 2)
  • Min req antenna height (min_req_antenna_height) = obstruction_height + (3/5) × radius
  • Obstruction height (obstruction_height) = min_req_antenna_height - 3/5 × radius
  • Number Zones (numberZones) = (250/18737) × frequency × pow(rnmax, 2)/D

18 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
