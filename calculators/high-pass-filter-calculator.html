High Pass Filter Calculator

The high pass filter calculator assists you in designing and building a high-pass filter circuit. Includes passive (RC and RL) and active (op-amp based) filters.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The High Pass Filter Calculator works with 22 quantities and 18 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Resistance inv feedback (Resistance_inv_feedback) = -Resistance_inv_input × gain_inv
  • Resistance rl (Resistance_rl) = 2 × PI × Frequency_rl × Inductance_rl
  • Resistance inv input (Resistance_inv_input) = -Resistance_inv_feedback/gain_inv
  • Capacitance rc (Capacitance_rc) = (1/2)/(PI × Frequency_rc × Resistance_rc)
  • Inductance rl (Inductance_rl) = (1/2) × Resistance_rl/(PI × Frequency_rl)
  • Resistance rc (Resistance_rc) = (1/2)/(PI × Capacitance_rc × Frequency_rc)
  • Frequency rl (Frequency_rl) = (1/2) × Resistance_rl/(PI × Inductance_rl)
  • Resistance ninv feedback (Resistance_ninv_feedback) = Ground_resistance_ninv × (gain_ninv - 1)

10 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
