Paper Weight Calculator

Estimate the total mass and area of a paper order using sheet size, paper grammage and quantity.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the values that match your situation, then select Calculate. Review the assumptions and units before relying on the estimate.

Formula and methodology

Area per sheet (m²) = width (cm) × height (cm) ÷ 10,000. Sheet mass (g) = area × GSM. Total mass = sheet mass × sheet count.

Worked example

An A4-sized sheet (21 × 29.7 cm) at 80 GSM weighs about 4.99 g. A batch of 500 sheets weighs about 2.49 kg.

Important limitations

Results are estimates based only on the values and assumptions you enter. They do not replace an actual meter reading, supplier quote, employer policy or professional assessment where one is needed.

Frequently asked questions

What does GSM mean?

GSM means grams per square metre, a measure of paper grammage.

Does this include packaging?

No. It estimates paper only; packaging, moisture and manufacturing tolerances are excluded.
