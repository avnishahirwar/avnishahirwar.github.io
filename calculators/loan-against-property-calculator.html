Loan Against Property Calculator

Estimate monthly EMI, total repayment and interest for a loan secured against property.

How to use this calculator

Enter the expected loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure. This calculator does not estimate property valuation, eligible loan-to-value, legal checks, processing fees or foreclosure terms.

Enter figures in the same currency and time period, check the assumptions and use the result as an estimate. The calculator runs in your browser and does not verify a lender offer, bank statement or investment product.

Formula and calculation method

Monthly EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1). P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Understanding the result

Compare both the periodic payment and the total amount paid. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest. Fees, insurance, taxes, rate resets, repayment dates and provider-specific terms may affect the actual amount.

Important limitations

This page provides general arithmetic estimates, not personalised financial, tax, credit or investment advice. It does not recommend a loan or investment product, guarantee eligibility or returns, or fetch live rates. Confirm current terms with the relevant regulated provider and read the official agreement before making a decision.
