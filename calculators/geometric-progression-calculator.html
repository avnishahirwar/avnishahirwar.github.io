Understanding the result

A geometric progression is a sequence in which each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by the same ratio. The ratio may be positive, zero or negative.

Important limitations

This calculator accepts 1 to 1,000 terms. Very large values can overflow ordinary numeric precision; results are estimates using JavaScript number arithmetic.

Frequently asked questions

What is a geometric progression?

It is a sequence where each term is obtained by multiplying the previous term by a constant ratio.

What if the ratio is one?

Every term equals the first term, so the sum is n times the first term.

Can the ratio be negative or zero?

Yes. The formula works for negative and zero ratios as well.
