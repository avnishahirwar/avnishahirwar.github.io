Formula and assumptions

FD estimate = principal × (1 + annual rate ÷ 4)4 × years. RD and SIP estimates use the future value of end-of-month contributions: monthly deposit × (((1 + monthly rate)number of months − 1) ÷ monthly rate). If the monthly rate is zero, the result equals total contributions.

FD/RD rates are user-entered assumptions. SIP is a contribution method for market-linked investments, not a guaranteed-return product. Taxes, bank-specific deposit dates, premature-closure rules, fees and actual market performance can change outcomes. This comparison is not investment advice.
