Pipe Flow Rate Calculator

Calculate volume flow rate from pipe inner diameter and flow velocity.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Flow area A = π ÷ 4 × d². Flow rate Q = A × velocity.

Worked example

With Inner diameter 100, Flow velocity 2, the calculator gives: Cross-sectional area (m²) = 0.0079; Flow rate (m³/s) = 0.0157; Flow rate (litres per second) = 15.708; Flow rate (m³/hour) = 56.5487.

How to read the result

Water in building pipes is often designed for velocities around 1 to 3 m/s to limit noise and wear. Friction loss and pressure are not calculated here.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include pressure drop?

No. Pressure loss depends on length, roughness and fittings and needs a separate calculation.

Should I use inner or outer diameter?

Use the inner diameter, because it sets the actual flow area.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
