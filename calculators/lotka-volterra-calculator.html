Compute one Euler time step for the classic predator–prey model.

Formula and method

dN/dt = αN − βNP; dP/dt = δβNP − γP; Euler: state(next)=state+dt×rate

Worked sample

N=40, P=9, α=.1, β=.01, δ=.01, γ=.1, dt=.1 → N≈39.24, P≈8.676

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Deterministic educational model; coefficients and discrete step strongly affect results. Not a field prediction. Keep dt small and populations nonnegative.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
