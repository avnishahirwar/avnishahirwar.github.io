Passive House Savings Calculator

Estimate annual energy cost savings from a reduction in heating/cooling energy use.

Enter values

Formula and method

Baseline energy use × fractional reduction × energy price

Worked sample

10,000 kWh × 0.5 × $0.20 = $1,000/year

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Illustrative arithmetic only. Actual savings depend on climate, occupancy, building, tariffs and retrofit costs; not a guarantee or investment advice.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
