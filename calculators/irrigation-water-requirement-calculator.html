Irrigation Water Requirement Calculator

Estimate gross irrigation water volume from crop water depth, field area, irrigation duration and application efficiency.

Enter your values

Formula and method

Net water (m³) = field area (ha) × water depth (mm) × days × 10. Gross irrigation water = net water ÷ application efficiency as a decimal.

Worked example

For 2 hectares requiring 5 mm/day for 10 days at 80% efficiency, net demand is 1,000 m³ and gross water to apply is 1,250 m³.

Assumptions and limitations

This treats entered crop water requirement as a net depth for each day. It does not estimate evapotranspiration, rainfall, soil moisture, crop coefficients, water rights or pump capacity. Confirm agronomic assumptions locally.

Frequently asked questions

Why is gross water higher than net crop demand?

Application and conveyance losses mean more water may need to be applied than the crop's net requirement.

Does this determine an irrigation schedule?

No. Use local weather, soil, crop stage, rainfall and irrigation-system data to plan timing and frequency.
