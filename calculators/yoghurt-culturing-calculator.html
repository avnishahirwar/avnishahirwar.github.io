Formula and method

Starter at about 2% of milk weight; incubate near 43 °C for 4-8 hours until set.

Worked example

A 1-litre batch uses about 20 g of starter held at 43 °C for roughly 6 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Too sour?

Shorter incubation or less starter gives a milder taste.

Thicker yoghurt

Strain the set yoghurt through muslin to make Greek-style yoghurt.

Ultra-pasteurised milk?

It works well and often sets firmer without pre-boiling.
