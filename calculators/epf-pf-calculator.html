EPF calculator guide: employee and employer contributions

The Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a retirement savings arrangement governed by India's EPF framework. This calculator provides an approximate view of how eligible contributions and assumed interest may build over time. Actual account credits depend on wages, statutory rules, contribution allocation, service history and rates notified for the relevant period.

Inputs that affect an EPF estimate

A projection may use eligible monthly wages, employee and employer contribution assumptions, an annual interest assumption and the service period. The employer's statutory contribution is not necessarily credited entirely to the EPF balance; a portion may be allocated to the pension component under applicable rules. Check the inputs and displayed assumptions to understand what the result includes.

How compounding affects the balance

Each eligible contribution adds to the accumulated balance. Interest is credited under EPF rules and the notified rate. A simplified calculator may model monthly contributions and periodic compounding, while the official passbook reflects actual credits and adjustments. Use the passbook for account reconciliation.

Verify these details

  • UAN-linked passbook entries and employer deposits.
  • Wage components used for contributions.
  • Current notified interest rate and crediting rules.
  • Employer contribution allocation, including pension amounts.
  • Transfer, withdrawal, nomination and service history.

Why actual balances can differ

Differences may result from salary revisions, joining or exit dates, contribution ceilings, non-contributory periods, delayed deposits, transfer balances, withdrawals and interest-credit timing. This calculator does not access EPFO records or verify employer compliance. Use it to compare planning scenarios, then check your official passbook and EPFO guidance for account-specific decisions.
