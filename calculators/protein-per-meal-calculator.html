Formula and method

Daily grams = weight × g/kg; per meal = daily ÷ number of meals.

Worked example

A 70 kg adult at 1.6 g/kg needs 112 g a day, about 37 g per meal across three meals.

How to use the result

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Muscle synthesis window?

Roughly 20-40 g per meal supports muscle protein synthesis in most adults.

Which g/kg?

0.8 g/kg is the general RDA; 1.2-2.2 g/kg suits active people.

Supplements needed?

Whole food usually suffices; shakes are convenience, not necessity.
