Formula and step-by-step calculation

The screening follows the broad landholding-family basis and listed exclusion categories. It does not access beneficiary records or issue an official eligibility decision.

  1. Check whether the applicant is part of a landholding farmer family.
  2. Review institutional landholder status and the listed higher-income exclusion categories.
  3. Treat land acquired after 1 February 2019 as a flag for official review, not an automatic decision by this tool.
  4. Confirm beneficiary status and mandatory eKYC on the official PM-KISAN portal.

The tool explains the arithmetic used; it does not access private bank, employer, land-record or government-beneficiary databases.
