DIY Shed Cost Calculator

Use our DIY shed cost calculator to estimate the cost of building a DIY shed. It'll help you decide on its size, roof type, and overhang length, and find the span of the roof rafter required.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The DIY Shed Cost Calculator works with 18 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Floor Area (floorArea) = (shedLength × shedWidth)
  • Cost Slab (costSlab) = (priceSlab × (shedLength × shedWidth))
  • Overhang Rise (overhangRise) = overhang × roofRise × (ceil(shedType - 1/10) + 1) × ceil(shedType)/shedWidth
  • Shed Width (shedWidth) = overhang × roofRise × (ceil(shedType - 1/10) + 1) × ceil(shedType)/overhangRise
  • Overhang (overhang) = overhangRise × shedWidth/(roofRise × (ceil(shedType - 1/10) + 1) × ceil(shedType))
  • Roof Rise (roofRise) = overhangRise × shedWidth/(overhang × (ceil(shedType - 1/10) + 1) × ceil(shedType))
  • Wall Area (wallArea) = (roofRise × shedWidth × ceil(shedType) + 2 × shedLength × wallHeight + 2 × shedWidth × wallHeight)
  • Cost Wall (costWall) = (priceWall × (roofRise × shedWidth × ceil(shedType) + 2 × shedLength × wallHeight + 2 × shedWidth × wallHeight))

4 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
