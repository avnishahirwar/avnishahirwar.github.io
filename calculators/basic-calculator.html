Basic Calculator

A real, interactive calculator for everyday arithmetic. Use the on-screen keypad or your keyboard to add, subtract, multiply, divide and calculate percentages. It works in your browser without an account.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter a number using the keypad or your keyboard.
  2. Choose an arithmetic operator.
  3. Enter the next number and press equals.
  4. Use AC to reset or Backspace to correct the current number.

Supported calculations

  • Addition (+), subtraction (−), multiplication (×) and division (÷).
  • Decimal numbers and positive/negative values.
  • Percentage conversion. For example, 25% becomes 0.25.
  • Keyboard and touch-screen input.

Basic arithmetic formulas

Addition: a + b
Subtraction: a − b
Multiplication: a × b
Division: a ÷ b (b must not be zero)

What the basic calculator does

It handles addition, subtraction, multiplication and division with an on-screen keypad and keyboard support. It also has a percent key, a sign toggle (±), backspace and clear. It is meant for quick everyday sums, bills and shopping, not for programming or scientific work.

Using the percent key

The % key converts the number on the display into hundredths: 10 becomes 0.1 and 5 becomes 0.05. To find 5% of 500, enter 500 × 5 % = and the answer is 25. To add 10% to 200, first find the 10% (200 × 10 % = 20) and then add it, or multiply 200 by 1.1. For discounts and GST, the dedicated calculators on this site show the working with labels.

Keyboard shortcuts

Click or tap the calculator first so it has focus, then use the number keys, + − * /, the decimal point and Enter for equals; Backspace removes the last digit and Escape clears everything. On a phone, use the keypad directly. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Worked example

A grocery bill of ₹1,249.50 + ₹386.25 + ₹92.75 totals ₹1,728.50. To split it among 4 people, divide by 4 to get ₹432.125, which you can round to ₹432.13. To work out a 5% tip, enter 1728.50 × 5 % to get ₹86.425, then add it to the bill for ₹1,814.925.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Pressing % on its own when you wanted a percentage of another number; use × then the number then %.
  • Chaining operations without checking the order of evaluation; the calculator works left to right, one step at a time.
  • Using the basic calculator for tax or loan problems that need dedicated formulas.
  • Forgetting to clear before starting a new calculation.

Frequently asked questions about the basic calculator

Does it follow the order of operations?

It works step by step like a simple desk calculator. To control the order, calculate the parts separately and combine them.

How many digits can it handle?

It uses standard double-precision numbers, which are accurate to about 15 digits. Very large or very precise values may show small rounding differences.

Why does 0.1 + 0.2 show a tiny difference?

Computers store decimals in binary, which cannot represent some values exactly. The calculator rounds the display to hide it.

Is my history saved?

Only the current expression is shown above the display. Nothing is stored or uploaded.

Where can I find loan, GST or tax calculators?

All of them are on the home page of this site, each with its own guide.

Accuracy and limitations

Results use JavaScript number arithmetic and are displayed with practical precision. The calculator is intended for everyday arithmetic. For tax, lending, investment, accounting or regulated decisions, verify figures with the relevant official source or a qualified professional.
