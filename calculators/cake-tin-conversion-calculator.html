Formula and method

Factor = new tin area ÷ original tin area, using circle areas (diameter squared).

Worked example

Going from a 20 cm tin to a 24 cm tin scales the batter by about 1.44 times.

Frequently asked questions

Square tin?

The formula applies to any shape; compute areas the same way.

Baking time?

Larger volumes bake longer; check with a skewer.

Shallower tin?

It bakes faster; reduce the time and test early.
