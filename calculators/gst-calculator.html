Formula and calculation method

GST on base amount = amount × rate ÷ 100. Total = amount + GST. For an inclusive price, taxable value = inclusive amount ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100); GST = inclusive amount − taxable value.

Worked example

For a taxable value of ₹10,000 at 18%, GST is ₹1,800 and the invoice total is ₹11,800. For an intra-state supply, the illustration splits this into CGST ₹900 and SGST ₹900; for an inter-state supply, it shows IGST ₹1,800. If ₹11,800 is already inclusive of 18% GST, the taxable value is ₹10,000 and the embedded GST is ₹1,800.

Important information

GST is generally split into CGST and SGST for intra-state supplies, while IGST generally applies to inter-state supplies. This tool shows combined GST. Select a rate only after checking official classification guidance.

GST calculation questions

How do I add GST to a price before tax?

Multiply the taxable value by the GST rate and divide by 100. Add that tax amount to the taxable value to get the price including GST. For ₹10,000 at 18%, GST is ₹1,800 and the total is ₹11,800.

How do I remove GST from a GST-inclusive price?

Divide the inclusive price by 1 plus the GST rate expressed as a decimal. At 18%, divide ₹11,800 by 1.18 to get a taxable value of ₹10,000; the included GST is ₹1,800.

When are CGST and SGST shown instead of IGST?

For an intra-state supply, GST is generally split into CGST and SGST. For an inter-state supply, IGST generally applies. The correct treatment depends on place-of-supply rules and the transaction facts; this calculator does not determine legal classification.

Does this calculator identify the correct GST rate for a product?

No. Enter the rate after checking the current official classification and applicable notification. The tool performs arithmetic only and does not classify goods or services or validate an invoice.

Practical GST example: inclusive and exclusive prices

GST calculation guide for Indian invoices

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax applied to taxable supplies under India's GST framework. This calculator handles the arithmetic of adding tax to a pre-tax price or extracting tax from a tax-inclusive amount. It does not determine the correct HSN/SAC classification, rate, place of supply or input-tax-credit eligibility; those depend on the transaction and current rules.

Adding GST to a base amount

GST amount = taxable value × rate ÷ 100. For an illustrative taxable value of ₹10,000 at 18%, GST is ₹1,800 and the total is ₹11,800 before other adjustments. Depending on the place of supply, tax may be split into CGST and SGST or charged as IGST. Percentage arithmetic alone cannot decide which treatment applies.

Extracting GST from an inclusive price

Taxable value = inclusive amount ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). At an assumed rate of 18%, ₹11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000; the included GST is ₹1,800. Do not calculate 18% of ₹11,800 to find the included tax, because that uses the wrong base.

Checklist before relying on the result

  • Verify the current rate and product or service classification.
  • Confirm whether the displayed amount includes GST.
  • Check place of supply and invoice-specific treatment.
  • Review discounts, exemptions, credit notes and input tax credit separately.

Common mistakes and use cases

Consumers can understand the tax component of a quoted price; sellers can estimate tax-inclusive prices; and students can practise percentages. Frequent errors include applying the rate to the wrong base, treating all supplies as having the same rate, and assuming a calculator validates an invoice. Keep all inputs on a consistent tax basis and verify the final treatment with current official GST guidance or a qualified tax professional.

Limits of this tool

This page performs arithmetic only. It does not classify goods or services, prepare returns, verify registration status or determine legal compliance. Use the result as a cross-check, not as a substitute for accounting records or professional review.

Results are estimates based on entered values and assumptions. They are not professional advice. Verify important decisions with the relevant provider or a qualified professional.
