Cigarette Butts Cleanup Calculator

Estimate cleanup effort and collected mass from observed litter.

Enter values

Formula and method

Butts ÷ collection rate = minutes; butts × mass per butt = mass

Worked sample

200 butts × 0.3 g = 60 g

Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite, nonnegative values; fraction inputs should be in the range 0–1; invalid fractions are rejected. Mass is approximate; do not handle litter without suitable protection. This does not assess toxicological exposure or disposal requirements.

All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is sent to a server. Results are estimates, not a substitute for measurement or professional assessment.
