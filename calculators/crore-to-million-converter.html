Crore to Million Converter

Convert crore amounts to millions without changing the underlying quantity or currency. This tool converts number scales; it does not exchange currencies.

Calculator

The conversion uses 1 crore = 10 million. Any currency or physical unit attached to the amount remains unchanged.

Enter values and calculate.

Formula and method

million = crore × 10. One crore is 10,000,000; one million is 1,000,000, so 10,000,000 ÷ 1,000,000 = 10.

The calculation runs in your browser. Review the inputs, units and assumptions before relying on the result.

Worked example

3.5 crore × 10 = 35 million. Check: 3.5 × 10,000,000 = 35,000,000, which is 35 × 1,000,000.

Scope and limitations

  • This is a number-scale conversion, not a currency exchange or a conversion between physical units.
  • The meaning assumes the Indian-English crore (10⁷) and the international short-scale million (10⁶).
  • Large and fractional values are calculated with JavaScript floating-point numbers, so extremely precise decimal inputs may display rounding.

Frequently asked questions

How many millions are in one crore?

One crore equals ten million because 10,000,000 divided by 1,000,000 is 10.

Does this convert rupees to another currency?

No. If the input is ₹3.5 crore, the output is ₹35 million; the currency remains rupees.
