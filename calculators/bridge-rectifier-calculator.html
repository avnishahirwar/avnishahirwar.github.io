Bridge Rectifier Calculator

You can use this bridge rectifier calculator to compute various rectification attributes of the conversion of AC (alternating current) into DC (direct current).

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Bridge Rectifier Calculator works with 10 quantities and 14 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Voltage dc (voltage_dc) = R_L × current_dc
  • Current dc (current_dc) = voltage_dc/R_L
  • Voltage peak (voltage_peak) = (1/2) × PI × voltage_dc
  • Current max (current_max) = sqrt(2) × rms_current
  • R L (R_L) = voltage_dc/current_dc
  • Voltage max (voltage_max) = current_max × (R_L + 2 × R_f)
  • Rms current (rms_current) = (1/2) × sqrt(2) × current_max
  • Ripple factor (ripple_factor) = sqrt(-1 + pow(rms_current, 2)/pow(current_dc, 2))

6 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
