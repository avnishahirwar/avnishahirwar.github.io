How this calculator works

This tool applies a transparent mathematical estimate to the values entered above. Review the labels, units and result before relying on it. Where an interest rate, fee, tax rate, eligibility limit or scheme term is requested, enter the value that applies to your own situation and confirm it with the relevant official provider.

The calculator does not connect to banks, brokers, government portals or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, tax classification, exemptions or compliance obligations. It excludes charges and adjustments unless a field explicitly asks for them.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • All figures are based on user-entered assumptions and may differ from actual provider calculations.
  • Rounding, payment dates, compounding, fees and applicable law can affect real outcomes.
  • For tax and government-scheme matters, verify current rules and official notifications before acting.
  • This tool is educational and is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Worked bonus example

Assume the salary basis entered is ₹30,000 and the bonus percentage is 8.33%. The calculator applies the percentage directly: ₹30,000 × 8.33% = ₹2,499 estimated bonus. This is a percentage-based illustration only. It does not determine statutory bonus eligibility, the legally applicable salary base, minimum or maximum limits, days worked, set-on/set-off, deductions or tax treatment. Check the applicable rules and employer policy separately.

Bonus-calculation questions

Which salary amount should I enter?

Enter the salary basis specified by the bonus policy or the scenario you want to estimate. This tool does not decide which salary components are legally included for statutory bonus calculations.

Does the percentage automatically match India's statutory bonus rules?

No. The percentage is user-entered and the calculator simply multiplies it by the salary basis. It does not test eligibility, statutory minimums or maximums, wage ceilings or other legal conditions.

Does this estimate include deductions or tax?

No. The displayed amount is a gross percentage calculation before any deductions, withholding or tax treatment.

Can I use this for a performance incentive?

Yes, as a basic arithmetic estimate if the incentive is defined as a percentage of the entered salary basis. Confirm the actual calculation period, eligible pay components and employer policy before relying on it.
