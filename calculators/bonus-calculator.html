Frequently asked questions

How is the bonus estimated?

The salary basis is multiplied by the bonus percentage and divided by 100.

Does this confirm statutory bonus eligibility?

No. Eligibility and statutory limits depend on current law and employment details.

Can I change the percentage?

Yes. Enter the percentage that applies under the policy you are reviewing.
