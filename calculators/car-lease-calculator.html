How it works

Formulas used by this calculator

The Car Lease Calculator works with 20 quantities and 38 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Negotiated price (negotiated_price) = msrp - negotiation_ded
  • Negotiation ded (negotiation_ded) = msrp - negotiated_price
  • Residual factor (residual_factor) = 100 × residual_value/msrp
  • Residual value (residual_value) = (1/100) × msrp × residual_factor
  • Msrp (msrp) = negotiated_price + negotiation_ded
  • Depreciation value (depreciation_value) = -monthly_interest + monthly_pay_notax
  • Monthly pay notax (monthly_pay_notax) = depreciation_value + monthly_interest
  • Monthly interest (monthly_interest) = -depreciation_value + monthly_pay_notax

30 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
