How the calculation works

Estimated total = (fence length × installed cost rate per selected length unit + gate count × separate cost per gate + other project costs) × (1 + contingency percentage ÷ 100).

Missouri Extension provides a customizable fence-budget workbook and states that costs vary with project design and conditions. Iowa State Extension and USDA NRCS likewise describe fence type, layout, livestock purpose, gates, materials, and labor as project-specific.

Worked example

For 100 m at a user-entered rate of 10 currency units per metre, two separately priced gates at 50 each, and no other costs or contingency, the estimate is 100 × 10 + 2 × 50 = 1,100 currency units. These are illustrative arithmetic inputs, not a price recommendation.

Important limits

This is arithmetic from user-entered costs, not a market-price forecast, contractor quote, or guarantee. Enter the same currency for every cost and the installed-rate unit must match the chosen feet/metres unit. The installed rate excludes the separately entered gates and other costs; if a quote includes them, enter zero in those separate fields to prevent double-counting. Check local fence design, livestock needs, regulations, utilities, materials, and site conditions before building.

Frequently asked question

Are local market prices included?

No. The estimate uses only the costs you enter. Obtain current local supplier or contractor quotes and confirm fence design and site requirements for your project.
