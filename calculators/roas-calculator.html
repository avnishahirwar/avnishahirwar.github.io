ROAS Calculator

Calculate return on advertising spend from advertising cost and attributed revenue, including the revenue-to-cost multiple and net difference.

Use the ROAS Calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter campaign advertising spend and the revenue attributed to that campaign. The calculator reports the revenue-to-spend multiple, its percentage equivalent and revenue less ad spend. It does not subtract product, fulfilment, agency or overhead costs.

Formula

ROAS = Attributed revenue ÷ Advertising spend. ROAS percentage = ROAS × 100.

Worked example

If advertising spend is 10,000 and attributed revenue is 30,000, ROAS is 3.0x (300%). Revenue less ad spend is 20,000 before other business costs.

Important limitations

This calculator provides a general mathematical estimate based on user-entered values. It does not connect to financial, advertising, accounting or payment accounts, verify source data, determine tax treatment or replace professional advice. Check the applicable contracts, accounting policy and official guidance before relying on results.

Frequently asked questions

Is ROAS the same as profit?

No. ROAS compares attributed revenue with ad spend only; it does not include cost of goods, salaries, fees or other expenses.

What revenue should I enter?

Use a consistent attribution window and the revenue amount your reporting system attributes to the campaign.
