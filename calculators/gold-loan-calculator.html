Gold Loan Calculator

Estimate gold loan repayment using either equal monthly instalments or monthly interest with principal due at maturity.

Estimate your gold loan EMI

This calculator models equal monthly instalments on a reducing balance. It does not model interest-only or bullet repayment, gold valuation or lender-specific loan-to-value limits.

Illustrative estimate based only on the values entered. Actual terms, fees, rate changes, repayment dates and lender policies may differ. This tool does not provide loan approval or personalised financial advice.

How to use this calculator

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and months. Select the repayment structure stated by the lender. Valuation, loan-to-value limits, fees and renewal terms are not estimated.

Enter figures in the same currency and time period, check the assumptions and use the result as an estimate. The calculator runs in your browser and does not verify a lender offer, bank statement or investment product.

Formula and calculation method

For EMI mode, the reducing-balance formula is used. For bullet mode, monthly interest = principal × annual rate ÷ 1,200 and maturity due = principal + monthly interest × months.

Understanding the result

Compare both the periodic payment and the total amount paid. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest. Fees, insurance, taxes, rate resets, repayment dates and provider-specific terms may affect the actual amount.

Important limitations

This page provides general arithmetic estimates, not personalised financial, tax, credit or investment advice. It does not recommend a loan or investment product, guarantee eligibility or returns, or fetch live rates. Confirm current terms with the relevant regulated provider and read the official agreement before making a decision.
