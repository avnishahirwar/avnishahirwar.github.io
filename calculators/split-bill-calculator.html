How the split bill calculator works

The calculator applies the optional tip percentage to the entered bill, then divides the resulting total equally by the number of people.

Total = Bill amount + (Bill amount × Tip % ÷ 100)
Each share = Total ÷ Number of people

How to use it

  1. Enter the total bill amount.
  2. Enter how many people will share the bill.
  3. Add a tip percentage if required.
  4. Select Split bill to see the total and equal share.

Rounding note

Amounts are displayed to two decimal places. When a bill cannot be divided evenly into the smallest currency unit, the final payment may need a small rounding adjustment between participants. This calculator does not assign different amounts to individual people.

Frequently asked questions

Can I include a tip?

Yes. Enter the tip percentage; the tip is added before the bill is divided.

Can I split a bill among different numbers of people?

Yes. Enter any whole number from 1 to 1,000.

Does it support unequal shares?

This version calculates equal shares only. It does not allocate different items or individual amounts to participants.
