Formulas used by this calculator

The Partially Amortized Loan Calculator works with 8 quantities and 8 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Total after amortization (total_after_amortization) = total_during_period + (balloon_payment)
  • Total during period (total_during_period) = total_after_amortization - (balloon_payment)
  • Monthly payment (monthly_payment) = (annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time)/(pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time) - 1))
  • Payment period (payment_period) = total_during_period/((annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time)/(pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time) - 1)))
  • Balloon payment (balloon_payment) = (annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, payment_period) - pow(annual_interest + 1, payment_period) × ((annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time)/(pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time) - 1))) + ((annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time)/(pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time) - 1))))/annual_interest
  • Full loan (full_loan) = pow(annual_interest + 1, -payment_period) × (annual_interest × balloon_payment + pow(annual_interest + 1, payment_period) × ((annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time)/(pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time) - 1))) - ((annual_interest × full_loan × pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time)/(pow(annual_interest + 1, amortization_time) - 1))))/annual_interest

2 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
