Formula and assumptions

The calculator compounds the current balance and each monthly contribution at an assumed monthly return. The target grows with the entered inflation rate, so the comparison is between projected wealth and the future purchasing-power equivalent of today’s target.

This is an educational scenario, not a forecast or investment advice. Returns are not guaranteed. Taxes, fees, changing contributions, asset allocation, market volatility and personal circumstances can materially change the outcome.

Worked example

With ₹10,00,000 today, a ₹50,000 monthly contribution, an 8% assumed return and 5% inflation, the calculator compares a growing balance with the inflation-adjusted value of a ₹1,00,00,000 target. Change the inputs to see your own scenario.
