Google AdSense Revenue Calculator

Estimate display-ad revenue from page views and an assumed page RPM. Enter your own RPM rather than relying on an unverified benchmark.

Use the Google AdSense Revenue Calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the monthly page-view estimate and your assumed page RPM. RPM is the estimated revenue per thousand page views, so the result scales linearly with traffic and the RPM you provide.

Formula

Estimated revenue = Page views ÷ 1,000 × Page RPM.

Worked example

For 100,000 monthly page views and a page RPM of 2.50 in your chosen currency, the estimate is 100 × 2.50 = 250 per month. This is an illustration, not a forecast or a promise of AdSense earnings.

Important limitations

This calculator provides a general mathematical estimate based on user-entered values. It does not connect to financial, advertising, accounting or payment accounts, verify source data, determine tax treatment or replace professional advice. Check the applicable contracts, accounting policy and official guidance before relying on results.

Frequently asked questions

What is page RPM?

Page RPM is estimated earnings divided by page views, multiplied by 1,000.

Does this connect to my AdSense account?

No. It uses only the page views and RPM you enter; it does not access account or payment data.
