Formulas used by this calculator

The J-Pole Antenna Calculator works with 15 quantities and 27 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Wavelength (wavelength) = speed/freq
  • Speed (speed) = freq × wavelength
  • Freq (freq) = speed/wavelength
  • Dielectric constant (dielectric_constant) = pow(velocity, -2)
  • Spacing (spacing) = (9/400) × wavelength
  • Velocity (velocity) = 40 × feed_point/wavelength
  • Dielectric constant custom (dielectric_constant_custom) = pow(velocity_custom, -2)
  • Long section (long_section) = (3/4) × velocity × wavelength

19 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
