Conversion formula

Blood glucose: mmol/L = mg/dL ÷ 18.0182. Cholesterol: mmol/L = mg/dL ÷ 38.67. The analyte-specific factor is essential; do not use one factor for every laboratory measurement.

This page only converts units. It does not classify results as normal or abnormal, diagnose a condition, suggest medication or dosage, or replace the laboratory report or clinician's advice. Reference intervals can vary by laboratory and individual context.

Frequently asked questions

Why are the conversion factors different?

Different substances have different molecular weights, so their mass-to-molar conversion factors are different.

Can this tool interpret my test result?

No. It performs arithmetic unit conversion only. Ask a qualified healthcare professional to interpret laboratory results.
