Conic Sections Calculator

Just by cutting a cone with a plane, you can get many interesting curves, the conic sections! Try our conic section calculator to know the types of conic sections and how to calculate their eccentricity, and much more!

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Conic Sections Calculator works with 33 quantities and 19 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Semi latus rectum circle (semi_latus_rectum_circle) = (circle_radius)
  • Semi latus rectum parabola (semi_latus_rectum_parabola) = (2 × directrix_distance_parabola)
  • Focal parameter parabola (focal_parameter_parabola) = (2 × directrix_distance_parabola)
  • Semi latus rectum ellipse hor (semi_latus_rectum_ellipse_hor) = (pow(minor_axis_ellipse_hor, 2)/major_axis_ellipse_hor)
  • Semi latus rectum ellipse ver (semi_latus_rectum_ellipse_ver) = (pow(minor_axis_ellipse_ver, 2)/major_axis_ellipse_ver)
  • Semi latus rectum hyperbola hor (semi_latus_rectum_hyperbola_hor) = (pow(minor_axis_hyperbola_hor, 2)/major_axis_hyperbola_hor)
  • Semi latus rectum hyperbola ver (semi_latus_rectum_hyperbola_ver) = (pow(minor_axis_hyperbola_ver, 2)/major_axis_hyperbola_ver)
  • Linear eccentricity ellipse hor (linear_eccentricity_ellipse_hor) = sqrt((pow(major_axis_ellipse_hor, 2) - pow(minor_axis_ellipse_hor, 2)))
