Roman Numeral Converter

Convert a whole number from 1 to 3,999 into standard Roman numeral notation.

How to use this calculator

Enter a whole number from 1 to 3,999. The tool returns the standard uppercase Roman numeral form. This page converts decimal integers to Roman numerals; it does not parse Roman numeral text.

Formula and method

The converter applies the standard descending values M (1000), CM (900), D (500), CD (400), C (100), XC (90), L (50), XL (40), X (10), IX (9), V (5), IV (4) and I (1), subtracting each value as many times as allowed.

Worked example

For 1,944: 1,000 = M; 900 = CM; 40 = XL; 4 = IV. Combining the parts gives MCMXLIV.

Understanding the result

Roman numerals use letters rather than place-value digits. Subtractive pairs such as IV and IX avoid four repeated symbols and are part of the standard modern notation used by this converter.

Important limitations

  • Only integers from 1 through 3,999 are supported. Zero, negative numbers, fractions and larger values are not represented.
  • The tool does not check whether a user-entered Roman numeral string is valid.
  • Historical inscriptions and specialist contexts may use non-standard variants.

Frequently asked questions

What is the largest number supported?

The supported range ends at 3,999, written as MMMCMXCIX in standard notation.

Why is 4 written as IV instead of IIII?

In the standard subtractive form, I before V means one less than five, giving four.

Can this tool convert Roman numerals back to numbers?

No. It converts decimal whole numbers from 1 to 3,999 into Roman numerals only.
