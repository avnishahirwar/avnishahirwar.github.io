Formula and method

Ratio = water ÷ coffee by weight; the common cold brew range is 1:5 to 1:10.

Worked example

100 g of coffee to 800 ml of water is a 1:8 concentrate, best served diluted 1:1.

Frequently asked questions

Grind size?

Coarse, similar to a French press; fine grounds over-extract and clog filters.

Room or fridge?

Room temperature extracts faster; the fridge takes slightly longer but stays fresher.

How strong?

1:5 to 1:8 suits milk drinks; 1:10 and above suits light iced servings.
