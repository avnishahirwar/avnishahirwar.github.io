Stock Break-Even Price After Fees Calculator

Calculate the selling price needed to recover your purchase cost after buy and sell charges.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Break-even sell price = purchase price × (1 + buy charges %) ÷ (1 − sell charges %).

Worked example

With Purchase price per share 500, Number of shares 100, Buy-side charges 0.1, Sell-side charges 0.1, the calculator gives: Break-even sell price = 501; Rise needed from purchase price (%) = 0.2; Total cost of buying = 50,050.

How to read the result

Charges as a percentage of trade value are small for long-term holders but add up for frequent traders. Flat fees per order should be added to the total cost separately.

Frequently asked questions

Are taxes included?

Only if you fold them into the charge percentages. Capital-gains tax is not calculated here.

Why is the rise needed slightly above total charges?

Because the sell-side charge is applied to the sale value, not to the purchase price.
