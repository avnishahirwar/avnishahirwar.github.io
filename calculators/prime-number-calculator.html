Prime Number Calculator

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter a whole number from 2 to 1,000,000,000,000. The upper limit keeps trial-division checks responsive in a browser. The calculator checks divisibility using integer candidates up to the square root of the number.

Formula and methodology

A prime number is an integer greater than 1 with no positive divisors other than 1 and itself. The test checks candidate divisors up to the square root of n.

Worked example

97 is prime because no integer from 2 through its square root divides it exactly. 91 is composite because 7 × 13 = 91.

Important limitations

This tool performs a mathematical calculation on the values entered. It does not replace subject-specific judgement. Check the method and assumptions before using results in academic or professional work.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1 a prime number?

No. A prime number must have exactly two distinct positive divisors.

Why only test divisors up to the square root?

If a number has a factor larger than its square root, its paired factor must be smaller than the square root.
