How to use this calculator

Enter a number, choose 0 to 10 decimal places and select a rounding method. Floor and ceiling are directional operations, including for negative values.

Formula and method

Nearest uses JavaScript Math.round behavior. Floor rounds toward negative infinity; ceiling rounds toward positive infinity.

Limitations

This tool is intended for general arithmetic and learning. Finite computer precision can affect some decimal results. Verify high-precision work with an appropriate specialist tool.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between floor and ceiling?

Floor moves toward negative infinity, while ceiling moves toward positive infinity, including for negative numbers.

Does rounding change the original number?

No. It creates a new displayed value at the requested precision.
