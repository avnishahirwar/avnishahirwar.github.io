What is a wedding budget calculator?

A wedding budget calculator is a planning tool that helps you list expected costs, add them together and compare the total with an amount you have decided to spend. Weddings can involve several events and suppliers, so keeping expenses in one place makes it easier to see which categories use the largest share of the budget. This calculator is designed for Indian rupee estimates, but the same planning method can be adapted to another currency by using that currency consistently.

The tool separates a total budget from individual estimates for the venue, catering, decoration, photography, clothing, travel, gifts and other costs. It then calculates the estimated spend, the amount remaining or the amount over budget, the percentage of the budget used and the catering estimate per guest. It does not quote vendor prices or tell you what a wedding should cost.

Formula and Indian example

Total estimated spend = venue + catering + decoration + photography + clothing + travel + gifts + other costs.

Remaining budget = planned budget − total estimated spend. A negative result means the estimates exceed the planned limit.

Budget used (%) = (total estimated spend ÷ planned budget) × 100.

Catering per guest = catering estimate ÷ number of guests.

Example: assume a family sets a planning limit of ₹10,00,000 for an event with 300 guests. They enter ₹2,00,000 for the venue, ₹3,00,000 for catering, ₹1,20,000 for decoration, ₹80,000 for photography, ₹70,000 for clothing, ₹60,000 for travel, ₹50,000 for gifts and ₹70,000 for other costs. The estimated total is ₹9,50,000, leaving ₹50,000. The budget used is 95%. Catering works out to ₹1,000 per guest using the entered estimate. These are example inputs, not market-price guidance.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the overall spending limit you want to plan around.
  2. Enter the expected guest count and your best current estimate for each expense category.
  3. Select Calculate budget and check the total, remaining balance, percentage used and category breakdown.
  4. Adjust estimates as written vendor quotations arrive, then download or share the revised summary with the people involved in planning.

Tips for keeping a wedding budget organised

Start with a total limit agreed by the people responsible for the budget. Separate confirmed quotations from rough estimates and update the figures when a supplier provides a written quote. Ask what each quote includes: taxes, service charges, delivery, setup, overtime, travel, cancellation terms and any refundable deposit can change the final amount.

Keep a contingency amount for costs that are easy to overlook, such as extra guests, transport changes, last-minute printing, venue timing extensions and small purchases. Avoid counting the same item in two categories. If several events are planned, decide whether each event should have its own budget or whether all events should roll into one combined total.

When comparing vendors, compare the same service scope and guest count rather than headline prices alone. Review contracts and payment dates with a responsible adult or qualified adviser where appropriate. Do not borrow or commit money based only on this calculator's estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator show actual wedding prices in India?

No. It adds the estimates you enter. Actual supplier prices vary by city, date, venue, guest count, service scope and contract terms.

What happens if my estimated spend is higher than the budget?

The remaining amount becomes negative and the budget-used percentage goes above 100%. Review the category estimates and decide which assumptions need to be discussed or revised.

Does catering per guest include every wedding expense?

No. It divides only the catering amount by the guest count. It is not the total wedding cost per guest.

Can I use this for multiple wedding events?

Yes. You can combine estimates from several events in the category fields, or calculate each event separately and combine the totals in your own planning sheet.

Are taxes and vendor charges included automatically?

No. Include them in the relevant category estimate after checking the supplier quotation. The calculator does not fetch current tax rates or vendor prices.
