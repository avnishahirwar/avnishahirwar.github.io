Exercise Calorie Burn Calculator

Estimate exercise energy expenditure from body mass, duration and an activity-intensity value.

Enter values to calculate an estimate.
Important: This is a broad energy estimate, not a measurement, diet prescription or medical recommendation.

Formula and assumptions

The estimate uses the standard MET approach: calories ≈ MET × 3.5 × body mass in kilograms ÷ 200 × minutes. A MET is a reference multiple of resting oxygen use. The value depends on the activity and intensity, so the result is only as useful as the MET value chosen. Fitness level, efficiency, terrain, temperature and pauses can change actual energy expenditure.

Use it responsibly

Do not use the output to decide whether to exercise through pain or illness. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or postpartum, or are returning after injury, seek individual guidance. Stop and seek appropriate help for concerning symptoms such as chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting or sudden severe pain.
