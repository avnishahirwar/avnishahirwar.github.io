BMI Prime Calculator

Calculate BMI and BMI Prime, the ratio of your BMI to the upper limit of the commonly used healthy range.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². BMI Prime = BMI ÷ 25.

Worked example

With Weight 70, Height 172, the calculator gives: BMI = 23.66; BMI Prime = 0.95; Weight at BMI 25 (kg) = 73.96.

How to read the result

A BMI Prime below 1.0 means a BMI under 25. BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat and is not reliable for athletes, older adults, children or pregnancy.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI Prime a diagnosis?

No. It is a screening ratio that must be interpreted by a health professional alongside other measurements.

Does it apply to children?

No. Children and teenagers use age- and sex-specific percentiles.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives general informational estimates only. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Individual needs vary with age, health conditions, medicines and activity, so speak with a qualified health professional before changing diet, training or medication.
