Break-Even Point Calculator

Find the unit sales and revenue needed to cover fixed costs.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (selling price − variable cost); break-even revenue = units × price.

Worked example and limitations

10,000 fixed costs and a 20 unit contribution margin require 500 units to break even.
