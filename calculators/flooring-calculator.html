Flooring Calculator

Estimate how many flooring boxes are needed for one or more identical rectangular rooms.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Enter the dimensions and product information that match your project, then select Calculate. Confirm measurements and product coverage before purchasing materials.

Formula and methodology

Net area = length × width × number of rooms. Required area = net area × (1 + allowance ÷ 100). Boxes = round up(required area ÷ coverage per box).

Worked example

An 18 ft by 12 ft room is 216 sq ft. With 10% allowance and 20 sq ft per box, the estimate is 12 boxes.

Important limitations

These are planning estimates, not construction drawings, structural calculations, building-code determinations or installation instructions. Actual quantities vary with site conditions, product dimensions, cuts, patterns and workmanship. Have a qualified installer confirm measurements and safety-critical design.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find coverage per box?

Use the net coverage stated on the flooring package.

Does this include stairs or irregular rooms?

No. Calculate those separately or have an installer confirm the layout.
