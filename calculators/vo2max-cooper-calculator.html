Formula and method

VO2 max = 22.351 × distance in km − 11.288 (Cooper, 1968).

Worked example

Running 2,400 m in 12 minutes: 22.351 × 2.4 − 11.288 = 42.4 ml/kg/min.

Frequently asked questions

What is VO2 max?

It is the maximal oxygen uptake, a common indicator of aerobic fitness.

Who developed this test?

Dr Kenneth Cooper introduced the 12-minute run test in 1968 for the US Air Force.

Can I walk the test?

The formula assumes running. Walking shortens the covered distance and underestimates fitness.
