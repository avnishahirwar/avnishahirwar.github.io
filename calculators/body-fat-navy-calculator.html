Formula and method

US Navy formula using log10 of circumference differences; coefficients differ by sex.

Worked example

A man at 175 cm with a 38 cm neck and an 85 cm waist: 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log10(47) + 0.15456 × log10(175)) − 450 ≈ 18%.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the tape method?

Typically within 3-4% of lab methods when done carefully; consistency of technique matters most.

Why is hip needed for women?

The female formula distributes measurements across waist and hip for gluteal tissue.

Measuring tips?

Keep the tape level and snug without compressing the skin, and measure at the same time of day.
