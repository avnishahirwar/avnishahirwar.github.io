Formula and method

Cartons per layer = (pallet length ÷ carton length) × (pallet width ÷ carton width), floored; total × layers.

Worked example

A 120 × 100 cm pallet carries 40 × 30 cm cartons three per row, four per layer, 20 cartons over 5 layers.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Rotate cartons?

Turning cartons 90 degrees can fit more or fewer per layer; test both.

Overhang?

Loads should not overhang the pallet or cartons get crushed in transit.

Pallet height limits?

Check the truck or container height limit before stacking high.
