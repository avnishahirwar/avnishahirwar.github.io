Acoustic Impedance Calculator

The acoustic impedance calculator will help you determine a material's specific acoustic impedance and the intensity reflection and transmission coefficients of a sound wave at the boundary of two materials.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Acoustic Impedance Calculator works with 20 quantities and 27 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Sound velocity (sound_velocity) = 1000000 × impedance/density
  • Density (density) = 1000000 × impedance/sound_velocity
  • Impedance (impedance) = (1/1000000) × density × sound_velocity
  • Transmission coeff (transmission_coeff) = 4 × impedance1 × impedance2/pow(impedance1 + impedance2, 2)
  • Reflection coeff (reflection_coeff) = pow(impedance1 - impedance2, 2)/pow(impedance1 + impedance2, 2)
  • Transmission coeff custom (transmission_coeff_custom) = 4 × impedance1_custom × impedance2/pow(impedance1_custom + impedance2, 2)
  • Transmission coeff custom2 (transmission_coeff_custom2) = 4 × impedance1 × impedance2_custom/pow(impedance1 + impedance2_custom, 2)
  • Reflection coeff custom (reflection_coeff_custom) = pow(impedance1_custom - impedance2, 2)/pow(impedance1_custom + impedance2, 2)

19 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
