How this calculator works

This tool applies a mathematical estimate to the values entered above. Where a rate, fee, tax rate, eligibility limit or scheme term is requested, enter the value that applies to your situation and confirm it with the relevant official provider.

The calculator does not connect to banks, brokers, government portals or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, tax classification, exemptions or compliance obligations. It excludes charges and adjustments unless a field explicitly asks for them.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • Figures are based on user-entered assumptions and may differ from actual provider calculations.
  • Rounding, dates, compounding, fees and applicable law can affect real outcomes.
  • For tax and government-scheme matters, verify current rules and official notifications before acting.
  • This tool is educational and is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Formula and worked example

This estimator uses simple interest: estimated interest = unpaid tax × annual rate (%) ÷ 100 × delayed days ÷ 365. It uses a 365-day denominator and does not calculate delayed days from calendar dates.

Illustration: ₹25,000 at an entered annual rate of 18% for 30 days gives ₹25,000 × 0.18 × 30 ÷ 365 = approximately ₹369.86.

Important GST law distinction

Section 50 of the CGST Act distinguishes interest on delayed tax payment from interest on certain wrongly availed and utilised input tax credit. The rate and calculation base depend on the applicable subsection, notification, facts and period. The official CGST Act text (Section 50) and Notification 6/2017 on notified interest rates describe distinct provisions. Do not assume one entered rate applies to every GST interest situation. Sources checked 14 November 2026.

What this estimate excludes

  • It does not determine statutory due dates or count delayed days from filing/payment dates.
  • It does not decide whether interest applies to the full tax amount or a specific amount in the circumstances.
  • It does not model wrongly availed and utilised ITC, reversals, amendments, notices, penalties or late fees.
  • It does not prepare a GST return or connect to the GST portal.

Frequently asked questions

Should I always enter 18%?

No. Enter only the rate confirmed for the specific liability and period. Different statutory situations can have different notified rates and calculation bases.

Does the calculator count days automatically?

No. Enter the delayed-day count after checking the applicable start and end dates.

Is this the amount payable on the GST portal?

No. It is a simple-interest illustration. Reconcile it with the portal, current law and applicable notifications.
