How this calculator works

This tool applies a mathematical estimate to the values entered above. Where a rate, fee, tax rate, eligibility limit or scheme term is requested, enter the value that applies to your situation and confirm it with the relevant official provider.

The calculator does not connect to banks, brokers, government portals or live market feeds. It does not determine legal eligibility, tax classification, exemptions or compliance obligations. It excludes charges and adjustments unless a field explicitly asks for them.

Worked business-loan example

For an illustrative business loan of ₹5,00,000 at a constant annual interest rate of 12% over 3 years (36 monthly instalments), a reducing-balance EMI calculation gives an estimated monthly payment of ₹16,607.15. Total scheduled repayment is ₹5,97,857.58 and estimated interest is ₹97,857.58. This excludes processing fees, collateral costs, insurance, taxes, rate resets and lender-specific charges.

Use the amount actually borrowed and the rate and tenure stated in the lender quotation. A business loan may have a different repayment structure from this standard monthly-EMI illustration.

Business-loan questions

Is the business-loan EMI calculated on a reducing balance?

This example uses the standard reducing-balance EMI method with a constant monthly rate and equal monthly instalments. Confirm whether the lender uses the same method and repayment dates.

Are processing fees and collateral costs included?

No. Fees, valuation, documentation, insurance, guarantee charges and other lender costs are outside the principal-and-interest estimate unless included in the amount you enter.

Does this calculator confirm business-loan eligibility?

No. It does not assess turnover, credit history, cash flow, collateral, lender policy or government-scheme eligibility. Only the lender can assess and approve an application.

What if the lender offers a moratorium or irregular repayments?

This standard EMI illustration does not model a moratorium, bullet repayment, seasonal instalments or changing disbursals. Ask the lender for a repayment schedule that matches the actual facility terms.
