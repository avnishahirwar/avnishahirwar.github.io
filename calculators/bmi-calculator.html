Guide: understanding your BMI Calculator results

How BMI is calculated

Body Mass Index is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. A person who is 165 cm tall and weighs 72 kg has a BMI of 72 ÷ (1.65 × 1.65) = 26.4. The tool accepts centimetres or feet and inches, and kilograms or pounds, and converts them before calculating.

What the categories mean

The standard adult categories used here are underweight below 18.5, healthy 18.5–24.9, overweight 25.0–29.9 and obesity 30.0 or above. Several Indian and Asia-Pacific guidelines use lower cut-offs because health risks such as type 2 diabetes appear at lower BMI in South Asian populations: overweight from 23 and obesity from 25. On those guidelines, a BMI of 26.4 would fall in the obesity range, so a doctor's advice matters more than a single label.

What BMI cannot tell you

BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, does not show where fat is stored and does not account for age, sex or ethnicity. Waist circumference, waist-to-height ratio, blood tests and a clinical examination give a fuller picture. For people under 18, interpretation needs age- and sex-specific growth percentiles, so this calculator shows no category for them.

Worked example with real numbers

Height 165 cm, weight 72 kg, age 28: BMI 26.4, which the standard adult table calls overweight. To reach 24.9 at the same height, weight would need to be about 67.8 kg, a reduction of roughly 4 kg. Use it as a conversation starter with a doctor, not a goal on its own.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using the adult categories for children and teenagers.
  • Treating a single BMI reading as a diagnosis.
  • Entering height in the wrong unit when switching between cm and feet-inches.
  • Ignoring muscle mass in athletes or older adults who have lost muscle.

Important information

BMI cannot diagnose a condition. For children and teenagers, consult a qualified healthcare professional for age- and sex-specific interpretation.

Frequently asked questions about the BMI Calculator

Is a BMI of 24 healthy for an Indian adult?

On the standard table it is in the healthy range. Under Asia-Pacific and Indian consensus guidance, values from 23 are treated as overweight, so ask your doctor how to interpret it with your waist size and health history.

How do I convert feet and inches?

Choose the feet and inches option and enter both values. The tool converts to centimetres internally (1 foot = 30.48 cm, 1 inch = 2.54 cm).

Why does the tool not show a category for under-18 users?

Children's BMI is compared with percentiles for their age and sex, which this adult calculator does not have.

Is BMI accurate for pregnant women?

No. BMI is not a suitable measure during pregnancy. Talk to your doctor or midwife.

Is my data stored?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and the values are not sent anywhere.
