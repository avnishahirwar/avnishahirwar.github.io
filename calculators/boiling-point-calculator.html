Boiling Point Calculator

Estimate a liquid's boiling temperature at a specified pressure by inverting the Antoine equation.

Enter your values

Formula

Antoine inverse: T = B ÷ (A − log₁₀(P)) − C.

How to use this calculator

  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.

Assumptions and limitations

The result depends on a compatible Antoine coefficient set and its valid pressure/temperature range. Default coefficients are for water near atmospheric pressure.
