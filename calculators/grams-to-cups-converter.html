Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite and positive; denominators and target values must be greater than zero. Cups = grams ÷ ingredient-specific grams per cup. Density differs substantially by ingredient; enter a known reference. Results are estimates based only on the entered assumptions. For food safety, follow current official guidance and product instructions; this tool does not replace them.
