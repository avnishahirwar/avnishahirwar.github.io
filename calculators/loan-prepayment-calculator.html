How this calculator works

The calculator first estimates the remaining payoff time and interest using your current EMI. It then applies the one-time prepayment immediately against principal and adds any recurring monthly prepayment to each EMI. Both schedules use the same assumed monthly reducing-balance rate.

The one-time prepayment is assumed to reduce principal before the next monthly interest calculation. The model assumes the lender keeps your EMI unchanged and shortens the payoff period. Actual lender treatment, prepayment dates, minimum amounts, fees, rate resets and part-payment rules may differ.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • All figures are based on user-entered assumptions and may differ from actual provider calculations.
  • Rounding, payment dates, compounding, fees and applicable law can affect real outcomes.
  • For tax and government-scheme matters, verify current rules and official notifications before acting.
  • This tool is educational and is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
