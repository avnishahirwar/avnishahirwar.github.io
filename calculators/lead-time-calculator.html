Formulas used by this calculator

The Lead Time Calculator works with 11 quantities and 10 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Receiving order (receiving_order) = o_lead_time + placing_order
  • Supply lead time (supply_lead_time) = reorder_delay + supply_delay
  • Placing order (placing_order) = -o_lead_time + receiving_order
  • Reorder delay (reorder_delay) = -supply_delay + supply_lead_time
  • O lead time (o_lead_time) = -placing_order + receiving_order
  • Supply delay (supply_delay) = -reorder_delay + supply_lead_time
  • Post production (post_production) = m_lead_time - pre_production - production
  • Pre production (pre_production) = m_lead_time - post_production - production

2 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
