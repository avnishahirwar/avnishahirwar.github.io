How to use this calculator

Enter the amount financed after any down payment, the annual interest rate and the repayment tenure. Compare the monthly instalment with total repayment; the lowest EMI may not mean the lowest overall cost.

Enter figures in the same currency and time period, check the assumptions and use the result as an estimate. The calculator runs in your browser and does not verify a lender offer, bank statement or investment product.

Formula and calculation method

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is the financed amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Understanding the result

Compare both the periodic payment and the total amount paid. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest. Fees, insurance, taxes, rate resets, repayment dates and provider-specific terms may affect the actual amount.

Important limitations

Confirm current terms with the relevant regulated provider and read the official agreement before making a decision.

Car loan questions

Should I enter the ex-showroom price or the financed amount?

Enter the principal the lender will finance after your down payment and any amount paid separately. The EMI formula applies to the financed principal, not automatically to the vehicle's sticker price.

Does this calculator include on-road charges?

No. Registration, road tax, insurance, accessories, handling charges and processing fees are excluded unless you include a financed amount for them in the loan principal.

Can a longer car-loan tenure reduce the EMI?

Usually, yes, when the principal and rate are unchanged. It can also increase total interest because repayment runs for more months. Compare total repayment and the loan agreement, not only the monthly instalment.

Does the calculator use live bank or dealer rates?

No. Enter the annual rate quoted to you. The page does not fetch lender offers or confirm approval, eligibility or current vehicle-finance promotions.

Who should use this car loan calculator?

People comparing vehicle-finance scenarios can estimate the instalment and scheduled interest before requesting a formal lender quotation. Use the amount that will actually be financed after the down payment and any upfront payments.

Car loan inputs explained

  • Amount financed: the principal borrowed, not automatically the ex-showroom or on-road vehicle price.
  • Annual interest rate: the rate used in this scenario. It is not fetched from a bank, dealer or finance company.
  • Repayment tenure: the number of monthly instalments. Use the same tenure when comparing offers.

Common mistakes and practical use

  • Including a down payment in the loan principal even though it is paid upfront.
  • Comparing only EMI while ignoring total interest and fees.
  • Assuming a quoted floating or promotional rate will remain unchanged.
  • Forgetting that insurance, registration, road tax, accessories and maintenance may sit outside the EMI.

For a practical comparison, calculate the financed amount for two down-payment choices, then compare EMI and total repayment alongside the vehicle's full ownership costs.
