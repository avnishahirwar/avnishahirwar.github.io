Customer Retention Rate Calculator

Calculate the share of starting customers retained after excluding new customers acquired during the period. Enter your own values to calculate an estimate.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Retained starting customers = Ending customers − New customers acquired. Retention rate = Retained starting customers ÷ Starting customers × 100.

Inputs are processed in your browser. Results are estimates based on the values and period definitions you enter. Keep all figures on the same accounting period and basis.

Worked example

Starting with 1,000 customers, ending with 980 and adding 30 new customers means 950 original customers were retained: 95% retention.
