Purchasing Power of Money Calculator

Estimate what a sum of money will be worth in today’s terms after years of inflation.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Value in today’s money = amount ÷ (1 + inflation ÷ 100)^years.

Worked example

With Amount of money 1,00,000, Expected inflation 6, Years from now 15, the calculator gives: Value in today’s money = 41,726.51; Purchasing power lost = 58,273.49; Share of purchasing power lost (%) = 58.27.

How to read the result

Cash that earns less than inflation loses real value every year. Compare the result with the after-tax return on deposits or investments.

Frequently asked questions

Is inflation constant in real life?

No. It changes year to year, so this is an estimate under a constant assumption.

Can this be used backwards in time?

Not directly. For past periods use actual historical price indices.
