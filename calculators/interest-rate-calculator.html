Formula and methodology

The calculator solves the standard fixed-payment loan equation for the monthly rate using numerical bisection, then multiplies that rate by 12 for the nominal annual rate. The effective annual rate compounds the monthly rate for 12 months.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values requested in the form.
  2. Check the units and assumptions before calculating.
  3. Review the result and use it as an estimate, not a substitute for professional advice.

Calculations run locally in your browser. Do not enter passwords, account numbers or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result the lender’s APR?

No. It is an implied rate based only on the loan amount, payment and term. Fees, insurance and other charges are not included.

Why might my lender’s rate differ?

Lenders may use different payment dates, rounding, fees, compounding conventions or repayment structures.
