DNA Concentration Calculator

Estimate nucleic-acid concentration from absorbance at 260 nm, dilution factor and sample type.

Enter your values

Formula

Concentration = A₂₆₀ × conversion factor × dilution factor; dsDNA 50, ssDNA 33, RNA 40 µg/mL per A₂₆₀ unit.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values using the units shown beside each field.
  2. Select the relevant option where a dropdown is provided.
  3. Choose Calculate to see the estimated result and intermediate values.

Worked example

The pre-filled values provide a sample calculation. Change one input at a time to understand how the result changes.

Assumptions and limitations

This UV-absorbance estimate assumes a blank-corrected reading and a suitable optical path length. Protein, phenol, turbidity and other contaminants can distort A₂₆₀. Check A₂₆₀/A₂₈₀ and A₂₆₀/A₂₃₀ ratios separately.

Updated: 17 November 2026. This educational calculator provides an estimate from the inputs and formula shown. Verify important laboratory or research work independently.
