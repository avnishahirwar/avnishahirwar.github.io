EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments. At 0% interest, EMI = P ÷ n.

Frequently asked questions

How is home loan EMI calculated?

The calculator applies the reducing-balance EMI formula using a constant monthly rate and equal instalments.

Are processing fees and insurance included?

No. Fees, insurance, taxes and other lender charges are excluded.

Does this confirm home loan approval?

No. It is a mathematical estimate only. The lender determines eligibility and approval.
