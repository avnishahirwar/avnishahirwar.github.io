What is an RD calculator?

A recurring deposit lets a customer deposit a fixed amount at regular intervals, commonly every month, for a selected tenure. Interest is earned according to the bank or institution's product rules. An RD calculator estimates the possible maturity amount from the instalment, rate and duration you enter.

Use the result to understand how the deposit amount, interest assumption and tenure affect the illustration. It does not check current bank rates, account eligibility, tax treatment or product-specific conditions.

Formula and calculation method

Monthly-equivalent rate i = (1 + r/4)1/3 − 1. Estimated maturity = P × [((1+i)n − 1) / i], where P is the monthly instalment, r is the annual rate as a decimal and n is the number of monthly instalments. For a zero rate, maturity equals P × n.

This simplified model assumes each instalment is made at the end of the month and converts a nominal quarterly-compounding assumption into an equivalent monthly rate. Financial institutions may use different day counts, deposit dates, compounding conventions and rounding. Their maturity advice is authoritative.

Worked example: ₹1,000 per month

For 12 monthly instalments of ₹1,000 at an assumed annual rate of 6.5%, the total amount deposited is ₹12,000. The calculator estimates interest using the formula above. The actual maturity value may differ from a bank's quote because the institution's RD method and dates may differ.

What changes the result?

  • A higher monthly instalment increases the total amount deposited.
  • A longer tenure gives earlier instalments more time to earn interest.
  • The assumed rate and the institution's compounding method affect estimated interest.
  • Missed instalments, late fees and premature closure can change actual proceeds.

RD versus FD

An RD generally spreads deposits across regular instalments, while an FD usually starts with a lump-sum deposit. They serve different cash-flow needs. Compare the institution's rate, tenure, payout option, withdrawal conditions, tax treatment and deposit-protection rules rather than comparing headline rates alone.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the exact maturity amount offered by my bank?

No. It is an illustrative estimate. Banks can use different compounding conventions, deposit dates, rounding and product rules. Check the official bank quote.

Can I use this for any tenure?

Use a tenure up to 20 years in this tool. Actual institutions may offer different minimum and maximum tenures.

Does the result include tax or penalties?

No. Tax, TDS, late fees, missed-instalment charges and premature-closure adjustments are not included.

Does the calculator use live bank rates?

No. Enter the annual rate supplied by the institution. The tool does not fetch or recommend a bank rate.
