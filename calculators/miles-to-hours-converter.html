Estimate travel time from distance and speed, or solve for the missing value in the distance–speed–time relationship. Keep units consistent.

Enter values

Distance, speed and time

Use consistent units in the relationship between distance, speed and time. The calculator solves for the missing value when the other two are known.

Formula

Time = distance ÷ speed

Speed = distance ÷ time

Distance = speed × time

Worked example

A 150-mile journey at an average speed of 50 miles per hour takes 3 hours before stops or delays.

Assumptions

This is a mathematical estimate. Traffic, route changes, weather, stops and actual average speed affect real travel time.
