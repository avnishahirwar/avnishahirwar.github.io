Formulas used by this calculator

The Parallelogram Perimeter Calculator works with 12 quantities and 11 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Perimeter1 (perimeter1) = 2 × side_a1 + 2 × side_b1
  • Side b1 (side_b1) = (1/2) × perimeter1 - side_a1
  • Side a1 (side_a1) = (1/2) × perimeter1 - side_b1
  • Perimeter3 (perimeter3) = 2 × height/sin(angle1) + 2 × side_a3
  • Side a3 (side_a3) = -height/sin(angle1) + (1/2) × perimeter3
  • Angle1 (angle1) = asin(2 × height/(perimeter3 - 2 × side_a3))
  • Height (height) = ((1/2) × perimeter3 - side_a3) × sin(angle1)
  • Perimeter2 (perimeter2) = 2 × side_a2 + sqrt(2 × pow(diagonal_e, 2) + 2 × pow(diagonal_f, 2) - 4 × pow(side_a2, 2))
