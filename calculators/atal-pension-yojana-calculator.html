Formula and step-by-step calculation

There is no formula inferred by this calculator. It looks up the prescribed amount in the PFRDA annex table for entry age 18–40, pension slab ₹1,000–₹5,000, and monthly, quarterly or half-yearly contribution.

Worked example: for entry at age 18 with a target pension of ₹5,000 per month, the PFRDA table lists a monthly contribution of ₹210. The selected frequency changes the prescribed amount.

Limitations

A new subscriber must meet scheme eligibility, including age 18–40, Indian citizenship, a qualifying savings account and the current income-tax-payer exclusion. Existing subscribers may continue if they later become taxpayers. Contribution schedule checked against the original PFRDA notification annex; verify operational terms with the bank or PFRDA.

Rules and figures checked against official sources on 21 November 2026. Rules and rates can change; verify the current official guidance before relying on a result.

Frequently asked questions

Does this result guarantee a benefit or final liability?

For new enrolment, a person who is or has been an income-tax payer on the application date is not eligible under the current rule. PFRDA distinguishes this from existing subscribers who later become taxpayers.

What should I verify before relying on a result?

The contribution is looked up from the prescribed PFRDA table by age, pension amount and frequency. This page does not calculate charges or investment returns.
