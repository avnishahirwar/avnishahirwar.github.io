Manometer Calculator

This manometer calculator will determine pressures at specific points in any common manometer. Manometers are simple to use and knowing how to use them is a fantastic way to learn more about fluid statics.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Manometer Calculator works with 27 quantities and 55 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Gravitational Acceleration (gravitationalAcceleration) = gaugePressureB/(density × height)
  • Gauge Pressure B (gaugePressureB) = density × gravitationalAcceleration × height
  • Gauge Pressure A (gaugePressureA) = -density × gravitationalAcceleration × height
  • Density (density) = gaugePressureB/(gravitationalAcceleration × height)
  • Height (height) = gaugePressureB/(density × gravitationalAcceleration)
  • Gauge Pressure B u Tube Diff (gaugePressureB_uTubeDiff) = densityFluid1 × gravitationalAcceleration × height1_AtoB
  • Atmospheric Pressure (atmosphericPressure) = absolutePressureA + density × gravitationalAcceleration × height
  • Absolute Pressure A (absolutePressureA) = atmosphericPressure - density × gravitationalAcceleration × height

47 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
