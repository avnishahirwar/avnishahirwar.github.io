Theoretical Yield Calculator

Calculate theoretical product mass from a reactant mass, molar masses and balanced-equation stoichiometric coefficients.

Formula

Theoretical product mass = (reactant mass ÷ reactant molar mass) × (product coefficient ÷ reactant coefficient) × product molar mass.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each value in the unit shown beside its field.
  2. Select Calculate to view the result and intermediate values.
  3. Change the inputs to compare scenarios.

Worked example

Assumptions and limitations

This single-reactant estimate assumes the entered reactant is limiting and the balanced equation is correct. It does not compare multiple reactants or predict side reactions.
