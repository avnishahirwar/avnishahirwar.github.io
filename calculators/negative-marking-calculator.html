Formula used

Net score = (Correct answers × Marks per correct answer) − (Incorrect answers × Penalty per incorrect answer)

  • Attempted = Correct + Incorrect.
  • Unattempted = Total − Attempted.
  • Accuracy = Correct ÷ Attempted × 100.
  • Attempt rate = Attempted ÷ Total × 100.
  • Score percentage = Net score ÷ Maximum possible marks × 100.

Worked example

For 100 questions, 62 correct, 18 incorrect, 4 marks per correct answer and a 1-mark penalty:

  • Marks earned: 62 × 4 = 248.
  • Negative marks: 18 × 1 = 18.
  • Net score: 248 − 18 = 230 out of 400.
  • Accuracy: 62 ÷ 80 × 100 = 77.5%.
  • Attempt rate: 80 ÷ 100 × 100 = 80%.
  • Unattempted: 20.

Important assumptions

  • This is a configurable arithmetic tool, not an official score predictor for JEE, NEET, CUET, UPSC or another examination.
  • Check the current official exam notification for marks per correct answer, penalties, section rules and partial-credit rules.
  • This calculator does not model normalization, percentile conversion, bonus questions, sectional cut-offs or rank prediction.
  • Accuracy describes attempted answers only. It does not measure question difficulty or guarantee future performance.

Frequently asked questions

How is negative marking calculated?

Multiply correct answers by the marks awarded, then subtract the incorrect-answer count multiplied by the penalty per wrong answer.

Does this use the official JEE or NEET marking scheme?

No. Enter the current values from the official information bulletin for the exact exam and paper.

Are unattempted questions counted as wrong?

No. This calculator treats unattempted questions as zero marks. Check your exam's official rules for exceptions.

Can the net score be negative?

Yes. The arithmetic result can be negative if penalties exceed marks earned. Some exams apply a floor or another special rule that this general calculator does not model.
