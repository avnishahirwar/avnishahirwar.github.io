Specific Heat Energy Calculator

Calculate the heat energy needed to change the temperature of a mass using its specific heat capacity.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Heat energy Q = m × c × ΔT.

Worked example

With Mass 2, Specific heat capacity 4,186, Temperature change 40, the calculator gives: Heat energy (joules) = 3,34,880; Heat energy (kilojoules) = 334.88; Heat energy (kWh) = 0.09.

How to read the result

Water is about 4,186 J/kg·K. The kWh figure shows why heating water electrically has a measurable cost. Heat losses to surroundings make real use higher.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include phase change?

No. Melting or boiling needs latent heat in addition to this formula.

Is a temperature change in °C the same as in K?

Yes. A change of 1 °C equals a change of 1 K.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
