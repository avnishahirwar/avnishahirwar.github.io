Days Sales Outstanding Calculator

Measure how many days it takes on average to collect payment from credit sales.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

DSO = accounts receivable ÷ credit sales × days in the period.

Worked example

With Accounts receivable 8,00,000, Credit sales in the period 60,00,000, Days in the period 365, the calculator gives: Days sales outstanding = 48.67; Receivables as a share of sales (%) = 13.33.

How to read the result

A rising DSO means customers pay more slowly and cash is tied up. Compare DSO with your stated payment terms and with the same period in earlier years.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use total sales or credit sales?

Use credit sales when you can. Using total sales understates DSO if many customers pay in cash.

What is a good DSO?

It depends on industry and payment terms. A DSO well above your terms suggests collection problems.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool provides planning estimates from the figures you enter. Definitions vary between companies and accounting standards, so align the inputs with your own policies and verify results with a qualified accountant or adviser before making decisions.
