Insurance Premium Calculator

Estimate the premium breakdown for a life, health, motor, travel, property or other insurance quote using figures supplied by you.

Calculate an estimated premium

Enter your quote values and select Calculate premium.

Important: This is a quote-breakdown estimator, not an insurer quote. It does not calculate underwriting risk, age-based rates, medical loading, no-claim bonuses or policy-specific fees.

Formula and methodology

First, add the base premium and optional add-on or rider premium. Subtract the discount you enter. The calculator then applies the tax rate you provide to that discounted amount.

Premium before tax = Base premium + Add-on premium − Discount

Estimated tax = Premium before tax × Tax rate ÷ 100

Estimated total = Premium before tax + Estimated tax

The payment estimate divides the annual total by the number of payments selected. Some insurers add instalment charges or use different payment schedules; those are not included unless you account for them in your input.

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose the type of insurance and the currency shown on your quote.
  2. Enter the base premium, optional add-ons and any discount from the insurer's illustration.
  3. Enter the applicable tax rate from the current quote or official/provider source. The default is zero because rates vary by jurisdiction and product.
  4. Select a payment frequency to see an equal-payment estimate.
  5. Compare the result with the insurer's formal benefit illustration, policy wording and payment schedule.

Calculations run locally in your browser. Do not enter policy numbers, identity documents, health details or other sensitive personal information.

Worked example

If the base premium is 12,000, add-ons are 1,500, the discount is 500 and the entered tax rate is 0%, the pre-tax estimate and total are both 13,000. Choosing quarterly payments gives an indicative 3,250 per payment. These are example inputs, not a market quote or a recommended policy price.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator provide an insurance quote?

No. It adds values from a quote or illustration that you already have. An insurer determines the actual premium after applying its product terms, eligibility rules and underwriting process.

Does it know the current insurance tax rate?

No. Tax rules can depend on the country, policy type and date. Enter the rate shown by the insurer or confirmed from an official source. The default is zero so the calculator does not assume a rate.

Can I use it for life, health, motor or travel insurance?

Yes, as a general premium breakdown tool. It does not estimate risk-based pricing or decide how much cover you need for any of those products.

Why can the insurer's instalment differ?

Payment mode charges, rounding, policy fees, taxes, underwriting changes and product-specific conditions can change the final amount. Use the insurer's written schedule as authoritative.
