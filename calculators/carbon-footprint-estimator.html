Carbon Footprint Estimator

Estimate operational carbon emissions from electricity, fuel and travel using emission factors supplied for your location and activity.

Enter your values

Formula and method

Emissions for each activity = activity data × matching emission factor. Total emissions = electricity + fuel + travel emissions, expressed as kg CO₂e and tonnes CO₂e.

Worked example

Using 100 kWh at 0.7 kg CO₂e/kWh, 20 litres at 2.3 kg CO₂e/litre and 50 km at 0.12 kg CO₂e/km gives 128 kg CO₂e in total.

Assumptions and limitations

Enter factors from a consistent, current and authoritative source. Factors vary by electricity grid, fuel, vehicle, occupancy and geography. This simplified estimate omits many upstream and downstream emissions and is not a verified corporate inventory.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get emission factors?

Use current official government, utility or recognized greenhouse-gas accounting datasets appropriate to your geography and reporting boundary.

Is this a complete Scope 1, 2 and 3 inventory?

No. It covers only the three activity types entered and should not be presented as a complete emissions inventory.
