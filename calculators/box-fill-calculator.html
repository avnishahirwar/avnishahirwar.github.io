Box Fill Calculator

Use this box fill calculator to find the correct size of electrical utility box to fit the conducting wires, grounding wires, and devices or equipment you would need to install and have it pass the National Electrical Code®.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Box Fill Calculator works with 26 quantities and 20 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • A Wires (aWires) = (nWires)
  • A Clamps (aClamps) = (clampVS)
  • A Supports (aSupports) = (supportVS)
  • A Device (aDevice) = (2 × nDevice)
  • Max Conductor VA (maxConductorVA) = maxWireVolume
  • Max Wire Volume (maxWireVolume) = maxConductorVA
  • Max Grounding Wire Volume (maxGroundingWireVolume) = maxGroundingVA
  • Wire Volume (wireVolume) = (maxWireVolume)

12 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
