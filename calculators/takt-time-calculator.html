Formulas used by this calculator

The Takt Time Calculator works with 15 quantities and 15 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Net production time (net_production_time) = demand × takt_time
  • Takt time (takt_time) = net_production_time/demand
  • Demand (demand) = net_production_time/takt_time
  • Net production time weekly (net_production_time_weekly) = demand_weekly × takt_time_weekly
  • Takt time weekly (takt_time_weekly) = net_production_time_weekly/demand_weekly
  • Demand weekly (demand_weekly) = net_production_time_weekly/takt_time_weekly
  • Net production time batch (net_production_time_batch) = demand_batch × takt_time_batch/items_per_batch
  • Takt time batch (takt_time_batch) = items_per_batch × net_production_time_batch/demand_batch

7 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
