Formulas used by this calculator

The SSA Triangle Calculator works with 10 quantities and 12 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Angle Alpha (angleAlpha) = asin(a × sin(angleBeta)/b)
  • Angle Gamma (angleGamma) = asin(c × sin(angleBeta)/b)
  • Angle Beta (angleBeta) = asin(b × sin(angleAlpha)/a)
  • b (b) = a × sin(angleBeta)/sin(angleAlpha)
  • a (a) = b × sin(angleAlpha)/sin(angleBeta)
  • c (c) = b × sin(angleGamma)/sin(angleBeta)

6 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
