Trigonometry formulas

sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse
cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse
tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent
cot θ = 1 ÷ tan θ · sec θ = 1 ÷ cos θ · csc θ = 1 ÷ sin θ

For the right-triangle solver, opposite = hypotenuse × sin(θ), adjacent = hypotenuse × cos(θ), and the remaining acute angle = 90° − θ.

Degrees and radians

A full turn is 360 degrees or 2π radians. Convert degrees to radians by multiplying by π/180; convert radians to degrees by multiplying by 180/π. The first calculator accepts either unit.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter an angle and select degrees or radians.
  2. Choose the number of decimal places and calculate the six functions.
  3. For a right triangle, enter its hypotenuse and an acute angle in degrees, then select Solve triangle.

Frequently asked questions

What does a trigonometry calculator calculate?

It evaluates sine, cosine, tangent, cotangent, secant and cosecant for the angle you enter. It also solves two missing sides and the other acute angle of a right triangle when its hypotenuse and one acute angle are known.

When are trigonometric values undefined?

Cotangent is undefined when sine is zero, secant when cosine is zero, and cosecant when sine is zero. Tangent is undefined when cosine is zero. The calculator identifies these cases rather than displaying an infinite value.

Can I enter radians?

Yes. Select Radians in the angle-unit field. The right-triangle solver uses degrees and clearly labels that input.
