Rent vs Buy Home Calculator

Compare renting and buying using your own home price, down payment, loan tenure, rent and resale assumptions.

Formula and calculation method

Loan principal = home purchase price − down payment.

Monthly EMI = P × i × (1 + i)n ÷ ((1 + i)n − 1), where P is the loan principal, i is the monthly interest rate and n is the loan tenure in months. At 0% interest, EMI = principal ÷ tenure months.

Remaining loan balance is calculated after the EMIs paid during the comparison period. Estimated home equity = assumed resale value − remaining loan balance.

Buying path net cost = down payment + EMIs paid + ownership maintenance + transaction costs − estimated home equity.

Rent path cost = sum of monthly rent, applying the entered annual rent increase at each year boundary. The difference shown is rent path cost − buying path net cost; a positive result means buying is lower in this simplified model.

Worked example

Assume a home price of ₹50,00,000, a ₹10,00,000 down payment, an 8.5% annual loan rate and a 20-year loan tenure. Compare 10 years of ownership with monthly rent of ₹20,000 and a 5% annual rent increase. Enter monthly maintenance of ₹3,000, transaction costs of ₹1,50,000 and an assumed resale value of ₹70,00,000.

  • Loan principal: ₹40,00,000.
  • Estimated monthly EMI: about ₹34,713.
  • EMIs paid over 10 years: about ₹41,65,552.
  • Estimated remaining loan balance: about ₹27,99,753.
  • Estimated home equity at resale: about ₹42,00,247.
  • Estimated buying path net cost: about ₹14,75,304.
  • Estimated rent paid over 10 years: about ₹30,18,694.

Illustration only. These figures depend on the assumptions entered and exclude costs listed below.

Important assumptions and limitations

  • The comparison period and loan tenure are separate. EMI payments stop when the loan is fully repaid; any remaining balance is included in the equity estimate.
  • The resale value is supplied by you. The calculator does not forecast property prices or verify market values.
  • Rent increases are applied once per year. Actual lease terms may use different timing or escalation.
  • Enter stamp duty, registration and other purchase charges in transaction costs. The tool does not calculate statutory rates or tax benefits.
  • It does not fully model the opportunity cost of the down payment, differences in monthly cash flow, rent deposits, property tax, insurance, major repairs, selling costs, vacancy or investment returns.
  • Mortgage rates, maintenance, rent and resale values are uncertain. A lower modeled cost is not a guarantee or a recommendation to buy or rent.

Frequently asked questions

Does a lower modeled cost mean I should buy a home?

No. This is a simplified scenario comparison, not a recommendation. Location, liquidity, stability, household needs, legal checks and costs outside the model also matter.

Does the calculator include the remaining home-loan balance?

Yes. It estimates the balance after the number of EMI payments made during the comparison period and subtracts that balance from the assumed resale value to estimate equity.

Are stamp duty, registration and taxes included?

Only if you enter applicable purchase and transaction costs in that field. The calculator does not determine statutory rates or tax benefits.

What if the comparison period is longer than the loan tenure?

The model stops counting EMI payments after the loan is fully repaid and treats the remaining loan balance as zero.
