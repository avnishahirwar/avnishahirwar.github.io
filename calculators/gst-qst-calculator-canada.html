Formula and step-by-step calculation

For a taxable Québec supply, the two-step method is GST = pre-tax amount × 5% and QST = pre-tax amount × 9.975%; total before transaction-specific rounding is 114.975% of the net amount. For a tax-included amount, this page backs out the net amount using total ÷ 1.14975.

Worked example: a CAD 100 taxable Québec purchase gives GST CAD 5.00 and QST CAD 9.98 when each tax is rounded to cents, for an estimated total of CAD 114.98.

Limitations

Exempt and zero-rated supplies, special place-of-supply rules, recipient status, and transaction-specific rounding can change the result. Revenu Québec describes separate two-step and one-step till methods; this page uses the two-step method.

Frequently asked questions

Does this result guarantee a benefit or final liability?

No. This calculator is deliberately limited to Québec. Other provinces and territories use different GST/HST/PST systems and rates.

What should I verify before relying on a result?

It is an estimate for general taxable supplies. Exempt, zero-rated or special transactions need the official CRA/Revenu Québec rules.
