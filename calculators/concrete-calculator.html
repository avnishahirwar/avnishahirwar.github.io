Formula and methodology

Wet volume = length × width × thickness. The estimate multiplies wet volume by a nominal dry-volume factor of 1.54, then divides dry material volume according to the entered cement:sand:aggregate parts. Cement mass assumes a nominal bulk density of 1,440 kg/m³ and 50 kg bags.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values requested in the form.
  2. Check the units and assumptions before calculating.
  3. Review the result and use it as an estimate, not a substitute for professional advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a structural mix design?

No. This is a rough quantity estimator only. Structural concrete requires a specified mix design and engineering review.

Does it include wastage?

No. Add an appropriate allowance separately based on site conditions and contractor advice.
