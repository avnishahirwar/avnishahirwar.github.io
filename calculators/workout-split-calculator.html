Estimate weekly sets, exercises and repetitions from a simple training split.

Enter your planned split.

What this calculates

The output multiplies training days by exercises per session, sets per exercise and repetitions per set. It shows the planned totals only; it does not decide which muscles to train, how often to rest, or whether the workload is appropriate for you.

Planning note: A larger weekly total is not automatically better. Balance training with technique, recovery, sleep and individual guidance.
