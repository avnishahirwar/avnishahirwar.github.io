The solar panel wattage calculator will help you find your recommended solar panel wattage requirement depending on your electricity consumption.

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Solar Panel Wattage Calculator works with 15 quantities and 20 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Bill offset percentage (bill_offset_percentage) = 365 × efficiency_percentage × solar_array_size × solar_hours_per_day/kWh_per_year
  • Efficiency percentage (efficiency_percentage) = (1/365) × bill_offset_percentage × kWh_per_year/(solar_array_size × solar_hours_per_day)
  • Solar hours per day (solar_hours_per_day) = (1/365) × bill_offset_percentage × kWh_per_year/(efficiency_percentage × solar_array_size)
  • K Wh per year (kWh_per_year) = 365 × efficiency_percentage × solar_array_size × solar_hours_per_day/bill_offset_percentage
  • Solar array size (solar_array_size) = (1/365) × bill_offset_percentage × kWh_per_year/(efficiency_percentage × solar_hours_per_day)
  • Solar hours per day USA (solar_hours_per_day_USA) = (1/365) × bill_offset_percentage × kWh_per_year/(efficiency_percentage × solar_array_size_USA)
  • Solar hours per day known (solar_hours_per_day_known) = (1/365) × bill_offset_percentage × kWh_per_year/(efficiency_percentage × solar_array_size_known)
  • Solar hours per day canada (solar_hours_per_day_canada) = (1/365) × bill_offset_percentage × kWh_per_year/(efficiency_percentage × solar_array_size_canada)

12 further rearrangements of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
