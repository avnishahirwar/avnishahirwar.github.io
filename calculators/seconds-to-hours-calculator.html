How to use this calculator

Enter a non-negative duration in seconds. Fractional seconds are supported. The calculator converts the duration into decimal hours, total minutes and a breakdown of complete hours, minutes and remaining seconds.

Example: 7,200 seconds ÷ 3,600 = 2 hours, or 120 minutes.

Formula and method

Hours = seconds ÷ 3,600. Minutes = seconds ÷ 60.

Understanding the result

Use decimal hours for spreadsheet calculations or time-based estimates. The hours-minutes-seconds breakdown is easier to read as a duration.

Important limitations

This tool converts units only. It does not determine payroll, overtime, billable hours or contractual working time. Apply the relevant workplace or service rules separately.

Frequently asked questions

How many seconds are in one hour?

One hour contains 3,600 seconds.

How do I convert 7,200 seconds?

7,200 ÷ 3,600 = 2 hours.

Can I enter a duration longer than one day?

Yes. The result is shown as total hours, not a clock time.
