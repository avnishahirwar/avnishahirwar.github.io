Formula and methodology

The estimate subtracts 266 days (38 weeks) from the estimated due date, reflecting a commonly used average interval from conception to the due date. The actual date can vary by several days or more.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the values requested in the form.
  2. Check the units and assumptions before calculating.
  3. Review the result and use it as an estimate, not a substitute for professional advice.

Calculations run locally in your browser. Do not enter passwords, account numbers or other sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Can this identify the exact conception date?

No. It provides an approximate date only. Biological timing varies, and an estimated due date may itself be revised.

Should I use this for medical decisions?

No. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for pregnancy dating or medical questions.
