Formula and method

Square feet = Bigha × locally verified square feet per Bigha. Square metres = square feet × 0.09290304. Acres = square feet ÷ 43,560.

For land purchases, mutation, registry, partition or boundary disputes, use the unit and area recorded by the relevant local revenue authority. A conversion website cannot establish the legal area of a parcel.

Frequently asked questions

Is one Bigha the same size in every state?

No. Bigha varies by region and local practice. Confirm the correct factor for the exact district and village.

Why can two websites show different Bigha values?

They may use different regional definitions. Always check the factor against the local land record or registration authority.

Can I change the conversion factor?

Yes. Enter the square-feet-per-Bigha value used by the local authority or survey record.

Is this suitable for a property registry?

It is a mathematical estimate only. Use official land records and obtain local professional confirmation for a legal transaction.
