Portfolio Rebalancing Calculator

Formula and method

Total portfolio value = current asset 1 + current asset 2 + current asset 3. Target value for each asset = total portfolio value × target weight ÷ 100. Trade amount = target value − current value. A positive trade amount indicates a theoretical buy; a negative amount indicates a theoretical sell.

Worked example

If current holdings total ₹2,00,000 and target weights are 50%, 30% and 20%, the target values are ₹1,00,000, ₹60,000 and ₹40,000. Compare these with current holdings to estimate the rebalance amount.

Important limitations

This educational calculator does not recommend an allocation or a trade. It excludes taxes, spreads, brokerage, liquidity, minimum order sizes and changes in market value during execution. Consider applicable rules and qualified guidance before making financial decisions. Values are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to Friendly Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Do target weights guarantee returns?

No. They are allocation inputs only and do not predict performance.

Are taxes and dealing costs included?

No. Add those costs separately before acting on an illustrative rebalance.
