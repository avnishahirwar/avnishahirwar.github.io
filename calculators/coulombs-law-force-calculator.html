Coulomb’s Law Force Calculator

Calculate the electrostatic force between two point charges from their charges and separation distance.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

F = k × q1 × q2 ÷ r², where k = 8.9875 × 10⁹ N·m²/C². Enter charge magnitudes; like charges repel and unlike charges attract.

Worked example

With Charge 1 0.000002, Charge 2 0.000003, Distance 0.05, the calculator gives: Force magnitude (N) = 21.5701.

How to read the result

The force falls with the square of distance, so doubling the separation cuts it to a quarter. This applies to point charges in a vacuum or air.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know whether the force attracts or repels?

Like charges repel and opposite charges attract. The calculator reports magnitude only.

What changes in a material instead of air?

The force is reduced by the material’s relative permittivity.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool uses idealised textbook formulas for learning and quick estimates. Real systems include friction, losses, tolerances and measurement error. Do not use it as the only basis for safety-critical or regulated engineering design.
