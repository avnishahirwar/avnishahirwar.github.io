Rates and calculation method

For a standard residential purchase, the calculator applies 0% to the first £125,000, 2% to the portion up to £250,000, 5% up to £925,000, 10% up to £1.5 million and 12% above £1.5 million. Higher rates for additional properties add 5 percentage points. The non-UK resident surcharge adds 2 percentage points where applicable.

Eligible first-time buyers pay 0% on the first £300,000 and 5% on the portion up to £500,000. First-time buyer relief is unavailable if the purchase price exceeds £500,000.

Rates checked 16 November 2026 against GOV.UK residential SDLT rates and the 2026 tax rates and allowances.

Important limitations

This estimate covers standard residential transactions in England and Northern Ireland. Scotland uses LBTT and Wales uses LTT. Special reliefs, exemptions, leasehold transactions, company purchases and linked transactions are not modelled. Check HMRC guidance or a qualified adviser before relying on a result.

Frequently asked questions

Which UK tax year does this calculator use?

It uses the residential Stamp Duty Land Tax rates in force from 1 April 2025, including the higher-rate and non-resident surcharges shown on the page.

Does this cover Scotland or Wales?

No. Scotland uses Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (LBTT), and Wales uses Land Transaction Tax (LTT). This calculator covers England and Northern Ireland.

Is first-time buyer relief available above £500,000?

No. The first-time buyer relief modelled here is unavailable when the purchase price exceeds £500,000. Check the current HMRC eligibility conditions.
