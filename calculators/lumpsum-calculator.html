How the lumpsum formula works

Future value = P × (1 + r)t
Inflation-adjusted value = Future value ÷ (1 + i)t

Here, P is the one-time investment, r is the assumed annualised return expressed as a decimal, t is the investment period in years, and i is the assumed annual inflation rate as a decimal. This page uses a constant annualised growth assumption (CAGR-style) for the entire period.

Worked example: ₹1,00,000 invested once at an assumed 12% annualised return for 10 years has an estimated future value of about ₹3,10,585 before taxes, expenses and other costs. This is a formula illustration—not a forecast of any mutual fund's NAV or return.

What this projection does—and does not—show

A mutual fund's actual NAV changes over time. This calculator does not download historical or live NAVs, model daily market movements, select a scheme, or predict future performance. The constant annualised assumption is a simplified way to compare possible outcomes from a one-time investment.

  • It models one initial amount; it does not add monthly SIP contributions or withdrawals.
  • It does not include fund expense ratios, exit loads, brokerage, transaction costs or taxes.
  • The inflation-adjusted figure is a purchasing-power illustration, not a tax-adjusted redemption amount.
  • A negative assumed return is allowed so you can model a loss scenario as well as positive growth.

Mutual-fund investments are market-linked and can lose value. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Review scheme documents and relevant official disclosures before making an investment decision.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Lumpsum Calculator estimate?

It estimates the future value of one initial investment using the assumed annualised return and period entered. It is a mathematical illustration, not a prediction or guarantee.

What formula does the Lumpsum Calculator use?

Future value = P × (1 + r)^t, where P is the initial investment, r is the assumed annual return expressed as a decimal, and t is the investment period in years.

Does this calculator use actual mutual-fund NAV data?

No. It applies a constant assumed annualised rate. It does not fetch NAV data or simulate the changing market value of a mutual fund.

Does the estimate include taxes and fund expenses?

No. Taxes, expense ratios, exit loads and transaction costs are excluded. The optional inflation input estimates purchasing power but does not calculate tax or fees.
