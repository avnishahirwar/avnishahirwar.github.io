Crop Yield per Hectare Calculator

Convert total harvested weight and field area into yield per hectare in kilograms, quintals and tonnes.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Yield per hectare = total production ÷ area in hectares.

Worked example

With Total production 5,400, Cultivated area 1.5, the calculator gives: Yield (kg per hectare) = 3,600; Yield (quintals per hectare) = 36; Yield (tonnes per hectare) = 3.6.

How to read the result

Compare yield with district averages and with your own earlier seasons to judge the effect of seed, fertiliser or water changes. Use the same moisture basis each time.

Frequently asked questions

How many square metres are in a hectare?

One hectare is 10,000 square metres.

Should production be measured wet or dry?

Use the same basis each season, usually the standard moisture level used when selling the crop.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives planning estimates. Actual requirements depend on soil tests, crop variety, season, local recommendations and input quality. Follow advice from your local agricultural extension service before purchasing inputs.
