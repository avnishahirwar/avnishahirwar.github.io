Formula and method

Predicted maximum HR = 208 − 0.7 × age (Tanaka population equation). Broad reference zones are calculated as 50–60%, 60–70%, 70–80%, 80–90%, and 90–100% of that estimate. Cycling-specific training models may use different boundaries or a measured threshold.

Worked sample

At age 40, predicted maximum HR is 180 bpm; the five broad ranges are 90–108, 108–126, 126–144, 144–162, and 162–180 bpm (rounding to whole bpm).

Validation and limitations

Age must be between 1 and 120. Age-based maximum heart rate varies substantially between individuals; these ranges are estimates, not medical targets or training prescriptions. Stop exercise and seek qualified advice for concerning symptoms; this page is not medical advice.
