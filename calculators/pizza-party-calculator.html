Pizza Party Calculator

Enter the number of guests and your planned slices per guest and per pizza.

Use the calculator

How to use this calculator

Use the number of guests and your preferred serving assumption. Adjust slices per pizza to match the size and cut used by your vendor.

Formula and methodology

Total slices = guests × slices per guest. Pizzas to order = ceiling(total slices ÷ slices per pizza).

Worked example

8 guests at 3 slices each need 24 slices. With 8 slices per pizza, the estimate is 3 pizzas.

Important limitations

Food quantities and preparation outcomes vary by ingredient, equipment, recipe and method. Treat yield factors and serving assumptions as estimates and adjust them to your own recipe.

Frequently asked questions

Does this predict how hungry guests will be?

No. It is a planning estimate based only on the serving assumptions you enter.

Can I change the slices per pizza?

Yes. Enter the slice count supplied by the restaurant or expected from your chosen pizza size.
