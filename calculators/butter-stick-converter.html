Formula

US convention: 1 butter stick = ½ US cup = 8 tablespoons = 4 weight ounces ≈ 113.4 g. The converter scales these fixed factors linearly.

The calculation runs in your browser. Check units and assumptions before relying on a result.

Worked example

1 stick = ½ cup = 8 tablespoons = 4 weight ounces ≈ 113.4 g. Two sticks ≈ 226.8 g.

Assumptions and limitations

  • This is the US stick convention; butter packaging and stick sizes can differ in other countries.
  • The gram figure is approximate and assumes a standard US stick of about 113.4 g.
  • Ounces here are weight ounces, not US fluid ounces. Actual product density, salted/unsalted packaging and cutting accuracy can affect a recipe.
