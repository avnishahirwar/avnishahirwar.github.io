Frequently asked questions

Monthly milk = animals × average daily milk per animal × days. Revenue = milk × sale price. Operating surplus = revenue − feed cost − other monthly costs. This simplified estimate excludes dry periods, veterinary emergencies, labour, depreciation, breeding, loan costs and capital investment unless entered in other costs.
