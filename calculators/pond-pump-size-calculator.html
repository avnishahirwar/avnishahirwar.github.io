Formula and method

Required flow = pond volume × daily turns ÷ 24.

Frequently asked questions

Why turn the water?

Filtration and oxygen exchange. Koi-heavy ponds turn 2+ times daily.

Head height?

Add flow loss for every metre of lift; check pump curves.

Which is fine?

Most healthy fish tolerate 8-12% salt for short therapeutic baths.
