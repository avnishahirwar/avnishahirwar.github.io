Post Office MIS formula and current rate

Updated: 13 November 2026. The Post Office Monthly Income Scheme rate is 7.4% per annum for Q3 FY 2026–27 (1 October–31 December 2026). Verify the quarterly government notification and India Post scheme conditions.

Formula: Estimated monthly interest = Deposit × annual interest rate ÷ 12. Principal is separate and is returned at maturity under the scheme rules.

Worked example: A ₹5,00,000 deposit at 7.4% produces an estimated monthly interest of ₹3,083.33 (before any applicable rounding). The actual payout follows the official account terms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Post Office MIS rate for October–December 2026?

The rate is 7.4% per annum for Q3 FY 2026–27. Check the latest official notification before opening or renewing an account.

How is monthly MIS interest estimated?

Multiply the deposit by the annual rate and divide by 12. The official payout and rounding rules apply to the account.

Does the monthly interest include return of principal?

No. Monthly payouts represent interest. Principal is handled separately at maturity according to the scheme terms.
