Music Duration Calculator

The music duration calculator determines how long your musical piece will take to perform by using the piece's time signature, tempo, and number of measures, along with an easy formula.

Enter values

How it works

This calculator evaluates the displayed equation variants locally in your browser. Enter the known values; results are shown for equations whose required inputs are available.

Check units, assumptions and professional guidance before using results for medical, financial, legal, engineering or safety-critical decisions.

Formulas used by this calculator

The Music Duration Calculator works with 7 quantities and 7 relations between them. Enter the values you know, leave the one you want to find blank, and press Calculate. Blank fields are never treated as zero. Each formula below is solved for one quantity.

  • Num staffs (num_staffs) = stafftime/duration
  • Stafftime (stafftime) = duration × num_staffs
  • Duration (duration) = stafftime/num_staffs
  • Number of measures (number_of_measures) = (1/60) × duration × tempo/beats_per_measure
  • Beats per measure (beats_per_measure) = (1/60) × duration × tempo/number_of_measures
  • Tempo (tempo) = 60 × beats_per_measure × number_of_measures/duration

1 further rearrangement of the same relations let you solve for other quantities.
