How PPF interest works

Maturity = sum of yearly deposits, each compounding annually at the announced rate. Assuming deposits at the start of each year: FV = P × [((1+r)ⁿ − 1) ÷ r] × (1+r)
Example: ₹60,000/year at 7.1% for 15 years, assuming each deposit is made at the start of the year → invested ₹9,00,000, estimated interest ≈ ₹7.27 lakh, maturity ≈ ₹16.27 lakh.

Key PPF rules

  • Tenure: 15 years, extendable in 5-year blocks.
  • Investment limit: ₹1.5 lakh per financial year (all accounts combined).
  • Tax: deposits qualify under Section 80C; maturity is fully tax-free (EEE).
  • Loan and partial withdrawal facilities are available from specific years.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current PPF interest rate?

The government revises PPF rates quarterly; it has stayed at 7.1% p.a. compounded annually in recent years. Enter the latest announced rate above.

What is the minimum and maximum PPF deposit?

Minimum ₹500 per financial year; maximum ₹1.5 lakh per financial year across all PPF accounts.

Can I extend PPF after 15 years?

Yes — in blocks of 5 years, with or without further deposits.

PPF calculator guide: contributions and maturity

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a Government of India small-savings scheme with rules for deposits, interest, tenure, extensions and withdrawals. This calculator estimates how contributions may grow under the interest assumption entered. Rates and scheme rules can change, so verify current information through official government or post-office sources.

How a PPF projection works

A simplified annual model compounds an eligible balance and adds assumed contributions. Actual PPF interest follows scheme-specific rules, including the balance eligible for interest during a month. Deposit dates within a financial year can therefore affect the official interest calculation, and a simplified annual projection may not match the passbook.

Why timing matters

Deposits made earlier in the financial year can have a different interest impact from deposits made later, subject to the scheme's monthly balance rules. If you contribute in multiple instalments, timing matters. Check whether the calculator assumes one annual contribution or a different pattern.

PPF planning checklist

  • Confirm the current notified interest rate.
  • Check current annual deposit limits and eligibility.
  • Understand original tenure and extension conditions.
  • Review loan and partial-withdrawal rules.
  • Keep deposit receipts and account statements.

Tax and eligibility

Tax treatment depends on current law and individual circumstances. A calculator cannot verify eligibility, account status, contribution limits across accounts or withdrawal conditions. Use the result to compare contribution scenarios, then confirm account-level figures against your passbook and official scheme rules.

Limits

A simplified estimate may not include every deposit date, account irregularity, rule change, extension option or official rounding convention. It is a planning aid, not an official maturity quotation or tax determination.
