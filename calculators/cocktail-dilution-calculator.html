Formula and method

Dilution is roughly 20% stirred or 25% shaken of the total pre-ice volume.

Worked example

A 60 ml spirit plus 20 ml mixer stirred gains about 16 ml of water, reaching 96 ml total.

Frequently asked questions

Why dilute?

Water softens alcohol burn and opens up aroma.

Ice and temperature?

Large, wet ice dilutes slower; crushed ice dilutes much faster.

Batching?

Add bottled water at the same percentage when batching ahead.
