How to use this calculator

Enter a non-negative number of minutes. Fractional minutes are supported. The calculator converts the same duration into decimal hours and complete hours plus remaining minutes.

Example: 150 minutes ÷ 60 = 2.5 hours, which is 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Formula and method

Decimal hours = minutes ÷ 60. Whole hours = floor(minutes ÷ 60); remaining minutes = minutes mod 60.

Understanding the result

Use decimal hours for spreadsheet calculations or time-based estimates. Use the hours-and-minutes breakdown when you need to read a duration quickly.

Important limitations

This tool converts units only. It does not determine payroll, overtime, billable hours or contractual working time. Apply the relevant workplace or service rules separately.

Frequently asked questions

How many minutes are in one hour?

One hour contains 60 minutes.

How do I convert 150 minutes?

150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 hours, or 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Can I enter fractional minutes?

Yes. Enter a decimal value such as 12.5 minutes.
