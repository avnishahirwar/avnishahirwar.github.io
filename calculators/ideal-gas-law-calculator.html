Calculate pressure for an ideal gas from amount, absolute temperature, and volume.

Formula and method

P = nRT/V with R = 8.314462618 Pa·m³/(mol·K). Inputs must use the units stated above. This calculator performs only the displayed idealized calculation; it does not model unlisted effects.

Worked sample

1 mol at 273.15 K in 0.022414 m³ gives about 101325 Pa.

Validation and limitations

Inputs are checked for finite values and physically required positive quantities. Results are browser-only and are educational estimates; verify assumptions and units before practical use. This is not an engineering or safety certification tool.
