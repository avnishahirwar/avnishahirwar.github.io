Formula and method

Puppies use RER = 70 × weight to the power 0.75, multiplied by a growth factor near 2-3.

Worked example

A 5 kg, 16-week puppy at factor 2 needs about 658 kcal, roughly 173 g of 3,800 kcal/kg food.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Important limitations

This page performs one calculation only and models neither every real-world factor nor professional advice. Do not use a single estimate as the basis for a medical, financial or safety decision.

Frequently asked questions

Always check the breed chart

Large-breed puppies need controlled calcium and slower growth; follow a vet plan.

Free feeding?

Scheduled meals make house-training and growth monitoring easier.

Weight weekly?

Weigh pups weekly and adjust portions with the growth curve.
