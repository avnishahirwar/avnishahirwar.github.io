Formula and method

Day rate = hourly rate × billable hours per day.

Worked example

An 8,000-rupee day rate over 8 billable hours equals 1,000 an hour.

How to use the result

Treat the result as an educational estimate built from the inputs you entered and the formula above. Real-world results vary with measurement accuracy, local conditions and rounding, so verify important decisions against an authoritative source.

Frequently asked questions

Same result both ways?

Only if the same billable hours are always used in both directions.

Short days?

Trial days and half-days are often booked at 50-70% of the day rate.

Do travel hours count?

Decide and state the policy in your contract.
