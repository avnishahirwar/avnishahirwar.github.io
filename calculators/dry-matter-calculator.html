Dry Matter Calculator

How the calculation works

Dry matter (%) = net dried sample mass ÷ net original as-fed sample mass × 100. Moisture by difference (%) = 100 − dry matter (%). Use the same mass unit for both measurements.

University of Georgia and Nebraska Extension describe feed dry-matter calculations from net original and dried sample weights. AAFCO cautions that loss on drying can include volatile substances other than water.

Worked example

For 100 g of net as-fed sample and 40 g of net dried sample, dry matter = 40 ÷ 100 × 100 = 40%; moisture by difference = 60%.

Important limits

Scope is an animal-feed/forage sample only. This result is not a ration, nutrient conversion, feed intake, or feeding recommendation. Use net sample masses, exclude container tare, and follow a suitable drying method. In some feeds, drying may remove volatile substances as well as water, so moisture by difference is not necessarily true water content. Use a validated laboratory method for consequential decisions.
