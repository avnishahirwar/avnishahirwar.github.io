Validation and limitations

Inputs must be finite and positive; denominators and target values must be greater than zero. Servings estimate = batch liters × servings per liter. Serving size is user-defined; this does not formulate a safe recipe. Results are estimates based only on the entered assumptions. For food safety, follow current official guidance and product instructions; this tool does not replace them.
