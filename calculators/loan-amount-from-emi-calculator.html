Loan Amount from EMI Calculator

Work out how large a loan you can afford from the monthly EMI you can pay, the interest rate and the tenure.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Loan amount = EMI × (1 − (1 + r)^−n) ÷ r, where r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Worked example

With Affordable monthly EMI 25,000, Annual interest rate 9, Loan tenure 15, the calculator gives: Loan amount you can service = 24,64,835.22; Total of all EMIs = 45,00,000; Total interest paid = 20,35,164.78.

How to read the result

Start from what your monthly budget allows rather than from the property or vehicle price. Lenders also apply income, credit-score and obligation limits, so the amount shown is a ceiling for planning, not an approval.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer tenure raise the loan amount I can afford?

Yes. A longer tenure lowers the EMI for the same loan, so the same EMI supports a larger principal. The trade-off is much more total interest.

Are processing fees included?

No. Fees, insurance and taxes are separate costs that reduce the amount actually available to spend.

Assumptions and limitations
