Formula and method

Total flow = emitters × emitter flow. Runtime = target litres ÷ flow per plant unit.

Worked example

Fifty 2 L/h emitters need 100 L/h; a 10 L plant target with one plant per emitter runs about 5 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Pressure matters?

Emitter ratings assume a stated pressure, typically around 1-2 bar; low pressure lowers flow.

Blocks or laterals?

Zone by matching flow demand to your supply capacity.

Filter?

Always filter; emitters clog with unfiltered water.
