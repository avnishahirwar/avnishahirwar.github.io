Formula and method

The calculator reads the final three digits first, then groups remaining digits in pairs. The group names follow Indian place values. For example, 12,34,567 is read as twelve lakh, thirty-four thousand, five hundred sixty-seven.

This is a spelling aid. It does not verify the source amount or create a legally valid cheque, invoice or receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Does this use lakh and crore?

Yes. Indian grouping is used instead of million and billion.

Can I enter decimals or paise?

No. This version accepts whole numbers only. Decimal amounts and paise need a separate conversion step.

Is the result suitable for legal documents?

It is a spelling aid. Verify the amount and exact document wording before use.
