Triangle Angle Calculator

Calculate all three interior angles from the side lengths of a valid triangle.

How to use this calculator

Enter the three positive side lengths in the same unit. The values must satisfy the triangle inequality before angles can be calculated.

Enter measurements in consistent units and check the selected shape or method. The calculator uses the dimensions entered on this page and does not infer missing measurements.

Formula and method

The cosine rule calculates A = cos⁻¹((b²+c²−a²)/(2bc)); the other angles are calculated cyclically. The three interior angles sum to 180°.

Understanding the result

Area is expressed in square units, volume in cubic units, and perimeter or circumference in linear units. Keep those dimensions distinct when interpreting or comparing results.

Important limitations

These tools model ideal two-dimensional shapes and regular geometric solids. They do not account for measurement uncertainty, irregular boundaries, material thickness, openings or real-world construction tolerances.
