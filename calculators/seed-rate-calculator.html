Seed Rate Calculator

Calculate the seed quantity needed for a field from area, target plant population, seed count per kilogram and germination rate.

Enter your values

Formula and methodology

Seeds needed = area (m²) × target plants per m² ÷ germination rate. Seed weight = seeds needed ÷ seeds per kilogram.

Worked example

With Field area 2, Target plants per m² 40, Seeds per kilogram 28,000, Germination rate 90, the calculator gives: Total seeds needed = 8,88,888.89; Seed quantity (kg) = 31.75; Seed per hectare (kg) = 15.87.

How to read the result

Add a margin for seedling loss from pests, weather or poor soil contact. Use the seed count per kilogram printed on your seed lot, because it changes with variety and size.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find the germination rate?

It is shown on the seed packet or test certificate. Old seed usually germinates less well.

Does this include a safety margin?

No. Add extra seed if your conditions are difficult.

Assumptions and limitations

This tool gives planning estimates. Actual requirements depend on soil tests, crop variety, season, local recommendations and input quality. Follow advice from your local agricultural extension service before purchasing inputs.
