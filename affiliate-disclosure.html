Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Friendly Calculator aims to keep its calculators and educational resources useful and accessible. Some pages may include clearly identified affiliate or sponsored links to third-party products or services that are relevant to the page.

Amazon Associates disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Our The ONE Thing (Hindi Edition) book recommendation contains an Amazon affiliate link. It is labelled near the link so visitors can identify the commercial relationship before leaving this website.

How affiliate links work

If an affiliate link is introduced, Friendly Calculator may receive a commission when a visitor makes a qualifying purchase or completes an eligible action after following that link. The visitor should not pay an additional amount because of the referral, unless the partner's own terms say otherwise.

How recommendations are selected

Any future recommendation should be relevant to the calculator or educational topic, clearly distinguish editorial information from paid placement, and avoid claims of guaranteed savings, returns, loan approval or financial outcomes. A commercial relationship must not determine calculator results or editorial calculations.

Clear labelling

Affiliate links will be labelled near the link with plain language such as “Affiliate link” or “We may earn a commission”. Sponsored placements will be identified separately as “Sponsored”. Partner-specific wording will be added only after the relevant programme accepts the website and provides its required disclosure text.

Independent decisions

Visitors should review a provider's eligibility rules, fees, risks, privacy terms and product conditions before deciding whether to use its service. Friendly Calculator does not provide personalised investment, tax, credit or legal advice. Calculator outputs are estimates based on the inputs and assumptions shown on each tool.

Advertising and privacy

Third-party partners may apply their own privacy and tracking practices after a visitor leaves Friendly Calculator. Please review the provider's privacy notice. For information about this website's own data practices, read our Privacy Policy.
