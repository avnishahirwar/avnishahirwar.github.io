How the EMI is estimated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

For this example, P = ₹5,00,000, r = 8% ÷ 12 ÷ 100, and n depends on the selected tenure.

What this estimate excludes

  • Processing fees, insurance, taxes and other lender charges
  • Floating-rate changes, missed payments or prepayments
  • Different lender conventions for payment dates and rounding

Actual EMI and total cost depend on the lender’s amortisation schedule and loan agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Does a longer tenure reduce the EMI?

Usually yes, because the principal is spread over more monthly payments. However, total interest paid is generally higher.

Will my bank quote exactly this EMI?

Not necessarily. Fees, payment dates, rate type, rounding and lender-specific terms can change the final schedule.
