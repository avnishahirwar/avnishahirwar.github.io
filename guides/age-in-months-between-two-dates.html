How to Calculate Age in Months Between Two Dates

Use calendar months—not a fixed 30-day shortcut—when you need the number of complete months between two dates. The result can differ from dividing total days by 30 because months have different lengths.

Example

From 1 January 2020 to 1 January 2026 is exactly 72 calendar months (6 years). If the end date falls before the matching day of the month, the final partial month is not counted as a complete month.

Steps

  1. Enter the start date or date of birth.
  2. Enter the end date or “as of” date.
  3. Use the age calculator to read years, months and days, then combine years × 12 with the remaining complete months when a month total is needed.

Important distinction

“Complete months” and “approximate months” are different measures. For legal, financial or eligibility deadlines, follow the relevant authority’s date-counting rules rather than relying on a general calculator.
