CGPA to Percentage in India: Check Your University Formula

There is no single CGPA-to-percentage conversion formula that applies to every Indian university, board or academic programme. The conversion factor and rounding rules are institution-specific.

Before converting your CGPA

  1. Check the grading or examination regulations for your university and academic year.
  2. Look for an official conversion formula on your marksheet, transcript or university website.
  3. Apply the stated multiplier or formula and follow the institution’s rounding rule.
  4. If no conversion rule is published, ask the examination or registrar office instead of assuming a generic multiplier.

What about Mumbai University?

Do not assume a formula from another university applies to University of Mumbai. Confirm the applicable ordinance or examination circular for your programme and year before using a conversion result for admissions, employment or official submissions.
