GLOBAL ACCOUNTING TOOLKIT

Chartered Accountant & Accounting Calculators

A practical collection for accountants, auditors, business owners, students and finance teams: profitability, liquidity, working capital, cash flow, leverage, tax-input estimates and reporting checks.

Global scope: These tools use transparent formulas and your own inputs. They do not assume one country’s tax rate, accounting standard, filing deadline or audit rule. Confirm statutory outputs with the relevant authority or a qualified CA/CPA/ACCA professional.
