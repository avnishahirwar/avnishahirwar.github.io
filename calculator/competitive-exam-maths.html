Key formulas to revise

Percentages

Percentage = (part ÷ whole) × 100. Percentage change = ((new value − original value) ÷ original value) × 100.

Average

Arithmetic mean = sum of observations ÷ number of observations.

Ratio and proportion

For a:b = c:d, cross-products satisfy a × d = b × c. Reduce an integer ratio by dividing both terms by their greatest common divisor.

Simple probability

For equally likely outcomes, P(event) = favourable outcomes ÷ total possible outcomes.

Permutation and combination

Permutation: nPr = n! ÷ (n − r)!. Combination: nCr = n! ÷ (r! × (n − r)!).

How to practise effectively

  1. Attempt the question without a calculator and write down the formula or steps.
  2. Use the matching calculator to verify the numerical result.
  3. If the result differs, check units, signs, order of operations and whether the question asks for a percentage, percentage-point change or ratio.
  4. Repeat with new values and explain the method in your own words.
