Global Business Calculators

A practical worldwide toolkit for founders, operators, finance teams, freelancers, marketers and small businesses. These existing calculators support planning, pricing, sales, customer metrics, operations and accounting across currencies and jurisdictions.

Use across countries: Most tools perform general arithmetic from your entered values and currency. They do not determine local tax, accounting standards, employment law, statutory filing rules, payment fees or market benchmarks. Confirm jurisdiction-specific assumptions before using a result for a contract, filing or major business decision.

Business workflow

Start with the business question, use comparable periods and units, separate tax-inclusive from tax-exclusive amounts, include overlooked costs such as payment, delivery and platform fees, then reconcile the estimate with invoices, accounts and contracts. For regulated or jurisdiction-specific decisions, use the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
