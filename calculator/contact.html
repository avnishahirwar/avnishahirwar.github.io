Customer Support

Need help using a calculator or found something that is not working? Use the support options below. We welcome calculation corrections, broken-link reports, accessibility feedback and general questions.

How to get help

For the fastest investigation, send the page address, calculator name, device and browser, the steps you followed, and sample values that do not identify you. Tell us what result you expected and what appeared instead.

  • Calculation or formula issue: Include the calculator URL and example inputs/outputs.
  • Page, loading or broken-link issue: Include the page URL and the step where the issue occurs.
  • Mobile or accessibility issue: Include your device, browser and whether you use keyboard or assistive technology.
  • How-to question: Tell us which field or step is unclear. You can also review our calculation methodology.

Email customer support: Send a support request by email

Email is not an instant chat service. Response time depends on the issue and the information provided; we do not promise a fixed response time.
