Everyday calculations made simpler

Everyday calculators help with quick numerical tasks at home, while travelling or when checking routine information. Choose the tool that matches the question, enter values in the units requested and review the assumptions shown with the result. A useful estimate depends on accurate inputs as much as on the formula.

Arithmetic, dates and household costs

The Basic Calculator handles addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and common percentage calculations. The Age Calculator compares two calendar dates and reports the difference in years, months and days; official eligibility rules may use a separate cut-off date. The Electricity Bill Calculator estimates energy charges from units and a tariff assumption, while actual bills can include slabs, fixed charges, duties and adjustments.

Travel, prices and general reference

The Fuel Cost Calculator estimates trip fuel requirements from distance, mileage and price per litre. Real mileage changes with traffic, vehicle condition, route and driving style. The Discount Calculator helps compare an original price with a sale price. The Inflation Calculator illustrates how a cost could change under a selected constant rate; it is not a forecast of a specific future price.

Use health-related estimates carefully

The BMI Calculator performs a height-and-weight calculation. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis or a complete description of health. Adult BMI categories should not be applied to children or teenagers; clinical interpretation for younger people uses age- and sex-specific growth references.

Tips for reliable results

  • Check whether the tool expects centimetres or metres, kilometres or miles, and monthly or annual values.
  • Use the same time period for all inputs in a comparison.
  • Check local tariffs and prices before relying on cost estimates.
  • For official or medical decisions, consult the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
