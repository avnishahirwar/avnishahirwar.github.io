Women & Girls Calculators

A practical collection of respectful, browser-based wellness, body-measurement, pregnancy-date, age and personal-planning tools for women, girls and families.

Friendly Calculator uses this collection to bring related tools together without making assumptions about a person’s identity, health history or goals. Some tools estimate dates or show a standard formula. They do not diagnose a condition, predict a medical outcome or replace a doctor, nurse, midwife, dietitian or other qualified professional.

Use health estimates carefully

Menstrual cycles, ovulation timing, pregnancy dates, body measurements and fertility can vary substantially from one person to another. Stress, illness, medication, contraception, breastfeeding, postpartum changes, perimenopause and many other factors can affect a calendar estimate. A late period, severe pain, unusual bleeding, pregnancy concern or other symptom should be discussed with an appropriate healthcare professional. If there is an urgent concern, seek local medical help rather than relying on an online calculator.

For younger users, a trusted parent, guardian, school nurse or clinician can help explain health information in an age-appropriate way. Do not enter names, medical records or other identifying information into a browser calculator. These tools are designed to calculate from the values you enter locally; always read the individual page’s privacy, method and limitation notes.
