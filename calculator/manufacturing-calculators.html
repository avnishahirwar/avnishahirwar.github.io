Manufacturing workflow

Define the product and process boundary, standardize units, separate theoretical capacity from available capacity, include scrap and downtime assumptions, then reconcile estimates against production records and approved specifications. For safety-critical, pressure, electrical, structural or regulated decisions, use the applicable standard and a qualified engineer.
