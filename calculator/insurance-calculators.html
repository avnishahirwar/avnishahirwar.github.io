Insurance Calculators & Premium Estimator

Use the free insurance premium estimator to break down figures from an existing life, health, motor, travel or property insurance quote. The tool helps organize the numbers you enter; it does not provide an insurance quote, compare insurers or determine the price or suitability of a policy.

What the estimator calculates

Enter the base premium shown in a quote, optional add-on or rider premium, any discount displayed by the provider, the applicable tax rate and a payment frequency. The calculator totals those values and divides the estimated annual amount into equal payments.

Premium before tax = base premium + add-ons − discount.

Estimated total = premium before tax + entered tax.

The tool does not estimate risk-based pricing, medical loading, age-based rates, underwriting, no-claim bonuses, policy exclusions, claim outcomes or the amount of cover a person needs.

Insurance types and quote checks

  • Life and term insurance: check the premium breakdown and benefit illustration supplied by the insurer; policy term, exclusions, riders and underwriting can change the final terms.
  • Health insurance: review the policy schedule, waiting periods, exclusions, co-payments, room limits and renewal terms with the provider.
  • Motor insurance: compare the quote's own-damage, third-party, add-on and deductible components against the policy wording.
  • Travel and property insurance: confirm covered events, exclusions, deductibles, limits and geographic scope in the written policy.

Important limitations

This page is an educational calculation resource, not an insurance broker, insurer, quote engine or recommendation. It does not collect policy applications or personal health details. Do not enter policy numbers, identity documents or sensitive personal information. Always rely on the insurer's formal quote, policy wording and current official disclosures.
