Friendly Calculator Embed Network

Let readers calculate without leaving your article. The widget runs on Friendly Calculator, so your website does not receive visitor inputs from the iframe. The attribution below the widget connects readers to the relevant calculator page.

  1. Open the free embed builder.
  2. Select a calculator and copy the responsive iframe code.
  3. Paste it into your site’s HTML or custom HTML block.
  4. Keep the attribution line and related calculator page link unchanged.

Keep the attribution visible. Do not frame a calculator inside another iframe, remove the credit, copy the site’s content in bulk, or present an estimate as professional advice or an official quote.

Embed Network FAQ

Who can use the free widgets?

Blogs, schools, colleges, education portals, publishers, community websites and businesses may use the public iframe builder for genuine reader or learner utility, subject to the attribution and acceptable-use rules.

Does Friendly Calculator receive visitor inputs?

The browser calculator performs its calculation on the Friendly Calculator page inside the iframe. The host website does not receive those inputs through the embed. Review the Privacy Policy for analytics, hosting and API details.

Can I remove the Friendly Calculator branding?

No. The free network requires visible “Powered by Friendly Calculator” attribution and a link to the related calculator page. Contact us for approved custom or white-label arrangements.
