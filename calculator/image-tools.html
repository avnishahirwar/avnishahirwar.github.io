Privacy: Image decoding, resizing and conversion happen locally in your browser. This tool does not upload your selected image to Friendly Calculator or a third-party processing service.

Supported formats and limitations

  • Input: PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF (first frame only).
  • Output: PNG, JPEG and WebP, depending on browser support.
  • Maximum output dimension: 6,000 pixels per side and 20 megapixels total.
  • Transparent areas are preserved in PNG and WebP. JPEG output uses a white background.
  • Animated GIF frames, metadata, EXIF details and colour profiles are not preserved as a complete original file.

The output is a newly rendered image. Keep your original file if you need animation, metadata or archival fidelity.
