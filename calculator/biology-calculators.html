Scope and responsible use

This directory organizes biology-related tools already published in the Friendly Calculator catalogue; it is not a claim to list every biology calculator worldwide. Coverage depends on current catalogue tags and available calculator pages. Results use the assumptions stated by each calculator and may be educational or approximate.

Animal-health, veterinary, medication, toxicity, nutrition and breeding results are informational only. Confirm health or treatment questions with a qualified veterinarian. Verify laboratory, ecological, agricultural and species-specific assumptions with appropriate scientific or local sources before relying on an estimate.
