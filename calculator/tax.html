Understanding tax and salary estimates

Tax and payroll calculations depend on the applicable financial year, assessment year, tax regime, income type, exemptions, deductions and individual eligibility. A calculator can help explain arithmetic and compare assumptions, but it cannot determine every person's final tax liability. Confirm the relevant year and rules before using an estimate for payroll, filing or a financial decision.

GST: inclusive and exclusive amounts

For an exclusive price, the GST amount is calculated on the taxable base amount at the rate entered. For an inclusive price, the tax portion is extracted from the total rather than added again. The calculator performs this arithmetic only: it does not classify a product or service, decide the correct GST rate, determine place of supply, or decide whether CGST/SGST or IGST applies. Check current official GST guidance and the applicable invoice details.

Income tax and salary TDS

Income-tax estimates require the correct tax year and regime. Actual tax can depend on salary components, other income, deductions, exemptions, rebates, surcharge, cess, special-rate income, residency and age-related rules. A simplified TDS estimate may not model every payroll adjustment or tax-credit situation. Compare the calculator assumptions with current Income Tax Department guidance and your employer's payroll records.

HRA and take-home salary

HRA exemption depends on eligible salary components, rent paid, location category and the applicable tax regime and rules. CTC is not the same as take-home salary: employer contributions, gratuity, insurance, variable pay, professional tax and income-tax withholding can affect the amount received. Review the salary structure and payslip rather than assuming every CTC component is paid monthly in cash.

Worked example: check an inclusive GST price

If a quoted price already includes GST, do not add the tax rate to that total a second time. Use the inclusive-price option, enter the total and the rate confirmed for the supply, then review the extracted taxable value and tax component. This example explains the calculation method; it does not determine the legally applicable rate for a particular product or service.

Official verification and corrections

For current rules, use the Income Tax Department and the GST portal. The relevant tool page explains its configured assumptions and exclusions.
