Finance and savings calculators: compare scenarios

Financial calculators help translate assumptions about borrowing, saving and investing into understandable estimates. They are most useful for comparing scenarios, such as how a different loan tenure changes an EMI or how regular contributions affect a long-term projection. They do not know your complete financial circumstances and should not be treated as a recommendation or guaranteed outcome.

Borrowing and repayment

The Loan EMI Calculator estimates a regular payment using principal, interest rate and tenure. Compare total repayment as well as the monthly instalment, and check lender fees, rate resets, insurance and prepayment conditions. A longer tenure may reduce EMI while increasing total interest.

Deposits and savings

The Fixed Deposit Calculator illustrates maturity under a selected rate and compounding assumption. The Savings Interest Calculator estimates interest from a balance and rate. Actual bank products can use different payout frequencies, daily balance methods, day counts, rounding and premature withdrawal rules. Verify the deposit advice and product terms before acting.

Long-term projections

SIP, PPF and EPF tools model different products and rules. SIP projections use assumed market returns, which are not guaranteed. PPF estimates depend on notified scheme rules and contribution timing. EPF balances depend on eligible wages, statutory allocation, service history and notified interest credits. Check official statements and current rules for account-specific figures.

Make comparisons responsibly

  • Use the same principal, contribution amount and time horizon when comparing scenarios.
  • Test more than one interest or return assumption.
  • Separate guaranteed contractual rates from market-linked assumptions.
  • Consider inflation, taxes, fees, liquidity and risk.
  • Verify account and product terms with the provider or official source.

These calculators provide estimates for learning and planning. They do not access bank, EPFO or mutual-fund accounts and do not replace professional financial advice.
