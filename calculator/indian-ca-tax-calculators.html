Indian CA Tax Calculators

A dedicated India-focused workflow for GST, Income Tax, TDS, salary and business-tax estimates. This hub reuses Friendly Calculator's existing tools so related calculations stay in one place without duplicate pages.

Year and scope notice: Income-tax examples on this hub reference the official AY 2026–27 guidance available when reviewed. GST rates, classifications, place-of-supply rules, TDS tables, state professional-tax rules and filing deadlines can change. Results are estimates from your inputs, not tax-return preparation or professional advice.

Suggested CA review sequence

1 · ClassifyConfirm taxpayer type, supply type, tax year, state and applicable regime.
2 · CalculateRun GST, salary, income-tax and TDS scenarios using documented inputs.
3 · ReconcileCompare with invoices, payroll records, Form 16, AIS/26AS and GST ledgers.
4 · VerifyCheck current notifications and obtain professional review before filing or payment.

Important limitations

These tools perform transparent arithmetic from user-entered assumptions. They do not establish eligibility, classify a supply, determine residential status, apply every exemption or special-rate income rule, prepare an ITR/GST return, or replace a CA, tax practitioner or official filing utility. Professional tax is state-specific. GST output also depends on rate, classification, place of supply, registration status and applicable notifications. Never enter PAN, Aadhaar, bank details, passwords or other sensitive information.
