PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Cookie Policy

Understand how cookies and similar technologies may be used on Friendly Calculator, why they matter and how you can manage them.

Home / Privacy / Cookies

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Friendly Calculator is designed with privacy-first defaults. Public calculators do not need cookies for their calculations, and optional analytics and advertising scripts are disabled by default.

Cookies, local storage and offline cache

  • Calculator inputs: processed in your browser; they are not deliberately stored in cookies or sent to the calculator server by ordinary public calculators.
  • Preferences: language, country, currency, units, theme, text size, contrast and motion preferences may be saved in browser local storage.
  • Favourites and recent tools: calculator page paths and titles may be stored locally. Query strings and entered calculator values are excluded.
  • Offline support: the service worker may cache public pages and static assets on your device. Private/admin/API paths are excluded from the public cache.

Analytics and advertising status

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, session replay and heat-map tracking are not loaded by the privacy-first site build. Google AdSense is behind a disabled-by-default gate and will not load until a suitable certified consent management platform (CMP) has been published and verified by the publisher, and the advertising gate is deliberately enabled. We do not claim that a certified CMP is currently active or that advertisements are being served.

How to clear browser data

You can remove locally saved preferences, favourites, recent calculator paths and offline cache through your browser's site-data or storage settings. Clearing site data may reset your language, country, currency, units and accessibility preferences, and may remove offline copies. It does not delete records held by an external provider or messages you independently sent by email.

Third-party websites

External links are controlled by their destination websites. If you open them, their cookies and privacy practices apply. This policy does not describe or control the storage used by external websites.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising or other optional third-party technologies are introduced, we will review the applicable consent requirements, update this policy and verify the actual live controls before enabling them.

Contact

For a general question about this policy, you may email the site owner voluntarily. No contact form is required.
