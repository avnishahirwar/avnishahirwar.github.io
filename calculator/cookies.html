What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third-party service may store in your browser or device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags and scripts. They can help a site remember settings, measure how pages are used, protect services and support advertising.

Types of cookies and similar technologies

1. Strictly necessary and functional technologies

These technologies may be used where needed to deliver a requested website feature, maintain basic security, prevent abuse or remember a choice you have made. If a feature does not require storage, we do not describe a cookie as necessary merely because a third-party script is present.

2. Analytics technologies

Friendly Calculator has Google Analytics 4 (GA4) configured for website measurement. Depending on browser settings and implementation, Google Analytics may use first-party cookies such as _ga and related identifiers to distinguish visits, understand usage patterns and measure site performance. Google Tag Manager is also loaded by the shared site shell on eligible pages to manage tags; Tag Manager is a tag-management service and does not, by itself, mean every tag or cookie is active.

We aim not to send calculator input values or sensitive form contents to analytics. Analytics configuration and identifiers can change; consult the Privacy Policy for more information about data handling.

3. Advertising technologies

Friendly Calculator includes Google AdSense publisher code. If Google approves the site and serves advertisements, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, ad personalisation. Advertising technologies may include identifiers associated with Google or DoubleClick services. The presence of AdSense code alone does not mean that ads are approved, currently being served or that a consent platform is active.

Personalised advertising and non-personalised advertising can involve different data uses. The options available depend on your location, the publisher account configuration, the consent signals received and applicable law. See How AdSense uses cookies and Google advertising technologies.

Third-party services

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google AdSense are Google services. Their technologies and processing are governed by Google's own documentation and policies, which may change over time. Review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

How to manage cookies

  • Browser controls: Most browsers let you view, block, restrict or delete cookies. Use your browser's privacy or site-settings menu for instructions specific to your device.
  • Google advertising settings: You can review personalisation choices at Google Ads Settings. This may not stop all advertising or all storage technologies.
  • Third-party choices: Learn about participating advertising providers' choices at aboutads.info.

Blocking or deleting cookies may affect some website features, analytics measurement or advertising. Browser controls are not a substitute for a legally required consent mechanism.
