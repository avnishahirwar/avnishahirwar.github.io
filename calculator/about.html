How the tools work

The main website calculators are designed to process entered values in the visitor's browser. Enquiry forms, where enabled, may transmit the information deliberately submitted by the visitor to the service backend so the enquiry can be reviewed and answered. Please do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment-card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive information.

Business and developer services

Potential business models include advertising, affiliate partnerships, premium tools, calculator widgets, white-label licensing, custom calculator development and API access. These are not all active services. API access is limited, subject to manual review and documented at the API documentation page. See Business Models & Partnerships for status information.

Accuracy and responsible use

Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values and assumptions entered. They are not professional tax, legal, accounting, lending, investment or medical advice. Verify important decisions with a qualified professional or the relevant provider.
