About Friendly Calculator

Friendly Calculator is a free set of calculators for everyday money, tax and planning questions in India: GST, loan EMI, fixed deposits, SIP, EPF, salary, income tax, TDS and more. Every calculator runs in your browser and comes with a plain-language guide, a formula and a worked example, so you can see how the answer is reached and not only the number.

Who runs it

The site is created and maintained by Avnish Ahirwar from Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. You can reach us on the contact page.

How we keep results accurate

  • Each calculator uses a standard, published formula, shown on its page.
  • The formulas are covered by automated tests that check known results, for example the EMI, FD and SIP examples in the guides.
  • Tax and GST pages state the rules they follow and are updated when the rules change. See methodology and sources.
  • If you find an error, tell us and we will check it against the formula and the official source.

Privacy

Calculator values are processed in your browser and are not uploaded. Only the details you choose to send through a contact form are transmitted. See the privacy policy.

How the site is funded

The calculators are free and need no account. The site may show advertising to cover running costs. Advertising never changes a calculation result, and we do not accept payment to influence results.

Accuracy and responsible use

Results are estimates based on the values and assumptions you enter. They are not professional tax, legal, accounting, lending, investment or medical advice. Verify important decisions with a qualified professional or the relevant provider.
