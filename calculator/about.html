About Friendly Calculator

Straightforward browser-based tools for everyday users, independent professionals and growing businesses.

Home / About Friendly Calculator

Friendly Calculator is an independent online calculator platform created by Avnish Ahirwar. It brings together 201 public calculators for common finance, tax, salary, business, mathematics and everyday tasks. The platform has an India-first focus and also offers general-purpose tools for visitors in other regions.

Creator and maintainer: Avnish Ahirwar · Platform: 201 public calculators · Approach: browser-based tools with published assumptions, formulas and limitations.

What Friendly Calculator is designed to do

Friendly Calculator helps users turn inputs into understandable estimates. Instead of presenting a number without context, the aim is to make each tool's purpose clear, show the values a visitor needs to enter, and explain the method or formula used where applicable. Visitors can compare scenarios by changing inputs and reviewing how the estimated result changes.

The website currently groups tools across several areas:

  • Personal finance: loan EMI, fixed deposits, recurring deposits, SIP and investment-return illustrations, retirement and savings estimates.
  • Tax and salary: GST, income-tax estimates, TDS, HRA, salary structure and other payroll-related calculations.
  • Business calculations: profit and loss, margins, break-even, cash flow, inventory, invoices and operating-cost estimates.
  • Maths and education: percentages, fractions, equations, statistics, geometry, number systems and grade-related calculations.
  • Everyday tools: age and date calculations, time conversions, unit conversions, fuel and electricity estimates, and other practical utilities.

Who can use Friendly Calculator?

The tools are intended for students, households, salaried employees, freelancers, small-business owners and anyone who needs to check a calculation. A visitor can open a relevant calculator page, enter their own figures, review the result and read the accompanying explanation. The website is designed to make common calculations easier to explore; it does not replace an accountant, tax professional, financial adviser, doctor, lawyer or other qualified professional.

How calculations are presented

Each calculator shows its inputs, formula or method and material assumptions where applicable. Outputs are estimates; rates, fees, rounding and eligibility rules can change. Review the calculation methodology for how results are produced and the site disclaimer for the single general statement of limitations.

Founder and the reason this site exists

Avnish Ahirwar — Founder and editor

Avnish is an MBA graduate, author and independent content creator with an interest in business development, practical research and digital publishing. He created Friendly Calculator to make common calculations easier to access and understand, without hiding the assumptions behind a result.

The project brings together practical calculators and plain-language guides so visitors can check a number, understand the method and explore related topics in one place.

Why Friendly Calculator was started: everyday decisions often involve calculations—loan costs, salary, taxes, savings, household bills and business margins. The goal is to make those calculations easier to check, explain the inputs and limitations clearly, and keep useful tools freely accessible.

Avnish maintains the site as an independent digital project. The site does not claim to be a bank, government department, accounting practice or regulated financial-advisory service. For corrections or feedback, use the Contact page.

Frequently asked questions

What is Friendly Calculator?

Friendly Calculator is an online platform offering browser-based calculators for finance, tax, salary, business, maths, conversions, dates and everyday calculations.

Is Friendly Calculator free to use?

Yes. The public calculators can currently be used without a paid subscription or account.

Are Friendly Calculator results exact?

Results are estimates calculated from the inputs and method shown on each tool page. They may differ from actual bank, tax, payroll, utility or investment outcomes because real-world rules, fees and rates can vary.

Does Friendly Calculator provide financial or tax advice?

No. The website provides calculators and general educational information. For the full limitations statement, read the site Disclaimer.

Can I use Friendly Calculator on a mobile phone?

Yes. The website is designed to be accessed through a web browser on mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers. The available input fields and page layout may vary by calculator.

How the tools work

The main website calculators are designed to process entered values in the visitor's browser. Enquiry forms, where enabled, may transmit the information deliberately submitted by the visitor to the service backend so the enquiry can be reviewed and answered. Please do not submit passwords, OTPs, payment-card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive information.

Using the website

Choose a calculator from the homepage or calculator directory, enter the values requested, and read the method and assumptions shown on that page. For deeper explanations, visit the Finance and Calculator Guides. If you spot an error, please send the page URL and a sample input that contains no personal or confidential information through the Contact page.

Accuracy and responsible use

Calculator outputs are estimates based on the values and assumptions entered. They are not professional tax, legal, accounting, lending, investment or medical advice. Verify important decisions with a qualified professional or the relevant provider.

Editorial approach

Articles are written to explain the calculation, assumptions and practical questions behind a topic. They link to relevant tools so readers can test their own inputs rather than rely on a generic example.

Sources, assumptions and corrections

Financial, tax and utility rules can change. Where a tool depends on external rules or rates, visitors should check the relevant official department, provider or current product terms before acting. The Calculation Methodology & Sources page explains how to interpret formulas and assumptions. If you notice a calculation issue or unclear instruction, contact us with the page URL and a sample input that does not contain personal or confidential information.

Navigation and accessibility

Visitors can reach the main tools from the homepage, browse all calculator pages in the Calculator Directory, and use the shared navigation and footer links to reach About, Methodology, Privacy and Terms. Calculator inputs are labelled to make their purpose clear, and important results should be reviewed before use.
