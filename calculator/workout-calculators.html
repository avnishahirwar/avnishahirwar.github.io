Workout Planning Calculators

Practical tools for planning workout duration, intervals, training splits and barbell loading.

This collection focuses on the planning math around a workout. It does not prescribe a specific programme or assess whether an exercise is safe for a particular person. Use the assumptions shown, adapt the numbers to your equipment and experience, and keep rest and recovery in mind.

Plan with reasonable limits

Workout mathematics can help organise a session, but it cannot evaluate technique, readiness, injury risk, medical conditions or recovery. Increase training gradually, use safe equipment, allow rest, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness. If you are pregnant or postpartum, returning after injury, managing a health condition, or unsure how to begin, seek individual advice from a qualified healthcare or exercise professional.

For younger users, a parent, guardian, teacher or appropriately qualified coach can help with age-appropriate activity. These tools are not a substitute for supervision. Read the calculation methodology and site disclaimer before relying on an estimate.
