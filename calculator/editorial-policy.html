Editorial Policy, Sources and Corrections

Friendly Calculator publishes browser-based calculators and educational explainers to help readers understand common calculations. Financial, tax, salary, GST, savings and borrowing topics can affect important decisions. We therefore aim for transparent assumptions, careful sourcing and clear limitations.

Publisher and responsibility

Friendly Calculator is an independent digital publishing project founded and edited by Avnish Ahirwar. The publisher is responsible for the content published on this website and for reviewing reported errors.

Our guides are educational explainers, not personalised tax, legal, accounting, lending or investment advice. We do not claim that the founder is a chartered accountant, tax practitioner, SEBI-registered investment adviser, lawyer, lender or other regulated financial professional. Unless a page explicitly identifies a real independent professional reviewer and their credentials, no such review should be inferred.

Research and primary sources

  • Prefer primary sources: government departments, regulators, statutory notifications, official scheme administrators and product documents.
  • Link to source material near the claim it supports wherever practical.
  • Distinguish official rules from calculator assumptions, hypothetical examples and editorial explanations.
  • State the relevant financial year, tax year, jurisdiction, rate date or product assumptions when they materially affect a result.
  • Avoid presenting projections as guaranteed outcomes. Market-linked investment values are uncertain; deposit and loan terms vary by provider and contract.
  • When a rule or rate may change, direct readers to the official source before acting.

Calculator methods and testing

Each calculator should explain its inputs, formula or calculation method, timing conventions, rounding and material exclusions. Worked examples should be reproducible from stated assumptions. We test calculation logic and site links during development, but a passed software test does not establish that every financial situation is modelled or that an institution will quote the same amount.

Read our Calculation Methodology for tool-specific assumptions and source portals. Actual tax liability, loan cost, salary credit, deposit maturity and investment value can differ because of law, product terms, fees, dates, rounding and personal circumstances.

Authorship and review limits

Articles are published by Friendly Calculator. Avnish Ahirwar is identified as founder and editor on the About page. A publisher byline identifies who is responsible for the page; it does not imply a regulated professional qualification or independent expert review. We do not create fictional authors, reviewers, testimonials, credentials or headshots.

If an independent specialist review is genuinely completed in future, the page will identify the reviewer, relevant credentials, review scope and date. Until then, treat the material as general educational content.

Financial and government-scheme (YMYL) page standard

Pages covering income tax, GST, salary, loans, investments, pensions, savings products and government schemes must make the calculation and its limits easy to inspect. Where relevant, each page should visibly provide:

  • The formula or calculation sequence, with units, timing and rounding conventions explained.
  • All material assumptions, including tax year, jurisdiction, rates, contribution timing, eligibility inputs and exclusions.
  • A reproducible worked example using clearly labelled hypothetical inputs.
  • A plain-language statement that results are educational estimates and are not financial, investment, tax, legal, accounting or lending advice.
  • Links to primary official sources such as the Income Tax Department, GST Portal/CBIC, RBI, SEBI, AMFI, PFRDA, EPFO, Ministry of Labour, or the relevant scheme administrator.
  • A truthful content update or review date. Updating a shared footer or disclosure does not mean every page's underlying formula or rate was independently revalidated.
  • A named author/publisher and an accurate description of credentials. Avnish Ahirwar is the Founder and Editor; this does not imply a regulated professional qualification or independent specialist review.

If a page lacks a material formula, assumption, example or current primary source, the page should state the limitation clearly and should not imply a complete or authoritative calculation.

Updates and corrections

We review content when material changes are identified or a reader reports a possible error. Substantive changes should be reflected in the visible update date and structured data. We do not change dates merely to make an old article appear new. If a correction changes a calculation or important factual statement, we aim to make the corrected method clear.

Report an issue through our Contact page. Include the page URL, statement or formula in question, and a source or reproducible example where possible. Do not send passwords, OTPs, bank credentials or sensitive identity information.

Advertising and commercial relationships

Friendly Calculator may display third-party advertising, including Google AdSense, and may add clearly identified affiliate or referral links. Advertising does not determine calculation formulas or editorial conclusions. Material commercial relationships should be disclosed near the relevant link or content. We do not sell favourable reviews or guarantee product outcomes.

What this site is—and is not

Friendly Calculator is a calculator and educational guide website, not a bank, tax-filing service, investment platform, regulator, accounting firm or professional advisory practice. Readers should confirm important decisions with official sources and qualified professionals who can review their individual circumstances.
