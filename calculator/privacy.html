Privacy Policy

How Friendly Calculator handles calculator inputs, enquiries, API access and advertising-related information.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Friendly Calculator handles information when you visit this website, submit an enquiry or use an approved API integration.

Calculator data

Calculator inputs and calculations on the main website are processed in your browser. These browser calculators are not designed to transmit the values you enter to Friendly Calculator servers.

Contact, advertising and API enquiry forms

When you submit a form, the information you provide is transmitted securely to our backend and stored so that we can review and respond to your enquiry. Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, company or project name, optional phone or budget details, enquiry type and message. Enquiries are accessible only to authorised administrators.

We use a hashed connection identifier to help prevent repeated automated submissions. It is not stored as a plain IP address. Older rate-limit entries are cleaned during subsequent form submissions, targeting a 48-hour window.

Please do not include passwords, OTPs, API secrets, payment-card details, government identity numbers or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

API credentials and usage

For approved API access, Friendly Calculator stores a cryptographic hash and short prefix of each API key, not the original secret key. The original key is shown only once when created. API usage records may include the key identifier, calculator endpoint, response status and timestamp; request payloads are not stored for usage analytics. Usage records are routinely cleaned when API requests occur, targeting a 90-day retention window.

API keys are intended for server-side use. Keep them confidential and contact us promptly if a key may have been exposed.

Hosting and data processors

The source code is maintained on GitHub. The hosting provider for a live deployment may be GitHub Pages or another configured hosting provider. Enquiry storage, authentication and API processing use Supabase. The Supabase project is configured in the ap-northeast-2 region (Seoul, South Korea). Business enquiry forms on the Contact page are embedded and processed through HubSpot (portal 246395822); information you choose to submit in that form is transmitted to HubSpot so the enquiry can be received and managed. Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is the only analytics service configured on this website. Yandex Metrica, Webvisor session replay and Yandex click-map tracking have been removed. GA4 may process technical and usage information such as page views, device and browser details, approximate location, traffic source and interactions. We do not intentionally send calculator input values, salary figures, tax amounts, BMI measurements, form contents, passwords or other sensitive information to GA4. Google AdSense publisher code is present as described below; advertising may involve additional cookies or similar technologies if ads are enabled. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider privacy notices.

For more information, see HubSpot’s Privacy Policy, Google’s Privacy Policy and Supabase’s Privacy Policy.

Premium waitlist

If you register interest through the Premium Waitlist, we collect the name, email address, selected plan and any optional product needs you choose to provide. We use these details to record and respond to your request about possible Premium availability. Joining the waitlist does not create an account, subscription, payment obligation or guaranteed offer. The submission is stored in our enquiry database with a submission timestamp. Please do not include passwords, payment details, government identity numbers or other sensitive information.

The option to receive a separate email when Premium availability is announced is optional and is not pre-selected. If you select it, the selected choice and the wording version shown are recorded with your enquiry. You may withdraw this email-update permission by contacting us using the address in the Retention and privacy requests section. A waitlist request without this optional choice is used only to respond to the request and is not treated as permission for general marketing.

Optional email updates

Marketing messages will be sent only where you have separately opted in. Submitting a general enquiry does not automatically subscribe you. Each marketing email will provide an unsubscribe method, and you may withdraw consent. Newsletter signup is not currently active.

Affiliate links, referrals and sponsored placements

If approved affiliate links, sponsored placements or referral partnerships are introduced, they will be identified clearly. Clicking an affiliate link may allow the partner to record that a referral came from this website using cookies or similar technologies. Any personal information you provide to a partner is handled under that partner's privacy policy. We do not sell visitors' personal data as lists, and we do not share enquiry details with a referral partner unless you explicitly ask us to make an introduction or otherwise consent.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand aggregate website usage, including page views, device and browser information, approximate location, traffic sources and selected interactions. GA4 may use cookies or similar technologies. Yandex Metrica, Webvisor session replay and Yandex click-map tracking are not used. Calculator input values are not required for analytics and must not be sent to GA4. Our configured calculator events record the calculator page/name and the fact that a calculation button was used; search measurement records the length of a search phrase rather than the phrase itself. We do not intentionally send salary amounts, tax figures, BMI measurements, loan amounts, form contents, passwords, email addresses or other sensitive personal information to GA4. Review Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s guidance on avoiding personally identifiable information in Analytics.
