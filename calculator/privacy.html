PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

How public calculators keep inputs on your device and minimise data collection.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Friendly Calculator is designed so that visitors can use public calculators without creating an account or providing personal information. Privacy is the default: calculator inputs and calculations are processed in the browser on your device.

Public calculator use

For ordinary public calculators, values you type are processed locally in your browser. The calculator does not need your name, email address, phone number, precise location, government ID, bank details or health records. Do not enter real passwords, account credentials or other sensitive information into any public calculator.

Recent calculator history and favourites, where available, are stored in your browser using local storage. Only page paths and calculator titles are saved; query strings and calculator input values are deliberately excluded. Language, country, currency, unit and accessibility preferences may also be stored locally so the site can remember choices on that device. These browser records are not sent to Friendly Calculator by the calculator interface. Clearing this site's browser storage removes those saved preferences and local lists.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

As of this update, the privacy-first site build does not load Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, session replay, heat-map tracking or embedded third-party marketing forms by default. The site does not intentionally transmit calculator input values, search phrases or personal details to an analytics provider.

Google AdSense publisher code is held behind a disabled-by-default gate. Advertising code will not load unless the publisher has first verified and published an appropriate certified consent management platform (CMP), and deliberately enabled the advertising gate. The presence of an advertising integration in source code is not a statement that ads are currently served. If advertising or analytics is introduced later, this policy and the live consent experience must be updated and verified before activation.

Cookies and browser storage

The public calculator experience is designed not to require advertising or analytics cookies. The site may use browser local storage for preferences, favourites and recently viewed calculator page paths, and a service worker cache for public pages and offline support. These are stored by your browser on your device. See the Cookie Policy for details and browser controls.

Optional email support

You can use the public website without contacting us or sharing personal information. If you choose the email-support link, your own email application opens and you decide whether to send a message and what information it contains. The website does not embed a third-party form or automatically collect contact details through that page. Any email you voluntarily send is received and handled through the email provider you use and the recipient's mailbox; do not include sensitive information.

Hosting and technical security

Like most websites, the hosting, content-delivery and security infrastructure may process basic connection information (such as IP address, request time, requested URL, browser headers and error details) to deliver pages, prevent abuse and maintain service reliability. These technical logs are controlled by the relevant infrastructure provider and are not calculator inputs. We do not use precise geolocation to identify visitors. Browser-based regional defaults may use language and region settings exposed by your browser; you can change country, currency, language and units in site settings.

Optional API and account services

Public calculators do not require an account. Separate optional API, account or premium services may process credentials, account identifiers or usage records if a visitor deliberately chooses to use those services. Such services are not needed for ordinary calculator use. Do not submit personal or confidential information unless an optional service clearly explains why it is required and how it is handled.

External links

Some pages may link to external websites or official sources. When you follow an external link, that provider's privacy policy and data practices apply. Friendly Calculator does not control third-party websites.

Data minimisation and no sale of visitor data

We do not sell visitors' personal information. Public calculator functionality is designed to work without collecting personal details. We aim to request no more information than a feature genuinely needs. No website can promise that hosting infrastructure will never process connection metadata, and visitors should review the privacy practices of their browser, network and any external service they choose to use.

Privacy questions

For a privacy question, you may send an optional email to the site owner. You do not need to provide an identity document or sensitive personal information to ask a general question. If you refer to a specific message you previously sent, include only the minimum details needed to locate it.

Changes

We will update this page when data practices, optional services or applicable requirements change. The current version and revision date appear above.
