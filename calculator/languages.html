How calculator translation is expanded

  1. Publish and review the locale homepage, navigation, metadata and language selector.
  2. Translate calculator terminology, formulas, units, assumptions, FAQs and structured data together.
  3. Verify reciprocal hreflang, canonical URLs, sitemap entries and calculator behavior.
  4. Expand the locale only when the translated page has passed formula and metadata checks.

Translation and correction policy

Locale homepages are live entry points, but a page count is not a claim that every calculator has been translated. Translated pages must preserve formulas, units, assumptions, safety notices and source links. Report a wrong translation or missing high-value page through the contact page. See the editorial policy and calculation methodology.
