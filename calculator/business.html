RESEARCH PRODUCTS

If privacy cannot be reliably protected, do not use the data. This reporting product is not currently active.

REVENUE STACKING & GROWTH

Stacked monetisation strategy

A calculator platform can combine several revenue channels around one useful free tool. The sequence below is a strategy framework—not a promise of earnings or a statement that every channel is live.

STACK 1 · AUDIENCE

Free tools with a clear user intent

Attract visitors with accurate, fast calculators and useful explanations. Prioritise finance, tax, salary and business use cases where a visitor's next question is closely related to a service or workflow.

STACK 2 · ADVERTISING

Display ads with UX safeguards

Use approved ad networks and restrained placements. Avoid ads between calculator inputs and results, intrusive overlays, deceptive native ads or layouts that shift the result. Protect page speed, accessibility and readability.

STACK 3 · AFFILIATE

Intent-matched recommendations

Show clearly disclosed, relevant partner resources after the user receives their result. Do not infer creditworthiness, investment suitability or medical needs from calculator inputs. Avoid unapproved broker, loan or financial-product claims.

STACK 4 · PREMIUM

Paid tiers for repeat workflows

Possible upgrades include saved scenarios, report exports, bulk calculations, team access and advanced assumptions. International examples such as US$6–$10/month are only market-reference ideas; no paid tier or checkout is active.

STACK 5 · API

Developer API access

Offer documented, rate-limited API plans with usage metrics, support levels and clear terms. A US$29/month price point is only an illustrative concept—not a live offer or confirmed market price. API access remains subject to approval.

STACK 6 · B2B

Sponsorship and white-label licensing

Sell fixed-scope sponsorship packages or license branded calculators to organisations when audience quality, product reliability and support capacity are established. Label sponsorships and preserve independent results.

STACK 7 · CONSENT

Qualified lead introductions

Capture only necessary information after explaining the receiving partner and purpose. Obtain explicit consent before sharing. Financial-product referrals must be limited to eligible adults and appropriately authorised providers.

SEO STRATEGY

Long-tail, task-specific search intent

Create useful pages for specific needs, such as mortgage payment estimates including taxes, rather than repeating broad keywords. Each page should include original explanations, transparent assumptions, formulas, worked examples and limitations.

Evaluate finance, loan, tax, salary and business tools first based on search demand, accuracy and partner suitability. Health tools require careful disclaimers and must not make diagnostic claims or promote supplements.
