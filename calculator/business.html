CONTEXTUAL AFFILIATES

Calculator-result recommendations

Offer relevant, clearly disclosed partner resources alongside results: loan education after EMI calculations, regulated investment-platform information after SIP examples, and insurance or tax-filing resources on relevant pages. Do not imply endorsement, guaranteed approval or guaranteed returns.

OPT-IN LEADS

Consented partner introductions

Visitors may explicitly request an introduction to an appropriately authorised provider. Explain who receives the enquiry, what data is shared and why before consent. Never sell contact lists or preselect consent. Regulated credit and financial-product leads must be restricted to eligible adults and compliant partners.

PREMIUM SAAS

Paid reports and advanced tools

Keep basic calculators free; validate demand for downloadable reports, saved history, scenario comparisons and business workflows before building checkout. Publish clear pricing, cancellation, refund and data-retention terms before accepting payment.

SEO & LOCALISATION

Hindi and English search strategy

Create genuinely useful English and Hindi calculator pages, examples, FAQs and explanations. Target intent-led phrases such as EMI calculator in Hindi, GST calculator and SIP calculator. Avoid doorway pages, duplicated translations and unsupported ranking promises.

EXIT OPTION

Website or asset sale

A mature calculator business may be sold as a digital asset if its traffic, earnings, ownership, code, analytics and compliance records are verifiable. Valuation depends on sustainable profit, growth, risk and buyer demand; monthly-profit multiples are not guaranteed.

Important commercial and safety rules: Any loan, credit-card, investment, insurance or tax-service referral must use properly authorised partners, transparent disclosures and adult-only eligibility checks where required. Do not target minors with credit products, collect sensitive financial details in general enquiry forms, promise loan approval or investment returns, or recommend supplements. Health-related links should be limited to general wellness or fitness services with appropriate disclaimers. All affiliate relationships and sponsored placements must be clearly labelled.
