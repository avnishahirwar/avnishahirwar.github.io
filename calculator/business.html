GROWTH & ACTIVATION PLAN

A focused path to sustainable growth

Rather than repeating multiple revenue forecasts and growth claims, we will use one practical sequence. Progress will be measured using real site and product data.

1. Build trust and usefulnessKeep calculators accurate, fast and mobile-friendly. Publish original explanations, transparent formulas, worked examples, limitations and useful internal links.
2. Measure real audience behaviourVerify indexing and use privacy-conscious analytics to understand visits, engagement, calculator usage and performance before making revenue assumptions.
3. Activate suitable channels carefullyApply to eligible ad and affiliate programmes, validate paid-feature demand, then expand API and B2B services when support and billing are ready.
Permanent catalogue policy

Business models listed on this page are retained as part of the Friendly Calculator platform roadmap. New models may be added. A model may be labelled planned, paused, unavailable or retired, but it must not be deleted from the catalogue without the owner’s explicit instruction.

Transparency, privacy and compliance

We will not promise traffic, search rankings, advertising RPM, commissions or income. Partner referrals must be clearly disclosed and shared only with appropriate consent. We do not sell personal contact lists or raw calculator inputs. Financial-product referrals must use appropriately authorised providers and comply with applicable age and eligibility requirements. Calculator results are estimates, not professional advice.
