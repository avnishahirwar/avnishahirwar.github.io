About the founder

Avnish Ahirwar is an MBA graduate, author and independent content creator interested in business development, practical research and digital publishing. He founded Friendly Calculator as an independent project focused on making useful calculations easier to explore, with clear methods, assumptions and limitations.

His approach is grounded in useful problem-solving: understand the question, make the method visible, keep the experience straightforward and improve the product through feedback and testing.

Why Friendly Calculator exists

Everyday decisions often involve numbers — from salary and taxes to loan estimates, savings, household expenses and business margins. Friendly Calculator brings practical tools and plain-language explanations together so people can check an estimate and understand how it was produced.

Practical utility

Tools designed around real questions people need to calculate.

Clarity

Visible formulas, assumptions and limitations wherever applicable.

Access

Public calculators kept freely accessible, without a paid gate.

Building with clear principles

  • Prioritise usefulness over unnecessary complexity.
  • Explain estimates honestly; never present them as guaranteed outcomes.
  • Use credible sources for rules, rates and changing information.
  • Test calculations and improve the experience across screen sizes.
  • Keep educational content accessible and credit its original source.

Friendly Calculator is an independent digital project, not a bank, government department or regulated financial-advisory service. Calculator results are estimates and should be checked against relevant official or professional guidance before important decisions.
